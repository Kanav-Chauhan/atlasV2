## Company

- CIN: U72200PN2009PTC134521 (fictional). Registered office: 7th Floor, Tower B,
  Panchshil Tech Park, Hinjewadi Phase 2, Pune 411057, Maharashtra.

  monitoring SaaS, ~140 customers).

- Brand colour: sky blue #1E88E5 with navy #0D2B4E. Tagline: "Engineering clarity."

## Key facts (use these exact values)
- Working week: Monday–Friday, 9.5 hours/day including breaks; hybrid: 3 office days/week.
- Leave (FY): 18 earned leave, 10 casual/sick leave, 12 public holidays (list varies by
  city), 26 weeks maternity (per Maternity Benefit Act), 2 weeks paternity. Up to 30 EL
  can be carried forward; EL beyond 30 lapses on 31 March.
- Notice period: 60 days (90 days for Senior Manager and above).
- Grades: L1 Trainee, L2 Software Engineer, L3 Senior SE, L4 Tech Lead, L5 Eng Manager /
  Architect, L6 Senior Manager / Principal, L7 Director. (Salary bands ONLY in Compensation.)
- Salary bands (₹ lakh per annum, fixed): L2 6–10, L3 10–18, L4 18–28, L5 28–42,
  L6 42–60, L7 60–90. FY26-27 merit budget 9.5% average; variable pay 10–20% of CTC.
- Password policy: 14+ characters, MFA mandatory, rotation only on compromise.
  Laptops: BitLocker/FileVault, auto-lock 5 min. Phishing reports: phish@nimbus.example.
- Incident severity: SEV1 (customer-wide outage, 15-min response, CISO + CTO paged),
  SEV2 (degraded, 30 min), SEV3 (single customer, 4 h), SEV4 (next business day).
- NimbusOps SLO: 99.9% monthly availability; RPO 15 min, RTO 1 h. Primary region AWS
  ap-south-1 (Mumbai), DR ap-south-2 (Hyderabad). Postgres 16 + Kafka + Kubernetes (EKS).
- Key clients (fictional): Harbor Logistics Inc. (US, $4.2M/yr, cloud migration),
  Zenith Retail (India, ₹38 crore, SAP S/4HANA), Al Noor Healthcare (UAE, managed SOC),
  Crestline Insurance (US, data platform). Harbor renewal due 30 Sept 2026.
- Board: met 14 May 2026; approved FY27 budget of ₹1,340 crore revenue target, a
  ₹45 crore Hyderabad campus expansion, and an ESOP pool top-up of 1.5%.
- Travel: domestic economy; international economy under 8 hours flight time, premium
  economy above. Daily allowance: metro ₹3,500, non-metro ₹2,500, international USD 75.
