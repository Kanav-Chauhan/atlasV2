# Bible: Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. (org slug `sanjeevani`)

## Company
- Mid-size Indian generics and branded-formulations company. Founded 1994 in Ahmedabad.
  Public limited (fictional; not actually listed anywhere real).
- CIN: L24230GJ1994PLC022817 (fictional). Registered office: Sanjeevani House,
  Plot 41, Sarkhej–Gandhinagar Highway, Ahmedabad 380054, Gujarat.
- Manufacturing sites: **Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar)**: oral solids (tablets, capsules),
  WHO-GMP and Schedule M compliant; **Unit II – Baddi, Himachal Pradesh**: oral liquids and
  ointments; **Unit III – Vizag (Visakhapatnam) SEZ**: export unit, US FDA inspected
  (2024 inspection: 3 Form 483 observations; Feb 2026 re-inspection: none). R&D centre: Hyderabad (Genome Valley).
- Employees: 2,850. FY2025-26 revenue ₹2,460 crore (domestic 58%, exports 42%).
  R&D spend 6.8% of revenue. ~180 field sales reps ("medical representatives") in India.
- Brand colour: deep teal #00796B with saffron accent #F9A825. Tagline: "Healing, made
  dependable."

## Key facts
- Leave: 21 privilege leave, 7 casual, 7 sick per calendar year; shift allowance at plants
  ₹2,800/month for night shift. Plant safety: PPE mandatory; gowning for Grade C/D areas.
- Sales: FY27 domestic target ₹1,520 crore; monthly call target 180 doctor visits per rep;
  doctor engagement must follow the UCPMP 2024 code (no gifts; sample limits). Sample
  allowance: max 10 strips per doctor per month for Glucosan-M.
- Glucosan-M 500 storage: below 30°C, protect from moisture; shelf life 36 months.
  Cough syrup shelf life 24 months; Dermacalm 30 months.
- Dissolution spec Glucosan-M 500 SR: not less than 80% (Q) released in 10 hours.
  Assay limit 95.0–105.0%. Batch size 3,00,000 tablets.
- Cleanroom: Grade D limit 3,520,000 particles ≥0.5 µm per m³ (at rest); temperature
  20–25°C, RH 45–60%. Purified water conductivity ≤ 1.3 µS/cm at 25°C.
- Deviation classes: Critical (QA head within 24 h), Major (within 3 working days), Minor
  (within 7 days). CAPA effectiveness check at 90 days.
- 2024 US FDA inspection of Unit III: 3 Form 483 observations (data-integrity audit trail
  review, cleaning validation for shared equipment, OOS investigation timeliness); all CAPAs
  closed by Oct 2024. Feb 2026 re-inspection: no observations (NAI).
- SJ-2041 Phase III trial (protocol SPL/CLN/PRT-017, CTRI-registered, fictional number
  CTRI/2025/03/061842): 1,240 patients, 38 sites in India, 24 weeks; primary endpoint
  HbA1c change from baseline; interim result −1.1% vs −0.6% comparator; dropout 7.4%;
  2 serious adverse events judged unrelated. Topline readout expected Jan 2027.
- Recall: Cough syrup batch CR-2309 voluntarily recalled Aug 2025 (label misprint, no
  safety impact), 18,400 bottles.
- Board (22 April 2026): approved ₹310 crore Vizag capacity expansion, dividend ₹4.50/share,
  and in-licensing talks for a biosimilar insulin (code BIO-INS-7).
