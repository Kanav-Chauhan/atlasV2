- SEBI-registered (fictional registration) asset manager and wealth advisory firm.
  Founded 2012 in Bengaluru. Manages mutual-fund-style schemes, a PMS (portfolio
  management service) and an AIF Category III long-short fund.
- CIN: U67190KA2012PTC065309 (fictional). SEBI PMS Reg. No. INP000006120 (fictional).
  Registered office: 12th Floor, Prestige Meridian II, MG Road, Bengaluru 560001, Karnataka.
  Branches: Mumbai (BKC), New Delhi (Connaught Place), Chennai, Pune.
- AUM (31 March 2026): ₹18,650 crore. Clients: 41,000 retail investors, 1,150 PMS clients,

## Key facts
- Leave: 20 privilege, 8 casual, 10 sick per calendar year. Mandatory block leave: 10
  consecutive working days per year for dealers and fund managers (fraud control).
- Personal trading: pre-clearance required; 30-day minimum holding; blackout on any stock
  on the restricted list; violations reported to CCO within 1 working day.
- KYC: CKYC lookup + PAN verification; re-KYC every 2 years for high-risk, 8 years for
  low-risk clients. PEP clients need CCO approval. Cash not accepted. STR to FIU-IND within
  7 days of suspicion being established.
- Risk limits: single stock max 10% of scheme NAV; sector max 25% (except banks 35% for
  Bluechip); liquidity: at least 15% of Small Cap portfolio liquidatable in 5 days at 30%
  participation; VaR (95%, 1-day) limit 2.5% for equity schemes.
- Q4 FY26 risk report: Small Cap fund liquidity 11.8% (BREACH of 15% floor; remediation
  plan: trim 6 illiquid names by 30 June 2026). Bluechip VaR 1.9%.
- Investment Committee (IC) meets monthly; IC 47 (12 March 2026) approved adding Indian
  Railways capex theme; IC 48 (9 April 2026) approved exit from "Vindhyagiri Cementworks" (fictional)
  in Small Cap due to governance concerns.
- Research coverage: 142 stocks; house view (April 2026): Nifty 50 year-end target 26,800;
  overweight banks and capital goods, underweight IT services and FMCG.
- Finance: FY26 revenue ₹212 crore, PAT ₹58 crore; FY27 budget revenue ₹248 crore,
  employee cost ₹96 crore, tech spend ₹14 crore. Audit firm: "Raghavan & Mehta LLP"
  (fictional), clean opinion.
- Board (25 April 2026): approved FY27 budget, a new GIFT City feeder fund (target launch
  Jan 2027), interim dividend ₹12 crore, and appointment of an independent director,
  Justice (retd.) S. Venugopal (fictional).
- Client agreement: PMS fee 2.0% p.a. fixed OR 1.0% + 20% profit share above 10% hurdle
  (high-water mark); exit load 3% in year 1, 2% year 2, 1% year 3.
