## 2. Match by investor need

### "I have surplus cash for a few months"
- **Kaveri Arbitrage Fund.** Low volatility, equity taxation, 1-year return of 7.3% (direct). Remind the client of the 0.25% exit load within 15 days.

### "I want steady income, better than a fixed deposit, for 1–3 years"
- **Kaveri Short Duration Fund.** YTM 6.94%, modified duration 2.34 years, over 87% in sovereign and AAA paper. Explain that NAV can fall if interest rates rise.

### "I am a first-time equity investor"
- **Kaveri Bluechip Fund** via SIP from ₹500 a month. Large caps, 48 stocks, 1-year return 14.2% vs 12.9% for the Nifty 100 TRI.

### "I want one core equity fund for the long term"
- **Kaveri Flexi Cap Fund.** Manager can move across market caps. 1-year return 17.6% vs 15.1% for the Nifty 500 TRI.

### "I can take high volatility for higher long-term growth"
- **Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities**, ideally as a satellite of no more than 15–20% of the equity allocation. Note that the fund underperformed its benchmark over 1 year (22.4% vs 24.8%) and that lump sums are capped at ₹2 lakh per PAN per day; SIPs are unrestricted.

### "I am an HNI and want a concentrated, personally held portfolio"
- **Kaveri Focused Equity PMS** (minimum ₹50 lakh). Fee choice: 2.0% fixed, or 1.0% plus 20% of returns above a 10% hurdle with a high-water mark.

### "I am a sophisticated investor seeking lower correlation with the market"
- **Kaveri Long-Short AIF III** (minimum ₹1 crore). Fee: 1.5% management plus 15% performance fee over hurdle. Only for accredited or eligible investors; refer to the Private Placement Memorandum.

## 5. Suitability red flags

Stop and escalate to the branch head if:

- the client wants to invest in Small Cap with money needed within 3 years;
- a senior citizen wants to move more than 50% of their savings into equity in one go;
- the client asks for "guaranteed" returns from any equity, PMS or AIF product;
- the client offers cash – **cash is never accepted** for any Kaveri product;
- the client is, or is related to, a politically exposed person (PEP) – onboarding needs Compliance approval.

## 6. Things you must say (and must not say)

**Always:**
- "Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully."
- Quote returns only in the SEBI-prescribed format, with benchmark returns alongside.

**Never:**
- Promise or imply guaranteed returns.
- Compare a Kaveri fund with a fixed deposit as if they carry the same risk.
- Share portfolio holdings beyond what is in the published factsheet.
