Answers to the questions our investor service team hears most often. This page covers Kaveri's mutual-fund-style schemes (Bluechip, Flexi Cap, Small Cap Opportunities, Short Duration and Arbitrage), the Kaveri Focused Equity PMS and the Kaveri Long-Short AIF III.

*Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.*

## 1. Buying and redeeming

**What is the cut-off time for same-day NAV?**
3:00 pm on business days, for both equity and debt schemes. Redemptions received before 3:00 pm get that day's NAV. For purchases, the money must also be credited to the scheme's bank account before 3:00 pm; otherwise the next business day's NAV applies.

**I placed a redemption on Saturday. Which NAV will I get?**
The next business day's NAV (normally Monday's).

**How long does a redemption take to reach my bank account?**
Within 3 working days of the redemption date for all schemes. For the Arbitrage and Short Duration funds we usually pay within 2 working days. If we are late beyond the regulatory timeline, we pay interest at 15% per annum for each day of delay.

**Where is my redemption money paid?**
Only to the bank account registered in your folio. We never pay redemption proceeds to a third-party account.

**Can I pay or invest in cash?**
No. Kaveri does not accept cash for any investment, in any amount. Please pay by cheque, NEFT, RTGS, UPI or mandate from a bank account in your own name.

## 2. Exit loads

**What exit load applies to the equity funds?**

| Scheme | Exit load |
|---|---|
| Kaveri Bluechip Fund | 1% if redeemed within 365 days of allotment |
| Kaveri Flexi Cap Fund | 1% if redeemed within 365 days of allotment |
| Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities | 1% if redeemed within 365 days of allotment |
| Kaveri Short Duration Fund | Nil |
| Kaveri Arbitrage Fund | 0.25% if redeemed within 15 days of allotment |

**Is there an exit load if I redeem my Flexi Cap units after 8 months?**
Yes. Eight months is within 365 days of allotment, so an exit load of 1% applies to those units. Units held for more than 365 days carry no exit load.

**How is the exit load worked out if I invested through a SIP?**
Each SIP instalment is treated separately on a first-in, first-out basis. When you redeem, the oldest units are redeemed first, and only units still within 365 days of their own allotment date carry the 1% load.

**Does a switch between Kaveri funds attract exit load?**
Yes. A switch is treated as a redemption from the first scheme for exit load purposes.

**Are dividend reinvestment units charged exit load?**
No.

**What is the exit load on the PMS?**

| Withdrawal within (from date of each contribution) | Exit load |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | 3% |
| Year 2 | 2% |
| Year 3 | 1% |
| After 3 years | Nil |

**And on the AIF?**
The Kaveri Long-Short AIF III has a 12-month lock-in from each contribution. After the lock-in, an exit load of 1% applies in months 13–24 and nil after that. Redemptions are monthly with 30 days' notice.

**I did not receive my statement. What should I do?**
Check your spam folder first, then email service@kaveri.example from your registered email address. We resend within 2 working days.

**How do I change my bank account, address or nominee?**
Submit the change request form with supporting proof through the website, the app or any Kaveri branch. Bank account changes need a cancelled cheque or bank statement in your name and take up to 10 working days, during which redemptions are paid to the existing verified account.

## 4. KYC

**Why is my folio on "KYC on hold"?**
Your periodic re-KYC was not completed within 90 days of the due date. Redemptions continue to be paid to your registered bank account, but new purchases and SIP instalments are blocked until you complete re-KYC.

## 5. How to raise a complaint

1. Email service@kaveri.example or call our toll-free number **1800-425-0112** (9:00 am to 7:00 pm, Monday to Saturday), or visit any branch: Bengaluru, Mumbai (BKC), New Delhi (Connaught Place), Chennai or Pune.
2. We acknowledge every complaint **within 1 working day** with a complaint ID and resolve it **within 21 calendar days**.
3. If you are not satisfied, write to our Grievance Redressal Officer (Head – Client Services).
4. You can also lodge a complaint on **SEBI SCORES**, and after that use the **SMART ODR** portal for conciliation and arbitration.

We publish our complaints data on the website by the 7th of each month.
