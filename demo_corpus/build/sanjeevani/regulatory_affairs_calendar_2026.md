Working calendar of recurring and one-off regulatory deadlines for calendar year 2026, maintained by the Regulatory Affairs team at the Ahmedabad head office. Owners must update the status column on the team wiki every Friday. Any deadline at risk must be escalated to Pooja Desai at least **10 working days** before the due date.

## 1. How to read this calendar

- **Owner** is the accountable person; the RA executive supporting them is listed in the RA team roster.
- **Status**: Done / On track / At risk / Not started.
- Internal due dates are set 5 working days before the external (statutory) deadline.

## 2. Recurring obligations

| Obligation | Authority | Frequency | Statutory deadline | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Periodic Safety Update Reports (PSUR) for products within 4 years of approval | CDSCO | Every 6 months for first 2 years, then annually | 30 days after data lock point | Pharmacovigilance lead |
| Manufacturing licence retention fee (Form 25 licences) | State Licensing Authorities | Every 5 years | Before 5-year anniversary of grant/retention | Nirav Pandya |
| WHO-GMP certificate / COPP renewal | State FDCA with CDSCO | Every 3 years | 3 months before expiry (application) | Site RA, Unit I |
| ANDA annual reports | US FDA | Annually | Within 60 days of the approval anniversary date | US RA lead (Unit III) |
| US establishment registration and drug listing | US FDA | Annually | Between 01 October and 31 December | US RA lead (Unit III) |
| GDUFA facility fees (FY2027) | US FDA | Annually | 01 October 2026 | Finance + US RA lead |
| Drug Master File (DMF) annual update to API suppliers' letters of authorisation | US FDA | Annually | Rolling | US RA lead |
| NPPA price revision (WPI-linked) for scheduled formulations | NPPA | Annually | Effective 01 April; price lists to stockists within 15 days | Pricing manager |
| Form V (price list) filings for new launches | NPPA | Per launch | Before launch | Pricing manager |
| CTRI trial status updates (SJ-2041) | ICMR – CTRI | When status changes | Within 30 days of change | Clinical operations |
| Export NOCs for unapproved products (export-only) | CDSCO zonal office | Per consignment / per order | Before shipment | Site RA, Unit III |
