## 1. Purpose and Scope

This manual sets the safety rules for everyone working in the Sanjeevani R&D centre, Genome Valley, Hyderabad: formulation, analytical, process chemistry and clinical pharmacology labs, the pilot plant and stores. It also applies to students, interns, contractors and visitors. Plant safety at manufacturing units is covered separately in the Plant Safety and EHS Policy.

## 2. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| President, R&D | Overall accountability for R&D safety |
| R&D Safety Officer | Inspections, incident investigation, training records |
| Lab In-charge (each lab) | Daily safety checks, chemical inventory, enforcement |
| Every scientist | Follows this manual; reports incidents and near-misses |
| R&D Safety Committee | Meets monthly; reviews incidents and inspection findings |

## 3. Basic Rules

- No work alone after 7:00 pm or on holidays without the "lone worker" permit approved by the Lab In-charge; a second person must be in the building.
- No food, drinks, chewing gum or cosmetics in labs. Mobile phones only in the designated zone near the lab entrance.
- Long hair tied back; closed shoes; no shorts or sandals.
- Know the location of the nearest safety shower, eyewash, fire extinguisher and spill kit before starting work.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

| Activity | Minimum PPE |
|---|---|
| General lab work | Lab coat, safety glasses with side shields, nitrile gloves |
| Handling corrosives (acids, bases) | Chemical splash goggles, face shield for > 1 L, acid-resistant apron |
| Potent compounds (OEB 4, e.g. SPL-DP41 API powder) | Work in a containment isolator or ventilated balance enclosure; double gloves; P3 respirator if outside containment |
| Cryogenic liquids | Cryogenic gloves, face shield |
| Pilot plant | Safety shoes, helmet, ear plugs above 85 dB(A) |

## 5. Fume Hoods and Ventilation

- All volatile, toxic or flammable solvent work must be done in a fume hood.
- Required face velocity: **0.4–0.6 m/s** at a sash height of 450 mm. Face velocity is checked every **6 months** by Engineering and labelled on the hood.
- Do not store chemicals permanently in fume hoods. Keep the sash at the marked working height.
- If the hood alarm sounds, stop work, close containers, lower the sash and inform the Lab In-charge.

## 6. Chemical Storage and Handling

| Class | Storage rule | Maximum in lab |
|---|---|---|
| Flammable solvents (methanol, acetonitrile, hexane) | Flammable storage cabinet, ventilated | 2 L working quantity on the bench; 50 L per cabinet |
| Acids | Acid cabinet, separate from bases and solvents | 10 L |
| Oxidisers (peroxides, nitrates) | Separate cabinet, away from organics | 5 kg |
| Toxic / potent APIs | Locked cabinet, logbook for each withdrawal | As per project need |
| Compressed gas cylinders | Chained upright, outside lab in the gas bank where possible | — |

- Every container must be labelled with name, concentration, date prepared, initials and expiry.
- Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for all chemicals are available on the R&D intranet; read the SDS before first use.
- Peroxide-forming solvents (e.g. THF, diethyl ether) are tested for peroxides every 3 months and discarded after 12 months.

## 7. Waste Management

| Waste | Container | Disposal |
|---|---|---|
| Halogenated solvents | Yellow 20 L carboy | Authorised hazardous waste handler (TSDF) |
| Non-halogenated solvents | Blue 20 L carboy | Authorised TSDF / solvent recovery |
| Aqueous acid/base | Neutralise to pH 6–9 | Effluent treatment plant |
| Broken glass | Puncture-proof "Glass" box | Scrap |
| Biological waste | Yellow bag, autoclave | Common bio-medical waste facility |
| API and potent compound waste | Sealed double bag, labelled | Incineration |

Waste carboys must not be filled above 80%. Waste records are maintained as required under the Hazardous and Other Wastes Rules, 2016.

## 8. Emergency Response

| Emergency | Action | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | Raise alarm (break-glass), evacuate via nearest exit, assemble at Assembly Point B (main gate lawn) | Security: extension 2222 |
| Chemical splash to eye | Eyewash for at least **15 minutes**, then occupational health centre | First aid: extension 2100 |
| Chemical spill < 1 L | Use spill kit; absorb; dispose as hazardous waste | Lab In-charge |
| Chemical spill ≥ 1 L or toxic gas | Evacuate lab; call Emergency Response Team | ERT: extension 2333 |
| Injury | First aid; report to Safety Officer within 1 hour | Ambulance on call at gate |

Fire drills are held twice a year. Safety showers and eyewashes are tested weekly by the lab and the test is logged.

## 9. Training

- Induction safety training (4 hours) before lab access is granted.
- Annual refresher (2 hours) for all R&D staff; potent compound handling training before working with OEB 4 compounds.
- Fire extinguisher hands-on training once a year.

## 10. Incident Reporting

All incidents, near-misses and unsafe conditions are reported in the EHS portal within **24 hours**. The R&D Safety Officer investigates within 3 working days. In FY2025-26 the R&D centre recorded 2 first-aid cases, 0 lost-time injuries and 38 near-miss reports.
