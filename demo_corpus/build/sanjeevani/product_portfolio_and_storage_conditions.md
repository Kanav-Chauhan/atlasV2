## 4. Warehouse rules

1. **FEFO** – First Expiry, First Out. Never dispatch a later-expiry batch while an earlier-expiry batch of the same product is available.
2. **Temperature mapping** – every warehouse and C&F location must have a calibrated data logger; record readings twice daily (10:00 and 16:00).
3. **Excursions** – if storage temperature goes above the labelled limit for more than 24 hours, quarantine affected stock and inform Site QA within 24 hours. Do not dispatch until QA disposition.
4. **Humidity** – keep tablets off the floor on pallets, at least 30 cm from walls; RH in the warehouse should not exceed 65%.
5. **Stacking** – maximum 6 shippers high for syrup, 8 for tablets and creams.
6. **Light** – keep cartons closed; do not store under skylights.
7. **Near-expiry** – stock with less than 6 months of shelf life remaining must not be dispatched to stockists without Sales Operations approval.
8. **Returns and damages** – keep in the "Returned Goods" cage, labelled, until QA decides on destruction.

## 5. Transport

| Product type | Transport requirement |
|---|---|
| Tablets (all four brands) | Closed body vehicle; no refrigeration required |
| Cough syrup | Closed body vehicle; protect from heat, keep upright ("This side up") |
| Dermacalm Cream | Insulated vehicle or thermal blanket from April to June for routes with expected transit above 30°C |

## 6. Shelf-life calculation example

Shelf life is counted from the month of manufacture. A Glucosan-M 500 batch manufactured in **May 2026** with a 36-month shelf life carries an expiry of **April 2029** ("EXP 04/2029"). The product may be used until the last day of the expiry month.
