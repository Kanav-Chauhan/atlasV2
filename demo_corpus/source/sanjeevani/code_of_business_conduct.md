# Code of Business Conduct

## 1. Purpose

This Code sets out the standards of behaviour that Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. expects from every director, employee, trainee, contract worker and agent acting on its behalf. It supports our tagline, "Healing, made dependable," by making clear that how we achieve results matters as much as the results themselves.

## 2. Scope

The Code applies to all 2,850 employees across the Ahmedabad head office, Unit I Changodar, Unit II Baddi, Unit III Vizag SEZ, the Hyderabad R&D centre and the field sales force. Third parties (distributors, C&F agents, CROs, consultants) must sign the Third-Party Code Acknowledgement before onboarding.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Every employee | Read the Code, complete annual e-learning, raise concerns |
| Managers | Lead by example, ensure team completion, never retaliate |
| Compliance Officer | Maintain the Code, run investigations, report to the Audit Committee |
| Ethics Committee | CEO, CFO, Head HR, Head QA and Compliance Officer; meets quarterly |
| Audit Committee of the Board | Oversight of the Code and the whistleblower mechanism |

## 4. Core principles

### 4.1 Product quality and patient safety
4.1.1 No employee may falsify, back-date, or selectively record GMP data. All records must meet ALCOA+ principles.
4.1.2 Any employee who suspects a quality or safety problem must report it to QA immediately. Shipment pressure is never an acceptable reason to release a batch.
4.1.3 Adverse events reported by doctors, patients or pharmacists must be forwarded to Pharmacovigilance within 24 hours of the employee becoming aware of them.

### 4.2 Interactions with healthcare professionals
4.2.1 All engagement with doctors, pharmacists and hospitals must follow the Uniform Code for Pharmaceutical Marketing Practices (UCPMP) 2024 and the company's UCPMP Compliance Guide for Field Staff.
4.2.2 No gifts, cash, travel, hospitality or personal benefits may be offered to healthcare professionals or their family members.
4.2.3 Drug samples may only be given in line with the Sample Distribution Policy.

### 4.3 Anti-bribery and anti-corruption
4.3.1 We do not offer, pay, solicit or accept bribes in any form, including facilitation ("speed") payments to officials at licensing, customs, or inspection offices.
4.3.2 Any request for a bribe must be reported to the Compliance Officer within 2 working days.

### 4.4 Gifts and hospitality received by employees
| Item | Limit | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Gifts from vendors (festivals, e.g. Diwali) | Up to ₹2,000 market value | Accept and record in Gifts Register |
| Gifts from vendors above ₹2,000 | Not permitted | Politely return or hand over to HR |
| Business meals with vendors | Up to ₹3,000 per person per occasion | Pre-approval by HOD |
| Cash or cash equivalents (vouchers, gift cards) | Never permitted | Return and report |

### 4.5 Conflicts of interest
4.5.1 Employees must declare any interest (own or relative's) in a supplier, distributor, CRO or competitor in the annual Conflict of Interest Declaration, due by 30 April each year.
4.5.2 Outside employment or directorships require prior written approval from Head HR.

### 4.6 Insider trading
4.6.1 Employees in possession of unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI), such as quarterly results, clinical trial readouts or in-licensing deals, must not trade in Sanjeevani shares.
4.6.2 The trading window closes from the first day after quarter-end until 48 hours after results are published. Designated persons must pre-clear trades above ₹10 lakh in value.

### 4.7 Confidential information and data privacy
4.7.1 Formulation know-how, clinical data, pricing and regulatory dossiers are confidential and may be shared only on a need-to-know basis.
4.7.2 Personal data of employees, patients and trial participants must be handled in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

### 4.8 Fair employment and respectful workplace
4.8.1 We prohibit discrimination on grounds of gender, caste, religion, region, disability, age or sexual orientation.
4.8.2 Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee constituted at each site under the POSH Act 2013.

### 4.9 Environment, health and safety
All employees must follow the Plant Safety and EHS Policy. PPE is mandatory in all manufacturing and laboratory areas.

## 5. Raising concerns

Concerns may be raised with the line manager, Head HR, the Compliance Officer, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline described in the Whistleblower Policy. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a breach of this Code.

## 6. Training and acknowledgement

6.1 New joiners must complete Code of Conduct e-learning within 15 days of joining.
6.2 All employees must complete annual refresher training and sign the electronic acknowledgement by 30 June each year.
6.3 Completion below 95% in any department is reported to the Ethics Committee.

## 7. Consequences of violation

| Severity | Examples | Typical consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Late COI declaration, missed training | Written warning |
| Serious | Gift above limit, undeclared conflict | Final warning, bonus forfeiture |
| Gross | Bribery, data falsification, retaliation | Termination; reporting to authorities |
