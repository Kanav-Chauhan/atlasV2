# SOP – Batch Manufacturing Record Review

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how executed Batch Manufacturing Records (BMRs) and Batch Packaging Records (BPRs) are reviewed by Production and Quality Assurance before a batch is released for sale or export. The aim is to confirm that every batch was made according to the approved master record, that all critical process parameters were met, and that any deviation is investigated and closed before release, as required by Schedule M and WHO-GMP.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all commercial and exhibit batches manufactured at Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar), Unit II – Baddi and Unit III – Vizag SEZ. It covers paper BMRs and the electronic batch record module used for oral solids at Unit I. It does not cover R&D development batches made at the Hyderabad R&D centre, which follow SPL/RND/SOP-011.

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Production Officer | Completes BMR entries contemporaneously; performs first-level review within 1 working day of batch completion |
| Production Manager | Second-level review; confirms yields and reconciliation; hands BMR to QA |
| QA Officer (IPQA) | Line-by-line QA review against master record; raises queries |
| QA Manager (site) | Approves BMR review; decides on release together with QC results |
| Head, Quality Assurance (Corporate) | Approves release of any batch with an open Major or Critical deviation (export only, by exception) |

## 4. Definitions

- **BMR**: Batch Manufacturing Record – the executed copy of the Master Formula Record for one batch.
- **BPR**: Batch Packaging Record – the executed packaging record for one batch.
- **Critical Process Parameter (CPP)**: a parameter whose variability affects a critical quality attribute, e.g. granulation end-point, compression force, coating weight gain.
- **Yield limit**: the acceptable range of theoretical yield defined in the master record.
- **Good Documentation Practice (GDP)**: recording practice that meets ALCOA+ principles (see SPL/QA/POL-007).

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Timelines

- Production first-level review: within **1 working day** of the last BMR entry.
- QA review: within **3 working days** of receipt of the complete BMR from Production.
- Total BMR-to-release target for Unit I oral solids: **7 working days** from end of packaging, subject to QC results.

### 5.2 Production review

1. Check that every page is present, numbered "Page x of y" and carries the batch number.
2. Verify that all entries are signed and dated by the doer and checker; no blank fields (strike unused fields with a single line, "NA", sign and date).
3. Verify dispensing records against the Bill of Materials, including weighing balance ID and calibration status.
4. Confirm in-process checks (weight variation, hardness, thickness, friability, disintegration where applicable) are within limits.
5. Calculate yields at each stage. For Glucosan-M 500 SR (batch size 3,00,000 tablets) the compression yield limit is 97.0–101.0% and the overall packed yield limit is 95.0–100.5%.
6. Attach equipment printouts (e.g. FBD temperature logs, compression force trends) and sign each printout.

### 5.3 QA review checklist

The QA Officer reviews the BMR using Form SPL/QA/F-012-01. Key checks:

| Check | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| Line clearance | Signed by Production and IPQA before each stage |
| Environmental conditions | Temperature 20–25°C, RH 45–60% for Grade D processing areas, recorded every 4 hours |
| CPPs | All within validated ranges; any excursion linked to a deviation number |
| Hold times | Granules ≤ 72 hours, coated tablets in bulk ≤ 60 days |
| Yield and reconciliation | Within limits; printed packaging material reconciliation 100% accounted |
| Deviations | All linked deviations closed or risk-assessed for release |
| Corrections | Single line strike-through, reason, sign and date; no overwriting or correction fluid |
| Audit trail (e-BMR) | Reviewed for edits after execution; reviewer signs audit trail report |

### 5.4 Handling of queries

1. QA raises queries on Form SPL/QA/F-012-02. Production must respond within **2 working days**.
2. Any GDP error that affects the reliability of data (missing entry for a CPP, back-dated signature) is escalated as a deviation under SPL/QA/SOP-004.
3. Repeat GDP errors by the same individual (3 or more in a calendar month) trigger retraining.

### 5.5 Release decision

A batch may be released only when:

- the BMR and BPR review is complete and signed by the site QA Manager;
- the QC Certificate of Analysis shows all tests within specification (e.g. SPL/QC/SPEC-005 for Glucosan-M 500 SR);
- there are no open Critical or Major deviations, unless the Head QA (Corporate) approves release in writing with a documented risk assessment;
- change controls affecting the batch are approved.

The QA Manager records the release in the ERP and applies the green "RELEASED" label to the pallets.

## 6. Records

|---|---|
