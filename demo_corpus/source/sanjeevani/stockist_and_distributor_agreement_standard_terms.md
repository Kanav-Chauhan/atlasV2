# Stockist and Distributor Agreement – Standard Terms

## 1. Purpose

This document summarises the standard terms of the agreement Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. signs with every stockist (distributor) in India. Area and Regional Sales Managers use it when appointing or reviewing a stockist. Any change from these terms needs written approval from the National Sales Manager; changes to credit terms also need Finance approval under the Stockist Credit and Collections Policy.

## 2. Parties and term

| Item | Standard term |
|---|---|
| Parties | Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd., Ahmedabad, and the stockist (a licensed wholesale drug dealer) |
| Term | 3 years from the date of signing, renewable by mutual written agreement |
| Licences the stockist must hold | Valid wholesale drug licences (Forms 20B and 21B) and GST registration |
| Supply point | The Sanjeevani C&F agent for the state |
| Governing law | Laws of India; disputes by arbitration seated in Ahmedabad |

## 3. Territory

3.1 Each stockist is appointed for a defined territory (one or more towns or a district), listed in Annexure A of the agreement.
3.2 In towns with a population below 10 lakh, Sanjeevani appoints only **one stockist per territory** for each division. In metros and cities above 10 lakh, up to 3 stockists may be appointed for the same area.
3.3 The stockist must sell only to licensed retailers, hospitals and sub-stockists **within its territory**. Selling outside the territory ("diversion") is a material breach.

## 4. Trade margins

Margins are calculated on the price to retailer (PTR). The price to stockist (PTS) is the PTR minus the stockist margin. Retail margins follow the price notified on the pack (MRP) and the NPPA rules for scheduled formulations.

| Brand | Stockist margin (on PTR) | Retailer margin (on MRP, excl. GST) |
|---|---|---|
| Glucosan-M 500 | 10% | 20% |
| Cardivas 10 | 10% | 20% |
| Amlosan 5 | 10% | 20% |
| Pantosan 40 | 10% | 20% |
| Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup | 12% | 20% |
| Dermacalm Cream | 10% | 20% |

4.1 The higher 12% margin on Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup reflects higher freight and breakage cost for bottles.
4.2 No additional schemes, free goods or bonus offers may be given without a scheme circular signed by the National Sales Manager.

## 5. Orders and stock

| Item | Standard term |
|---|---|
| Minimum order value | ₹1,50,000 per order (excluding GST) |
| Minimum monthly purchase | ₹10 lakh in metros; ₹4 lakh in other towns |
| Stock norm | 30 days of sales of every focus brand |
| Order cycle | Orders by Monday and Thursday; dispatch from C&F within 48 hours |
| Payment | 30 days from invoice, per the Stockist Credit and Collections Policy |

5.1 The stockist must maintain at least 98% availability of the four focus brands (Glucosan-M 500, Cardivas 10, Amlosan 5, Pantosan 40) to retailers in its territory.

## 6. Storage and handling duties

6.1 The stockist must store all Sanjeevani products as labelled:

| Brand | Storage condition | Shelf life |
|---|---|---|
| Glucosan-M 500 | Below 30°C, protect from moisture | 36 months |
| Cardivas 10 | Below 30°C, protect from light and moisture | 36 months |
| Amlosan 5 | Below 30°C, protect from light | 36 months |
| Pantosan 40 | Below 30°C, protect from moisture | 24 months |
| Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup | Below 30°C, do not refrigerate, protect from light | 24 months |
| Dermacalm Cream | Below 25°C, do not freeze | 30 months |

6.2 The stockist must keep a calibrated temperature data logger in the storage area and record readings twice daily (10:00 and 16:00). Where summer ambient temperature exceeds 30°C, Dermacalm Cream must be kept in an air-conditioned area.
6.3 Stock must be kept on pallets, off the floor, and sold **First Expiry, First Out (FEFO)**.
6.4 The stockist must allow Sanjeevani's Area Sales Manager or QA representative to inspect the storage area with 24 hours' notice. Two failed storage inspections in 12 months are a material breach.

## 7. Expiry, damage and recall returns

7.1 Expired, near-expiry and damaged goods are returned and credited under the Product Returns and Expiry Replacement Policy (SPL/SAL/POL-108). Expiry credit is limited to 2% of the stockist's purchases in the previous 12 months.
7.2 In a recall, the stockist must stop sale immediately on receiving the recall notice, quarantine the stock, retrieve it from retailers and return it to the C&F agent within the timeline in the notice. Sanjeevani pays full credit and the freight for recalled goods.

## 8. Compliance

8.1 The stockist must not pass any benefit, gift or free goods to doctors on Sanjeevani's behalf. Promotion to doctors is done only by Sanjeevani's field force under the UCPMP 2024 code.
8.2 Physician samples are never supplied to stockists and must not be sold. Any sample found in trade stock is reported to Sales Operations.
8.3 The stockist must forward any complaint about product quality or any side effect reported by a retailer or patient to Sanjeevani within 24 hours (drugsafety@sanjeevani.example).
8.4 The stockist must sign the Third-Party Code Acknowledgement under the Code of Business Conduct.

## 9. Termination

| Situation | Notice |
|---|---|
| Either party, without cause | **60 days' written notice** |
| Material breach (diversion, sale of samples, spurious or tampered stock, two failed storage inspections) | Immediate, by written notice from Sanjeevani |
| Payment default more than 90 days past due | Immediate, under the Stockist Credit and Collections Policy |
| Licence cancelled or suspended | Automatic, from the date of cancellation |

9.1 On termination, saleable stock with at least 12 months of shelf life remaining is bought back at the price paid, less 5% handling. The security deposit is refunded within 30 days after all dues are settled.
