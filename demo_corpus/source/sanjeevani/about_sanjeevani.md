# About Sanjeevani

## 1. Who we are

Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. is a mid-size Indian generics and branded-formulations company. We were founded in 1994 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, and today we make medicines for diabetes, cardiac care, hypertension, gastro-intestinal, respiratory and dermatology patients in India and in more than 30 export markets.

Our tagline is **"Healing, made dependable."** It is a promise to doctors, pharmacists and patients that every strip, bottle and tube carrying our name has been made the same careful way, every single time.

## 2. Our sites

### 2.1 Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar)
Our oldest and largest plant makes oral solids: tablets and capsules. Unit I is WHO-GMP certified and compliant with Schedule M of the Drugs Rules. It manufactures Glucosan-M 500, Cardivas 10, Amlosan 5 and Pantosan 40.

### 2.2 Unit II – Baddi, Himachal Pradesh
Unit II makes oral liquids and ointments/creams, including Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup and Dermacalm Cream. The Plant Head is Rajesh Thakur.

### 2.3 Unit III – Vizag (Visakhapatnam) SEZ
Unit III is our export unit, located in the Visakhapatnam Special Economic Zone. It is inspected by the US FDA and supplies regulated markets. The Plant Head is Venkat Raju.

### 2.4 R&D Centre – Hyderabad (Genome Valley)
Formulation development, analytical research, bioequivalence planning and clinical development are run from our Genome Valley campus under Dr. Lakshmi Narayanan, President, R&D.

## 5. Our values

1. **Quality first.** No shipment target is worth a compromised batch. Any employee may stop a line if they believe product quality is at risk.
2. **Integrity.** We follow the law, the Code of Business Conduct and the UCPMP 2024 code in every interaction with healthcare professionals.
3. **Patient focus.** We make medicines for chronic conditions; patients depend on us month after month.
4. **Safety.** Our people go home safe every day. PPE is mandatory on every shop floor.
5. **Learning.** Every GMP role requires documented qualification before independent work.
