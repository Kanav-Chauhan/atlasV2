# Shift and Overtime Policy

## 1. Purpose

To set out shift timings, rotation rules, shift allowances and overtime compensation at Sanjeevani's manufacturing units, in line with the Factories Act 1948 and applicable state rules, so that 24×7 operations run safely and employees are paid fairly.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees and fixed-term workers at Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar), Unit II – Baddi, Unit III – Vizag SEZ, and the plant warehouses. Head office, R&D and field staff are on general shift and are covered only by Section 8.

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Plant Head | Approves shift pattern and monthly overtime budget |
| Production / QC / Engineering HODs | Prepare shift rosters 7 days in advance |
| Plant HR | Shift allowance and overtime computation, statutory registers |
| Security | Biometric attendance and gate records |

## 4. Shift timings

| Shift | Timing | Break |
|---|---|---|
| A (Morning) | 06:00 – 14:30 | 30 minutes meal break |
| B (Afternoon) | 14:00 – 22:30 | 30 minutes meal break |
| C (Night) | 22:00 – 06:30 | 30 minutes meal break |
| General | 09:00 – 17:30 | 30 minutes meal break |

4.1 The 30-minute overlap between shifts is used for handover of batch records, line clearance status and pending deviations. Handover must be recorded in the shift log book.
4.2 Shifts rotate weekly (A → C → B) unless a different pattern is approved by the Plant Head.
4.3 No employee may be scheduled for more than 6 consecutive night shifts.
4.4 A minimum rest of 12 hours is required between two shifts.

## 5. Shift allowance

| Shift | Allowance |
|---|---|
| A (Morning) | Nil |
| B (Afternoon) | ₹1,200 per month |
| C (Night) | ₹2,800 per month |

5.1 The night-shift allowance of ₹2,800 per month is paid to plant employees who work at least 10 night shifts in the month; below 10 night shifts it is paid pro-rata at ₹280 per night shift.
5.2 Night-shift employees receive a free meal and company transport from fixed pick-up points.
5.3 Shift allowance is not part of basic salary and does not count for PF, gratuity or leave encashment.

## 6. Overtime

6.1 Overtime is any work beyond 9 hours in a day or 48 hours in a week, as defined under the Factories Act 1948.
6.2 Overtime is paid at **twice the ordinary rate of wages** (basic plus DA).
6.3 Limits:

| Limit | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Overtime per day | 3 hours |
| Spread-over in a day (including overtime) | 12 hours |
| Overtime per quarter | 75 hours |
| Consecutive days with overtime | 5 days |

6.4 Overtime must be pre-approved by the HOD in the HRMS. Unapproved overtime will not be paid except during declared emergencies (equipment breakdown, regulatory inspection, recall execution) approved by the Plant Head within 24 hours.
6.5 Overtime is applicable to workmen and staff in grades W1–W4 and M1. Employees in grades M2 and above are not paid overtime but may receive compensatory off.

## 7. Holiday working

7.1 Employees required to work on a declared public holiday receive double wages for the day, or a compensatory off within 30 days, at the employee's choice.
7.2 Weekly off: every employee is entitled to one weekly off; if worked, a substitute weekly off must be given within the same or next week.

## 8. General-shift employees (head office, R&D, field)

8.1 Head office and R&D: 09:00 – 17:30, Monday to Friday, with alternate Saturdays working (09:00 – 13:30).
8.2 R&D stability chamber and instrument monitoring outside working hours is done on an on-call roster; on-call allowance is ₹500 per weekend day.
8.3 Field staff timings are covered by the Field Force Guidelines FY27.

## 9. Women in night shifts

9.1 Women may work night shifts only with their written consent and subject to the conditions notified by the relevant state government.
9.2 Door-to-door company transport with a security guard, and a minimum of 3 women on the shift, are mandatory.

## 10. Health and safety

10.1 Night-shift workers receive an annual health check including blood pressure and sleep assessment.
10.2 Employees must not work in GMP areas if they have worked more than 12 hours in the previous 24 hours.
10.3 PPE and gowning requirements apply equally on all shifts.

## 11. Records

Plant HR maintains the Register of Overtime (Form 10 or the state equivalent), shift rosters and allowance computations for 5 years.
