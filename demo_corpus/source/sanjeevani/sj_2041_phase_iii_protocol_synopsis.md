# SJ-2041 Phase III Protocol Synopsis

## 1. Study Identification

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Protocol number | SPL/CLN/PRT-017, version 2.0 (amendment 1) |
| Title | A randomised, double-blind, double-dummy, active-controlled, multicentre Phase III study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of SJ-2041 (SPL-DP41 50 mg / metformin hydrochloride 1,000 mg SR fixed-dose combination) once daily compared with metformin SR 1,000 mg once daily in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus inadequately controlled on metformin |
| Short title | SJ-2041 Phase III (DIAMOND-India) |
| Registry | Clinical Trials Registry – India, CTRI/2025/03/061842 |
| Sponsor | Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd., Ahmedabad |
| Regulatory approval | CDSCO permission under the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019 (received January 2025) |
| Phase | III |
| Sites | 38 sites in India |
| Sample size | 1,240 patients (620 per arm) |
| Duration per patient | 2-week screening + 24 weeks treatment + 2-week safety follow-up |

## 2. Background and Rationale

Many patients in India remain above the HbA1c target on metformin alone and need a second agent. A single-tablet, once-daily combination of a DPP-4 inhibitor with sustained-release metformin may improve adherence and glycaemic control with a low risk of hypoglycaemia. SPL-DP41 is a new DPP-4 inhibitor developed at the Sanjeevani R&D centre in Hyderabad; Phase II (n = 286) showed an HbA1c reduction of −0.8% versus placebo add-on at 12 weeks with 50 mg once daily.

## 3. Objectives and Endpoints

| Type | Objective | Endpoint |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Show superiority of SJ-2041 over metformin SR alone in glycaemic control | **Change in HbA1c from baseline to week 24** |
| Key secondary | Glycaemic target attainment | Proportion of patients with HbA1c < 7.0% at week 24 |
| Secondary | Fasting glucose | Change in fasting plasma glucose (FPG) from baseline to week 24 |
| Secondary | Post-prandial glucose | Change in 2-hour post-prandial glucose at week 24 |
| Secondary | Body weight | Change in body weight at week 24 |
| Safety | Safety and tolerability | Adverse events, serious adverse events, hypoglycaemia (glucose < 70 mg/dL), laboratory parameters, ECG |

## 4. Study Population

### 4.1 Key inclusion criteria

1. Adults aged 18–70 years with type 2 diabetes for at least 6 months.
2. On a stable metformin dose of ≥ 1,000 mg/day for at least 8 weeks before screening.
3. HbA1c 7.5% to 10.5% (inclusive) at screening.
4. BMI 20–40 kg/m².
5. Written informed consent, including audio-video recording of consent as required by Indian regulations.

### 4.2 Key exclusion criteria

1. Type 1 diabetes or history of diabetic ketoacidosis.
2. eGFR < 45 mL/min/1.73 m².
3. Use of any DPP-4 inhibitor, GLP-1 agonist, SGLT2 inhibitor or insulin within 12 weeks.
4. History of pancreatitis.
5. ALT or AST > 3 × upper limit of normal.
6. Pregnancy, lactation, or not using effective contraception.
7. Myocardial infarction, stroke or heart failure hospitalisation within 6 months.

## 5. Treatments

| Arm | Treatment (once daily with evening meal) |
|---|---|
| A – SJ-2041 | SJ-2041 bilayer tablet (SPL-DP41 50 mg / metformin HCl 1,000 mg SR) + placebo matching metformin SR |
| B – Comparator | Metformin SR 1,000 mg (2 × 500 mg tablets, Glucosan-M 500) + placebo matching SJ-2041 |

Randomisation 1:1 via interactive web response system, stratified by baseline HbA1c (< 8.5% vs ≥ 8.5%) and region.

**Rescue therapy** (glimepiride, open-label) is given if FPG > 270 mg/dL between weeks 0 and 8, or FPG > 240 mg/dL between weeks 8 and 24, confirmed on repeat testing. Patients on rescue therapy remain in the study.

| Visit | Week | Key assessments |
|---|---|---|
| V1 Screening | −2 | Consent, eligibility, HbA1c, eGFR, ECG |
| V2 Randomisation | 0 | Baseline HbA1c, FPG, weight; dispense study drug |
| V3 | 4 | FPG, safety, compliance |
| V4 | 8 | FPG, safety |
| V5 | 12 | HbA1c, FPG, safety labs |
| V6 | 18 | FPG, safety |
| V7 End of treatment | 24 | HbA1c (primary), FPG, PPG, weight, labs, ECG |
| V8 Follow-up | 26 | Safety follow-up (telephone permitted) |

HbA1c is measured at a single NABL-accredited central laboratory.

## 7. Statistical Considerations

- **Sample size:** 525 evaluable patients per arm give 90% power to detect a difference of 0.2% in HbA1c change (SD 1.0%, two-sided α = 0.05). Allowing 15% dropout, 620 per arm are randomised (1,240 total).
- **Primary analysis:** ANCOVA on the full analysis set with treatment and stratification factors as fixed effects and baseline HbA1c as covariate; missing data handled by multiple imputation.
- **Interim analysis:** one pre-planned interim analysis when about 400 patients have completed week 24 or discontinued, reviewed by the independent Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) for safety and futility only; no stopping for efficacy. A Haybittle-Peto boundary (p < 0.001) protects the final α.

## 8. Safety Monitoring

- SAEs reported within 24 hours per the Serious Adverse Event Reporting SOP (SPL/CLN/SOP-005).
- Independent DSMB per the DSMB Charter (SPL/CLN/POL-003), meeting at least every 6 months.
- Adverse events of special interest: pancreatitis, lactic acidosis, severe hypoglycaemia, hypersensitivity, hepatic injury.
