# Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how Sanjeevani employees book, undertake and claim business travel, so that travel is safe, cost-effective and consistently reimbursed across the head office, the three manufacturing units, the Hyderabad R&D centre and the field force.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all permanent employees travelling on company business. Daily local travel by medical representatives within their headquarters town is covered by the Field Force Guidelines FY27 and is not covered here.

## 3. Grades

| Band | Grades | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Band A | M5 and above | CXOs, Plant Heads, Heads of Function |
| Band B | M3–M4 | Managers, Senior Scientists, Area Sales Managers |
| Band C | M1–M2 | Officers, Executives, Scientists, Medical Representatives |
| Band D | W1–W4 | Operators, technicians, staff |

## 4. Travel approval

4.1 All domestic travel must be raised in the HRMS travel module and approved by the reporting manager **before** booking.
4.2 International travel requires approval from the Head of Function and the CFO, at least 21 days before departure.
4.3 Travel booked without prior approval will not be reimbursed unless it was an emergency (for example a plant shutdown or a regulatory inspection) and is approved retrospectively within 3 working days.

## 5. Mode of travel entitlement

| Band | Air | Rail | Local taxi |
|---|---|---|---|
| Band A | Economy; business class for flights above 4 hours | AC First Class | Actuals |
| Band B | Economy | AC 2-tier | Actuals |
| Band C | Economy if journey exceeds 500 km or rail exceeds 12 hours | AC 2-tier | App cab, up to ₹1,200/day |
| Band D | Not entitled (except emergencies approved by Plant Head) | AC 3-tier | Auto/bus, up to ₹500/day |

5.1 Flights must be booked through the company travel desk at least 7 days in advance wherever possible. The lowest logical fare within a 2-hour window of the preferred time must be chosen.

## 6. Hotel and daily allowance (domestic)

City categories: **Metro** – Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad; **Tier 1** – Ahmedabad, Pune, Visakhapatnam, Chandigarh, Lucknow, Jaipur, and other state capitals; **Tier 2** – all other locations including Baddi and Changodar.

| Band | Hotel – Metro (per night) | Hotel – Tier 1 | Hotel – Tier 2 | Daily allowance (food & incidentals) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Band A | Actuals (company-contracted) | Actuals | Actuals | ₹2,500 |
| Band B | ₹7,000 | ₹5,500 | ₹4,000 | ₹1,500 |
| Band C | ₹4,500 | ₹3,500 | ₹2,500 | ₹1,000 |
| Band D | ₹2,500 | ₹2,000 | ₹1,500 | ₹600 |

6.1 Plant guest houses at Changodar, Baddi and Vizag must be used when rooms are available. When a guest house is used, the daily allowance is reduced by 50%.
6.2 Hotel limits exclude GST. A valid GST invoice in the company's name (GSTIN of the relevant site) is mandatory.

## 7. International travel

7.1 Per diem: USD 75 per day for Band A and USD 60 per day for other bands, covering meals and incidentals.
7.2 Hotels are booked by the travel desk within the limit approved by the CFO.
7.3 Travel insurance is arranged by the company for every international trip.
7.4 Typical international trips include US FDA meetings, customer audits for Unit III exports and conferences.

## 8. Expense claims

8.1 Claims must be submitted in the HRMS expense module within **15 days** of return. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be paid.
8.2 Original receipts are required for every item above ₹300.
8.3 Approved claims are reimbursed with the next salary cycle or within 10 working days, whichever is earlier.
8.4 Travel advances, if taken, must be settled within 7 days of return. Unsettled advances older than 30 days are recovered from salary.

## 9. Non-reimbursable items

- Alcohol, tobacco and minibar charges
- Personal entertainment, laundry for stays under 3 nights
- Fines, traffic challans and penalties
- Upgrades not covered by entitlement
- Expenses of family members accompanying the employee

## 10. Safety while travelling

10.1 Road travel between 10 pm and 6 am should be avoided; if unavoidable, inform the reporting manager.
10.2 Employees visiting plants must follow site safety induction and PPE requirements under the Plant Safety and EHS Policy.
