# Medicine Information and Side-Effect Reporting FAQ

This page is for patients, caregivers, doctors and pharmacists who use medicines made by Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. It explains how to report a side effect, how to ask a question about one of our medicines, how to store them at home and where to find recall notices. It does not replace the advice of your doctor or pharmacist.

## 1. How do I report a side effect?

You can report any unwanted or unexpected effect you think may be linked to a Sanjeevani medicine – for example muscle pain while taking **Cardivas 10**, stomach upset with **Glucosan-M 500**, or skin thinning with **Dermacalm Cream**. You do not need to be sure the medicine caused it.

### 1.1 What information should I give?

1. The name of the medicine (for example "Cardivas 10"), the batch number and expiry date printed on the strip, bottle or tube.
2. What happened, when it started, and whether it has stopped.
3. The age and sex of the person affected, and any other medicines they take.
4. Your contact number, so that our safety doctor can follow up if needed.

Even an incomplete report is useful. Your personal details are kept confidential and are used only for drug safety.

### 1.2 What happens after I report?

- We acknowledge your report within **2 working days**.
- A safety physician reviews every report. Serious reports are sent to the national regulator within the legal timelines (within 15 calendar days for serious unexpected reactions, and within 7 calendar days for fatal or life-threatening ones).
- Our Qualified Person for Pharmacovigilance, Dr. Rohit Kulkarni, oversees the system, reporting to Pooja Desai, Head, Regulatory Affairs.

If the side effect is severe (difficulty breathing, swelling of the face, fainting, very low blood sugar), **stop and seek medical help immediately** before reporting.

## 3. How should I store my medicines at home?

| Medicine | How to store | Shelf life (unopened) | After opening |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glucosan-M 500 | Below 30°C; protect from moisture – keep tablets in the strip until use; do not keep in the bathroom | 36 months | — |
| Cardivas 10 | Below 30°C; protect from light and moisture | 36 months | — |
| Amlosan 5 | Below 30°C; protect from light | 36 months | — |
| Pantosan 40 | Below 30°C; protect from moisture; swallow whole, do not crush | 24 months | — |
| Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup | Below 30°C; do not refrigerate; protect from light | 24 months | Use within 30 days after opening |
| Dermacalm Cream | Below 25°C; do not freeze; close the cap tightly | 30 months | Use within 3 months after opening |

General tips:

- Keep all medicines out of the sight and reach of children.
- Do not use any medicine after the expiry date ("EXP MM/YYYY"); it may be used until the last day of that month.
- Do not use tablets that are discoloured, broken or damp, or syrup that has changed colour.
- Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup must not be given to children below four years of age.
- Dermacalm Cream should not be used for more than 3 weeks without medical advice.

## 4. Where can I find recall notices?

When we recall a batch, we publish the product name, batch number, reason and what you should do on the "Product notices" page of our website, and we inform pharmacies, stockists and the drug regulators.

**Recent recall:**

| Product | Batch | Date | Reason | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup, 100 mL | CR-2309 | August 2025 | Label misprint (wrong manufacturing licence number on the label). No safety impact. | Return the bottle to your pharmacy for a free replacement. The recall was closed in October 2025. |

If your pack's batch number is not listed on the notices page, it is not affected by any recall.

## 5. How do I know a Sanjeevani medicine is genuine?

Every pack carries the brand name, batch number, manufacturing and expiry dates, the manufacturing licence number and our registered address (Sanjeevani House, Ahmedabad). If a pack looks different from usual, has spelling mistakes or a damaged seal, do not use it; email qa.helpdesk@sanjeevani.example with a photograph of the pack.

## 6. Is SJ-2041 available?

No. SJ-2041 is an investigational diabetes medicine still in clinical trials and is not available for sale or on prescription. Please do not buy any product claiming to be SJ-2041.
