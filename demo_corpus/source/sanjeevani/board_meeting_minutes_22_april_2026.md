## 2. Quorum and Chair

The Chairperson confirmed that the required quorum was present throughout the meeting. All directors confirmed there was no conflict of interest in any agenda item, except Vikram Sheth, who declared an interest in item 9 (related-party transaction) and did not participate in that item.

## 3. Confirmation of minutes

The minutes of the 127th meeting held on 28 January 2026 were confirmed and signed by the Chairperson.

## 4. Audited financial results for FY2025-26

The CFO presented the audited standalone and consolidated results for the year ended 31 March 2026, as recommended by the Audit Committee at its meeting held earlier the same day.

| Item | FY2025-26 | FY2024-25 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue from operations (₹ crore) | 2,460.0 | 2,215.0 |
| EBITDA (₹ crore) | 548.6 | 465.2 |
| Profit after tax (₹ crore) | 333.6 | 281.5 |
| EPS (₹) | 26.90 | 22.70 |

**Resolution 4.1:** RESOLVED THAT the audited standalone and consolidated financial results of the Company for the year ended 31 March 2026, together with the Auditor's Report, be and are hereby approved, and the MD & CEO and CFO be authorised to sign the same for submission to the stock exchanges.

### 4.2 Dividend
**Resolution 4.2:** RESOLVED THAT a final dividend of **₹4.50 per equity share** of face value ₹10 each (45%) for FY2025-26 be and is hereby recommended for the approval of the shareholders at the 32nd Annual General Meeting. The total outflow will be approximately ₹55.8 crore on 12.4 crore equity shares. The record date will be fixed by the Company Secretary.

## 5. Vizag capacity expansion

Venkat Raju presented the investment proposal for a new oral-solids block at Unit III – Vizag SEZ (detailed in the Vizag Expansion Investment Note). The Board noted that the US FDA re-inspection of Unit III in February 2026 closed with no observations, which supports the case for adding US-market capacity.

**Resolution 5.1:** RESOLVED THAT approval be and is hereby accorded for capital expenditure of **₹310 crore** for the Vizag capacity expansion project, to be funded through internal accruals of ₹190 crore and a term loan of up to ₹120 crore, and that the MD & CEO be authorised to finalise contracts and borrowings within this limit.

The Board directed that progress be reported at every quarterly meeting and that any overrun above 5% of the approved amount be brought back to the Board.

## 6. R&D and SJ-2041 update

Dr. Lakshmi Narayanan and Dr. Ananya Krishnan updated the Board on the SJ-2041 Phase III programme (protocol SPL/CLN/PRT-017): enrolment of 1,240 patients across 38 sites in India is complete. The pre-specified interim analysis showed an HbA1c reduction of −1.1% versus −0.6% for the comparator; dropout was 7.4%, and 2 serious adverse events were judged unrelated to study drug. The topline readout is expected in January 2027. The Board noted the update; no resolution was required. Directors were reminded that this is unpublished price-sensitive information.

## 7. Quality and compliance update

Dr. Farhan Qureshi reported that all CAPAs from the 2024 US FDA Form 483 were closed by October 2024 and that the February 2026 re-inspection resulted in no observations (NAI). He also reported closure of the voluntary recall of Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup batch CR-2309 (August 2025, label misprint, 18,400 bottles, no safety impact). The Board noted the update.

## 8. Biosimilar insulin in-licensing

The MD & CEO presented an opportunity to in-license a biosimilar insulin candidate (code **BIO-INS-7**) from a biologics developer, as described in the Biosimilar Insulin In-licensing Brief.

**Resolution 8.1:** RESOLVED THAT the MD & CEO be and is hereby authorised to commence in-licensing discussions for BIO-INS-7, to sign a non-binding term sheet and a confidentiality agreement, and to incur due-diligence costs of up to ₹3 crore; provided that any binding agreement shall be placed before the Board for approval.

## 9. Related-party transaction

The Board approved, on the recommendation of the Audit Committee, the renewal of the warehousing lease at Changodar with a related party at an annual rent of ₹1.8 crore, found to be at arm's length. Vikram Sheth did not participate.

## 10. FY27 Annual Operating Plan

The Board noted the FY27 Annual Operating Plan, including a domestic sales target of ₹1,520 crore, R&D spend of about 7% of revenue and capital expenditure of ₹420 crore (including the first-year spend on the Vizag project).

## 11. Corporate Social Responsibility

**Resolution 11.1:** RESOLVED THAT a CSR budget of ₹6.9 crore for FY2026-27 (2% of the average net profit of the preceding three financial years) be approved, focused on diabetes screening camps in rural Gujarat and Himachal Pradesh and on primary school sanitation near the Vizag SEZ.
