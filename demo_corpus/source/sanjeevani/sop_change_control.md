# SOP – Change Control

## 1. Purpose

To ensure that every proposed change to GMP-relevant facilities, equipment, utilities, materials, processes, analytical methods, computerised systems and documents is evaluated, approved, implemented and verified in a controlled way, and that regulatory obligations arising from the change are identified before implementation.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all three manufacturing sites, the corporate QA function and the Hyderabad R&D centre when R&D work supports a commercial product. Changes to marketed products registered in export markets (Unit III) must also be assessed by Regulatory Affairs for post-approval filing requirements.

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Change Initiator | Raises the change request in the eQMS with justification and proposed plan |
| Department Head | Endorses the request; confirms resources |
| Cross-functional reviewers (QA, QC, Production, Engineering, Regulatory Affairs) | Assess impact within 5 working days |
| Site QA Manager | Classifies the change; approves Minor and Major changes |
| Head, Quality Assurance (Corporate) | Approves Critical changes and all changes affecting export (Unit III) products |
| Head, Regulatory Affairs | Determines filing category (prior approval, notification, annual report) |

## 4. Definitions

- **Minor change**: no impact on product quality, validated state or regulatory filing (e.g. editorial SOP correction, like-for-like spare part).
- **Major change**: potential impact on product quality or validated state but no regulatory prior approval needed (e.g. new compression machine of the same design principle, change in batch size within 10-fold of the validated size).
- **Critical change**: change requiring prior regulatory approval or with significant impact on product quality (e.g. change of API source, change in manufacturing site, change in specification limits, change in formulation).
- **Temporary change**: a change limited in time or number of batches; handled through a planned deviation where appropriate.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Initiation

1. The initiator raises a Change Request (CR) in the eQMS. Numbering: CC/<Unit or CORP>/<YY>/<NNNN>.
2. The CR must describe the current state, proposed state, reason, affected products and documents, and a preliminary risk assessment.

### 5.2 Impact assessment and classification

Each reviewer completes the impact checklist (Form SPL/QA/F-006-01) covering: validation and qualification, stability, regulatory filings, training, documents, supplier qualification, computerised system validation and EHS.

| Class | Approver | Typical approval time | Regulatory input |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minor | Site QA Manager | 10 working days | Not required |
| Major | Site QA Manager + Head of affected department | 20 working days | Consulted |
| Critical | Head QA (Corporate) | 30 working days | Mandatory; filing before implementation |

### 5.3 Action plan and implementation

1. Approved CRs have an action plan with owners and target dates.
2. No change may be implemented before QA approval. Emergency changes (safety risk) may be implemented with verbal approval from Head QA (Corporate), documented within 2 working days.
3. For Critical changes affecting export products, implementation waits for regulatory approval, as tracked by Regulatory Affairs.
4. Stability commitment: for changes in formulation, packaging primary pack or site, place at least the first 3 commercial batches on long-term stability.

### 5.4 Closure and effectiveness

1. QA verifies that all actions are complete and evidence is attached.
2. A post-implementation review is performed after the first 3 batches or **90 days**, whichever is later, to confirm the change achieved its objective without unintended effects.
3. CRs open beyond 180 days are escalated at the monthly Quality Review Meeting.

### 5.5 Examples from 2025-26

| CR No. | Description | Class |
|---|---|---|
| CC/U1/25/0211 | Second bilayer compression machine for Glucosan-M 500 SR | Major |
| CC/U2/25/0094 | New label artwork for Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup after recall CR-2309 | Major |
| CC/U3/26/0017 | Alternate API source for Rosuvastatin calcium for export market | Critical |
| CC/CORP/26/0005 | eQMS upgrade to version 9 | Major |

## 6. Records

- Change Request with impact assessments and approvals (eQMS) – life of product + 5 years
- Action plan evidence and closure report – with the CR
- Change control log and monthly metrics – 10 years
