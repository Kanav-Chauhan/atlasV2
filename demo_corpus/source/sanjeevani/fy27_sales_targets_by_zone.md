# FY27 Sales Targets by Zone

## 1. Purpose

This document communicates the approved FY27 (April 2026 – March 2027) domestic secondary sales targets by zone, brand and quarter. It is the basis for the FY27 incentive scheme in the Field Force Guidelines FY27. Distribution is restricted to the sales management team and Leadership.

## 2. National target

| Item | FY26 actual | FY27 target | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic sales (₹ crore) | 1,426.8 | 1,520.0 | 6.5% |
| Number of MRs (average) | 176 | 180 | — |
| Sales per MR per month (₹ lakh) | 67.6 | 70.4 | 4.1% |

The FY27 domestic target of **₹1,520 crore** was approved by the MD & CEO on 10 April 2026, after the FY27 Annual Operating Plan review.

## 8. Target-setting rules

8.1 Zone targets are cascaded to regions, areas and HQs by the 20th of April, based on the last 12 months' secondary sales, market potential (IQVIA-type audit data), and number of A-category doctors.
8.2 HQ targets cannot be lower than 95% of the HQ's FY26 actual unless the HQ was split.
8.3 Vacant territories for more than 60 days have their target reallocated to adjacent HQs in the same area.
8.4 Mid-year target revisions are allowed only once (in October), with NSM and CFO approval.
