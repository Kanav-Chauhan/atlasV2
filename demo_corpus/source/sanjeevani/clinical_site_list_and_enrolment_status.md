# Clinical Site List and Enrolment Status

## 1. Purpose

This report lists all investigational sites participating in the SJ-2041 Phase III trial (protocol SPL/CLN/PRT-017, CTRI/2025/03/061842) and gives the enrolment and retention status as of the data snapshot date of **30 September 2026**. It is updated monthly by Clinical Operations and shared with the Clinical team and Leadership.

## 2. Status Summary

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Number of active sites | 38 sites in India, across 18 states and union territories |
| Planned sample size | 1,240 patients |
| Patients randomised | 1,240 (100%) – enrolment complete |
| First patient randomised | 07 April 2025 (site S11, New Delhi) |
| Last patient randomised | 13 March 2026 (site S29, Vijayawada) |
| Patients discontinued (all reasons) | 92 (7.4% of randomised) |
| Patients completed week 24 | 1,148 (all remaining patients) |
| Patients ongoing in treatment | 0 – treatment phase complete |
| Last patient last visit | 28 August 2026 (week 24); safety follow-up completed 11 September 2026 |
| Database lock (planned) | 30 November 2026 |
| Topline readout (expected) | January 2027 |

Randomisation is 1:1 (SJ-2041 vs comparator), stratified by baseline HbA1c (< 8.5% vs ≥ 8.5%) and region (North, South, East, West).

## 6. Site Performance Notes

- **Top enrollers:** S07 Harbour Medical College Hospital, Mumbai and S25 Brahmaputra Diabetes Centre, Guwahati (50 patients each).
- **Highest discontinuation:** S04 Surat (7 of 43, 16.3%) – site retrained on retention; a patient-reminder SMS service was added in March 2026.
- **Sites under monitoring focus:** S20 Ludhiana and S27 Hyderabad (Deccan Heights), each with discontinuation of 16% or more; additional on-site monitoring visit every 4 weeks until database lock.
- **Audits:** Clinical QA audited 8 sites in FY2025-26 (S04, S07, S11, S14, S22, S25, S26, S33); no critical findings; 3 major findings related to informed consent version control, all closed.

## 7. Monitoring Plan to Database Lock

1. 100% source data verification for the primary endpoint (HbA1c) and all SAEs.
2. Central laboratory (single NABL-accredited lab in Mumbai) for HbA1c to remove inter-lab variability.
3. Query resolution target: all queries answered within 5 working days; open queries at snapshot: 318 (target zero by 15 November 2026).
4. Site close-out visits started on 01 October 2026 and will be completed for all 38 sites by 20 November 2026.
