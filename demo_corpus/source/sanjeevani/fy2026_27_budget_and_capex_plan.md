# FY2026-27 Budget and Capex Plan

## 1. Purpose

This document sets out the approved revenue, cost and capital expenditure (capex) budget of Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. for FY2026-27 (1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027). The Annual Operating Plan was noted by the Board on 22 April 2026; this detailed budget was released to budget holders on 01 May 2026. All figures are standalone and in ₹ crore unless stated.

## 2. Summary

| Item | FY2025-26 actual | FY2026-27 budget | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue from operations | 2,460.0 | 2,690.0 | 9.3% |
| Domestic (India branded formulations) | 1,426.8 | 1,520.0 | 6.5% |
| Exports | 1,033.2 | 1,170.0 | 13.2% |
| EBITDA | 548.6 | 604.0 | 10.1% |
| EBITDA margin | 22.3% | 22.5% | +20 bps |
| R&D spend | 167.3 | 185.0 | 10.6% |
| R&D as % of revenue | 6.8% | 6.9% | |
| Capex | 240.0 | 420.0 | 75.0% |

The budget revenue growth of 9.3% is deliberately below the 10–12% guidance in the FY2025-26 Annual Results Summary. The difference is upside from four US launches that are not yet approved and is not budgeted.

## 5. R&D budget

The total FY27 R&D budget is **₹185 crore** (6.9% of budgeted revenue), made up of ₹177 crore of revenue expenses and ₹8 crore of capital equipment for the Hyderabad labs (part of the ₹33 crore Hyderabad capex line in Section 6). ₹62 crore of the total is for the SJ-2041 clinical programme (Phase III completion and NDA preparation). The programme split is in the R&D Pipeline Review FY27.

## 8. Budget control rules

1. Budget holders may move up to 10% between cost heads within their own budget with Finance controller approval.
2. Any capex item above ₹5 crore needs a capital expenditure request approved by the CFO; above ₹25 crore it also needs MD & CEO approval.
3. A cost overrun above 5% on any Board-approved project (including Vizag Block C) is reported to the Board at its next meeting.
4. Monthly budget-versus-actual reports are issued by FP&A by the 10th working day of the following month.
