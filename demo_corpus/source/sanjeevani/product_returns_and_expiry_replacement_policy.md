# Product Returns and Expiry Replacement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which Sanjeevani Pharma products can be returned by stockists and pharmacies, how near-expiry and expired stock is replaced or credited, how damaged goods are handled, and how products recalled from the market are refunded. It is written for our trade partners, pharmacies and patients, and is published on our website.

## 2. Scope

Covers all Sanjeevani-branded medicines sold in India through our C&F agents and stockists, including Glucosan-M 500, Cardivas 10, Amlosan 5, Pantosan 40, Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup and Dermacalm Cream. Export sales follow the terms of each export contract.

## 3. Shelf life of our products

Expiry is printed on every strip, bottle and tube as "EXP MM/YYYY". A product may be used until the last day of the expiry month.

| Product | Shelf life from manufacture | Storage |
|---|---|---|
| Glucosan-M 500 | 36 months | Below 30°C, protect from moisture |
| Cardivas 10 | 36 months | Below 30°C, protect from light and moisture |
| Amlosan 5 | 36 months | Below 30°C, protect from light |
| Pantosan 40 | 24 months | Below 30°C, protect from moisture |
| Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup | 24 months | Below 30°C, do not refrigerate |
| Dermacalm Cream | 30 months | Below 25°C, do not freeze |

## 4. What can be returned

| Category | Accepted? | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Near-expiry stock (3 months or less of shelf life remaining), sealed and in original pack | Yes – replaced with fresh stock | 100% replacement of the same product |
| Expired stock, returned within 6 months after the expiry month | Yes | Credit note at 100% of the price to stockist (PTS) |
| Expired stock returned more than 6 months after expiry | No | — |
| Damaged in transit (breakage, leakage, crushed cartons) | Yes, if reported within 48 hours of receipt | 100% replacement |
| Recalled batches | Yes, always | Full credit at invoice price plus return freight |
| Opened or partly used packs (e.g. opened bottles, tubes with broken seal) | No, except recalled batches | — |
| Physician samples ("Not for sale") | No – samples must never be sold | — |
| Stock damaged by improper storage at the stockist or pharmacy | No | — |
| Free goods received under a trade scheme | No | — |

4.1 **Near-expiry Dermacalm Cream:** yes, a pharmacy can return near-expiry Dermacalm Cream through its stockist for replacement, provided the tubes are sealed, in their cartons, and have 3 months or less of shelf life remaining. Because Dermacalm must be kept below 25°C, the pharmacy must confirm on the return form that it was stored as labelled.
4.2 Cut strips (part strips of tablets) are accepted only if the batch number and expiry are visible on the remaining part.

## 5. How a return works

1. The pharmacy hands the stock to its stockist with a completed Return Note (product, batch, expiry, quantity, reason).
2. The stockist sends returns to the Sanjeevani C&F agent once a month, by the 10th, with a consolidated return list.
3. The C&F agent checks the goods within 7 working days and keeps them in a locked "Returned Goods" cage.
4. Sanjeevani issues a **credit note within 30 days** of the C&F agent's acceptance, or dispatches replacement stock with the stockist's next order.
5. Returned goods are never resold. They are destroyed by an authorised waste handler after a QA decision, with a destruction certificate.

## 6. Limits

6.1 Expiry credit to a stockist in any financial year is capped at **2% of its purchases in the previous 12 months**. Returns above the cap are credited only with National Sales Manager approval.
6.2 Damage claims must include photographs of the damaged goods and the transporter's remark on the delivery receipt.

## 7. Recalled products

7.1 When Sanjeevani recalls a batch, we publish the product, batch number and reason on our website and inform stockists, pharmacies and the drug regulators. Pharmacies must stop selling the batch immediately.
7.2 **Refund:** all recalled stock returned through the trade receives **full credit at the invoice price plus the cost of return freight**, whether the packs are sealed, opened or part-used. The 2% expiry cap does not apply to recalls.
7.3 **Patients** holding a recalled product may return it to the pharmacy where it was bought; the pharmacy gives a replacement pack of the same medicine (or a refund if no replacement is available) and is reimbursed by Sanjeevani through its stockist.
7.4 **Example – batch CR-2309 (August 2025):** Sanjeevani voluntarily recalled batch CR-2309 of Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup in August 2025 because of a label misprint (the label showed the wrong manufacturing licence number). There was no safety impact. 18,400 bottles had been distributed. Stockists and pharmacies received full credit at invoice price for every returned bottle, and freight was reimbursed.
