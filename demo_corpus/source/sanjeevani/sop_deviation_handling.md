# SOP – Deviation Handling

## 1. Purpose

To define a uniform system for reporting, classifying, investigating and closing deviations from approved procedures, specifications, master records and GMP requirements, so that product quality and patient safety are protected and recurrence is prevented through CAPA.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all manufacturing sites (Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar), Unit II – Baddi, Unit III – Vizag SEZ), the warehouses attached to them and QC laboratories. Out of Specification (OOS) laboratory results are handled under SPL/QC/SOP-021 and are linked to a deviation only when a manufacturing cause is suspected. This SOP supersedes SPL/QA/SOP-003 (Deviation Handling, version 2.0, 2022).

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Any employee | Reports the deviation to the area supervisor immediately, and in any case within the same shift |
| Department Head | Raises the deviation in the eQMS within 1 working day of discovery; leads the investigation |
| Site QA | Assigns the deviation number, proposes classification, reviews investigation |
| Head, Quality Assurance (Corporate) | Approves Critical deviations; reviews the monthly deviation trend |
| CAPA Owner | Implements actions within the agreed due date |

## 4. Definitions

- **Deviation**: any departure from an approved instruction or established standard.
- **Planned deviation**: a pre-approved, temporary departure, approved through the eQMS before execution; not used for recurring changes (use Change Control, SPL/QA/SOP-006).
- **Critical deviation**: a deviation that has, or is likely to have, a direct impact on patient safety or product quality, or a serious data integrity breach (e.g. mix-up of products, sterility failure, falsified record).
- **Major deviation**: a deviation that could affect product quality but is not likely to harm the patient (e.g. CPP excursion, failed in-process check, unvalidated hold time).
- **Minor deviation**: a deviation with no expected impact on product quality (e.g. a GDP lapse corrected the same day, a missed signature on a non-critical log).

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Reporting and immediate action

1. Stop the activity if quality may be affected and secure the material (status label "ON HOLD").
2. Inform the supervisor and site QA verbally.
3. Department Head raises the deviation in the eQMS within 1 working day. The deviation number format is DEV/<Unit>/<YY>/<NNNN>, e.g. DEV/U1/26/0147.

### 5.2 Classification and escalation timelines

| Class | Escalation and initial impact assessment | Investigation closure target |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Head QA (Corporate) informed within 24 hours of discovery | 15 working days |
| Major | Initial assessment approved by site QA within 3 working days | 30 calendar days |
| Minor | Initial assessment approved by site QA within 7 days | 30 calendar days |

Extensions beyond the closure target require a documented justification approved by site QA (Major/Minor) or Head QA Corporate (Critical). No more than two extensions are allowed.

### 5.3 Investigation

1. Use the 5-Why tool for Minor deviations; use Ishikawa (6M) plus 5-Why for Major and Critical deviations.
2. Review other batches made on the same equipment, by the same people or with the same raw material lot, going back at least 6 months, to determine the scope of impact.
3. "Human error" may be recorded as root cause only if the investigation shows why the error happened (training, procedure clarity, workload) and a system-level CAPA is defined.
4. Product impact assessment must state the batch disposition: release, reprocess, reject or recall (see SPL/QA/SOP-015).

### 5.4 CAPA and effectiveness

1. Each Major and Critical deviation must have at least one CAPA recorded in the CAPA register.
2. A **CAPA effectiveness check is performed at 90 days** after CAPA implementation, using pre-defined criteria (e.g. no recurrence in the next 10 batches, trend chart within limits).
3. If the effectiveness check fails, a new investigation is opened.

### 5.5 Trending

Site QA presents a monthly deviation trend at the Quality Review Meeting. A recurrence of the same root cause three times in 6 months is automatically upgraded by one class.

## 6. Records

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| Deviation report (eQMS) | Life of product + 5 years |
| Investigation attachments (Ishikawa, interviews, data) | With the deviation |
| Monthly deviation trend report | 10 years |
