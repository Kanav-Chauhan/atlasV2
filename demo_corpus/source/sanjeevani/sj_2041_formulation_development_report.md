# SJ-2041 Formulation Development Report

## 1. Summary

SJ-2041 is a novel once-daily fixed-dose combination (FDC) of the in-house DPP-4 inhibitor **SPL-DP41 50 mg** with **metformin hydrochloride 1,000 mg sustained release**, developed at the Hyderabad R&D centre (Genome Valley) for adults with type 2 diabetes inadequately controlled on metformin alone. After evaluating three prototype designs, a **bilayer tablet** (immediate-release SPL-DP41 layer + hydrophilic-matrix metformin SR layer) was selected. Three registration batches of 60,000 tablets each were made at Unit I pilot facility and met all release specifications. The formulation was frozen for the Phase III trial (protocol SPL/CLN/PRT-017).

## 2. Target Product Profile

| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| Dosage form | Film-coated tablet, once daily with evening meal |
| Strength | SPL-DP41 50 mg / metformin HCl 1,000 mg SR |
| SPL-DP41 release | Not less than 85% in 30 minutes (immediate release) |
| Metformin release | Sustained over 10–12 hours, profile matching Glucosan-M 500 SR (2 tablets) |
| Tablet size | ≤ 22 mm long (capsule shape) for swallowability |
| Shelf life target | 24 months at 30°C/75% RH (Zone IVb) |
| Pack | Alu-Alu blister, 10 tablets |

## 3. Pre-formulation Findings

- SPL-DP41 is a BCS Class III molecule (high solubility, low permeability), hygroscopic above 60% RH.
- Binary mixture compatibility (40°C/75% RH, 4 weeks): SPL-DP41 showed a Maillard-type degradant (impurity RRT 1.32) with lactose; lactose was excluded. Compatible with microcrystalline cellulose, mannitol, HPMC K100M and magnesium stearate.
- Metformin HCl has poor compressibility; wet granulation with povidone K30 is needed for the SR layer.

## 4. Prototype Evaluation

| Prototype | Design | Key result | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Monolithic SR matrix containing both APIs | SPL-DP41 release only 61% at 30 minutes; dose dumping risk not acceptable | Rejected |
| P2 | Metformin SR core with SPL-DP41 drug coating | Coating assay variability RSD 4.8%; coating time 9 hours per batch | Rejected |
| P3 | Bilayer tablet: IR SPL-DP41 layer + metformin SR layer | Both release targets met; layer weight RSD ≤ 1.5% | **Selected** |

## 6. Process Development

- SR layer: high-shear wet granulation, drying in fluid bed dryer to LOD 1.5–2.5%, milling through 1.0 mm screen.
- IR layer: direct compression blend; blend uniformity RSD ≤ 2.0%.
- Bilayer compression: first-layer tamping force 3–5 kN; main compression 25–30 kN; hardness 18–26 kp.
- Coating: weight gain 3.0% ± 0.5%, inlet air RH controlled below 45% because of SPL-DP41 hygroscopicity.

Critical process parameters identified by risk assessment (FMEA): HPMC particle size, granulation end-point (power consumption), first-layer tamping force and coating weight gain.

Batch size: 60,000 tablets each, manufactured July–August 2024.

## 8. Stability (6 months)

At 40°C/75% RH for 6 months the highest total impurity was 0.46% (RB-02) and dissolution remained within limits. At 30°C/75% RH, no significant change was seen at 6 months. Long-term studies continue to 36 months; the proposed initial shelf life is 24 months.

## 9. Conclusions and Next Steps

1. The bilayer design (P3) is robust and ready for Phase III clinical supplies; 2.1 lakh tablets of active and matching placebo-comparator supplies to be manufactured by February 2025.
2. Scale-up to commercial batch size of 2,00,000 tablets at Unit I planned after topline readout, with a dedicated bilayer press (change control to be raised).
3. Patent application for the bilayer composition filed with the Indian Patent Office in October 2024.
