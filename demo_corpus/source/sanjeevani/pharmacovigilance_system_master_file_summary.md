# Pharmacovigilance System Master File Summary

## 1. Purpose

This document summarises the Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF) of Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. It describes how the company collects, assesses and reports adverse drug reactions (ADRs) for its marketed products in India and export markets, in line with the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, the Pharmacovigilance Programme of India (PvPI) guidance and the revised Schedule M. The full PSMF (version 4.0, 212 pages) is held by Regulatory Affairs and is available to CDSCO on request within 7 days.

## 3. Products Covered

All marketed products: Glucosan-M 500, Cardivas 10, Amlosan 5, Pantosan 40, Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup and Dermacalm Cream, plus 34 other generic products. Investigational product SJ-2041 is covered by clinical safety processes (Serious Adverse Event Reporting SOP, SPL/CLN/SOP-005) until marketing authorisation.

## 5. Reporting Timelines

| Report type | Timeline | Recipient |
|---|---|---|
| Serious unexpected ADR (domestic) | Within 15 calendar days of receipt | PvPI / CDSCO |
| Fatal or life-threatening ADR | Initial report within 7 calendar days; follow-up within 8 further days | PvPI / CDSCO |
| Non-serious ADR | Included in PSUR | CDSCO |
| PSUR for new drugs | Every 6 months for the first 2 years, then annually for 2 years | CDSCO |
| Export partner ICSR | Per safety data exchange agreement, typically within 5 working days | Partner |

Compliance with expedited timelines in FY2025-26 was **98.6%** (2 late reports out of 143 expedited reports, both by one day; CAPA raised).

## 6. Safety Database and Processes

- Validated safety database (commercial E2B(R3)-compliant system), hosted in India, access restricted to 14 named users.
- MedDRA version 28.1 used for coding; WHO-Drug Global for products.
- Case processing: triage within 1 working day, data entry within 3 working days, medical review within 5 working days.
- Signal detection: monthly disproportionality review and quarterly Safety Review Committee meeting chaired by the QPPV.

## 7. Signals and Risk Management (FY2025-26)

| Product | Signal / issue | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Glucosan-M 500 | Lactic acidosis (2 reports, both in patients with renal impairment) | Known risk; label already contraindicates eGFR < 30; no action |
| Cardivas 10 | Myalgia cluster from one city (7 reports) | Evaluated; consistent with known profile; no signal |
| Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup | Batch CR-2309 recall (label misprint) | No associated ADRs; no safety impact |
| Dermacalm Cream | Skin atrophy with prolonged use (3 reports) | Label strengthened: "not for use beyond 3 weeks without medical advice" |

## 8. Quality Management of the PV System

- PV SOPs: 18 active SOPs, reviewed every 2 years.
- Internal PV audit: annual; last audit April 2026 (Hyderabad PV unit) found 4 minor observations, with CAPAs due by 30 June 2026.
- Training: all employees receive ADR-awareness training at induction; field force refresher every 6 months.
- KPIs reported monthly to the Head, Regulatory Affairs: timeliness, case quality score (target ≥ 95%), backlog (target zero cases older than 5 days).

## 9. Outsourced Activities

Literature screening and case data entry are outsourced to a PV service provider in Pune under a safety data agreement; Sanjeevani retains responsibility for medical review and regulatory submission. The provider was audited in October 2025 with no major findings.
