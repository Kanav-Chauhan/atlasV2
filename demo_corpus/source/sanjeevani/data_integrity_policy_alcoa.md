# Data Integrity Policy – ALCOA+

## 1. Purpose

This policy states Sanjeevani Pharma's commitment to complete, consistent and accurate GMP, GLP and GCP data throughout the data lifecycle, and sets the minimum controls every site and function must apply. It forms part of the company's regulatory commitments to CDSCO, the US FDA and WHO prequalification, and was revised in June 2024 as CAPA/U3/24/014 following the 2024 US FDA inspection of Unit III, where audit trail review was a Form 483 observation.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all paper and electronic records generated in manufacturing (Units I, II and III), quality control, quality assurance, warehousing, R&D (Hyderabad), clinical development and pharmacovigilance, and to contract laboratories and CROs acting on Sanjeevani's behalf.

## 3. ALCOA+ Principles

| Principle | Meaning at Sanjeevani | Example control |
|---|---|---|
| **A**ttributable | Every entry identifies who performed it and when | Unique user IDs; no shared logins; signature log maintained by QA |
| **L**egible | Readable and permanent for the retention period | Indelible blue ink; no pencil; no correction fluid |
| **C**ontemporaneous | Recorded at the time the activity is performed | Entries made at the point of work; no "rough notes" transcribed later |
| **O**riginal | The first capture of the data, or a certified true copy | Instrument raw data retained; printouts are not the original for electronic systems |
| **A**ccurate | Correct, truthful and verified | Second-person verification for critical entries (dispensing, calculations) |
| **+ Complete** | All data including repeats, re-integrations and failed runs | All injections in a sequence retained; no deletion rights for analysts |
| **+ Consistent** | Chronological, date/time-stamped in sequence | Synchronised network time on all computerised systems |
| **+ Enduring** | Durable for the full retention period | Validated backup; archive restore test annually |
| **+ Available** | Retrievable for review and inspection | Retrieval within 2 hours for any record requested during inspection |

## 4. Policy Requirements

### 4.1 Computerised systems

1. All GxP computerised systems must be validated per GAMP 5 category and listed in the Computerised System Inventory (currently 146 systems).
2. Audit trails must be enabled, cannot be disabled by users, and capture who, what, when and why for every create, modify and delete action.
3. User roles follow segregation of duties: analysts cannot delete data, change methods after approval or alter system clock settings. Administrator rights are held by IT, not by laboratory users.
4. Passwords expire every 90 days; accounts lock after 3 failed attempts; screens lock after 10 minutes of inactivity.

### 4.2 Audit trail review

| Data type | Review frequency | Reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| Chromatography data (HPLC/GC) for release | Every batch, before release | QC reviewer (second person) |
| Dissolution and UV data for release | Every batch, before release | QC reviewer |
| e-BMR (Unit I) | Every batch | IPQA |
| System-level audit trail (all GxP systems) | Monthly | Site QA |
| LIMS user access review | Quarterly | Site QA + IT |

### 4.3 Paper records

1. Blank forms and logbooks are issued by QA with a unique control number and reconciled on return.
2. Corrections: single line strike-through so the original remains legible, with reason, signature and date.
3. Uncontrolled paper (sticky notes, scrap paper) must not be used to record GMP data.

### 4.4 Data retention

| Record type | Minimum retention |
|---|---|
| Batch records and QC raw data | 1 year after product expiry, minimum 5 years |
| Validation data | Life of the system/process + 5 years |
| Clinical trial essential documents | 15 years after trial completion |
| Pharmacovigilance case data | 10 years after product withdrawal |

## 5. Governance

- **Data Integrity Council**: chaired by Head QA (Corporate) with Head Regulatory Affairs, Head IT and the three Plant Heads; meets every quarter.
- **Annual data integrity audit** at each site by Corporate QA, including a sample of 50 audit-trail records.
- **Training**: all GxP staff complete ALCOA+ training at induction and annually; 100% completion is tracked as a KPI.

## 6. Reporting Concerns

Any employee who suspects falsification, deletion or manipulation of data must report it to the site QA Manager, the Head QA (Corporate) or through the Whistleblower Policy channel. Data integrity breaches are classified as **Critical deviations** and the Head QA (Corporate) must be informed within 24 hours. Retaliation against a reporter is a disciplinary offence.

## 7. Consequences

Deliberate falsification of GMP data is gross misconduct and may result in termination of employment, in addition to any regulatory reporting obligation.
