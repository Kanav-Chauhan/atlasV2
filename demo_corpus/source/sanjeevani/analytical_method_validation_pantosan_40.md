# Analytical Method Validation – Pantosan 40

## 1. Objective

To validate the reversed-phase HPLC method STP/QC/PT4/02 for the **assay** and **related substances** of Pantosan 40 (Pantoprazole Sodium Gastro-resistant Tablets IP, equivalent to pantoprazole 40 mg), in accordance with ICH Q2(R2). The method replaces the earlier UV assay and allows a single HPLC run for assay and impurities. It will be transferred to QC Unit I.

## 2. Method Summary

| Parameter | Condition |
|---|---|
| Column | C18, 250 × 4.6 mm, 5 µm |
| Mobile phase | Gradient: A = 0.01 M phosphate buffer pH 7.0; B = acetonitrile |
| Flow rate | 1.0 mL/min |
| Column temperature | 40°C |
| Detection | UV at 290 nm |
| Injection volume | 20 µL |
| Run time | 45 minutes |
| Sample concentration (assay) | 0.4 mg/mL pantoprazole in diluent (0.1 M NaOH : acetonitrile, 50:50) |
| Retention time | Pantoprazole about 17.5 minutes |

## 3. Validation Protocol and Acceptance Criteria

Protocol SPL/RND/PRT-052 was approved on 02 May 2025. Validation was performed from 05 May to 13 June 2025 at the Analytical R&D laboratory, Hyderabad.

## 4. Results

### 4.1 Specificity

Forced degradation (acid 0.1 N HCl 30 min; base 0.1 N NaOH 2 h; peroxide 3% 1 h; heat 105°C 24 h; light 1.2 million lux·h) produced degradation of 8–22%. Pantoprazole peak purity angle was less than purity threshold in all conditions. Resolution between pantoprazole and the closest impurity (sulphone impurity) was **3.4** (criterion ≥ 2.0). Placebo showed no interference at the retention times of pantoprazole and known impurities.

### 4.2 Summary table

| Parameter | Acceptance criterion | Result | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| System suitability – tailing factor | ≤ 2.0 | 1.12 | Pass |
| System suitability – theoretical plates | ≥ 5,000 | 14,860 | Pass |

| Linearity (assay), 50–150% of target | r² ≥ 0.999 | r² = 0.9998 | Pass |
| Linearity (impurities), LOQ–150% of limit | r² ≥ 0.99 | r² = 0.9991 (sulphone) | Pass |
| Accuracy (assay), 80/100/120%, n = 9 | Mean recovery 98.0–102.0% | 98.9–100.8% (mean 99.7%) | Pass |
| Accuracy (impurities), LOQ to 150% | Recovery 85.0–115.0% | 92.4–104.6% | Pass |
| Repeatability (assay), n = 6 | %RSD ≤ 2.0% | 0.6% | Pass |
| Intermediate precision (different analyst, day, column) | %RSD ≤ 2.0% (n = 12 overall) | 0.9% | Pass |
| LOD (sulphone impurity) | S/N ≥ 3 | 0.015% | Reported |

| Solution stability | Change ≤ 2.0% | Stable 24 h at 25°C (change 0.7%) | Pass |
| Filter validation (0.45 µm nylon vs PVDF) | Difference ≤ 2.0% | Nylon 0.3%; PVDF 0.5% | Both suitable |

### 4.3 Robustness

Deliberate variations were made: flow rate ± 0.1 mL/min, column temperature ± 5°C, buffer pH ± 0.2, wavelength ± 2 nm. System suitability passed in all conditions. The method is sensitive to buffer pH: at pH 6.8 the resolution of sulphone from pantoprazole fell to 2.6 (still above 2.0). Buffer pH must therefore be controlled at **7.0 ± 0.1** in the method.

### 4.4 Specification limits verified

| Test | Specification limit |
|---|---|
| Assay | 95.0–105.0% of label claim |
| Sulphone impurity | Not more than 0.5% |
| Any other individual impurity | Not more than 0.2% |
| Total impurities | Not more than 1.5% |

## 5. Deviations

One deviation was recorded: on 21 May 2025 a column oven failure during intermediate precision runs (instrument HPLC-07). Runs were repeated on HPLC-09 after qualification check. No impact on validation conclusions.

## 6. Conclusion

Method STP/QC/PT4/02 is specific, linear, accurate, precise and robust for the assay and related substances of Pantosan 40 tablets and is **suitable for its intended use** in release and stability testing.

## 7. Method Transfer

Transfer to QC Unit I (Dr. Meenal Joshi, QC Manager) by comparative testing on 3 batches: acceptance difference in mean assay ≤ 2.0% and impurities ≤ 0.05% absolute. Planned completion: September 2025, under change control.
