# Amlosan 5 Product Monograph

*Amlodipine Besilate Tablets IP 5 mg*
*For the use of a Registered Medical Practitioner, Hospital or Laboratory only*

## 1. Composition

Each uncoated tablet contains:

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Amlodipine Besilate IP equivalent to Amlodipine | 5 mg |
| Excipients | q.s. |

Excipients: microcrystalline cellulose, dibasic calcium phosphate anhydrous, sodium starch glycolate and magnesium stearate.

## 2. Description

White, round, flat tablets with a break-line on one side and "A5" debossed on the other. The break-line allows the tablet to be divided into equal halves (2.5 mg) where a lower dose is prescribed.

## 3. Pharmacology

### 3.1 Mechanism of action
Amlodipine is a dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker that inhibits the influx of calcium ions into vascular smooth muscle and cardiac muscle. It acts directly on vascular smooth muscle to cause peripheral arterial vasodilation, reducing peripheral vascular resistance and blood pressure. In angina it reduces total ischaemic burden by dilating peripheral arterioles and coronary arteries.

### 3.2 Pharmacokinetics
| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Time to peak (Tmax) | 6–12 hours |
| Absolute bioavailability | 64–80% |
| Protein binding | About 97.5% |
| Metabolism | Extensively hepatic (about 90%) to inactive metabolites |
| Half-life | 35–50 hours, permitting once-daily dosing |
| Steady state | Reached after 7–8 days of daily dosing |

## 4. Indications

- Hypertension, alone or in combination with other antihypertensives
- Chronic stable angina
- Vasospastic (Prinzmetal's) angina

## 5. Dosage and administration

5.1 Take once daily, with or without food, at about the same time each day.

| Population | Starting dose | Maximum dose |
|---|---|---|
| Adults – hypertension | 5 mg once daily | 10 mg once daily |
| Adults – angina | 5–10 mg once daily | 10 mg once daily |
| Elderly, small or frail patients | 2.5 mg once daily | 10 mg once daily |
| Hepatic impairment | 2.5 mg once daily, titrate slowly | Titrate with caution |
| Children 6–17 years (hypertension) | 2.5–5 mg once daily | 5 mg once daily |

5.2 Titrate over 7–14 days according to response. Renal impairment does not require dose adjustment; amlodipine is not dialysable.

## 6. Contraindications

- Hypersensitivity to amlodipine, other dihydropyridines or excipients
- Severe hypotension
- Shock, including cardiogenic shock
- Obstruction of the outflow tract of the left ventricle (e.g. high-grade aortic stenosis)
- Haemodynamically unstable heart failure after acute myocardial infarction

## 7. Warnings and precautions

7.1 **Heart failure:** use with caution in patients with heart failure; amlodipine may increase the risk of pulmonary oedema in NYHA class III–IV.
7.2 **Hepatic impairment:** half-life is prolonged; start at the lower dose.
7.3 **Interactions:** strong or moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g. clarithromycin, ketoconazole, diltiazem) may increase amlodipine exposure; monitor for hypotension and oedema. Limit simvastatin to 20 mg daily when co-administered. Grapefruit juice is not recommended.
7.4 Do not stop abruptly in patients with angina without medical advice.

## 8. Adverse reactions

Very common: peripheral (ankle) oedema. Common: headache, dizziness, flushing, palpitations, fatigue, nausea, abdominal pain. Uncommon: gingival hyperplasia, mood changes, hypotension. Ankle oedema is dose-related and more frequent at 10 mg.

## 9. Pregnancy and lactation

Safety in pregnancy has not been established. Use only when there is no safer alternative and the disease itself carries greater risk. Amlodipine is excreted in human milk; decide on breastfeeding after benefit-risk assessment.

## 10. Overdose

May cause excessive peripheral vasodilation, marked and probably prolonged hypotension, and reflex tachycardia. Provide active cardiovascular support, including elevation of extremities and monitoring of fluid volume. Activated charcoal within 2 hours of ingestion may reduce absorption.
