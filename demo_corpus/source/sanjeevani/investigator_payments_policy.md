# Investigator Payments Policy

## 1. Purpose

To set out how Sanjeevani Pharma pays clinical trial sites, investigators and participants, so that payments are at fair market value (FMV), linked to work actually done, transparent, and compliant with the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, the Code of Business Conduct and the UCPMP 2024 code. Payments must never be, or appear to be, an inducement to prescribe Sanjeevani products.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all Sanjeevani-sponsored clinical trials and BA/BE studies, including the SJ-2041 Phase III trial (38 sites in India), whether payments are made directly or through a CRO.

## 3. Principles

1. **Written agreement first:** no payment without a signed Clinical Trial Agreement (CTA) between Sanjeevani, the institution and the Principal Investigator (PI), with a budget annexure.
2. **Pay the institution:** payments go to the institution's bank account, not to an individual's personal account, unless the institution's policy specifies otherwise in writing.
3. **Fair market value:** per-patient fees are set from the FMV grid (Section 5), reviewed annually by Clinical Development and Finance.
4. **Pay for work done:** payments are linked to completed visits recorded in the EDC and verified by monitoring.
5. **No enrolment bonuses:** bonuses or extra payments for faster recruitment are prohibited.
6. **Transparency:** all payments are disclosed to the Ethics Committee in the budget submitted for approval.

## 4. Approval Authority

| Payment type | Approver |
|---|---|
| Site budget up to ₹50 lakh per site | Head, Clinical Development |
| Site budget above ₹50 lakh per site | Head, Clinical Development + Chief Financial Officer |
| Any deviation from the FMV grid above 15% | Chief Financial Officer |
| Payments to government institutions | As above, plus Legal review of institutional rules |

## 5. FMV Grid – SJ-2041 Phase III

| Item | Amount (₹) | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Site start-up fee (one-time, non-refundable) | 1,50,000 | Site initiation visit completed |
| Ethics Committee fee | As charged by the EC (pass-through, maximum 1,00,000) | EC invoice |
| Per randomised patient – full completion (8 visits) | 42,000 | Pro-rated by visit as below |
| Screening visit (V1) | 3,000 | Visit entered in EDC |
| Screen failure | 2,500 per screen failure, maximum 1 screen failure paid per 2 randomised patients | Visit entered in EDC |
| Randomisation visit (V2) | 7,000 | Visit entered in EDC |
| Visits V3–V6 | 5,000 each | Visit entered in EDC |
| End of treatment visit (V7) | 10,000 | Visit entered and SDV completed |
| Follow-up visit (V8) | 2,000 | Visit entered in EDC |
| Close-out fee | 50,000 | Close-out visit completed and all queries resolved |
| Archiving fee | 25,000 | Confirmation of archiving for 15 years |

Note: V1 + V2 + 4 × 5,000 (V3–V6) + V7 + V8 = 3,000 + 7,000 + 20,000 + 10,000 + 2,000 = ₹42,000 per completed patient.

## 6. Participant Payments

- Participants receive reimbursement of travel and meal expenses of **₹1,500 per completed visit**, paid by the site by bank transfer or UPI within 7 days of the visit.
- Reimbursement is described in the informed consent form approved by the Ethics Committee.
- Payment for participation beyond reimbursement is not allowed in the SJ-2041 trial.
- Compensation for trial-related injury is separate and governed by the Serious Adverse Event Reporting SOP (SPL/CLN/SOP-005).

## 7. Payment Process

1. CRO or Clinical Operations generates a visit-based payment report from the EDC on the 5th of each month.
2. Site confirms the report within 10 days; the site invoices the CRO/Sanjeevani.
3. Finance pays within **45 days** of a valid invoice. Payments are made quarterly for sites with fewer than 10 patients.
4. **Holdback:** 10% of patient-visit payments are held back until close-out and query resolution.
5. TDS is deducted per the Income-tax Act; GST is charged by the institution where applicable.

## 8. Prohibited Payments

- Cash payments of any kind.
- Gifts, travel or hospitality to investigators or site staff outside the CTA.
- Payments to investigators who are also high prescribers of Sanjeevani products for "advisory" roles without a documented, legitimate need (reviewed by Compliance).
- Any payment to a site with an unresolved major audit finding on informed consent, until the finding is closed.

## 9. Disclosure and Audit

- Investigator payments are recorded in the clinical trial payments register and reconciled with the trial budget quarterly.
- Internal Audit reviews a sample of 10 sites every year.
- SJ-2041 total site budget (38 sites): ₹6.4 crore, approved by the Chief Financial Officer on 18 February 2025.
