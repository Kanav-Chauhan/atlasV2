# SOP – Purified Water System Monitoring

## 1. Purpose

To define the sampling plan, test parameters, alert and action levels and response actions for the Purified Water (PW) generation and distribution systems, ensuring water used in manufacturing and cleaning consistently meets pharmacopoeial requirements.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the PW systems at Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar) (system PW-01, 6,000 L/h, RO + EDI), Unit II – Baddi (system PW-02, 4,000 L/h, double-pass RO + EDI) and Unit III – Vizag SEZ (system PW-03, 8,000 L/h, RO + EDI with ozonated storage). Potable water and Water for Injection are out of scope.

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Engineering | Operates the system, records online conductivity and TOC, performs sanitisation |
| QC Chemist | Collects samples and performs chemical tests |
| QC Microbiologist | Performs microbial and endotoxin-free testing where required |
| QC Manager | Reviews trends monthly; initiates excursion investigations |
| Site QA | Approves excursion investigations and release of the loop for use |

## 4. Definitions

- **Alert level**: a level that signals a drift from normal conditions; requires increased attention but not necessarily corrective action.
- **Action level**: a level that, when exceeded, requires investigation and corrective action.
- **Point of use (POU)**: an outlet from which water is drawn for manufacturing or cleaning.
- **Loop return**: the sampling point at the end of the distribution loop before the storage tank.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Specification and levels

| Parameter | Specification | Alert level | Action level | Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conductivity (online, loop return) | ≤ 1.3 µS/cm at 25°C | 1.0 µS/cm | 1.3 µS/cm | IP/USP <645> stage 1 |
| Total Organic Carbon | ≤ 500 ppb | 300 ppb | 500 ppb | Online TOC analyser |
| Total aerobic microbial count | ≤ 100 CFU/mL | 50 CFU/mL | 100 CFU/mL | Membrane filtration, R2A agar, 30–35°C, 5 days |
| Pathogens (E. coli, Pseudomonas aeruginosa) | Absent in 100 mL | — | Any detection | IP method |
| pH | 5.0–7.0 | — | — | IP method |
| Nitrates | ≤ 0.2 ppm | — | — | IP method |

### 5.2 Sampling frequency

| Point | Chemical | Microbial |
|---|---|---|
| Storage tank outlet | Daily | Daily |
| Loop return | Daily (plus continuous online) | Daily |
| Points of use (each POU) | Weekly | Rotational – every POU at least once per week |
| RO/EDI outlet | Weekly | Weekly |

Sampling sequence: flush the POU for 1 minute, sanitise the outlet with 70% IPA, flush again for 30 seconds, then collect 500 mL in a sterile bottle. Microbial samples must be tested within 2 hours of collection, or stored at 2–8°C and tested within 24 hours.

### 5.3 Sanitisation

- Hot-water sanitisation of the loop at ≥ 80°C for 60 minutes every week (Units I and II).
- Unit III: continuous ozonation of the storage tank at 0.02–0.04 ppm, with UV ozone destruction before the loop; loop ozone sanitisation weekly.
- Chemical sanitisation of RO membranes once per quarter.

### 5.4 Response to excursions

| Event | Action |
|---|---|
| Alert level exceeded once | Inform QC Manager; re-sample next day; review trend |
| Alert level exceeded at the same point 3 consecutive times | Treat as action level excursion |
| Action level exceeded | Stop use of the affected POU or loop; raise deviation (SPL/QA/SOP-004); sanitise; sample for 3 consecutive days; QA releases the loop after 3 passing results |
| Conductivity > 1.3 µS/cm online | System auto-diverts to drain; Engineering investigates RO/EDI performance |
| Pathogen detected | Critical deviation; Head QA (Corporate) informed within 24 hours; impact assessment on all batches made since the last passing result |

### 5.5 Trending

The QC Manager prepares a monthly trend chart for conductivity, TOC and microbial counts per sampling point. An annual PW system review is presented in the APQR. In 2025, Unit I PW-01 had 4 alert excursions and no action level excursions.

## 6. Records

- PW sampling and test log (Form SPL/QC/F-033-01) – 5 years
- Online conductivity and TOC printouts – 5 years
- Sanitisation records – 5 years
- Monthly trend reports – 10 years
