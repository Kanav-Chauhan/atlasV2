# Biosimilar Insulin In-licensing Brief – BIO-INS-7

## 1. Purpose

To brief the Leadership team on the proposed in-licensing of **BIO-INS-7**, a biosimilar insulin glargine (100 IU/mL) candidate, and on the status of discussions since the Board authorised in-licensing talks on 22 April 2026 (Resolution 8.1). This brief is highly confidential and constitutes unpublished price-sensitive information.

## 2. Strategic rationale

2.1 Diabetes is Sanjeevani's largest therapy area (Glucosan-M 500 domestic sales ₹398 crore in FY26), and SJ-2041 is in Phase III. A basal insulin would let us serve patients across the whole treatment pathway, from first-line metformin to insulin.
2.2 Sanjeevani has no in-house biologics capability. Building one would take 6–8 years; in-licensing a late-stage asset can bring a product to market by FY30.
2.3 Our field force already calls on about 24,000 diabetes prescribers, giving a ready commercial platform.

## 6. Regulatory pathway

6.1 Approval in India will follow CDSCO's Guidelines on Similar Biologics, with review by the Review Committee on Genetic Manipulation (RCGM) for preclinical data and CDSCO's Subject Expert Committee for clinical data.
6.2 A Phase III comparative safety and immunogenicity study in about 500 patients with type 2 diabetes over 26 weeks is expected to be required. Sanjeevani's Clinical Development team (Dr. Ananya Krishnan) would run this study, building on the SJ-2041 site network.
6.3 Expected timeline: CDSCO clinical trial permission Q1 FY28; study completion Q4 FY29; marketing authorisation Q2 FY30.

## 9. Key risks

1. Phase III immunogenicity outcome.
2. Price erosion from government tenders and possible NPPA price control on insulin.
3. Dependence on a single supplier for the first 5 years.
4. Cold-chain (2–8°C) distribution; Sanjeevani currently has limited cold-chain experience.

## 10. Next steps

- Finalise binding licence agreement terms by 30 September 2026.
- Place the definitive agreement before the Board at its October 2026 meeting for approval.
- Build a cold-chain distribution plan with two specialist logistics partners.
