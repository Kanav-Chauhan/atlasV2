# Specification – Glucosan-M 500 SR Tablets

## 1. Product Details

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Brand name | Glucosan-M 500 |
| Generic name | Metformin Hydrochloride Sustained Release Tablets IP 500 mg |
| Label claim | Each film-coated sustained release tablet contains Metformin Hydrochloride IP 500 mg |
| Manufacturing site | Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar) |
| Batch size | 3,00,000 tablets |
| Pack | Alu-Alu blister of 10 tablets; carton of 10 × 10 |
| Storage | Store below 30°C. Protect from moisture. |
| Shelf life | 36 months |
| Product code | SPL-FG-1012 |

## 2. Scope

This specification applies to release and shelf-life testing of finished Glucosan-M 500 SR tablets manufactured at Unit I. Release limits apply at batch release; shelf-life limits apply to stability samples and market complaint samples. Raw material specifications for Metformin Hydrochloride IP are given in SPL/QC/SPEC-001.

## 4. Dissolution Specification

Apparatus: USP Type II (paddle), 100 rpm. Medium: 1,000 mL phosphate buffer pH 6.8 at 37 ± 0.5°C. Analysis by UV at 233 nm.

| Time point | Amount released |
|---|---|
| 1 hour | 20–40% |
| 3 hours | 45–65% |
| 10 hours | Not less than 80% (Q) |

Acceptance follows the IP/USP acceptance table for extended-release dosage forms (Levels L1–L3). The 10-hour time point is the critical release criterion: not less than 80% (Q) released in 10 hours.

## 5. Sampling

- Release: 60 tablets drawn by IPQA from beginning, middle and end of compression and packaging (20 each), as per SPL/QC/SOP-002.
- Retained sample: twice the quantity required for full testing, stored for 1 year after expiry.
- Stability: first 3 commercial batches each year, as per the stability protocol.

## 6. Testing Frequency for Skip Testing
