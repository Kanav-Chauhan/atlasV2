# Pantosan 40 Product Monograph

*Pantoprazole Gastro-resistant Tablets IP 40 mg*
*For the use of a Registered Medical Practitioner, Hospital or Laboratory only*

## 1. Composition

Each enteric-coated (gastro-resistant) tablet contains:

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Pantoprazole Sodium Sesquihydrate IP equivalent to Pantoprazole | 40 mg |
| Excipients | q.s. |
| Colour | Iron Oxide Yellow |

The tablet core contains sodium carbonate as an alkaliser to protect the acid-labile drug; the enteric coat is methacrylic acid copolymer type C.

## 2. Description

Yellow, oval, biconvex enteric-coated tablets, plain on both sides. The tablet must be swallowed whole; crushing destroys the gastro-resistant coat.

## 3. Pharmacology

### 3.1 Mechanism of action
Pantoprazole is a substituted benzimidazole proton pump inhibitor. It is activated in the acidic environment of the parietal cell canaliculi and irreversibly inhibits the H⁺/K⁺-ATPase enzyme, blocking the final step of gastric acid secretion, both basal and stimulated.

### 3.2 Pharmacokinetics
| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Absolute bioavailability | About 77% |
| Time to peak (Tmax) | 2–2.5 hours |
| Protein binding | About 98% |
| Metabolism | Hepatic, mainly CYP2C19, also CYP3A4 |
| Half-life | About 1 hour (acid suppression lasts much longer due to irreversible binding) |
| Elimination | About 80% renal as metabolites |

## 4. Indications

- Gastro-oesophageal reflux disease (GERD) and reflux oesophagitis
- Long-term management and prevention of relapse of reflux oesophagitis
- Duodenal ulcer and gastric ulcer
- Eradication of *Helicobacter pylori* in combination with appropriate antibiotics
- Zollinger–Ellison syndrome and other pathological hypersecretory conditions
- Prevention of NSAID-associated gastroduodenal ulcers in at-risk patients

## 5. Dosage and administration

5.1 Take one hour before a meal, preferably before breakfast. Swallow whole with water; do not chew or crush.

| Indication | Adult dose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Reflux oesophagitis (treatment) | 40 mg once daily | 4 weeks; further 4 weeks if not healed |
| GERD maintenance | 20–40 mg once daily | As required |
| Duodenal ulcer | 40 mg once daily | 2 weeks; further 2 weeks if not healed |
| Gastric ulcer | 40 mg once daily | 4 weeks; further 4 weeks if not healed |
| *H. pylori* eradication | 40 mg twice daily with two antibiotics | 7–14 days |
| Zollinger–Ellison syndrome | 80 mg daily, adjusted to response | Long-term under specialist care |

5.2 **Hepatic impairment:** in severe hepatic impairment do not exceed 20 mg per day (Pantosan 40 should not be used; a 20 mg strength is required).
5.3 **Elderly and renal impairment:** no dose adjustment needed; in *H. pylori* combination therapy, do not exceed 40 mg per day in renal impairment.
5.4 Not recommended for children below 12 years.

## 6. Contraindications

- Hypersensitivity to pantoprazole, substituted benzimidazoles or excipients
- Co-administration with rilpivirine-containing products

## 7. Warnings and precautions

7.1 **Gastric malignancy:** exclude malignancy before treatment in patients with alarm symptoms (unintentional weight loss, recurrent vomiting, dysphagia, haematemesis, anaemia, melaena), since PPIs can mask symptoms.
7.2 **Long-term use (above 1 year):** review regularly; risks include hypomagnesaemia, vitamin B12 deficiency, fractures and *Clostridioides difficile*-associated diarrhoea.
7.3 **Interactions:** may reduce absorption of drugs that need gastric acid (ketoconazole, itraconazole, atazanavir). Monitor INR with warfarin. Pantoprazole has a lower interaction potential with clopidogrel than some other PPIs.
7.4 Stop at least 5 days before chromogranin A measurement.

## 8. Adverse reactions

Common: headache, diarrhoea, nausea, abdominal pain, flatulence, fundic gland polyps (benign). Uncommon: dizziness, rash, pruritus, raised liver enzymes. Rare: hypomagnesaemia, subacute cutaneous lupus erythematosus.

## 9. Pregnancy and lactation

Limited data; use in pregnancy only if clearly needed. Pantoprazole is excreted in breast milk in small quantities.

## 10. Overdose

Doses up to 240 mg intravenously over 2 minutes have been well tolerated. Not removed by haemodialysis. Treat symptomatically.
