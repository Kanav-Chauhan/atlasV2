# Clinical Research Services Agreement – SJ-2041 (CRO)

## 1. Purpose of this summary

This document summarises the Clinical Research Services Agreement (CRSA) between Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. (the Sponsor) and Trivenix Clinical Research Pvt. Ltd. (fictional), Bengaluru (the CRO), for the SJ-2041 Phase III trial. It is the reference for the clinical team on what the CRO must deliver, how it is paid and who owns what. The signed agreement (agreement number CRSA/SPL/2025/007, 64 pages) is held by Legal.

## 3. Scope of services

| Service | CRO responsibility | Retained by Sanjeevani |
|---|---|---|
| Site feasibility and start-up | Feasibility, EC submissions, site contracts on Sanjeevani's template | Final site selection; signing of Clinical Trial Agreements |
| Monitoring | On-site monitoring visit every 6 weeks per site; 100% source data verification for primary endpoint and SAEs | Co-monitoring visits; oversight |
| Data management | EDC build, data cleaning, query management, database lock | Approval of database lock |
| Medical monitoring | 24×7 medical monitor for investigator queries | Medical review of all SAEs |
| Safety reporting | Forward every SAE to Sanjeevani within 24 hours of the CRO becoming aware | Expedited reporting to CDSCO and the Ethics Committees under the Serious Adverse Event Reporting SOP |
| Biostatistics | Statistical analysis of the final data; topline tables | Statistical analysis plan approval |
| Interim analysis | Not in scope (done by the independent statistician of the DSMB) | — |
| Central laboratory | Vendor management of the NABL-accredited central lab in Mumbai | Contract with the central lab |
| Site payments | Pay sites from a pass-through account according to the Investigator Payments Policy | FMV grid and budget approval |
| Clinical Study Report | Draft CSR within 90 days of database lock | Final CSR approval |

## 4. Fees and payment milestones

The CRO service fee is **₹18.6 crore**, fixed price, excluding GST. It does **not** include investigator and site payments (budget ₹6.4 crore, paid as pass-through under the Investigator Payments Policy), Ethics Committee fees or central laboratory charges, which are reimbursed at actual cost.

| No. | Milestone | Share of fee | Amount (₹ crore) | Status (November 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Agreement signing | 10% | 1.86 | Paid (March 2025) |
| M2 | First patient randomised | 10% | 1.86 | Paid (April 2025) |
| M3 | 50% of patients randomised (620) | 15% | 2.79 | Paid (October 2025) |
| M4 | Last patient randomised | 15% | 2.79 | Paid (March 2026) |
| M5 | Last patient last visit | 15% | 2.79 | Paid (September 2026) |
| M6 | **Database lock** | **20%** | **3.72** | Due on database lock (planned 30 November 2026) |
| M7 | Acceptance of final CSR | 15% | 2.79 | Expected June 2027 |
| | **Total** | **100%** | **18.60** | |

4.1 Each milestone is invoiced only after Sanjeevani's Head, Clinical Development confirms it in writing. Invoices are paid within 45 days.
4.2 **Database lock (M6):** payment requires zero open queries, a clean SAE reconciliation between the clinical and safety databases, and the signed database lock form. The M6 payment is the largest milestone at 20% of the fee.
4.3 **Late database lock:** if database lock is more than 30 days later than the agreed date for reasons within the CRO's control, the M6 amount is reduced by 2% for every further week of delay, up to a maximum reduction of 10%.

## 5. Site payments through the CRO

5.1 The CRO pays sites only for visits recorded in the EDC and verified by monitoring, using the FMV grid of the Investigator Payments Policy (for example ₹42,000 per completed patient) and holding back 10% until close-out.
5.2 Sanjeevani funds the pass-through account monthly, against the CRO's visit report of the 5th of each month. The CRO may not add any mark-up to site payments.
5.3 The CRO must not make any payment to an investigator outside the signed Clinical Trial Agreement.

## 6. Ownership of data and results

6.1 **All trial data belong exclusively to Sanjeevani Pharma**: the clinical database, case report forms, source data copies, statistical outputs, reports and any inventions arising from the study. The CRO acquires no rights to them.
6.2 The CRO may not publish, present or use the data for any other purpose. Publication decisions are taken only by Sanjeevani.
6.3 At the end of the agreement the CRO transfers the full database and trial master file to Sanjeevani and keeps copies only as required by law. Archiving is for 15 years.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 All information about SJ-2041 (including the formulation, the interim results and the trial data) is confidential for the term of the agreement and **10 years** after it ends.
7.2 CRO staff working on the trial sign individual confidentiality undertakings. The CRO must report any data breach to Sanjeevani within 24 hours.
7.3 The CRO is not given unblinded interim data; the DSMB's closed-session minutes remain with the independent statistician until database lock.

## 8. Quality and audit

8.1 The CRO works under ICH E6(R3) GCP and the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019.
8.2 Sanjeevani may audit the CRO with 10 working days' notice, and without notice for cause. The CRO was audited in June 2025 and May 2026; no critical findings.
8.3 Monthly KPIs: query resolution within 10 days (target 95%), monitoring visits on schedule (target 95%), SAE forwarding within 24 hours (target 100%).

## 9. Liability, insurance and termination

| Item | Term |
|---|---|
| CRO professional indemnity insurance | ₹25 crore |
| CRO liability cap | Total fees paid under the agreement, except for breach of confidentiality or data protection |
| Trial-subject compensation insurance | Arranged by Sanjeevani (see Serious Adverse Event Reporting SOP) |
| Termination for convenience | Sanjeevani, 90 days' written notice; fees paid for work done |
| Termination for cause | Immediate, for serious GCP breach, fraud or data breach |
| Governing law and disputes | Laws of India; arbitration seated in Ahmedabad |
