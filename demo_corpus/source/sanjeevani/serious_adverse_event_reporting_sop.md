# Serious Adverse Event Reporting SOP

## 1. Purpose

To describe how serious adverse events (SAEs) occurring in Sanjeevani-sponsored clinical trials are recorded, assessed and reported to the Licensing Authority (CDSCO), Ethics Committees and investigators within the timelines of the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, and how compensation is assessed.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all interventional clinical trials and BA/BE studies sponsored by Sanjeevani Pharma, including the SJ-2041 Phase III trial (SPL/CLN/PRT-017), whether conducted by Sanjeevani staff or by a CRO. Post-marketing adverse drug reactions are handled under the Pharmacovigilance System Master File.

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Investigator | Reports every SAE to sponsor, Ethics Committee and CDSCO within 24 hours; provides analysis report |
| Medical Monitor (Sanjeevani) | Medical review, causality and expectedness assessment within 2 working days |
| Drug Safety Associate | Case entry in the clinical safety database; regulatory submissions |
| Head, Clinical Development | Approves sponsor's SAE analysis reports and compensation recommendations |
| QPPV | Signal review across trials; oversight of SUSAR reporting |

## 4. Definitions

- **Adverse Event (AE):** any untoward medical occurrence in a trial participant, whether or not related to the investigational product.
- **Serious Adverse Event (SAE):** an AE that results in death, is life-threatening, requires hospitalisation or prolongs hospitalisation, causes persistent or significant disability, is a congenital anomaly, or is otherwise medically important.
- **SUSAR:** Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reaction – an SAE with a reasonable possibility of relation to the investigational product and not listed in the Investigator's Brochure.
- **Causality categories:** related (certain, probable, possible) or unrelated (unlikely, unrelated).

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Reporting timelines

| Step | Who | Timeline (from awareness) |
|---|---|---|
| Initial SAE report to sponsor, Ethics Committee and CDSCO | Investigator | **Within 24 hours** |
| Sponsor medical review and causality | Medical Monitor | Within 2 working days |
| Investigator's analysis report (with causality) to CDSCO, sponsor and EC | Investigator | **Within 14 days** |
| Sponsor's analysis report to CDSCO | Sponsor | **Within 14 days** |
| SUSAR (fatal or life-threatening) to CDSCO | Sponsor | Within 7 days, follow-up within 8 further days |
| Ethics Committee opinion on SAE and compensation | EC | Within 30 days of receiving the report |
| Death: Licensing Authority decision on compensation | CDSCO | Within 150 days of receipt of SAE report (as per Rules) |

If the investigator fails to report within 24 hours, the reason for the delay must be given to CDSCO along with the report.

### 5.2 Initial report content

The SAE form (SPL/CLN/F-005-01) must contain at least: protocol number, site number, participant ID, event term, onset date, seriousness criterion, study drug details (blinded), action taken, outcome and reporter. Missing information must not delay the 24-hour report; follow-up information is sent as it becomes available.

### 5.3 Causality and expectedness

1. The Medical Monitor assesses causality using WHO-UMC criteria and expectedness against the current Investigator's Brochure (SJ-2041 IB version 5.0).
2. If the investigator and sponsor disagree on causality, both opinions are reported; the more conservative assessment is used for expedited reporting.

### 5.4 Unblinding

Unblinding for an individual participant is allowed only when knowledge of treatment is needed for clinical management or for SUSAR reporting. The investigator unblinds via the IWRS emergency function; the event is documented and the sponsor study team stays blinded where possible. Unblinded SUSAR reports go to the Drug Safety Associate only.

### 5.5 Compensation

Per the NDCT Rules, 2019, participants are entitled to free medical management for any injury as long as required, and financial compensation for trial-related injury or death. Sanjeevani pays compensation within 30 days of the order of the Licensing Authority. Clinical trial insurance for SJ-2041 covers up to ₹10 crore aggregate with ₹50 lakh per participant.

### 5.6 Reconciliation

The clinical safety database and the clinical database are reconciled every month and before database lock. All SAEs must be reconciled with zero discrepancies before database lock.

### 5.7 SJ-2041 status (for information)

As of the interim data cut-off (15 February 2026), 2 SAEs had been reported in the SJ-2041 Phase III trial, both judged unrelated to study drug and both reported within 24 hours.

## 6. Records

- SAE forms, analysis reports, CDSCO acknowledgements – retained 15 years after trial completion.
- Unblinding log – with the Trial Master File.
- Monthly reconciliation reports – with the Trial Master File.
