# Careers at Sanjeevani – Hiring and Campus Programme FAQ

Answers to the questions we are asked most often by job seekers, students and campus placement officers. Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. is an Indian generics and branded-formulations company founded in 1994 in Ahmedabad, with about 2,850 employees. Our tagline is "Healing, made dependable."

## 1. Where do you hire?

| Location | What happens there | Typical roles |
|---|---|---|
| Head office – Sanjeevani House, Sarkhej–Gandhinagar Highway, Ahmedabad | Corporate functions | Finance, HR, IT, supply chain, marketing |
| Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar) | Oral solids (tablets, capsules) | Production, QC, QA, engineering, warehouse |
| Unit II – Baddi, Himachal Pradesh | Oral liquids and creams | Production, QC microbiology, engineering |
| Unit III – Vizag (Visakhapatnam) SEZ | Export unit for regulated markets | Production, QC, QA, validation, engineering |
| R&D centre – Genome Valley, Hyderabad | Formulation, analytical, clinical and pharmacovigilance | Research associates, scientists, clinical research, drug safety |
| Field sales – across India | Promotion to doctors | Medical representatives (about 180 across four zones) |

## 2. Do you hire freshers?

Yes. Freshers are hired at all three plants, at the R&D centre and in the field force.

- **Plants (Units I, II and III):** B.Pharm, M.Pharm and M.Sc (Chemistry or Microbiology) graduates join as Production or QC Trainees. ITI and diploma holders join as Trainee Operators or Trainee Technicians.
- **Unit III – Vizag:** yes, Vizag hires freshers every year through campus drives at Andhra University and pharmacy colleges in Andhra Pradesh, mainly for QC, production and validation roles.
- **R&D, Hyderabad:** M.Pharm and M.Sc freshers join as Research Associates.
- **Field sales:** science graduates (B.Sc, B.Pharm) aged up to 27 years join as Medical Representative Trainees.

## 3. How do I apply?

1. Visit the Careers page of our website, choose an opening and apply online with your CV.
2. Or email your CV to **careers@sanjeevani.example**, with the job code in the subject line.
3. Walk-in interviews for plant roles are announced in local newspapers and on our website, with the date, venue and documents to bring.

We acknowledge every online application by email within **3 working days**.

## 4. What is the selection process?

| Stage | Plant and R&D roles | Field sales roles |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Online application and CV screening | Online application and CV screening |
| 2 | Written test (technical, 45 minutes) | Telephone interview with the Area Sales Manager |
| 3 | Technical interview with the department head | Field interview with the Regional Sales Manager |
| 4 | HR interview | HR interview |
| 5 | Pre-employment medical check (fitness for cleanroom work) | Reference check |

We aim to communicate the final decision within **10 working days** of the last interview. All offers are subject to background verification of education and previous employment.

## 5. Do you charge any fee?

**No. Sanjeevani never charges any fee at any stage of hiring** – not for applications, tests, interviews, training, uniforms, medical checks or "security deposits". We do not use agents who collect money from candidates.

If anyone asks you for money in Sanjeevani's name, or sends an offer letter from a free personal email address instead of an @sanjeevani.example address, it is fraud. Do not pay. Please report it to **careers@sanjeevani.example** or to the HR helpdesk at hr.helpdesk@sanjeevani.example.

## 6. Campus trainee programme

The **Sanjeevani Graduate Trainee Programme (SGTP)** is our main entry route for fresh graduates.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Eligible degrees | B.Pharm, M.Pharm, M.Sc (Chemistry, Microbiology, Biotechnology), B.Tech (Chemical, Mechanical) |
| Minimum marks | 60% aggregate, no active backlogs |
| Intake (FY2026-27) | 120 trainees: Unit I 40, Unit II 25, Unit III 35, R&D 20 |
| Campus season | August to December |
| Joining | June–July of the following year, after final results |
| Duration | 12 months |
| Structure | 2-week induction at Ahmedabad, then rotations in production, QC and QA (plant trainees) or formulation and analytical labs (R&D trainees) |
| Confirmation | On completing 12 months with a "Meets expectations" rating and passing all GMP qualifications |

During the first week every trainee completes Basic GMP and data integrity (ALCOA+) training, plant safety and PPE training, and the Code of Business Conduct. Trainees work in cleanroom areas only after gowning qualification.

## 7. Internships

- **Summer internships** of 8 weeks at the Hyderabad R&D centre for M.Pharm and M.Sc students between their first and second year.
- Applications open on **15 January** and close on **28 February** each year; about 30 interns are selected.
- Plant internships (4 weeks) are offered to B.Pharm students through college tie-ups only.

## 8. Other common questions

**Is there an age limit?** Candidates must be at least 18 years old. Medical Representative Trainees must be 27 or younger on the date of joining; there is no upper age limit for experienced roles.

**Do you hire women for plant and night-shift roles?** Yes. Women may work night shifts with their written consent; we provide door-to-door transport with a security guard.

**Can I apply for more than one opening?** Yes, but you may apply for a maximum of 3 openings in any 6-month period.

**I was not selected. When can I re-apply?** After 6 months for the same role.

**Are contract or temporary roles available?** Yes. Fixed-term contracts of 6 to 12 months are offered for some QC, warehouse and project roles, especially at Unit III during the expansion of the site.
