# Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup Product Sheet

*Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide and Chlorpheniramine Maleate Syrup*
*Schedule H prescription drug – to be sold by retail on the prescription of a Registered Medical Practitioner only*

## 1. Composition

Each 5 mL contains:

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide IP | 10 mg |
| Chlorpheniramine Maleate IP | 2 mg |
| Flavoured syrup base | q.s. |
| Colour | Sunset Yellow FCF |

The syrup base contains sucrose, sorbitol solution, glycerin, sodium benzoate (preservative), citric acid, menthol and raspberry flavour. It is alcohol-free.

## 2. Description

A clear, orange-coloured, raspberry-flavoured syrup with a cooling menthol note, filled in amber PET bottles.

## 3. Therapeutic class and action

- **Dextromethorphan** is a centrally acting non-opioid antitussive that raises the threshold of the cough centre in the medulla. It has no significant analgesic or respiratory-depressant effect at therapeutic doses.
- **Chlorpheniramine** is a first-generation H1 antihistamine that relieves rhinorrhoea, sneezing and post-nasal drip that commonly trigger cough.

## 4. Indications

Symptomatic relief of dry (non-productive) cough associated with the common cold, allergic rhinitis and upper respiratory tract irritation. It is not intended for productive cough, asthma or chronic cough due to smoking or COPD.

## 5. Dosage and administration

| Age group | Dose | Maximum frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Adults and children above 12 years | 10 mL | Every 6–8 hours; not more than 4 doses (40 mL) in 24 hours |
| Children 6–12 years | 5 mL | Every 6–8 hours; not more than 4 doses (20 mL) in 24 hours |
| Children 4–6 years | Only on medical advice | As directed by the physician |
| Children below 4 years | **Not to be used** | — |

5.1 Use the 10 mL measuring cup supplied with the pack. Do not use household spoons.
5.2 Do not use for more than 7 days. If the cough persists beyond 7 days or is accompanied by high fever, rash or persistent headache, consult a doctor.

**Mandatory label warning (as required by CDSCO for this fixed-dose combination):** "This fixed-dose combination should not be used in children below four years of age."

## 6. Contraindications

- Children below 4 years of age
- Patients taking monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs), or within 14 days of stopping them
- Hypersensitivity to any ingredient
- Patients at risk of respiratory failure, or during an acute asthma attack
- Narrow-angle glaucoma, urinary retention or prostatic enlargement (due to chlorpheniramine)

## 7. Warnings and precautions

7.1 **Drowsiness:** may cause drowsiness. Do not drive or operate machinery after taking this medicine.
7.2 Avoid alcohol and other CNS depressants (sedatives, tranquillisers).
7.3 **Serotonin syndrome risk:** avoid with SSRIs, SNRIs and linezolid unless advised by a doctor.
7.4 **Diabetic patients:** contains sucrose (approximately 3.0 g per 5 mL); take this into account.
7.5 Misuse: dextromethorphan can be abused at high doses. Pharmacists should be alert to repeated requests.
7.6 Elderly patients are more susceptible to the sedative and anticholinergic effects.

## 8. Adverse reactions

Common: drowsiness, dizziness, dry mouth, nausea. Uncommon: blurred vision, constipation, urinary retention, paradoxical excitation in children. Rare: skin rash, confusion.

## 9. Pregnancy and lactation

Not recommended during pregnancy and breastfeeding unless advised by the treating doctor.

## 10. Overdose

Symptoms may include excitation, confusion, nystagmus, ataxia, respiratory depression and, in children, convulsions. Seek emergency care. Naloxone has been used for dextromethorphan toxicity in children; treatment is otherwise supportive.

## 11. Storage and shelf life

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Storage | Store below 30°C. Protect from light. Do not refrigerate or freeze. |
| Shelf life | 24 months from the date of manufacture |
| After opening | Use within 30 days of first opening |
| Handling | Keep the cap tightly closed. Keep out of reach of children. |
