# Field Force Guidelines FY27

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines set the working standards, productivity norms, reporting rules and allowances for Sanjeevani's field force for FY27 (01 April 2026 – 31 March 2027). They apply together with the UCPMP Compliance Guide for Field Staff, the Sample Distribution Policy and the Doctor Engagement and CME Guidelines.

## 2. Field force structure

| Level | Designation | Approx. headcount | Reports to |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Medical Representative (MR) | ~180 | Area Sales Manager |
| L2 | Area Sales Manager (ASM) | 26 | Regional Sales Manager |
| L3 | Regional Sales Manager (RSM) | 8 | Zonal Sales Manager |
| L4 | Zonal Sales Manager (ZSM) – North, West, South, East | 4 | National Sales Manager |
| L5 | National Sales Manager | 1 (Nikhil Shah) | MD & CEO |

Each MR covers one headquarters (HQ) territory and its ex-HQ and out-station towns.

## 3. Productivity norms

### 3.1 Doctor coverage
| Norm | Target |
|---|---|
| Doctor calls per MR per month | 180 doctor visits |
| Working days in field per month | Minimum 22 |
| Average doctor calls per field day | 8–9 |
| Chemist calls per field day | 4 |
| Doctors on the MR's master list | 200 (A: 60, B: 80, C: 60) |

### 3.2 Visit frequency by doctor category
| Category | Definition | Visits per month |
|---|---|---|
| A (Core) | High-potential diabetologists, cardiologists, consulting physicians | 3 |
| B | Physicians and GPs with regular prescribing in focus therapies | 2 |
| C | Other GPs, dermatologists, ENT specialists | 1 |

3.3 **Call average** below 7 doctor visits per field day for two consecutive months triggers a performance improvement discussion with the ASM.
3.4 **Coverage:** at least 90% of listed doctors must be met at least once in the month; A-category coverage must be 100%.

## 4. Daily working

4.1 Field working hours: 09:30 to 13:30 (doctor and chemist calls) and 17:00 to 21:00 (evening clinics). The mid-day slot is used for stockist visits and reporting.
4.2 Daily Call Report (DCR) must be submitted on the SanjeevaniOne CRM app by **23:59 the same day**. DCRs older than 3 days cannot be submitted, and the day is marked as "not worked".
4.3 Doctor calls are geo-tagged; a call recorded more than 500 m from the doctor's registered clinic location is flagged for review.
4.4 Tour programme (TP) for the next month is submitted by the 25th and approved by the ASM by the 28th.

## 6. Allowances

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Daily allowance – HQ | ₹350 per field day |
| Daily allowance – ex-HQ | ₹500 per field day |
| Daily allowance – out-station (night stay) | ₹1,200 per day plus hotel up to ₹2,500 |
| Two-wheeler fuel reimbursement | ₹3.50 per km (ex-HQ and out-station) |
| Mobile and data | ₹800 per month |
| Monthly expense statement deadline | 5th of the following month |

6.1 MR allowances are covered here; inter-city travel for meetings follows the corporate Travel Policy.

## 7. Incentive scheme FY27 (summary)

| Achievement vs quarterly target | Incentive (% of quarterly fixed pay) |
|---|---|
| Below 90% | Nil |
| 90–99.9% | 8% |
| 100–109.9% | 15% |
| 110% and above | 22% (cap) |

7.1 Incentive is payable only if call average and coverage norms (Section 3) are met and there are no substantiated UCPMP violations in the quarter.
7.2 Glucosan-M 500 must reach at least 95% of its own brand target for the incentive slab above 100% to apply.

## 8. Compliance essentials

8.1 No gifts, cash, meals, travel or personal benefits to doctors (UCPMP 2024).
8.2 Samples only as per the Sample Distribution Policy (for Glucosan-M, a maximum of 10 strips per doctor per month).
8.3 Any adverse event or product complaint heard during a call must be reported to Pharmacovigilance within 24 hours via the CRM "Report AE" button.
8.4 Competitor disparagement is prohibited.

## 9. Review meetings
