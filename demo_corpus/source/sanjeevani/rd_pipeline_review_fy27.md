# R&D Pipeline Review FY27

## 1. Purpose

This review summarises the status of all active R&D projects at the start of FY27 (April 2026 – March 2027), the FY27 R&D budget, key milestones and the go/no-go decisions taken at the Pipeline Review Committee meeting held at the Hyderabad R&D centre (Genome Valley) on 12 May 2026.

## 2. FY2025-26 in Review

- R&D spend: **₹167 crore**, i.e. **6.8% of revenue** (FY2025-26 revenue ₹2,460 crore).
- R&D headcount: 312 scientists and staff (Hyderabad 268; site technology transfer cells 44).
- Filings: 3 ANDAs filed with the US FDA from Unit III and 3 ANDA approvals received; 6 new domestic product approvals.
- Patents: 3 Indian patent applications filed, including the SJ-2041 bilayer composition.
- Key events: SJ-2041 Phase III enrolment completed and interim analysis completed (see Clinical Trials collection for details, restricted to the clinical team).

## 5. SJ-2041 Programme Highlights

- Phase III trial (protocol SPL/CLN/PRT-017) is a 24-week study in 1,240 patients at 38 sites in India; primary endpoint is HbA1c change from baseline.
- Enrolment of 1,240 patients was completed in March 2026; the last patients finish 24-week treatment in August 2026 and database lock is planned for November 2026.
- Commercial scale-up: dedicated bilayer press for Unit I to be requested via change control in Q3 FY27, contingent on topline results.
- Peak domestic sales potential estimated at ₹350 crore by FY31 (Strategy team estimate; not a forecast).

## 6. Biosimilar Insulin (BIO-INS-7)

The Board meeting of 22 April 2026 approved in-licensing talks for a biosimilar insulin (code BIO-INS-7). R&D's role:

1. Technical due diligence of the partner's comparability data package (analytical, non-clinical, PK/PD clamp study).
2. Assessment of fill-finish requirements; no current Sanjeevani unit has sterile capability, so a contract manufacturer or a new facility is required.
3. Estimated R&D cost to Indian approval (bridging clinical study): ₹40–55 crore over 3 years.

## 8. Decisions and Actions

1. Stop SJ-0991; redeploy 6 scientists to SJ-1203.
2. Approve ₹8 crore for a second LC-MS/MS system for bioanalytical work at Hyderabad.
3. Next pipeline review: November 2026 (mid-year).
