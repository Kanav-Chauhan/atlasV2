# FY2025-26 Annual Results Summary

## 1. Highlights

- Revenue from operations of **₹2,460 crore**, up 11.1% from ₹2,215 crore in FY2024-25.
- Domestic business 58% of revenue (₹1,426.8 crore); exports 42% (₹1,033.2 crore).
- EBITDA of ₹548.6 crore, margin 22.3% (FY25: 21.0%).
- Profit after tax of ₹333.6 crore, up 18.5%.
- R&D spend of ₹167.3 crore, 6.8% of revenue.
- Board recommended a final dividend of ₹4.50 per share (FY25: ₹4.00).
- Unit III Vizag: US FDA re-inspection in February 2026 closed with no observations.

## 7. Research and development

- R&D spend: ₹167.3 crore (6.8% of revenue), up from ₹146.2 crore (6.6%).
- 3 ANDAs filed with the US FDA; 3 ANDA approvals received.
- SJ-2041 Phase III (1,240 patients, 38 sites) completed enrolment; topline readout expected January 2027.

## 8. People

Headcount at 31 March 2026: 2,850 (FY25: 2,710). Attrition 13.8% (field force 21%, plants 9%). Lost-time injury frequency rate: 0.24 per million hours.

## 10. FY27 outlook (internal guidance)

- Revenue growth of 10–12%, led by US generics and the diabetes franchise.
- Domestic sales target ₹1,520 crore.
- EBITDA margin 22–23%.
- Capex of ₹420 crore including the Vizag expansion.
- Key events: SJ-2041 topline (January 2027); BIO-INS-7 in-licensing due diligence.
