# Sample Distribution Policy

## 1. Purpose

To control how physician samples of Sanjeevani products are planned, issued, stored, distributed, recorded and reconciled, so that samples are used only to help doctors gain clinical experience with our products, in full compliance with UCPMP 2024 and the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all medical representatives (MRs), Area Sales Managers, Regional Sales Managers, the Sample Cell at the Ahmedabad warehouse and C&F agents handling sample consignments.

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Manager | Annual sample plan per brand, within budget |
| Sample Cell (Ahmedabad) | Monthly dispatch to MRs, stock records |
| MR | Safe storage, distribution to eligible doctors only, recording in CRM |
| ASM | Monthly verification of MR sample stock and records |
| Compliance Officer | Quarterly audit of sample records |

## 4. Definitions

- **Physician sample**: a pack marked "Physician's Sample – Not to be sold", supplied free of charge to a registered medical practitioner.
- **Strip**: one blister strip as defined in the product's sample pack.
- **Eligible doctor**: a registered medical practitioner on the MR's approved doctor list, qualified to prescribe the product.

## 5. Sample allowance by brand

| Brand | Sample pack | Max per doctor per month | Max per doctor per year | Eligible specialties |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glucosan-M 500 | Strip of 10 tablets | 10 strips | 60 strips | Diabetologists, physicians, GPs |
| Cardivas 10 | Strip of 5 tablets | 6 strips | 36 strips | Cardiologists, physicians |
| Amlosan 5 | Strip of 10 tablets | 6 strips | 36 strips | Physicians, GPs, cardiologists |
| Pantosan 40 | Strip of 5 tablets | 6 strips | 36 strips | Gastroenterologists, physicians, GPs |
| Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup | 30 mL sample bottle | 4 bottles | 12 bottles | GPs, ENT, paediatricians |
| Dermacalm Cream | 5 g tube | 4 tubes | 20 tubes | Dermatologists only |

5.1 The maximum for Glucosan-M is **10 strips per doctor per month**. Exceeding the monthly limit is not allowed even if the doctor has not received samples in earlier months.
5.2 For new product launches, the UCPMP limit of 12 sample packs per doctor per year applies, unless stricter limits are set above.
5.3 Total sample value for FY27 is budgeted within 1.5% of domestic sales, below the 2% ceiling in UCPMP 2024.

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Planning and dispatch
6.1.1 The Product Manager sets the monthly sample quantity per MR by the 20th of the previous month.
6.1.2 The Sample Cell dispatches samples to MRs by courier by the 1st working day of the month, with a delivery challan.
6.1.3 The MR acknowledges receipt in the CRM within 2 days of delivery.

### 6.2 Storage by the MR
6.2.1 Samples must be stored at home in a clean, dry place below 30°C, away from direct sunlight; Dermacalm sample tubes below 25°C.
6.2.2 Samples must never be kept in a vehicle boot overnight.
6.2.3 Expired or damaged samples must be returned to the Sample Cell, never thrown away or given out.

### 6.3 Distribution
6.3.1 Samples are handed only to the doctor personally (or to the clinic in-charge with the doctor's written acknowledgement).
6.3.2 Each issue is recorded in the CRM sample module at the time of the call with doctor name, brand, batch number and quantity. The doctor's e-signature or OTP confirmation is mandatory.
6.3.3 Samples must never be given to chemists, stockists, hospital pharmacies or patients directly.
6.3.4 Samples must never be sold or exchanged for prescriptions.

### 6.4 Reconciliation
6.4.1 Monthly reconciliation: Opening stock + Received – Distributed – Returned = Closing stock. Variance of more than 2% must be explained.
6.4.2 ASMs physically verify each MR's sample stock at least once a quarter.
6.4.3 The Compliance Officer audits a random sample of 10% of MRs every quarter, including telephone confirmation with doctors.

## 7. Disposal of expired samples

Expired samples returned to the Sample Cell are destroyed by an authorised biomedical waste agency, with a destruction certificate filed. MRs must return samples within 30 days of expiry.

## 8. Violations

| Violation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Exceeding per-doctor limits | Written warning; sample allocation reduced 50% for the next quarter |
| Unrecorded distribution | Written warning |
| Samples found with chemists | Final warning; investigation |
| Sale of samples | Termination and legal action |

## 9. Records

CRM sample records, delivery challans and destruction certificates are retained for 5 years.
