# Dermacalm Cream Product Sheet

*Mometasone Furoate Cream IP 0.1% w/w*
*Schedule H prescription drug – for external use only*

## 1. Composition

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Mometasone Furoate IP | 0.1% w/w (1 mg per g) |
| Chlorocresol IP (preservative) | 0.1% w/w |
| Cream base | q.s. |

The cream base contains white soft paraffin, cetostearyl alcohol, hexylene glycol, titanium dioxide, phosphoric acid and purified water.

## 2. Description

A smooth, white, non-greasy cream that spreads easily and absorbs quickly, packed in a lacquered aluminium tube.

## 3. Pharmacology

Mometasone furoate is a synthetic medium-to-high potency topical corticosteroid (Class III in the US classification; "potent" in the British classification). It has anti-inflammatory, antipruritic and vasoconstrictive properties. Its once-daily application is enabled by a long duration of action in the skin. Systemic absorption through intact skin is about 0.4% of the applied dose over 8 hours, but increases with occlusion, inflamed skin and large treatment areas.

## 4. Indications

Relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses, such as:
- Atopic dermatitis (eczema)
- Psoriasis (excluding widespread plaque psoriasis)
- Seborrhoeic dermatitis
- Allergic contact dermatitis

## 5. Dosage and administration

| Population | Dose | Maximum duration |
|---|---|---|
| Adults | Apply a thin film to the affected area once daily | Review after 2 weeks; usually not more than 3 weeks continuous use |
| Children 2 years and above | Apply a thin film once daily; use the least amount that is effective | Not more than 3 weeks; avoid use on more than 20% of body surface area |
| Children below 2 years | Not recommended | — |

5.1 Use the fingertip unit (FTU) as a guide: one FTU (about 0.5 g) covers an area equal to two adult palms.
5.2 Do not use more than 45 g per week in adults.
5.3 Do not apply under occlusive dressings or nappies unless directed by a doctor.
5.4 Wash hands after application unless the hands are being treated.
5.5 When the condition improves, the frequency may be reduced or the patient switched to a lower-potency steroid.

## 6. Contraindications

- Bacterial, viral (herpes simplex, varicella), fungal or parasitic skin infections, unless treated concurrently
- Rosacea, acne vulgaris, perioral dermatitis
- Skin atrophy, ulcerated skin, wounds
- Hypersensitivity to mometasone or any component

## 7. Warnings and precautions

7.1 **Not for use on the face, groin or armpits** for more than 5 days, unless directed by a dermatologist.
7.2 **Not for cosmetic use** (e.g. skin lightening). Misuse of topical steroids as fairness creams can cause serious damage to the skin.
7.3 Avoid contact with eyes; prolonged use around the eyes may cause glaucoma or cataract.
7.4 Prolonged use on large areas, or under occlusion, can suppress the hypothalamic–pituitary–adrenal (HPA) axis, especially in children.
7.5 Stop gradually after long-term use to avoid rebound flare.
7.6 Do not use the cream if the skin condition becomes infected; consult a doctor.

## 8. Adverse reactions

Uncommon: burning, itching, tingling, skin atrophy, folliculitis, hypopigmentation. With prolonged use: striae, telangiectasia, acneiform eruptions, perioral dermatitis, secondary infection.

## 9. Pregnancy and lactation

Use during pregnancy only if the expected benefit justifies the potential risk, and in the smallest amount for the shortest period. Do not apply to the breasts before breastfeeding.

## 10. Storage and shelf life

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Storage | Store below 25°C. Do not freeze. |
| Shelf life | 30 months from the date of manufacture |
| After opening | Use within 3 months of first opening |
| Handling | Replace the cap tightly after use. Keep out of reach of children. |
