# Plant Safety and EHS Policy

## 1. Purpose

To protect the health and safety of every person working at or visiting a Sanjeevani site, to prevent environmental harm, and to comply with the Factories Act 1948, the Environment (Protection) Act 1986, the Hazardous and Other Wastes Rules 2016, and applicable State Pollution Control Board consent conditions.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar), Unit II – Baddi, Himachal Pradesh, Unit III – Vizag SEZ, the Hyderabad R&D centre, warehouses and all contractors working on these premises.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Managing Director & CEO | Overall accountability for EHS performance |

## 4. Definitions

- **PPE**: personal protective equipment such as safety shoes, goggles, gloves, respirators and ear protection.
- **Near-miss**: an unplanned event that did not result in injury or damage but could have.
- **LTI**: lost-time injury, an injury that prevents the person from working the next scheduled shift.
- **Permit to Work (PTW)**: written authorisation for non-routine hazardous work.

## 5. Policy requirements

### 5.1 Personal protective equipment
5.1.1 PPE is mandatory in all manufacturing, warehouse, utility and laboratory areas. No PPE, no entry.
5.1.2 Minimum PPE by area:

| Area | Minimum PPE |
|---|---|
| Warehouse and dispatch | Safety shoes, high-visibility vest |
| Dispensing and granulation | Safety shoes, gloves, goggles, dust mask (N95 or better) |
| Solvent handling / coating | Chemical-resistant gloves, goggles, half-face respirator with organic vapour cartridge |
| Boiler, compressor, DG set areas | Safety shoes, ear plugs or muffs (noise above 85 dB(A)) |
| QC laboratory | Lab coat, safety glasses, nitrile gloves |
| Work at height (above 1.8 m) | Full-body harness with double lanyard |

5.1.3 Gowning for classified cleanrooms: entry into Grade C and Grade D areas requires the full gowning procedure defined in the Quality SOPs (Cleanroom Gowning). Gowning is a product-protection requirement and is in addition to the safety PPE listed above.

### 5.2 Permit to Work
5.2.1 A PTW issued by the Site EHS Officer is required for hot work, confined-space entry, work at height above 1.8 m, electrical isolation, and excavation deeper than 1 m.
5.2.2 A PTW is valid for one shift only (maximum 8 hours) and must be closed out before the shift ends.
5.2.3 Lock-out/tag-out (LOTO) is mandatory for all maintenance on powered equipment.

### 5.3 Chemical and solvent safety
5.3.1 Safety Data Sheets (SDS) must be available at the point of use.
5.3.2 Flammable solvent storage at any production area is limited to one day's requirement; bulk stock stays in the designated solvent yard.
5.3.3 Emergency eye-wash and safety showers must be tested weekly and within 10 seconds' walking distance of chemical handling points.

### 5.4 Fire safety
5.4.1 Fire drills are held at every site at least twice a year; evacuation must be completed within 4 minutes.
5.4.2 Fire extinguishers are inspected monthly and refilled annually.
5.4.3 Smoking is prohibited inside all site boundaries.

### 5.5 Environment
5.5.1 Effluent from Unit I and Unit II is treated in the on-site ETP; Unit III discharges to the SEZ common effluent treatment plant after primary treatment.
5.5.2 Hazardous waste (spent solvents, rejected product, ETP sludge) is sent only to authorised TSDF operators, with manifests retained for 5 years.
5.5.3 Target for FY27: reduce water consumption per tonne of product by 8% versus FY26.

### 5.6 Visitors and contractors
5.6.1 All visitors receive a 10-minute safety induction and are escorted at all times in production areas.
5.6.2 Contractors must complete site-specific safety training before their first shift; the training is valid for 12 months.

## 6. Incident reporting and investigation

| Event | Report to EHS within | Investigation lead | Closure target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fatality or major fire | Immediately (phone), written within 2 hours | Plant Head + Corporate EHS | 15 days |
| LTI | 4 hours | Site EHS Officer | 7 days |
| First-aid case | End of shift | Area supervisor | 3 days |
| Near-miss | 24 hours | Area supervisor | 7 days |
| Chemical spill / environmental release | Immediately | Site EHS Officer | 7 days |

Statutory notifications to the Inspector of Factories and Pollution Control Board are made by the Plant Head as required by law.

## 7. Health surveillance

7.1 Pre-employment medical examination is required for all plant staff.
7.2 Employees in dust, solvent or noise-exposed areas receive an annual medical check-up including lung function and audiometry.
7.3 Each site has an Occupational Health Centre with a qualified medical officer during the general shift.

## 8. Performance targets FY27

| Indicator | Target |
|---|---|
| Lost-time injury frequency rate (per million hours) | Below 0.20 |
| Near-miss reports per site per month | At least 25 |
| Safety training hours per employee per year | 16 hours |
| PTW compliance audit score | 95% or above |

## 9. Records

PTW books, incident reports, training records, medical records and waste manifests are retained for a minimum of 5 years (medical records for 40 years for exposed workers).
