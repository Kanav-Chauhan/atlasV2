# ANDA Submission Status Tracker

Status as of 30 September 2026. All Abbreviated New Drug Applications (ANDAs) listed are for products manufactured at **Unit III – Vizag SEZ**, the company's only US FDA-inspected site.

## 1. Summary

| Status | Number of ANDAs |
|---|---|
| Approved (final approval) | 9 |
| Tentative approval (patent/exclusivity barrier) | 1 |
| Under review | 3 |
| Complete Response Letter (CRL) – response in preparation | 1 |

| **Total filed to date** | **14** |

Site compliance status: the 2024 US FDA inspection of Unit III resulted in 3 Form 483 observations (audit trail review, cleaning validation for shared equipment, OOS investigation timeliness); all CAPAs were closed by October 2024. The February 2026 re-inspection closed with no observations (classified NAI). Facility status is acceptable for approval of pending ANDAs.
