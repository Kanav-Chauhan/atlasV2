## 4. Qualification approach

4.1 All qualification protocols are approved by Site QA before execution, following the Change Control SOP. Block C is handled as a major change (CC/U3/26/0012).
4.2 Sequence: Design Qualification (DQ) → Factory Acceptance Test → Installation Qualification (IQ) → Operational Qualification (OQ) → Performance Qualification (PQ).
4.3 Cleanroom acceptance: Grade D processing areas must meet 3,520,000 particles ≥0.5 µm per m³ at rest, temperature 20–25°C and RH 45–60%, over 3 consecutive days of PQ monitoring.
4.4 Purified water system (6,000 L/h): 3-phase PQ over 12 months; Phase 1 (2 weeks daily sampling) must show conductivity ≤ 1.3 µS/cm at 25°C at every point of use before water is used in production.
4.5 Lessons from the 2024 Form 483 are built in: audit-trail review is configured in MES and LIMS from day one, and cleaning validation for shared equipment is completed before any multi-product campaign.
