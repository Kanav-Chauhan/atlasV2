# Internal Audit Report – Unit II Baddi Q4 FY26

| Item | Detail |

## 2. Executive Summary

The site is in a generally satisfactory state of control. The audit raised **8 observations: 0 Critical, 2 Major and 6 Minor**. The Major observations relate to the oral liquid manufacturing area (temperature excursion handling in the syrup compounding room) and to line clearance for labelling, which was a contributing cause of the August 2025 recall of Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup batch CR-2309. Overall audit rating: **Acceptable with improvement** (score 82/100, previous 78/100).

## 3. Scope

- Oral liquids: Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup (compounding, filtration, filling, labelling).
- Ointments: Dermacalm Cream (manufacturing vessel, tube filling in Grade C).
- QC laboratory, stability chambers, purified water system PW-02, warehouse and QA systems.

## 4. Observations

| Obs. No. | Classification | Area | Observation | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BD-01 | Major | Syrup compounding room | Room temperature exceeded 25°C on 7 days in February 2026 (maximum 27.4°C); no deviation was raised, and the impact on 4 batches was not assessed. Grade D limit is 20–25°C | SPL/QA/SOP-004 |
| BD-02 | Major | Labelling line L-2 | Line clearance checklist does not require verification of the label text against the approved artwork at start-up; label proof sheet not retained in BPR for 2 of 10 BPRs sampled | SPL/QA/SOP-012 |
| BD-03 | Minor | Ointment filling (Grade C) | Gowning qualification of 2 operators expired in January 2026 (annual requalification overdue by 3 and 6 weeks) | SPL/PRD/SOP-008 |
| BD-04 | Minor | Purified water | PW-02 point of use POU-07 sampled only twice in February instead of weekly rotation plan | SPL/QC/SOP-033 |
| BD-05 | Minor | QC laboratory | Two volumetric flasks in use without calibration label | GMP general |
| BD-06 | Minor | Warehouse | Rejected material area not locked during audit tour | Schedule M |
| BD-07 | Minor | Stability | Stability chamber SC-04 (30°C/75% RH) alarm test not documented for Q3 | SPL/QC/SOP-040 |
| BD-08 | Minor | Documentation | Three logbooks with pages not numbered "Page x of y" | SPL/QA/POL-007 |

## 5. Positive Practices

- Dermacalm Cream batch records were complete and well-organised; zero GDP errors in 6 BMRs reviewed.
- PW-02 conductivity trend well within limit (≤ 1.3 µS/cm at 25°C); no action-level excursions in FY26.
- New label artwork for the cough syrup (change control CC/U2/25/0094) has a barcode verification step on line L-1.
- Shift allowance and night-shift staffing plans ensure a qualified IPQA officer on every shift.

## 6. Response Timelines Required

The auditee must submit a CAPA plan within **15 working days** of this report (by 17 April 2026). Major observation CAPAs must be implemented within 60 days and Minor within 90 days. Effectiveness checks follow SPL/QA/SOP-004 (90 days after implementation).
