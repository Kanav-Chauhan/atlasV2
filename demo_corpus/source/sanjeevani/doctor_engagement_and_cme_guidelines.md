# Doctor Engagement and CME Guidelines

## 1. Purpose

To define how Sanjeevani organises Continuing Medical Education (CME) programmes, engages doctors as speakers and advisors, and supports scientific activity, in line with UCPMP 2024, the National Medical Commission's professional conduct regulations and the Code of Business Conduct.

## 2. Scope

Applies to Sales, Marketing and Medical Affairs teams, and to any event management agency engaged by Sanjeevani.

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Medical Affairs | Scientific content, speaker selection, fair-market-value (FMV) rates, final approval |
| Product Manager | Annual CME plan and budget per brand |
| Area/Regional Sales Manager | Local logistics proposals, attendance |
| Compliance Officer | Quarterly audit; UCPMP disclosure |
| Finance | Payment only against complete documentation |

## 4. Definitions

- **CME**: an educational programme for healthcare professionals with a clear scientific agenda.
- **HCP**: healthcare professional (doctor, pharmacist, nurse).
- **FMV**: fair market value, the rate for a service based on the expert's qualification and time.
- **Advisory board**: a meeting where a small group of experts advises the company on scientific or medical questions.

## 5. CME requirements

### 5.1 Approval
5.1.1 Every CME must be proposed in the CRM at least **15 days** before the event and approved by Medical Affairs.
5.1.2 The proposal must include agenda, speaker, venue, estimated attendance and budget.

### 5.2 Content
5.2.1 At least 75% of the programme time must be scientific content.
5.2.2 Presentations by external speakers must be reviewed by Medical Affairs for fair balance.
5.2.3 Content must stay within approved indications; questions on off-label use are answered only by Medical Affairs.

### 5.3 Venue and hospitality
5.3.1 Venues must be business or hospital settings (hotel conference rooms, hospital auditoriums, medical association halls). Resorts, cruise ships, and venues outside India are not permitted.
5.3.2 Modest refreshments or a working meal may be provided during the CME only, not exceeding **₹1,500 per participant**.
5.3.3 No travel, accommodation or hospitality may be provided to attendees. Travel and stay may be provided only to the speaker of the CME.
5.3.4 Family members of HCPs may not attend at company expense.

### 5.4 Budget limits per event

| CME type | Max attendees | Max budget (excluding speaker fee) |
|---|---|---|
| Clinic-level round table | 15 | ₹25,000 |
| City CME | 60 | ₹1,20,000 |
| Regional symposium | 150 | ₹4,50,000 |

## 6. Speaker and consultant engagement

6.1 Speakers are engaged only for a legitimate need, through a written contract signed before the event.
6.2 Honoraria follow the FMV grid:

| Speaker tier | Typical profile | Honorarium per hour (max) |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | National key opinion leader, professor | ₹25,000 |
| Tier 2 | Senior consultant, 15+ years | ₹15,000 |
| Tier 3 | Consultant, 5–15 years | ₹8,000 |

6.3 A single HCP may not receive more than **₹2,50,000** in total honoraria from Sanjeevani in a financial year, and may not speak at more than 6 Sanjeevani events in a year.
6.4 Payment is made by bank transfer only, after the event, with TDS deducted under the Income Tax Act. Cash payments are prohibited.
6.5 Honoraria must never be linked to prescriptions or sales.

## 7. Advisory boards

7.1 Maximum 12 external experts per advisory board; maximum 4 advisory boards per therapy area per year.
7.2 A written report of the advice received must be filed within 30 days.

## 8. Sponsorship of conferences

8.1 Sanjeevani may sponsor stalls or scientific sessions at recognised medical association conferences (e.g. national diabetes, cardiology or dermatology conferences) through a contract with the organising association, not with individual doctors.
8.2 Sanjeevani does not sponsor individual HCPs' registration, travel or stay for conferences.

## 9. Research and grants

9.1 Research grants are made only to institutions, after scientific review by the President, R&D's office and approval by the Grants Committee.
9.2 Post-marketing studies must have a genuine scientific objective; "seeding studies" are prohibited.

## 10. Documentation and disclosure

10.1 Required documents for each CME: approved proposal, attendance sheet with registration numbers, photographs of the session, speaker contract, invoice, and Medical Affairs closure note.
10.2 Documentation must be uploaded within **7 days** after the event; otherwise the expense is not reimbursed.
10.3 CME details and expenditure are disclosed on the company website as required by UCPMP 2024.
