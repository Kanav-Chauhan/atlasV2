# Whistleblower Policy (Vigil Mechanism)

## 1. Purpose

Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. is committed to the highest standards of ethics and integrity. This policy establishes the vigil mechanism required under Section 177 of the Companies Act 2013 and Regulation 22 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, so that directors, employees and stakeholders can report genuine concerns without fear of retaliation.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers all directors, permanent and contract employees, trainees, and third parties such as vendors, distributors, CROs and clinical investigators.

## 3. What can be reported

Reportable concerns ("Protected Disclosures") include:

- Falsification, manipulation or deletion of GMP, laboratory or clinical data
- Release of product that does not meet specification, or suppression of deviations or OOS results
- Bribery or improper payments, including to regulators, doctors or hospitals
- Violations of the UCPMP 2024 code by field staff or managers
- Fraud, misappropriation of funds, or manipulation of financial records
- Leak of unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI)
- Serious safety, environmental or patient-safety hazards
- Sexual harassment (these will be routed to the Internal Committee under the POSH Act 2013)
- Retaliation against anyone who has made a report

Personal grievances about salary, appraisal or transfer are not covered and should go to HR.

## 5. Handling process

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acknowledgement to reporter (if contact details given) | Within 3 working days |
| 2 | Preliminary assessment by Compliance Officer | Within 10 working days |
| 3 | Investigation team appointed (HR, Internal Audit, QA or Legal as relevant) | Within 15 working days |
| 4 | Investigation completed and report submitted | Within 60 days of receipt |
| 5 | Action decided by Ethics Committee; outcome communicated to reporter | Within 15 days of report |

5.1 Any concern involving data integrity or product quality is also notified to the Head, Quality Assurance (Corporate) within 24 hours of receipt, so that impact on released batches can be assessed immediately.
5.2 Extensions beyond 60 days require approval of the Audit Committee Chairperson.

## 6. Protection of whistleblowers

6.1 The identity of the reporter is kept confidential and shared only with those who strictly need it for investigation.
6.2 No reporter acting in good faith will suffer dismissal, demotion, transfer, denial of increment, harassment or any other adverse action.
6.3 A reporter who believes they have been retaliated against may approach the Audit Committee Chairperson directly.
6.4 Protection is not available for reports made with malicious intent or knowingly false information; such reports may lead to disciplinary action.

## 7. Rights of the subject

The person against whom a complaint is made will be informed of the allegations at an appropriate stage, will be given an opportunity to respond, and must not interfere with the investigation or destroy evidence.

## 8. Reporting to the Board

8.1 The Compliance Officer presents a quarterly summary to the Audit Committee showing the number of complaints received, closed, pending and substantiated, by category.
8.2 FY2025-26 summary: 23 complaints received; 19 closed; 6 substantiated (4 UCPMP-related, 1 vendor fraud, 1 attendance record falsification); 4 pending at year-end.

## 9. Record retention

All complaints, investigation reports and decisions are retained for 8 years from closure.
