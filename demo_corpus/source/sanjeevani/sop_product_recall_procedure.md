# SOP – Product Recall Procedure

## 1. Purpose

To define how Sanjeevani Pharma decides on, initiates, executes and closes a product recall from the domestic or export market quickly and effectively, and how regulators and customers are informed.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all finished products manufactured at Unit I, Unit II and Unit III or by loan licensees for Sanjeevani, in the domestic market and export markets. It covers voluntary recalls and recalls directed by the State Licensing Authority, CDSCO or an overseas regulator. Mock recalls are also covered.

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Recall Committee | Decides on recall and recall class; chaired by Head QA (Corporate) |
| Head, Quality Assurance (Corporate) | Recall coordinator; signs recall notices |
| Head, Regulatory Affairs | Informs CDSCO, State Licensing Authority and overseas regulators |
| National Sales Manager | Mobilises the field force, C&F agents and stockists to stop sale and return stock |
| Distribution (Supply Chain) | Provides batch-wise distribution records within 4 hours of request |
| Plant Head | Quarantines returned stock; supports investigation |
| Managing Director & CEO | Approves Class I recalls and public communication |

The Recall Committee members are: Head QA (Corporate), Head Regulatory Affairs, National Sales Manager, Head Supply Chain, the Plant Head of the manufacturing unit and a Medical Advisor.

## 4. Definitions

- **Class I recall**: a situation where use of the product may cause serious adverse health consequences or death.
- **Class II recall**: use may cause temporary or medically reversible adverse health consequences, or the probability of serious harm is remote.
- **Class III recall**: use is not likely to cause adverse health consequences (e.g. labelling or packaging defects without safety impact).
- **Recall depth**: the level of the distribution chain to which the recall extends – stockist, retailer or consumer.
- **Effectiveness check**: verification that all consignees received the notice and acted on it.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Recall decision

1. Any quality signal (complaint, OOS on stability, regulatory communication) indicating a potential recall is escalated to Head QA (Corporate) within 24 hours.
2. The Recall Committee meets within 24 hours of escalation and decides on recall, class and depth.

### 5.2 Timelines by class

| Class | Committee decision | Recall notice to consignees | Regulator informed | Recall depth | Target recovery |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Class I | Within 24 hours | Within 24 hours of decision | Within 24 hours | Consumer level | 100% reconciliation within 30 days |
| Class II | Within 48 hours | Within 72 hours of decision | Within 72 hours | Retailer level | Within 45 days |
| Class III | Within 5 working days | Within 10 days of decision | Within 10 days | Stockist/retailer level | Within 60 days |

### 5.3 Execution

1. Supply Chain extracts the distribution list for the batch from the ERP: C&F agents, stockists, institutions and export consignees with quantity and invoice numbers.
2. QA issues the recall notice (Form SPL/QA/F-015-01) by email and courier; the field force confirms receipt with each stockist.
3. Returned stock is received at the manufacturing unit, counted, labelled "RECALLED – DO NOT USE" and stored in the locked recall area.
4. Daily recall status is reported to the Recall Committee until closure.

### 5.4 Reconciliation

Recovery percentage = (quantity returned + quantity verified destroyed at consignee) ÷ quantity distributed × 100. Unaccounted quantity must be explained (e.g. consumed by patients). A recall is closed when recovery and explanations account for the full distributed quantity, or the Recall Committee accepts the residual with justification.

### 5.5 Investigation and CAPA

Root cause investigation follows SPL/QA/SOP-004. A recall report (e.g. SPL/QA/RPT-039 for batch CR-2309 of Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup) is issued within 30 days of recall closure. CAPA effectiveness is checked at 90 days.

### 5.6 Destruction

Recalled stock is destroyed through an authorised waste handler under QA and Finance witness, with a destruction certificate.

### 5.7 Mock recall

One mock recall per unit per year, choosing a batch distributed in at least 3 states. Acceptance: distribution records traced within 4 hours and at least 95% of consignees contacted within 24 hours.

## 6. Records

- Recall Committee minutes, recall notices, consignee acknowledgements – 10 years
- Reconciliation sheet and destruction certificate – 10 years
- Mock recall reports – 5 years
