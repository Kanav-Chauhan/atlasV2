# CDSCO Product Licence Renewal Summary

## 1. Purpose

To summarise the status of Sanjeevani's manufacturing licences, product permissions and GMP certificates in India, the renewal and retention-fee obligations for each, and actions due in FY27. Although manufacturing licences for our units are granted by the respective State Licensing Authorities, product permissions for new drugs and fixed-dose combinations (FDCs) are granted by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO). This summary covers both.

## 2. Background – how licences are maintained

2.1 Under the Drugs Rules, 1945 (as amended), a manufacturing licence in Form 25 remains valid **perpetually**, provided the licensee pays a **licence retention fee every 5 years** before the anniversary of the grant. If the fee is not paid on time, a late fee applies for up to 6 months; after that, the licence is deemed cancelled.
2.2 Each product (and each strength) must be endorsed on the manufacturing licence. New strengths or new pack sizes may require a fresh endorsement or a post-approval change.
2.3 FDCs such as Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup require permission from the Drugs Controller General (India) at CDSCO before the State Licensing Authority can endorse them.
2.4 WHO-GMP certificates, required for exports to many markets, are valid for 3 years.

The revised Schedule M (Good Manufacturing Practices and Requirements of Premises, Plant and Equipment) applies to all three units. Sanjeevani, being a large manufacturer, was required to comply from the first applicability date. Gap assessments were completed in 2024 and all units were declared compliant by Corporate QA. Remaining improvement actions (pharmaceutical quality system documentation and product quality reviews) are tracked by QA.
