# Salary Grades and Allowances FY2026-27

## 1. Purpose

This document sets out Sanjeevani Pharma's grade structure, the annual pay range (cost to company, CTC) for each grade, the fixed allowances paid on top of salary, and the FY2026-27 increment budget. It is the only approved reference for pay ranges. Line managers may share an individual's own grade and range with that individual, but the full table must not be circulated outside Corporate HR, Plant HR and Leadership.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all 2,850 permanent employees at the Ahmedabad head office, Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar), Unit II – Baddi, Unit III – Vizag SEZ, the Hyderabad R&D centre (Genome Valley) and the field sales force of ~180 medical representatives. Contract workers, apprentices and fixed-term trainees are paid under their contracts and are not covered.

Sanjeevani uses 11 grades: four workmen grades (W1–W4) and seven management grades (M1–M7). The travel and insurance bands (A to D) are mapped to these grades.

| W1 | 2.4 | 2.7 | 3.0 |

| W3 | 3.4 | 3.85 | 4.3 |
| W4 | 4.0 | 4.6 | 5.2 |
| M1 | 4.2 | 5.1 | 6.0 |

| M3 | 8.0 | 10.25 | 12.5 |
| M4 | 12.0 | 15.0 | 18.0 |
| M5 | 18.0 | 23.0 | 28.0 |
| M6 | 28.0 | 36.5 | 45.0 |

4.1 A Grade M2 Senior Medical Representative is therefore paid between ₹5.6 lakh and ₹8.4 lakh CTC per year, excluding the quarterly sales incentive.
4.2 Field force incentives (up to 22% of quarterly fixed pay) are paid under the Field Force Guidelines FY27 and are not part of CTC.
4.3 New hires are offered at or below the mid-point. An offer above the mid-point needs approval from the Head, Human Resources; an offer above the maximum needs approval from the Managing Director & CEO.
4.4 Ranges are reviewed every April against a pharma-sector salary survey of 22 Indian companies.

## 6. Fixed allowances (paid in addition to CTC)

| Allowance | Who gets it | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Afternoon (B) shift allowance | Plant employees on B shift | ₹1,200 per month |
| Night (C) shift allowance | Plant employees with at least 10 night shifts in the month | ₹2,800 per month (₹280 per night shift pro-rata below 10) |

6.1 Shift allowances follow the Shift and Overtime Policy (SPL/HR/POL-105); field allowances follow the Field Force Guidelines FY27. If either of those documents changes, it prevails over this table.
6.2 Allowances in Section 6 do not count for PF, gratuity or leave encashment.

## 7. FY2026-27 increment budget

The FY27 increment budget is **₹28.5 crore**, an average increase of **9.5%** of fixed pay, approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee on 18 April 2026. Increments are effective 01 April 2026 and are paid with the June 2026 salary, with arrears for April and May.

### 7.1 Merit matrix

| Performance rating | Share of employees (guideline) | Increment (% of fixed pay) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 – Outstanding | 10% | 14% |
| 4 – Exceeds expectations | 25% | 11% |
| 3 – Meets expectations | 50% | 8.5% |
| 2 – Partly meets | 12% | 4% |
| 1 – Does not meet | 3% | Nil |

7.2 Employees below the minimum of their range after the increment are brought to the minimum (range correction). Employees above the maximum receive the increment as a one-time lump sum, not added to fixed pay.
7.3 A promotion adds 8% of fixed pay on top of the merit increment, or takes the employee to the new grade minimum, whichever is higher.

### 7.4 Budget split by job family

| Job family | Headcount (1 April 2026) | Increment budget (₹ crore) | Average increment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plant (Units I, II, III) | 1,710 | 11.9 | 9.0% |
| Field force | 236 | 3.4 | 10.5% |
| R&D (Hyderabad) | 412 | 6.1 | 10.0% |
| Corporate and head office | 492 | 7.1 | 9.5% |
| **Total** | **2,850** | **28.5** | **9.5%** |

The field force and R&D receive higher averages because attrition in the field force was 21% in FY26 and scientist pay at Hyderabad lags the survey mid-point by about 6%.

## 8. Confidentiality
