# Stability Study Report – Glucosan-M 500 SR

## 1. Objective

To evaluate the long-term and accelerated stability of Glucosan-M 500 (Metformin Hydrochloride SR Tablets 500 mg) in the commercial Alu-Alu blister pack, and to justify extending the shelf life from 24 months to **36 months** with the storage statement "Store below 30°C. Protect from moisture."

## 2. Study Design

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Protocol | SPL/RND/PRT-041, Stability Protocol for Glucosan-M 500 SR (Alu-Alu) |
| Batches | GM5-22-014, GM5-22-015, GM5-22-016 (commercial scale, 3,00,000 tablets each, Unit I) |
| Manufacturing dates | February–March 2022 |
| Pack | Alu-Alu blister, 10 tablets, in carton |
| Long-term condition | 30°C ± 2°C / 75% ± 5% RH (Climatic Zone IVb) |
| Accelerated condition | 40°C ± 2°C / 75% ± 5% RH |
| Long-term time points | 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36 months |
| Accelerated time points | 0, 1, 2, 3, 6 months |
| Specification | SPL/QC/SPEC-005 (shelf-life limits) |
| Testing laboratory | QC Unit I (release); Analytical R&D Hyderabad (stability review) |

## 3. Acceptance Criteria (shelf-life)

- Assay: 95.0–105.0% of label claim
- Dissolution: not less than 80% (Q) released in 10 hours; 1 h 20–40%; 3 h 45–65%
- Water content: not more than 3.0% w/w
- Total impurities: not more than 1.0%; cyanoguanidine not more than 0.02%
- Description: no change

## 4. Results – Long-term (30°C/75% RH), mean of 3 batches

| Time (months) | Assay (%) | Dissolution 10 h (%) | Dissolution 1 h (%) | Water (% w/w) | Total impurities (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100.6 | 93 | 29 | 1.4 | 0.08 |
| 3 | 100.3 | 93 | 29 | 1.6 | 0.09 |
| 6 | 100.1 | 92 | 30 | 1.7 | 0.10 |
| 12 | 99.7 | 92 | 30 | 1.9 | 0.12 |
| 18 | 99.4 | 91 | 31 | 2.1 | 0.14 |
| 24 | 99.0 | 90 | 31 | 2.3 | 0.16 |
| 36 | 98.4 | 89 | 32 | 2.6 | 0.21 |

The lowest individual result at 36 months was assay 97.9% (GM5-22-016) and dissolution 10 h of 87% (individual tablet minimum 84%). Cyanoguanidine remained below 0.01% at all points.

## 5. Results – Accelerated (40°C/75% RH), mean of 3 batches

| Time (months) | Assay (%) | Dissolution 10 h (%) | Water (% w/w) | Total impurities (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100.6 | 93 | 1.4 | 0.08 |
| 3 | 99.8 | 91 | 2.2 | 0.15 |
| 6 | 99.1 | 89 | 2.8 | 0.24 |

No significant change (as defined in ICH Q1A(R2)) occurred at accelerated conditions.

## 6. Statistical Evaluation

Regression analysis (ICH Q1E) of long-term assay data gives a slope of −0.061% per month (pooled; batch poolability confirmed, p > 0.25). The 95% one-sided lower confidence limit crosses 95.0% at **58 months**, well beyond 36 months. Water content increases linearly at 0.033% per month; the projected value at 36 months (2.6%) remains below the 3.0% limit. The water-content shelf-life limit of 3.0% was therefore set in specification version 6.0.

## 7. Photostability and In-use

- Photostability (ICH Q1B option 2) on one batch: no change; tablets are not light-sensitive in the Alu-Alu pack.
- Blister removed from carton and stored at 30°C/75% RH for 3 months: water rose to 2.0%, all other results unchanged.

## 8. Out-of-Trend and Out-of-Specification Events

One out-of-trend event: batch GM5-22-015 dissolution at 1 h was 38% at 18 months (within the 20–40% limit but above trend). Investigation found a dissolution bath temperature drift of +0.8°C; repeat result 31%. No OOS occurred during the study.

## 9. Conclusion

The data support a shelf life of **36 months** for Glucosan-M 500 SR in the Alu-Alu pack with the storage statement **"Store below 30°C. Protect from moisture."** The specification SPL/QC/SPEC-005 was revised to version 6.0 (effective 01 March 2026) and a notification was filed with the State Licensing Authority through Regulatory Affairs. Ongoing stability: 1 commercial batch per year will be placed on long-term stability.
