# UCPMP Compliance Guide for Field Staff

## 1. Purpose

This guide translates the Uniform Code for Pharmaceutical Marketing Practices (UCPMP) 2024, notified by the Department of Pharmaceuticals, into practical do's and don'ts for Sanjeevani medical representatives, area managers and marketing staff. Following it is a condition of employment and of incentive eligibility.

## 2. Scope

All field staff, marketing staff, medical affairs personnel and any agency or third party acting on Sanjeevani's behalf in promoting products to healthcare professionals (HCPs) in India.

## 3. Key principles of UCPMP 2024

1. Promotion must be accurate, balanced, fair and capable of substantiation.
2. Claims must be consistent with the approved prescribing information.
3. No gifts, pecuniary advantages or benefits in kind may be offered or provided to HCPs or their family members.
4. Free samples are permitted only within defined limits and must be properly recorded.
5. Travel and hospitality for HCPs is prohibited, except where the HCP is a speaker at a CME conducted in line with the code.
6. Companies must disclose CME expenditure and maintain records.

## 4. Do's and don'ts

| Topic | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Product claims | Use only the approved visual aid and monograph | Make comparative "best/safest" claims without data |
| Gifts | Share approved scientific literature | Give pens, diaries, sweets, Diwali hampers, gadgets or gift vouchers |
| Samples | Give samples as per the Sample Distribution Policy and record them | Give samples to chemists or for resale |
| Meals | — | Pay for doctor's meals, family outings or clinic events |
| Travel | — | Book flights, hotels or holidays for HCPs or their family |
| CME | Organise CMEs through Medical Affairs, in approved venues | Hold CMEs at resorts, foreign locations or leisure venues |
| Brand reminders | Leave approved brand reminders of value below ₹1,000 only if they are for patient education (e.g. diet chart pads) | Brand reminders that have personal utility for the doctor |
| Off-label | Refer off-label questions to Medical Affairs | Promote any use not in the monograph |

## 5. Samples (summary)

5.1 Samples must be marked "Physician's Sample – Not to be sold".
5.2 Samples may only be given to HCPs qualified to prescribe the product.
5.3 Under UCPMP 2024, the number of free samples of a product given to an HCP must not exceed 12 sample packs per HCP per year for a new product; the value of samples given by the company must not exceed 2% of its domestic sales in a year.
5.4 Sanjeevani's internal limits are stricter for some brands; for example, a maximum of 10 strips per doctor per month for Glucosan-M. See the Sample Distribution Policy for full details.

## 6. CMEs and speaker engagements

6.1 All CMEs must be approved by Medical Affairs at least 15 days in advance.
6.2 Speaker honoraria may be paid only under a written contract for genuine services, at fair-market-value rates set by Medical Affairs.
6.3 CME expenditure and details are published on the company website in line with the UCPMP disclosure requirements.

## 7. Reporting and investigations

7.1 Any HCP request for a gift, sponsorship or benefit must be politely declined and reported to the ASM and to compliance@sanjeevani.example within 2 working days.
7.2 Complaints of UCPMP violations against Sanjeevani may be filed by anyone with the company's Ethics Committee for Marketing Practices (ECMP). The ECMP is chaired by the Compliance Officer and includes the National Sales Manager and Head, Medical Affairs.
7.3 Complaints are disposed of within 90 days. If a complaint is not resolved, it may be escalated to the industry association's ethics committee and then to the Apex Committee for Pharma Marketing Practices of the Department of Pharmaceuticals.

## 8. Penalties for violations

| Violation | Internal consequence |
|---|---|
| Unrecorded or excess sample distribution | Written warning; sample privileges suspended for 1 month |
| Gift to HCP (any value) | Final warning; forfeiture of quarterly incentive |
| Sponsoring HCP travel or hospitality | Termination |
| Off-label promotion | Final warning or termination |
| Falsified DCR or sample records | Termination |

Penalties under UCPMP 2024 itself may include reprimand, recovery of money or items given, and public disclosure.

## 9. Self-declaration

Every field employee must sign the annual UCPMP self-declaration in the CRM by **30 April**. Managers sign for their teams' compliance.

## 10. Training

UCPMP e-learning (45 minutes, pass mark 80%) is mandatory for new joiners within 15 days and annually thereafter.

## 11. Quick checklist before every call

- Am I using the current approved e-detailer?
- Am I carrying only samples that I can record against this doctor?
- Am I offering anything other than information and samples? If yes, stop.
- Do I know how to report an adverse event within 24 hours?
