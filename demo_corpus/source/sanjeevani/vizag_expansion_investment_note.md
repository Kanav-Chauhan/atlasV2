# Vizag Expansion Investment Note

## 1. Proposal

To build **Block C**, a new oral-solids manufacturing block at Unit III – Vizag (Visakhapatnam) SEZ, at a total capital cost of **₹310 crore**, to raise Unit III's annual capacity from **3.2 billion to 5.0 billion tablets/capsules**. The proposal was approved by the Board on 22 April 2026 (Resolution 5.1).

## 2. Background and rationale

2.1 Unit III ran at 88% capacity utilisation in FY2025-26 and is expected to exceed 95% in FY28 based on current ANDA approvals and launches. Above 90%, changeover losses and scheduling constraints sharply increase the risk of supply failures to US customers.
2.2 The 2024 US FDA inspection of Unit III resulted in 3 Form 483 observations; all CAPAs were closed by October 2024 and the February 2026 re-inspection closed with no observations (NAI). The site's compliance status is therefore strong enough to support a Prior Approval Supplement (PAS) for site transfer.
2.3 Four pending ANDAs and six products in development need dedicated capacity, including high-potency handling (containment up to OEB 4) not available in the existing blocks.
2.4 US generics revenue grew 16.9% to ₹512 crore in FY26.

## 3. Scope

| Area | Description |
|---|---|
| Building | Four-storey Block C, 18,500 m² built-up, adjacent to Block B |
| Process | 2 granulation trains (high-shear + fluid bed), 4 compression machines, 2 coaters, 2 encapsulators |
| Containment | One OEB 4 suite with isolators for high-potency products |
| Packing | 4 blister lines, 1 bottle line with serialisation |
| Cleanrooms | Grade D processing areas, temperature 20–25°C, RH 45–60% |
| Utilities | Purified water generation 6,000 L/h, HVAC, compressed air, nitrogen |
| QC | Expansion of QC lab by 1,200 m², 18 additional HPLC systems |
| Warehouse | Automated high-rack warehouse, 4,200 pallet positions |

## 8. Financial evaluation

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|

| Incremental EBITDA margin at peak | 26% |

| Payback period | 5.2 years from commissioning |
| Incremental headcount | 420 |

### 8.1 Sensitivity

| Scenario | IRR |
|---|---|
| Base case | 21.4% |
| Revenue 15% lower | 16.2% |
| 12-month delay in US approvals | 17.8% |
| Capex 10% overrun | 19.5% |

The project remains above the 14% hurdle rate in all scenarios.

## 9. Risks and mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Delay in regulatory approvals | Early engagement with US FDA through pre-submission; PAS filings staggered |
| Cost overrun | Fixed-price EPC contract for civil works; overrun above 5% to be reported to the Board |
| Compliance risk in a new block | Data-integrity-by-design (audit trail review built into MES and LIMS), learning from 2024 observations |
| Skilled manpower availability | Hiring from Andhra University and pharmacy colleges; 6-month training under the Training and GMP Qualification Policy |
| SEZ policy changes | Block also eligible for domestic sales on payment of duty |

## 10. Recommendation

Management recommends approval of ₹310 crore for Block C. The project is strategically important for the US generics business and is financially attractive, with an IRR of 21.4% against a 14% hurdle rate.
