# Glucosan-M Brand Plan FY27

## 1. Executive summary

Glucosan-M 500 (metformin 500 mg SR) is Sanjeevani's largest brand. In FY26 it delivered ₹398.0 crore of domestic sales. The FY27 brand target is **₹441.0 crore (10.8% growth)**, about 29% of the company's domestic target. The plan focuses on (a) converting immediate-release metformin prescriptions to once-daily SR, (b) deepening coverage among consulting physicians in Tier 2/3 towns, and (c) patient adherence programmes. Glucosan-M also builds the prescriber base for the future SJ-2041 fixed-dose combination, which is in Phase III.

## 5. Positioning and key messages

**Positioning:** "Once-daily metformin that patients stay on."

Approved messages (all consistent with the product monograph):
1. Once-daily with the evening meal: simpler regimen.
2. SR matrix with gradual release over about 10 hours; fewer GI side effects than immediate-release metformin in many patients.
3. Assured quality: assay 95.0–105.0%, dissolution not less than 80% (Q) released in 10 hours, made at our WHO-GMP Unit I.
4. Maximum dose 2,000 mg once daily; titrate weekly in 500 mg steps.

No claims of superiority over other SR brands may be made.

## 6. Strategy and tactics

### 6.1 Doctor engagement
- Target list: 24,000 doctors (A: 7,200; B: 9,600; C: 7,200), aligned with the Field Force Guidelines FY27.
- Detailing priority 1 in every call for diabetologists and consulting physicians.
- Samples: maximum 10 strips per doctor per month and 60 per year, as per the Sample Distribution Policy.

### 6.2 Medical education
- 320 CMEs on "Metformin in 2026: still the foundation" through Medical Affairs, in line with the Doctor Engagement and CME Guidelines.
- 12 regional advisory boards with diabetologists (contracted at fair-market value).

### 6.3 Patient adherence – "Sanjeevani Sugar Saathi"
- Free WhatsApp and IVR reminders in 8 languages; dietary tips; HbA1c test reminders every 3 months.
- Enrolment only with patient consent, data handled per the DPDP Act 2023. No prescriber incentives linked to enrolment.

### 6.4 Trade
- Ensure 98% availability at the top 40,000 chemists; stock norm 30 days at stockists.
