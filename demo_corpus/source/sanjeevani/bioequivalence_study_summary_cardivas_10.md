# Bioequivalence Study Summary – Cardivas 10

## 1. Study Synopsis

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Study number | SPL-BE-2023-04 |
| Title | An open-label, balanced, randomised, two-treatment, two-period, two-sequence, single-dose, crossover oral bioequivalence study of Rosuvastatin Calcium Tablets 10 mg in healthy adult human subjects under fasting conditions |
| Test product (T) | Cardivas 10 (Rosuvastatin 10 mg tablet), Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd., batch CV10-EX-007, Unit I |
| Reference product (R) | Innovator rosuvastatin 10 mg tablets, procured from the Indian market |
| CRO and clinical site | Medivance Clinical Research Pvt. Ltd., Ahmedabad (fictional), CDSCO-registered BA/BE centre |
| Ethics Committee approval | 14 March 2023 |
| Clinical phase | 10 April – 28 April 2023 |
| Washout | 10 days |
| Subjects | 36 randomised, 34 completed (2 withdrew for personal reasons) |

## 2. Objective

To compare the rate and extent of absorption of Cardivas 10 with the reference product and to establish bioequivalence for the domestic market and as supporting data for the export dossier from Unit III.

## 3. Methods

- **Dosing**: single oral dose of 10 mg with 240 mL water after 10-hour overnight fast.
- **Sampling**: 21 blood samples per period, pre-dose to 72 hours post-dose.
- **Bioanalysis**: validated LC-MS/MS method; LLOQ 0.10 ng/mL; calibration range 0.10–40.0 ng/mL; incurred sample reanalysis passed in 96% of 120 samples.
- **Statistics**: ANOVA on log-transformed Cmax, AUC0-t and AUC0-∞; 90% confidence intervals of the geometric mean ratio (T/R).
- **Acceptance**: 90% CI within 80.00–125.00% for Cmax and AUC.

## 4. Pharmacokinetic Results (n = 34)

| Parameter | Test (geometric mean) | Reference (geometric mean) | T/R ratio (%) | 90% CI (%) | Intra-subject CV (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cmax (ng/mL) | 6.21 | 6.31 | 98.4 | 91.2–106.1 | 18.6 |
| AUC0-t (ng·h/mL) | 58.9 | 58.2 | 101.2 | 95.8–106.9 | 12.4 |
| AUC0-∞ (ng·h/mL) | 61.7 | 61.1 | 101.0 | 95.7–106.5 | 12.1 |
| Tmax (h), median | 4.0 | 4.0 | — | — | — |
| t½ (h), mean | 19.4 | 19.8 | — | — | — |

All 90% confidence intervals lie within 80.00–125.00%. Cardivas 10 is **bioequivalent** to the reference product under fasting conditions.

## 5. Safety

| Event | Test | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Subjects with any adverse event | 3 | 2 |
| Headache | 2 | 1 |
| Myalgia (mild) | 1 | 0 |
| Nausea | 0 | 1 |
| Serious adverse events | 0 | 0 |

All adverse events were mild and resolved without treatment. No clinically significant laboratory changes were observed.

## 6. Dissolution Comparison (supportive)

Comparative dissolution in three media (pH 1.2, 4.5, 6.8; USP Type II, 50 rpm, 900 mL) showed f2 similarity factors of 68, 72 and 64 respectively (f2 ≥ 50 indicates similarity). More than 85% was released in 15 minutes in pH 1.2 and 4.5 for both products.

## 7. GCP Compliance and Audits

- The study was conducted per the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019 and ICH E6(R2).
- Sanjeevani Clinical QA audited the CRO on 24–25 April 2023 (clinical phase) and 12 June 2023 (bioanalytical); 3 minor findings, all closed.
- Data integrity: bioanalytical audit trails reviewed for 100% of runs.

## 8. Conclusion and Use

The test product Cardivas 10 (batch CV10-EX-007) meets the bioequivalence criteria for Cmax and AUC. The study report supports:

1. The domestic marketing of Cardivas 10 (CDSCO approval held).
2. The export dossier for Rosuvastatin 10 mg tablets from Unit III, which was among the products reviewed during the March 2024 US FDA inspection.
3. A biowaiver request for the 5 mg strength based on proportional formulation and comparable dissolution.
