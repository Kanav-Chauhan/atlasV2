# CAPA Register Q1 FY27

## 1. Purpose and Period

This register consolidates Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPAs) open, raised or closed across all Sanjeevani sites during **Q1 FY27 (1 April – 30 June 2026)**. It is reviewed at the monthly Quality Review Meeting chaired by the Head QA (Corporate). Per SPL/QA/SOP-004, every implemented CAPA receives an **effectiveness check at 90 days**.
