# Leave Policy 2026

## 1. Purpose

To define the types of leave available to Sanjeevani employees, how leave accrues, how it is applied for and approved, and how unused leave is treated, so that employees can rest and attend to personal needs while plant and field operations continue without disruption.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent employees of Sanjeevani Pharma Ltd. at the Ahmedabad head office, Unit I Changodar, Unit II Baddi, Unit III Vizag SEZ, the Hyderabad R&D centre and the field force. Workmen covered by a long-term settlement at a site follow the settlement where it is more favourable. Leave is administered on a **calendar-year basis (January–December)**.

## 3. Leave entitlement summary

| Leave type | Days per calendar year | Accrual | Carry forward | Encashment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Privilege leave (PL) | 21 | 1.75 days per month | Up to 45 days total balance | Yes, above 30 days at year-end; full balance at exit |
| Casual leave (CL) | 7 | Credited on 1 January | No (lapses on 31 December) | No |
| Sick leave (SL) | 7 | Credited on 1 January | Up to 21 days total balance | No |
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks | As per Maternity Benefit Act 1961 | — | — |
| Paternity leave | 5 working days | Per event | — | — |
| Bereavement leave | 3 working days | Per event (immediate family) | — | — |
| Marriage leave | 3 working days | Once during service | — | — |

In short, every employee receives 21 privilege leave, 7 casual leave and 7 sick leave per calendar year.

## 4. Privilege leave (PL)

4.1 PL accrues at 1.75 days for every completed month of service. Employees joining mid-year receive pro-rated PL.
4.2 PL must be applied for at least 7 days in advance for absences of 3 days or more.
4.3 A minimum of one PL block of 5 consecutive working days is encouraged each year.
4.4 At plants, PL during planned shutdowns (typically the Diwali week) may be mandated by the Plant Head with 30 days' notice.
4.5 Balances above 30 days on 31 December are encashed at basic salary plus DA, up to a maximum of 15 days per year. The total balance carried forward is capped at 45 days.

## 5. Casual leave (CL)

5.1 CL is meant for short, unplanned personal needs. A maximum of 3 consecutive days of CL may be taken at one time.
5.2 CL cannot be combined with PL in the same spell.
5.3 Unused CL lapses at the end of the calendar year.

## 6. Sick leave (SL)

6.1 SL of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner.
6.2 Plant employees returning after SL of more than 5 days must obtain a fitness clearance from the site Occupational Health Centre before returning to the shop floor, because of GMP hygiene requirements.
6.3 Employees in GMP areas must report any infectious illness, open wound or skin lesion to their supervisor; they will be moved to non-product-contact work or placed on SL.

## 7. Maternity, paternity and adoption

7.1 Maternity leave of 26 weeks with full pay for the first two children; 12 weeks for the third child onwards.
7.2 Adoption leave of 12 weeks for a mother adopting a child below 3 months of age.
7.3 Paternity leave of 5 working days, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.
7.4 Expecting employees in solvent handling or hormone/cytotoxic areas will be moved to a safe role on request without loss of pay.

Plant employees required to work on a declared holiday receive double wages or a compensatory off within 30 days, as per the Shift and Overtime Policy.

## 9. Applying for leave

9.1 All leave is applied for and approved in the HRMS (SanjeevaniOne) portal.
9.2 Approvers must act within 2 working days; unactioned requests escalate to the next-level manager.
9.3 Medical representatives must also update their call plan so that doctor coverage is not lost.
9.4 Unauthorised absence for more than 8 consecutive days will be treated as abandonment of service after due notice.

## 10. Leave on separation

On resignation or retirement, the full PL balance is encashed at basic salary plus DA. CL and SL are not encashed. Leave cannot be adjusted against the notice period without the Head of Function's approval.
