# FDA Re-inspection February 2026 Summary

## 1. Summary

The US FDA re-inspected Unit III – Vizag (Visakhapatnam) SEZ from **16 to 20 February 2026** (5 inspection days). At the close-out meeting on 20 February 2026 the investigators stated that **no Form FDA 483 was issued**. The inspection was subsequently classified **No Action Indicated (NAI)**. This is the first inspection since the March 2024 inspection, which resulted in 3 Form 483 observations (data-integrity audit trail review, cleaning validation for shared equipment and OOS investigation timeliness), all closed by October 2024.

## 2. Inspection Details

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Inspection type | Surveillance re-inspection, including verification of 2024 CAPAs |
| Investigators | 2 (one investigator, one microbiologist) |
| Opening meeting | 16 February 2026, 09:30 |
| Close-out meeting | 20 February 2026, 16:00 |
| Sanjeevani attendees | Venkat Raju (Plant Head), Dr. Farhan Qureshi (Head QA Corporate), Pooja Desai (Head Regulatory Affairs), site QA and QC managers |
| Form 483 observations | None |
| Classification | NAI |
| Establishment Inspection Report (EIR) received | 27 April 2026 |

## 3. Areas Covered

| Day | Area | Key records reviewed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Site tour; warehouse; dispensing | Material status labels, temperature mapping of warehouse (15–25°C zone) |
| 2 | QC laboratory; data integrity | CDS audit trails for 30 random release batches; OOS log 2025 |
| 3 | Production; cleaning validation | Cleaning validation for shared equipment; worst-case matrix; swab recovery data |
| 4 | Water system; HVAC; microbiology | PW-03 trends (conductivity ≤ 1.3 µS/cm at 25°C), ozone levels; environmental monitoring of Grade D |
| 5 | Quality system | Deviations, CAPA, change control, APQR, complaints; close-out |

## 4. Verification of 2024 CAPAs

| 2024 Observation | What the investigators checked | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Audit trail review | Audit trail review records for 30 batches released in 2025; asked analysts to demonstrate the review report | No issues; reviewer signatures present for all 30 |
| Cleaning validation (shared equipment) | Worst-case matrix, MACO calculations, re-validation report for BB-03 and CM-05 | Accepted |
| OOS timeliness | OOS log 2025: 41 OOS, 40 closed within 30 days, 1 extended with Head QA approval (closed day 37) | Accepted |

## 5. Discussion Items (verbal, not observations)

The investigators raised three verbal comments. They are not observations but Sanjeevani has opened improvement actions:

| No. | Comment | Action | Owner | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Environmental monitoring trend reports could show alert-level excursions by room | Add room-wise trend chart | QC Manager, Unit III | 30 April 2026 |
| D2 | Line clearance checklist for packaging could include a photo record | Pilot photo record on 2 packaging lines | Site QA | 31 May 2026 |
| D3 | Training records for contract cleaners were held by the contractor | Copy into site LMS | HR, Unit III | 31 March 2026 |

All three actions are tracked in the CAPA Register Q1 FY27 as improvement actions.

## 6. Preparation Undertaken

- Two mock inspections by Corporate QA (September 2025 and January 2026), with an external consultant for the January mock.
- Front room / back room set-up with a document request tracker; median document retrieval time during the inspection was 11 minutes.
- Refresher training on inspection conduct for 74 employees.

## 7. Business Impact

- The NAI outcome removes the risk to pending ANDA approvals from Unit III; Regulatory Affairs expects 2 ANDA approvals in FY27.
- The result supports the Board decision of 22 April 2026 to approve the ₹310 crore Vizag capacity expansion.

## 8. Recommendations

1. Maintain the 6-monthly mock FDA audit at Unit III.
2. Extend the audit-trail review dashboard to Unit I by 30 September 2026.
3. Share the inspection readiness playbook with Unit I and Unit II.
