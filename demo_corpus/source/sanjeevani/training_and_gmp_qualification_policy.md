# Training and GMP Qualification Policy

## 1. Purpose

To ensure that every person whose work can affect product quality is trained and qualified before working independently, as required by Schedule M of the Drugs Rules, WHO-GMP and 21 CFR Part 211 (for Unit III exports). The policy also covers general, safety and compliance training for all employees.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees, contract workers and trainees at Unit I, Unit II, Unit III, the Hyderabad R&D centre, warehouses and head office functions that support GMP (for example QA, Regulatory Affairs and IT for computerised systems).

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Head, HR | Training infrastructure, LMS, induction |
| Head, QA (Corporate) | GMP training content, qualification standards, effectiveness |
| Site QA Training Coordinator | Training needs, schedule, records, matrix |
| Department heads | Identify job-specific SOPs, nominate trainers, sign off qualification |
| Qualified trainers | Deliver training and practical assessment |
| Employees | Attend, complete assessments, never perform tasks they are not qualified for |

## 4. Definitions

- **Training matrix**: the list of SOPs and modules each job role must complete.
- **Qualified**: the employee has been trained, assessed and signed off for a task.
- **Read-and-understand**: self-study of a revised SOP followed by an online quiz.
- **OJT**: on-the-job training under the supervision of a qualified trainer.

## 5. Training programme

### 5.1 Induction (all new joiners)
| Module | Duration | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Company orientation, Code of Business Conduct | 4 hours | Day 1 |
| Plant safety and EHS, PPE | 4 hours | Day 1 |
| Basic GMP and data integrity (ALCOA+) | 8 hours | Within first week |
| POSH awareness | 1 hour | Within 15 days |
| Cleanroom gowning (production, QC microbiology) | 4 hours + practical | Before first entry to Grade C/D |

### 5.2 Job-specific qualification
5.2.1 Each role has a training matrix approved by the department head and QA.
5.2.2 Qualification sequence: classroom/SOP reading → written assessment (pass mark **80%**) → OJT (minimum 3 supervised executions for critical tasks) → practical assessment → sign-off by department head and Site QA.
5.2.3 An employee who scores below 80% may retake the assessment once after 3 days; a second failure requires full re-training.
5.2.4 Critical tasks include: dispensing, granulation, compression, coating, filling (liquids), aseptic-like handling, HPLC/dissolution analysis, batch record review and line clearance.

### 5.3 Gowning qualification
5.3.1 Personnel entering Grade C areas must pass a gowning qualification with three consecutive successful microbial contact-plate assessments.
5.3.2 Gowning re-qualification is required every 12 months, or after any failed routine gown monitoring result.

### 5.4 Analyst qualification (QC)
5.4.1 New analysts must analyse a previously tested reference sample; results must agree within the method's precision limits (typically ±2.0% for assay).
5.4.2 Analysts are qualified per technique (HPLC, GC, dissolution, KF, microbiology) and not generically.

## 6. SOP revisions and refresher training

6.1 When an SOP is revised, all affected staff must complete training before the SOP's effective date. SOPs are issued with a minimum training window of 7 days.
6.2 Annual GMP refresher training of at least 8 hours is mandatory for every GMP employee.
6.3 Employees absent for more than 60 days from a GMP role must be re-assessed before resuming independent work.

## 7. Training targets

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| GMP training hours per GMP employee per year | 24 hours minimum |
| Training matrix compliance at any time | 98% or above |
| Overdue trainings older than 30 days | Zero |
| Training effectiveness (post-training assessment pass rate first attempt) | 90% |

## 8. Training effectiveness

8.1 Effectiveness is checked through assessments, observation on the floor, and trend review of deviations caused by human error.
8.2 If more than 2 deviations attributed to the same SOP occur in a quarter, Site QA must re-evaluate the SOP and its training.

## 9. Records

9.1 Training is recorded in the validated Learning Management System (LMS). Paper records, where used, are scanned within 5 working days.
9.2 Training records are retained for the duration of employment plus 7 years.
9.3 During regulatory inspections, the training record of any employee must be retrievable within 30 minutes.

## 10. Consequences

Performing a GMP task without qualification is a reportable deviation and may lead to disciplinary action for both the employee and the supervisor who assigned the task.

## 11. References
