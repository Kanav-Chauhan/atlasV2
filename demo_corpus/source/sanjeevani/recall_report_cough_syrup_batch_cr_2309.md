# Recall Report – Cough Syrup Batch CR-2309

## 1. Summary

In August 2025 Sanjeevani Pharma voluntarily recalled batch **CR-2309** of Sanjeevani Cough Relief Syrup (Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide + Chlorpheniramine Maleate, 100 mL bottle) manufactured at Unit II – Baddi, because of a **label misprint**. The recall covered **18,400 bottles**. The investigation concluded there was **no safety impact**: the product met all specifications and the dosage information on the label was correct. The recall was classified as **Class III** and closed on 15 October 2025.

## 3. Nature of the Defect

The bottle label of batch CR-2309 carried the **manufacturing licence number of Unit I (Ahmedabad) instead of Unit II (Baddi)**. The carton was correct. The error arose when an outdated label artwork file was loaded on the label printing machine of line L-2 after a software restore. Product name, composition, dose, batch number, dates, storage and warnings were all correct, so the defect could not lead to misuse.

## 6. Root Cause Investigation

Investigation under deviation DEV/U2/25/0117 (Major):

- **Primary root cause:** After a software restore of the label printer on line L-2 on 27 February 2025, the artwork library reverted to a 2021 template that pre-dated the Unit II-specific licence number field. No verification of the artwork version was required after the restore.
- **Contributing cause:** Line clearance checklist did not require a comparison of the first printed label with the approved artwork; the label proof was not checked against the carton.
- **Scope:** Only batch CR-2309 was printed on L-2 between the restore and 10 March 2025; batches CR-2310 to CR-2312 were printed on line L-1 and were verified correct.

Effectiveness check at 90 days: no labelling error in the next 40 batches and successful mock recall.
