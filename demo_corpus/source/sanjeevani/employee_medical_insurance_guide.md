# Employee Medical Insurance Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains the Group Mediclaim (hospitalisation), Group Personal Accident and Group Term Life covers that Sanjeevani provides, who is covered, how much, and how to make a claim. The policy year runs from 01 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

## 2. Who is covered

2.1 All permanent employees from their date of joining.
2.2 Family floater: employee, spouse and up to two dependent children (up to 25 years of age).
2.3 Parents or parents-in-law (any two) can be added under the optional parental cover at the employee's cost.
2.4 Employees covered under ESIC (gross wages up to ₹21,000 per month) are covered by ESIC and not by this Group Mediclaim policy.

## 3. Sum insured – Group Mediclaim

| Band | Grades | Family floater sum insured |
|---|---|---|
| Band A | M5 and above | ₹10,00,000 |
| Band B | M3–M4 | ₹7,00,000 |
| Band C | M1–M2 | ₹5,00,000 |
| Band D | W1–W4 (non-ESIC) | ₹3,00,000 |

3.1 A corporate buffer of ₹50,00,000 is available for the whole company for critical illnesses (cancer, organ transplant, major cardiac surgery, stroke) after the family sum insured is exhausted. Use of the buffer requires approval from Head HR; maximum ₹5,00,000 per family per year.

## 6. How to claim

### 6.1 Cashless (network hospitals)
1. Check that the hospital is in the TPA network (TPA: fictional "MediAssist Health TPA"; helpline 1800-425-7712).
2. Show the e-card at the hospital insurance desk.
3. For planned hospitalisation, request pre-authorisation at least **48 hours** in advance.
4. For emergencies, the hospital must inform the TPA within **24 hours** of admission.
5. Pay only non-medical items (toiletries, attendant food, etc.) at discharge.

### 6.2 Reimbursement (non-network hospitals)
1. Inform the TPA within 24 hours of admission.
2. Submit the claim form with original bills, discharge summary, investigation reports and cancelled cheque within **30 days** of discharge.
3. Post-hospitalisation bills must be submitted within 15 days of completing treatment.
4. Claims are generally settled within 30 days of receiving complete documents.

## 7. Group Personal Accident (GPA)

| Event | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Accidental death | 3 × annual CTC (minimum ₹10,00,000) |
| Permanent total disability | 3 × annual CTC |
| Permanent partial disability | As per policy table |
| Temporary total disability | 1% of sum insured per week, up to 100 weeks |

GPA covers accidents 24×7, both on and off duty, worldwide.

## 8. Group Term Life (GTL)

8.1 Life cover of 3 × annual CTC, subject to a maximum of ₹1.5 crore.
8.2 Nominations must be updated in HRMS; the claim is paid to the registered nominee.

## 9. Annual health check-up

Employees aged 35 and above receive a free annual health check-up at empanelled diagnostic centres. Plant employees in exposed areas receive additional tests under the Plant Safety and EHS Policy.

## 10. Exclusions (summary)

Cosmetic surgery, non-medically-necessary treatment, self-inflicted injuries, injuries under the influence of alcohol or drugs, infertility treatment, spectacles and contact lenses, and OPD consultations (unless covered under the OPD wallet for Band A and Band B, ₹15,000 per year).

## 11. Coverage on exit

Cover ends on the last working day. Employees may convert to an individual policy with the insurer within 30 days of exit without fresh medical underwriting.
