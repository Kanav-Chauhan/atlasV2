# SOP – Cleanroom Gowning

## 1. Purpose

To describe the gowning and de-gowning sequence for personnel entering Grade D and Grade C classified areas, so that personnel do not contaminate product or the controlled environment.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees, contract workers, engineers and visitors entering classified areas at Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar), Unit II – Baddi and Unit III – Vizag SEZ. Oral solid and oral liquid processing areas are Grade D. The Unit II ointment filling room and the microbiology testing area are Grade C.

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All personnel | Follow the gowning sequence; report illness or open wounds before entry |
| Area Supervisor | Ensures only gowning-qualified persons enter; checks gown condition |
| Training Coordinator | Conducts initial and annual gowning qualification |
| Microbiology (QC) | Performs finger-dab and gown-contact plate monitoring |
| Site QA | Approves gowning qualification and investigates excursions |

## 4. Definitions

- **Grade D**: classified area with an at-rest limit of 3,520,000 particles ≥0.5 µm per m³; temperature 20–25°C and RH 45–60%.
- **Grade C**: classified area with an at-rest limit of 352,000 particles ≥0.5 µm per m³ and an in-operation limit of 3,520,000 particles ≥0.5 µm per m³.
- **Gowning qualification**: a documented demonstration that a person can gown correctly, verified by microbiological contact plates.
- **Step-over bench**: the physical boundary in the change room separating the "unclean" and "clean" sides.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Before entry

1. Remove watches, jewellery, mobile phones, wallets and cosmetics. Make-up, nail polish and artificial nails are not permitted.
2. Personnel with fever, cough, diarrhoea, skin infection or open wounds must inform the supervisor and must not enter.
3. Wash hands with liquid soap for at least 30 seconds and dry with the hand dryer.

### 5.2 Grade D gowning sequence (Primary change room)

1. Remove outer footwear on the unclean side; place in the shoe rack.
2. Sit on the step-over bench and swing legs over to the clean side; wear dedicated area footwear.
3. Wear the hair cover (bouffant cap) covering all hair, ears included. Beard covers are mandatory for facial hair.
4. Wear the two-piece Grade D garment (top and trouser), colour-coded **light blue** for Unit I and Unit II, **green** for Unit III.
5. Wear a face mask.
6. Sanitise hands with 70% IPA and allow to dry.
7. Check the full-length mirror and enter through the airlock. Both airlock doors must never be open at the same time (interlock).

### 5.3 Additional steps for Grade C (Secondary change room)

1. Sanitise hands with 70% IPA.
2. Wear a sterile hood, then a sterile one-piece coverall without letting it touch the floor.
3. Wear sterile overshoes over the Grade D footwear.
4. Wear sterile powder-free nitrile gloves; sanitise gloved hands with 70% IPA.
5. Wear safety goggles.

### 5.4 Change frequency

| Item | Grade D | Grade C |
|---|---|---|
| Garment | Once per shift or when soiled | Each entry |
| Mask | Every 4 hours | Each entry |
| Gloves | When torn or soiled | Every 2 hours and at each re-entry |
| Laundry | In-house validated cycle | Laundered and sterilised (validated cycle) |

### 5.5 De-gowning

Reverse the sequence on exit. Place used garments in the marked "USED" bin. Disposable masks and gloves go into the yellow bio-waste bin.

### 5.6 Gowning qualification

1. New employees complete classroom training and 3 consecutive successful gowning demonstrations before first entry to Grade C.
2. Contact plates (RODAC, 55 mm) are taken from 5 locations: both gloved hands (finger dab), forearms, chest and hood.
3. Acceptance for Grade C qualification: finger dab ≤ 5 CFU/glove; gown contact plates ≤ 5 CFU/plate.
4. Requalification is annual. A person who fails twice is retrained and may not enter Grade C until requalified.

### 5.7 Visitors

Visitors enter Grade D only, accompanied by a qualified employee, after signing the visitor health declaration. Visitors may not enter Grade C.
