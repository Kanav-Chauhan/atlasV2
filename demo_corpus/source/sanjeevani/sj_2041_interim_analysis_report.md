# SJ-2041 Interim Analysis Report

## 1. Purpose

This report summarises the pre-planned interim analysis of the SJ-2041 Phase III trial (protocol SPL/CLN/PRT-017, CTRI/2025/03/061842) and the recommendation of the independent Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB). It is for internal decision-making by the Clinical team and Leadership. Sponsor personnel involved in trial conduct remain blinded at patient level; this report presents aggregate results released by the DSMB with Leadership authorisation.

## 2. Interim Analysis Details

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Data cut-off | 15 February 2026 |
| Patients randomised at cut-off | 1,180 of 1,240 planned (95%) |
| Interim efficacy population | 412 patients who had completed week 24 or discontinued (206 per arm) |
| Independent statistician | External statistics group (unblinded), reporting only to the DSMB |
| DSMB closed session | 20 March 2026 |
| DSMB recommendation | **Continue the trial without modification** |
| Futility boundary | Conditional power < 20% – not crossed (conditional power 96%) |

## 4. Efficacy Results at Week 24

| Endpoint | SJ-2041 | Comparator (metformin SR 1,000 mg) | Difference (95% CI) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HbA1c change from baseline (primary) | **−1.1%** | **−0.6%** | −0.5% (−0.68 to −0.32) | < 0.001 |
| Patients reaching HbA1c < 7.0% | 41.7% | 22.3% | 19.4 percentage points | < 0.001 |
| FPG change (mg/dL) | −31 | −14 | −17 (−23 to −11) | < 0.001 |
| 2-h PPG change (mg/dL) | −52 | −24 | −28 (−36 to −20) | < 0.001 |
| Body weight change (kg) | −0.4 | −0.3 | −0.1 (−0.6 to 0.4) | 0.71 |
| Rescue therapy required | 3.9% | 9.2% | — | — |

The primary endpoint result at interim: HbA1c change from baseline of −1.1% with SJ-2041 versus −0.6% with comparator. The interim p-value is below the Haybittle-Peto boundary of 0.001, but per protocol the trial does not stop for efficacy; the final analysis will be performed at full sample size.

## 5. Patient Disposition (all randomised at cut-off)

| Disposition | SJ-2041 | Comparator | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Randomised | 590 | 590 | 1,180 |
| Discontinued | 40 | 47 | 87 |
| Dropout rate | 6.8% | 8.0% | **7.4%** |

Reasons for discontinuation (total 87): withdrawal of consent 33, lost to follow-up 26, adverse event 15, protocol deviation 8, lack of efficacy 5.

## 6. Safety Results (all randomised and treated at cut-off, n = 1,180)

| Safety measure | SJ-2041 (n = 590) | Comparator (n = 590) |
|---|---|---|
| Any adverse event | 31.8% | 29.0% |
| Gastrointestinal AEs (nausea, diarrhoea) | 9.2% | 10.4% |
| Nasopharyngitis | 4.4% | 3.9% |
| Hypoglycaemia (glucose < 70 mg/dL) | 2.1% | 1.6% |
| Severe hypoglycaemia | 0 | 0 |
| AEs leading to discontinuation | 1.2% | 1.4% |
| Serious adverse events | 1 | 1 |
| Deaths | 0 | 0 |
| Pancreatitis / lactic acidosis | 0 | 0 |

**Serious adverse events:** 2 serious adverse events occurred, one in each arm, and both were **judged unrelated** to study drug by the investigator and the DSMB:

1. Road traffic accident with femur fracture (SJ-2041 arm, site S17), hospitalised, recovered.
2. Acute appendicitis requiring appendectomy (comparator arm, site S22), recovered.

Both were reported to CDSCO, the Ethics Committees and the sponsor within 24 hours per SPL/CLN/SOP-005. No liver enzyme elevation > 3 × ULN was recorded in either arm.

## 7. DSMB Comments

- Safety profile consistent with the DPP-4 inhibitor class and metformin.
- Recommend continued monitoring of hypoglycaemia in patients aged > 65 years (n = 79 at cut-off).
- No change in sample size required.

## 8. Implications and Next Steps

1. Trial continues to full enrolment of 1,240 patients; the remaining 60 patients are expected to be randomised by mid-March 2026.
2. Topline readout expected January 2027, after database lock planned for November 2026.
3. Leadership authorised preparatory CMC work for commercial scale-up at Unit I (no capital commitment until topline).
4. Results must not be disclosed outside the Clinical team and Leadership. Interim results are not to be used in any promotional or investor communication.
