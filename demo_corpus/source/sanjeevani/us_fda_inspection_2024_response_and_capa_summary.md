# US FDA Inspection 2024 – Response and CAPA Summary

## 1. Inspection Overview

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Site | Unit III – Vizag (Visakhapatnam) SEZ, export oral solids unit |
| Inspection type | Routine surveillance (cGMP) and pre-approval for one pending ANDA |
| Dates | 11–19 March 2024 (7 inspection days) |
| Investigators | 2 US FDA investigators, 1 chemist |
| Products reviewed | Rosuvastatin 10 mg tablets (export equivalent of Cardivas 10), Amlodipine 5 mg tablets, Pantoprazole 40 mg delayed-release tablets |
| Outcome at close-out | Form FDA 483 issued with **3 observations** |
| Response submitted | 05 April 2024 (within 15 business days) |
| Final classification | Voluntary Action Indicated (VAI) |
| CAPA status | All CAPAs closed by October 2024 (last closure 25 October 2024) |

## 2. Summary of Observations

| Obs. No. | Area | Observation (summary) | Sanjeevani risk rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data integrity | Audit trails of the chromatography data system (CDS) for HPLC assay and dissolution were not reviewed as part of batch release; 14 instances of re-integration in 2023 had no documented justification | High |
| 2 | Cleaning validation | Cleaning validation for shared equipment (bin blender BB-03 and compression machine CM-05) did not include worst-case product rationale; swab recovery studies were absent for two APIs | High |
| 3 | Laboratory controls | Out of Specification investigations were not completed in a timely manner; 6 of 22 OOS investigations in 2023 took more than 60 days, the longest 118 days | Medium |

## 3. Responses and CAPA

### 3.1 Observation 1 – audit trail review

**Root cause:** The QC review SOP required review of results only; audit trail review was not defined, and the CDS audit trail report was not configured for easy review.

| CAPA No. | Action | Owner | Target | Closed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CAPA/U3/24/011 | Configure CDS audit trail review report filtered for re-integration, deletion and method changes | QC Manager, Unit III | 30 April 2024 | 26 April 2024 |
| CAPA/U3/24/012 | Revise QC data review SOP to make audit trail review mandatory before batch release | Site QA | 15 May 2024 | 10 May 2024 |
| CAPA/U3/24/013 | Retrospective review of all CDS audit trails for 3 years for batches in US market (412 batches); no result changed batch disposition | Corporate QA | 31 August 2024 | 22 August 2024 |
| CAPA/U3/24/014 | Data Integrity Policy – ALCOA+ revised; training of 186 QC and QA staff | Head QA (Corporate) | 30 June 2024 | 28 June 2024 |

### 3.2 Observation 2 – cleaning validation for shared equipment

**Root cause:** The cleaning validation master plan selected the worst-case product only on solubility; potency (PDE) and cleanability were not considered.

| CAPA No. | Action | Owner | Target | Closed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CAPA/U3/24/015 | New worst-case matrix based on PDE, solubility and cleanability for all 11 products on shared equipment | Validation Head | 31 May 2024 | 29 May 2024 |
| CAPA/U3/24/016 | Swab recovery studies (stainless steel coupons, ≥ 70% recovery) for all APIs | QC Manager | 31 July 2024 | 19 July 2024 |
| CAPA/U3/24/017 | Re-validation of cleaning for BB-03 and CM-05: 3 consecutive runs, all results below MACO limit | Production Head | 30 September 2024 | 27 September 2024 |

### 3.3 Observation 3 – OOS investigation timeliness

**Root cause:** No time limit for OOS closure in the OOS SOP; Phase II investigations waited for Production inputs without escalation.

| CAPA No. | Action | Owner | Target | Closed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CAPA/U3/24/018 | Revise OOS SOP (SPL/QC/SOP-021 v5.0): 30-calendar-day closure limit, Head QA approval for extension | Head QA (Corporate) | 15 November 2024 (effective date) | Approved 25 October 2024 |
| CAPA/U3/24/019 | Weekly OOS ageing report to Plant Head | QC Manager | 31 May 2024 | 24 May 2024 |

## 4. Effectiveness Checks

Effectiveness was verified at 90 days after implementation of each CAPA:

| Observation | Effectiveness criterion | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100% of release batches have audit trail review signed; zero unjustified re-integrations in 3 months | Met (Jan 2025 check: 168 batches, 0 findings) |
| 2 | All shared-equipment cleaning results below MACO for 3 months | Met |
| 3 | ≥ 95% of OOS closed within 30 days | Met (Jan–Mar 2025: 19 of 19 closed within 30 days) |

## 5. Commitments to the Agency

1. Quarterly CAPA status updates were sent on 28 June 2024 and 30 September 2024; the final update on 31 October 2024 confirmed closure.
2. The data integrity remediation was extended to Unit I and Unit II by 31 March 2025.
3. The final update of 31 October 2024 informed the agency that Unit III is ready for re-inspection at any time.

## 6. Lessons Learned

- Audit trail review must be part of routine data review, not a periodic exercise.
- Worst-case selection must be science- and risk-based (ICH Q9).
- Time limits drive behaviour: every investigation SOP now has a closure limit and escalation path.
- Corporate QA now runs a mock FDA audit of Unit III every 6 months.
