# SOP – Out of Specification Investigation

## 1. Purpose

To describe how an Out of Specification (OOS) test result obtained in a Sanjeevani QC laboratory is reported, investigated and concluded in a scientifically sound and timely manner, so that no result is invalidated without an assignable cause and batch disposition decisions are justified.

## 2. Scope

Applies to QC laboratories at Unit I – Ahmedabad (Changodar), Unit II – Baddi and Unit III – Vizag SEZ, for raw materials, packaging materials, in-process, finished product and stability samples. It also applies to Out of Trend (OOT) stability results, which follow Phase I of this SOP. Microbiology OOS results for purified water follow Section 5.6 together with SPL/QC/SOP-033.

## 3. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Analyst | Stops testing, preserves all solutions and informs the QC supervisor within 1 hour of observing the OOS result |
| QC Supervisor | Performs the Phase I laboratory investigation with the analyst |
| QC Manager | Approves Phase I conclusion; decides on Phase II |
| Site QA | Leads Phase II full-scale investigation with Production |
| Head, Quality Assurance (Corporate) | Approves any invalidation of an OOS result in Phase II and all OOS closures for export batches |

## 4. Definitions

- **OOS result**: a test result that falls outside the specification or acceptance criteria in the approved specification, e.g. Glucosan-M 500 SR assay outside 95.0–105.0%.
- **OOT result**: a stability result within specification but outside the expected trend (outside 3 standard deviations of the regression line).
- **Assignable cause**: a documented, demonstrable laboratory error (e.g. wrong dilution, instrument malfunction confirmed by calibration check).
- **Retest**: re-analysis of the original sample preparation or a new aliquot from the same sample, performed only in Phase II under an approved retest protocol.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Reporting

1. The analyst records the result in the LIMS; the LIMS automatically flags the OOS and generates an OOS number (OOS/<Unit>/<YY>/<NNN>).
2. Solutions, standards, glassware and the instrument must not be disturbed until the supervisor has reviewed them.

### 5.2 Phase I – laboratory investigation (target: 5 working days)

The QC Supervisor and analyst complete checklist SPL/QC/F-021-01:

| Check | Example |
|---|---|
| Calculation | Re-check all calculations and dilution factors |
| Method | Correct version of method used; system suitability passed |
| Instrument | Calibration status; audit trail of the chromatography data system reviewed for the sequence |
| Standards and reagents | Within expiry; correctly prepared |
| Sample | Correct sample, container and sample preparation |
| Analyst | Trained and qualified on the method |

If an assignable laboratory cause is clearly identified, the result is invalidated with QC Manager approval and the test repeated by a different analyst. If not, the investigation moves to Phase II.

### 5.3 Phase II – full-scale investigation (target: 20 working days from OOS)

1. Site QA opens a linked deviation under SPL/QA/SOP-004 and the batch remains on hold.
2. Production reviews the BMR, equipment logs and raw material lots.
3. A retest protocol is approved before any retest. Retesting is done by a second analyst in **triplicate**. The number of retests is fixed in the protocol and must not be extended to "test into compliance".
4. Resampling is allowed only if the original sample is shown to be non-representative.
5. Averaging of an OOS result with passing results to obtain a passing value is not permitted.

### 5.4 Timeliness

All OOS investigations must be closed within **30 calendar days** of the OOS result. An extension requires written approval from Head QA (Corporate) with an interim report. This requirement was strengthened in version 5.0 following the 2024 US FDA inspection of Unit III, where OOS investigation timeliness was a Form 483 observation.

### 5.5 Conclusion and batch disposition

| Outcome | Batch disposition |
|---|---|
| Assignable laboratory error confirmed | Valid repeat result reported; batch can be released if passing |
| Manufacturing cause confirmed | Batch rejected or reprocessed under approved protocol |
| No cause identified, retests pass | Batch disposition decided by Head QA (Corporate); OOS is not ignored and is included in the APQR |
| No cause identified, retests fail | Batch rejected |

### 5.6 Microbiology OOS

For purified water results above the action level (100 CFU/mL), identify the organism to genus level, sanitise the loop and sample the affected points for 3 consecutive days before resuming use.

## 6. Records

- OOS report (LIMS) with Phase I checklist and Phase II report – retained life of product + 5 years.
- Retest protocols and raw data – retained with the OOS report.
- Monthly OOS trend (number of OOS, % invalidated, % closed within 30 days) – presented at the Quality Review Meeting. Target: at least 95% closed within 30 days.
