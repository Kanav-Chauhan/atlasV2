# Capital Goods and Railways Capex Theme

*Theme paper submitted to the Investment Committee for IC Meeting 47 (12 March 2026). Version 1.1 updated after IC approval and the Konkan Rail Systems initiation.*

## 1. Recommendation

We recommend that the Investment Committee adopt **"Indian Railways capex"** as a formal investment theme across Kaveri equity portfolios, with an emphasis on signalling, rolling stock and electrification suppliers. The theme was **approved at IC 47 on 12 March 2026** with the allocation ranges set out in section 7.

## 2. Why now

1. **Sustained spending.** Railway capital expenditure has risen from about ₹1.4 lakh crore in FY21 to a budget estimate of ₹2.65 lakh crore in FY27. We expect spending to stay above ₹2.5 lakh crore a year through FY30.
2. **Mix shift to higher-value work.** Spending is moving from track laying (civil, low margin) to signalling, safety systems, rolling stock and station redevelopment, which favours listed equipment suppliers.
3. **Safety mandate.** Rollout of automatic train protection across 34,000 route-km by FY30, from about 6,500 route-km today, is a multi-year, non-discretionary programme.
4. **Order books at record levels.** Our six theme companies have a combined order book of ₹38,600 crore, equal to 3.9x trailing revenue.

## 5. Valuation framework

Capital goods stocks have re-rated sharply since FY22: the sector trades at 34x one-year forward earnings against a 10-year average of 26x. We are therefore **selective**. We prefer companies where:

- order book covers at least 3 years of revenue;
- working capital is below 25% of sales (railway payments are now typically received in 60–75 days);
- return on capital employed is above 18%;
- the stock trades at a PEG ratio below 1.2x.

Konkan Rail Systems and Sethu Signalling meet all four filters. Trishul Heavy Engineering misses on valuation but is included for its liquidity and diversified order book.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigant |
|---|---|
| Budget cuts or slower execution after 2029 elections | Safety programmes are statutory and multi-year |
| Aggressive bidding compressing margins | Prefer technology-led suppliers (signalling) over civil contractors |
| Working-capital stretch from delayed payments | Track receivable days quarterly; exit trigger at 120 days |
| Valuation de-rating | Position sizing caps; staggered entry |
| Liquidity in small and mid caps | Respect the Small Cap fund's 5-day liquidity rule |

## 8. Monitoring

- Research will publish a quarterly theme tracker covering order inflows, tender awards and receivable days.
- The theme will be reviewed by the IC every six months; the first review is scheduled for IC 53 in September 2026.
- Exit triggers: railway capex budget cut of more than 15%; theme stocks' average order book falling below 2.5x revenue; or sector P/E above 40x one-year forward.
