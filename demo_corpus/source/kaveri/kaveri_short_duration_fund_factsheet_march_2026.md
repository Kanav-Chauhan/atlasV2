## 8. Fund manager commentary

The RBI reduced the repo rate by a cumulative 75 basis points during FY2025-26 to 5.50%, and maintained surplus system liquidity through the final quarter. Short-term yields fell faster than long-term yields, and the 3-year AAA corporate bond yield ended the year near 6.95%.

The fund returned 7.6% over the year against 7.4% for its benchmark. We held portfolio duration near the upper end of the range, at around 2.5 years, through most of the year, which added to returns as yields fell. We increased sovereign holdings from 24% to 32.5% to improve liquidity and reduce spread risk.

With the rate cycle close to its low point, we expect returns over the next year to be driven mainly by accrual. We plan to keep Macaulay duration between 2.2 and 2.6 years and maintain a high-quality portfolio with over 87% in sovereign and AAA instruments.
