## 2. Investment philosophy

"Patient capital, clear thinking." The strategy owns a concentrated portfolio of 20–25 businesses that we believe can compound earnings at 15% or more over five years, bought at reasonable valuations. Three filters are applied to every stock:

1. **Quality of business** – return on capital employed above 15% through a cycle; low or no debt; free-cash-flow positive.
2. **Quality of management** – clean governance, sensible capital allocation, no history of related-party abuse.
3. **Valuation discipline** – entry only when expected 5-year IRR exceeds 15%.

The portfolio is benchmark-aware but not benchmark-hugging: active share is typically above 75%.

## 6. Fee options

Clients choose one fee structure at onboarding. The structure may be changed once a year, on the account anniversary.

| Option | Management fee | Performance fee |
|---|---|---|
| Option A – Fixed | 2.0% per annum | None |
| Option B – Hybrid | 1.0% per annum | 20% of returns above a 10% hurdle, subject to a high-water mark |

- Fees are charged quarterly on daily average AUM; the performance fee under Option B is charged annually on the account anniversary.
- GST applies at the prevailing rate.
- Exit loads and other charges are as set out in the PMS Client Agreement and the Disclosure Document.

**Illustration (Option B):** A client invests ₹1 crore. In year 1 the portfolio grows to ₹1.18 crore before performance fees (an 18% return). The return above the 10% hurdle is 8%, i.e. ₹8 lakh, so the performance fee is 20% of ₹8 lakh = ₹1.6 lakh, plus GST. The high-water mark is reset to the post-fee value.

## 7. Onboarding process

1. Receipt of Disclosure Document and PMS Client Agreement; risk-profiling questionnaire.
2. KYC through CKYC lookup and PAN verification (in-person or video KYC).
3. Account opening with the custodian and a dedicated demat account in the client's name.
4. Funding by cheque or bank transfer from the client's own bank account (cash is not accepted) or transfer of eligible securities.
5. Portfolio deployment typically within 15 working days.

Clients receive a monthly statement, a quarterly portfolio review with the relationship manager, and an annual audited performance certificate.

## 8. Why clients choose the strategy

- A CIO-managed portfolio with a 10-year track record.
- Concentrated, high-conviction approach supported by a 34-analyst research team covering 142 stocks.
- Securities held directly in the client's own demat account, giving full transparency.
- Choice of fixed or performance-linked fees.
