# KYC and Customer Due Diligence Policy

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This Policy sets out how Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. ("Kaveri" or "the Company") identifies, verifies and periodically reviews every client before and during the business relationship, across its mutual-fund-style schemes, its Portfolio Management Service (PMS) and the Kaveri Long-Short AIF III.

1.2 It gives effect to the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA), the PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005, and the SEBI Master Circular on AML/CFT obligations of intermediaries, as applicable to Kaveri.

1.3 This version (4.0) replaces the KYC Policy 2022 (KCA/CMP/POL-010, version 2.1), which is withdrawn with effect from 01 April 2026. The main change is a shorter periodic re-KYC cycle for high-risk and low-risk clients (see clause 7).

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Policy applies to all clients onboarded directly, through distributors, through the Kaveri website and app, and through institutional and family-office mandates.

2.2 It applies to all employees of Operations, Client Services, Sales and Compliance at the Bengaluru head office and the Mumbai (BKC), New Delhi (Connaught Place), Chennai and Pune branches.

## 4. Customer Identification

4.1 **CKYC lookup first.** For every new individual or non-individual client, the KYC Desk shall first search the Central KYC Records Registry (CKYC) using the client's PAN. Where a CKYC record exists, the record shall be downloaded and used, and only missing or outdated fields collected from the client.

4.2 **PAN verification.** The PAN of every client, every joint holder, every guardian and every authorised signatory shall be verified online against the Income Tax Department database. The PAN status must be "Valid" and the PAN must be linked with Aadhaar where the law requires it. Accounts with an inoperative PAN shall not be activated.

4.3 **Officially Valid Documents.** In addition to PAN, individuals shall provide one Officially Valid Document (OVD) for proof of address: passport, driving licence, voter ID, or masked Aadhaar (with the first 8 digits redacted).

4.4 **In-person or video verification.** In-Person Verification (IPV) is mandatory. Video-based IPV through the Kaveri app is permitted provided the session is recorded, the client's live location is captured within India, and a random question is asked.

4.5 **Non-individual clients.** Companies, LLPs, trusts and family offices shall provide incorporation documents, board resolution or authority letter, list of authorised signatories, and the **beneficial ownership** declaration. A beneficial owner is any natural person holding more than 10% ownership or control in a company, or more than 15% in a partnership or unincorporated body.

## 5. Customer Due Diligence and Risk Categorisation

5.1 Each client shall be scored at onboarding on the KYC Desk risk matrix and placed in one of three categories.

| Risk category | Typical profile | Approval level |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Salaried resident individuals, SIP investors, investment below ₹10 lakh | KYC Desk officer |
| Medium | Self-employed individuals, HUFs, PMS clients, NRIs from FATF-compliant jurisdictions | KYC Desk supervisor |
| High | PEPs, trusts and charities, clients from high-risk jurisdictions, complex ownership chains, AIF investors with offshore structures | Chief Compliance Officer |

5.2 **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD).** High-risk clients require EDD: a documented source-of-wealth and source-of-funds review, adverse-media screening, and senior approval as above. PEP onboarding follows the Politically Exposed Persons Procedure (KCA/CMP/SOP-014) and always needs CCO approval.

5.3 **Sanctions screening.** Every client and beneficial owner shall be screened against the UN Security Council consolidated list and the lists notified under Section 51A of the UAPA before activation, and daily thereafter through the screening tool. A true match shall freeze the account immediately and be reported to the CCO the same day.

## 6. Payment Rules

6.1 **Cash is not accepted** for any subscription, PMS contribution or AIF drawdown, in any amount. Branch staff who are offered cash shall decline and record the attempt in the KYC exceptions register.

6.2 Subscriptions shall come only from a bank account held in the name of the investor (first holder). Third-party payments are refused except for the exemptions permitted for minors (parent/guardian) and employer payroll SIPs, each with a declaration.

## 7. Periodic Updation (Re-KYC)

7.1 Kaveri shall refresh the KYC of existing clients on the following cycle, measured from the date of the last completed KYC:

| Risk category | Re-KYC frequency |
|---|---|
| High | Every 2 years |
| Medium | Every 5 years |
| Low | Every 8 years |

7.2 Re-KYC shall also be triggered immediately on: a change in the client's risk category; a sanctions or adverse-media alert; an STR being filed; or a change in beneficial ownership of a non-individual client.

7.3 Clients shall be notified 60 days before the re-KYC due date and reminded at 30 and 7 days. If re-KYC is not completed within 90 days after the due date, the folio or PMS account shall be marked "KYC on hold": redemptions continue to be paid to the registered bank account, but fresh subscriptions and SIP instalments are blocked.

## 8. Ongoing Monitoring

8.1 Transactions shall be monitored against the client's profile using the alert rules in the Anti-Money Laundering Policy (KCA/CMP/POL-012). Alerts not explained within 15 working days shall be escalated to the CCO.

8.2 Where suspicion is established, the matter shall follow the Suspicious Transaction Reporting SOP (KCA/CMP/SOP-013), under which the STR is filed with FIU-IND within 7 days of suspicion being established.

## 9. Record Keeping

9.1 KYC records, including the CKYC download, PAN verification output, IPV recording and risk score, shall be kept for at least **5 years after the business relationship ends** or the account is closed, whichever is later, or longer if an investigation is pending.

## 10. Exceptions and Breaches

10.1 No exception to clauses 4, 6 or 7 may be granted by anyone other than the CCO, and every exception shall be recorded with a reason and an expiry date not exceeding 30 days.

10.2 Processing a transaction on incomplete KYC is a reportable breach. The employee's manager shall report it to the CCO within 1 working day, and the breach shall be tabled at the next quarterly Compliance Committee.

## 11. Review

11.1 This Policy shall be reviewed annually by the CCO, or earlier on any change in law or SEBI circulars, and approved by the MD & CEO.
