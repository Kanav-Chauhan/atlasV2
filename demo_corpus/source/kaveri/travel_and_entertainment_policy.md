# Travel and Entertainment Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for business travel, accommodation, client entertainment and the reimbursement of related expenses. The aim is to let employees travel safely and comfortably while keeping costs reasonable and transparent. Travel and entertainment is budgeted at ₹6.8 crore for FY2026-27.

## 2. Scope

All employees of Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. travelling on Company business from any of our offices (Bengaluru, Mumbai, New Delhi, Chennai, Pune).

## 3. Employee grades

| Grade | Designations |
|---|---|
| G1 | MD & CEO, CXOs, CIO |
| G2 | Fund managers, function heads, senior vice presidents |
| G3 | Vice presidents, senior analysts, relationship managers |
| G4 | Analysts, associates, executives |

## 4. Approval

4.1 All travel must be approved in advance in the travel portal by the reporting manager.

4.2 International travel needs additional approval from the CFO; for G1 travel, from the MD & CEO.

4.3 Bookings are made through the Finance travel desk. Self-booked travel is reimbursed only if the fare is no higher than the travel desk quote.

4.4 Domestic flights must be booked at least **7 days in advance** unless the trip is urgent and the manager confirms this in writing.

## 5. Mode and class of travel

| Grade | Air (domestic) | Air (international) | Rail |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Business | Business | AC First Class |
| G2 | Economy (Business for flights over 3 hours) | Business for flights over 6 hours | AC First Class |
| G3 | Economy | Economy | AC 2-tier |
| G4 | Economy | Economy | AC 2-tier |

Local travel: app-based cabs are reimbursed at actuals. Personal car use is reimbursed at ₹12 per km.

## 6. Hotel limits (per night, excluding taxes)

| Grade | Mumbai / New Delhi | Bengaluru / Chennai / Pune | Other cities |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | At actuals | At actuals | At actuals |
| G2 | ₹12,000 | ₹9,000 | ₹7,000 |
| G3 | ₹8,500 | ₹7,000 | ₹5,500 |
| G4 | ₹6,000 | ₹5,000 | ₹4,000 |

Employees should use the Company's empanelled hotels where available. Staying with friends or family attracts a flat allowance of ₹1,500 per night.

## 7. Daily allowance

| Grade | Domestic (per day) | International (per day) |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | At actuals | USD 120 |
| G2 | ₹2,500 | USD 90 |
| G3 | ₹1,800 | USD 75 |
| G4 | ₹1,200 | USD 60 |

The daily allowance covers meals, tips and incidental expenses. It is not paid when meals are provided by the host or included in a conference fee.

## 8. Client entertainment

8.1 Entertainment of clients, distributors and prospects is permitted where there is a clear business purpose.

8.2 Limits per guest per occasion:

| Type | Limit per guest |
|---|---|
| Business meal | ₹4,000 |
| Distributor meet / seminar (per attendee) | ₹2,500 |
| Sporting or cultural event | ₹10,000 (pre-approval by function head) |

8.3 Entertainment of government officials or regulator staff is not permitted.

8.4 Entertainment must also comply with the gifts and hospitality limits in the Code of Conduct and Ethics, and must be entered in the gift and hospitality register by Compliance.

8.5 Alcohol may be included within the meal limit but must not exceed 30% of the bill.

## 9. Expenses not reimbursed

- Fines (traffic, overstay, late check-out not approved in advance)
- Personal entertainment, minibar, laundry for trips under 4 nights
- Upgrades paid by the employee for personal convenience
- Spa, gym or leisure charges
- Travel insurance purchased individually (the Company holds a group policy)

## 10. Claims and settlement

10.1 Claims must be submitted in the expense portal within **15 days** of return, with original bills or GST invoices for every item above ₹500.

10.2 Claims submitted after **60 days** will not be reimbursed.

10.3 Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run if approved by the 20th of the month.

10.4 Travel advances, where needed, are capped at ₹25,000 for domestic travel and must be settled within 15 days of return.

## 11. Corporate card

G1 and G2 employees, and relationship managers at G3, are issued a corporate card. Personal expenses on the corporate card must be repaid within 7 days. Cash withdrawals on the corporate card are not permitted.

## 12. Audit

Finance audits 10% of claims every month. Claims found to be false or inflated are treated as misconduct under the Code of Conduct and Ethics.
