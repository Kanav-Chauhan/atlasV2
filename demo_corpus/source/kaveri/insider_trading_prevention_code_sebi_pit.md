# Insider Trading Prevention Code – SEBI PIT

## 1. Introduction

1.1 Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. is a fiduciary and intermediary that, through its research and fund management activities, regularly receives information about listed companies. This Code, adopted under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 ("PIT Regulations"), sets out the minimum standards Kaveri applies to prevent the misuse of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI).

1.2 This Code governs the handling of UPSI. The mechanics of employees' own dealing (pre-clearance, holding period and restricted list) are in the Personal Trading Policy in the Company Handbook; where the two documents overlap, the stricter rule applies.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 **UPSI** means information about a listed company or its securities that is not generally available and that, on becoming available, is likely to materially affect the price, including: financial results, dividends, changes in capital structure, mergers, demergers, acquisitions, delistings, disposals, and changes in key managerial personnel.

2.2 **Designated Persons** means: all directors; all employees in Investment, Research, Dealing, Risk, Compliance and Operations; the MD & CEO's office; and any other employee named by the Compliance Officer. As of 1 April 2026, Kaveri has 168 Designated Persons.

2.3 **Immediate Relatives** means the spouse, and parents, siblings and children of the Designated Person or spouse who are financially dependent on, or consult, the Designated Person in trading decisions.

2.4 **Compliance Officer** for this Code is the Chief Compliance Officer, Shalini Kapoor.

## 3. Prohibitions

3.1 No Designated Person or Immediate Relative shall trade in a security while in possession of UPSI about that security.

3.2 No person shall communicate UPSI to anyone except for a legitimate purpose, in the performance of duties, or in compliance with law.

3.3 No person shall procure UPSI from a company insider other than through a sanctioned wall-crossing under clause 5.

## 4. Information Barriers (Chinese Walls)

4.1 Kaveri maintains the following information barriers:

| Wall | Separates | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Research / Sales | Research analysts from Sales and Distribution | Separate floors in Bengaluru; no access to research drafts before publication |
| Investment / Advisory | Fund managers from the Institutional Advisory team | Separate shared drives; access approved by Compliance |
| Dealing room | Dealers from all other staff | Swipe-card access; recorded phone lines retained for 5 years |

4.2 Breaching a wall requires a wall-crossing approval under clause 5.

## 5. Wall-Crossing and the Structured Digital Database

5.1 Where an employee needs to receive UPSI for a legitimate purpose (for example, an issuer approaches Kaveri as a potential anchor investor in a block deal or QIP), the employee shall obtain prior approval from the Compliance Officer through the wall-crossing form.

5.2 On approval:

- the security is added to the **restricted list** immediately, and no Kaveri employee or scheme may trade in it until the information is public or stale;
- the names, PANs and date and time of receipt of every person who receives the UPSI are recorded in the **Structured Digital Database (SDD)**;
- the SDD entry cannot be edited or deleted; it carries an automatic time-stamp and audit trail.

5.3 SDD records are retained for at least 8 years.

5.4 The Compliance Officer shall remove the security from the restricted list only after the UPSI becomes generally available, and in no case earlier than **48 hours** after public disclosure.

## 6. Trading by Designated Persons

6.1 Designated Persons shall obtain pre-clearance before trading in any listed security, follow the 30-day minimum holding period, and shall not trade in any security on the restricted list, as set out in the Personal Trading Policy.

6.2 **Contra trades.** A Designated Person who sells or buys back within 30 days in breach of the holding rule shall have any profit disgorged and remitted to the SEBI Investor Protection and Education Fund.

6.3 **Disclosures.**

| Disclosure | By whom | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Initial holdings | New Designated Person | Within 7 days of becoming a Designated Person |
| Annual holdings | All Designated Persons | By 30 April each year, as of 31 March |
| Continual (trades above ₹10 lakh in a calendar quarter) | Designated Person | Within 2 trading days of the transaction |
| Names of Immediate Relatives and persons with material financial relationship | Designated Person | Annually by 30 April and within 7 days of any change |

## 7. Reporting of Breaches

7.1 Any breach or suspected breach of this Code, including a leak of UPSI, shall be reported to the Compliance Officer **within 1 working day** of the employee becoming aware of it.

7.2 The Compliance Officer shall investigate and present findings to the MD & CEO within 15 working days. Breaches by Designated Persons shall be reported to the Board at its next meeting and, where required, to SEBI.

7.3 Sanctions include warning, freeze on personal trading for up to 12 months, recovery of profits, suspension, termination and referral to authorities.

## 8. Leak of UPSI Inquiry

8.1 On a suspected leak, the Compliance Officer shall constitute an inquiry committee of the CCO, CRO (Varun Pillai) and Head of HR (Deepa Nambiar), which shall complete its inquiry within 30 days.

## 9. Training and Certification

9.1 All Designated Persons shall complete PIT training within 30 days of designation and annually thereafter, and shall certify compliance with this Code by 30 April each year.

## 10. Review

10.1 The Board shall review this Code at least once a year. The Audit Committee shall review the Compliance Officer's report on its operation every quarter.
