# FY2026-27 Budget

Annual budget for 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2027, approved by the Board of Directors on 25 April 2026. Version 2.0 reflects the Board's changes to the March draft (technology spend raised from ₹13.2 crore to ₹14 crore). All amounts in ₹ crore unless stated.

## 1. Headline Numbers

| Metric | FY2025-26 Actual | FY2026-27 Budget | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total revenue | 212.0 | 248.0 | +17.0% |
| Employee cost | 84.6 | 96.0 | +13.5% |
| Technology spend | 11.8 | 14.0 | +18.6% |
| Total operating expenses | 129.3 | 148.8 | +15.1% |
| Cost-to-income ratio | 61.0% | 60.0% | −1.0 pt |
| Profit after tax | 58.0 | 69.7 | +20.2% |
| Closing AUM | 18,650 | 21,900 | +17.4% |
| Headcount (31 March) | 310 | 338 | +28 |

## 2. Key Assumptions

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Equity market return (Nifty 50) | 9% for the year |
| Net inflows – mutual-fund-style schemes | ₹1,450 crore |
| Net inflows – PMS | ₹180 crore |
| Net inflows – AIF | ₹60 crore |
| Average fee yield on MF-style AUM | 0.89% (FY26: 0.90%) |
| PMS clients on Option B (hybrid fee) | 38% of PMS AUM |
| Salary increments (average) | 9.5% |
| Inflation on non-staff costs | 5% |
| GIFT City feeder fund | Launch January 2027; no material revenue in FY27 |

2.1 The budget does not assume any market fall. The March 2026 stress test estimated that a 20% equity fall would reduce annualised fee income by about ₹38 crore; a downside case is being prepared for the July 2026 Board Risk Committee.

## 8. Budget Control

8.1 Department heads may reallocate up to 10% between their own expense lines with CFO approval. Any overspend above 5% of a department's annual budget requires MD & CEO approval. The CFO reports actual vs budget to the Board each quarter.

8.2 New hires beyond the 28 approved roles require MD & CEO approval and a matching saving or revenue case.
