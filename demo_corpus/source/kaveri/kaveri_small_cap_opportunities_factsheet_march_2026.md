## 7. Fund manager commentary

FY2025-26 was a strong year for small caps, with the Nifty Smallcap 250 TRI returning 24.8%. The fund returned 22.4%. The shortfall came mainly from our deliberate underweight in defence and PSU stocks, which rallied sharply on order announcements but where we found valuations difficult to justify, and from two holdings in building materials that disappointed on margins.

We have used the year to strengthen the portfolio's quality and liquidity. We have reduced positions in several less-liquid names and raised cash to 7.1%. Lump-sum inflows remain capped at ₹2 lakh per PAN per day to protect existing investors from dilution in a segment where valuations are stretched; SIP flows continue without restriction.

Our long-term record is intact: since inception the fund has compounded at 19.6% a year against 16.4% for the benchmark. We continue to focus on owner-operated businesses with clean balance sheets, return on capital above 15% and transparent governance.
