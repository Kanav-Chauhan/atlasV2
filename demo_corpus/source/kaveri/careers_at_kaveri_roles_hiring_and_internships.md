# Careers at Kaveri – Roles, Hiring and Internships

> *Patient capital, clear thinking.*

This page is for people who are thinking of joining Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. It explains which teams hire, where we work, how our hiring process runs, which certifications some roles need, and how our internships work. It is published on the careers section of our website and reviewed every year by HR.

## 1. Who we are, in one paragraph

Kaveri Capital is a SEBI-registered asset manager and wealth advisory firm founded in 2012 in Bengaluru. We manage ₹18,650 crore (31 March 2026) across mutual-fund-style schemes, the Kaveri Focused Equity PMS and the Kaveri Long-Short AIF III. We are 310 people, including 34 research analysts, and we plan to grow to 338 people by 31 March 2027.

## 4. How hiring works

| Step | What happens | Typical time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Apply | Send your CV to careers@kaveri.example with the role name in the subject line | — |
| 2. Screening call | 20-minute call with HR | Within 10 working days of applying |
| 3. Skills test | Research: a written stock note on a company we choose (48 hours). Operations and Technology: online test. RMs: case role-play | 1 week |
| 4. Panel interviews | Two rounds with the hiring manager and a senior colleague from another team | 1–2 weeks |
| 5. Final round | Function head; for G2 and above, also the MD & CEO | 1 week |
| 6. Offer | Written offer, background verification and reference checks | Within 5 working days of final round |

From application to offer usually takes **4 to 6 weeks**. Every candidate who reaches the panel stage gets written feedback.

### 4.1 Background checks

Because we handle client money and price-sensitive information, every offer is subject to education, employment and address verification, a credit check, and a check that the candidate is not debarred by SEBI. Joiners must declare their own and their immediate relatives' securities holdings on Day 2 under our Personal Trading Policy.

## 5. Certifications some roles need

SEBI requires certain roles to hold a certificate from the National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM). You do not need it to apply, but you must pass it by the deadline after joining. Kaveri reimburses the exam fee for the first two attempts.

| Role | NISM certification needed | Deadline after joining |
|---|---|---|
| Research analyst | Series XV: Research Analyst | 90 days |
| PMS relationship manager | Series XXI-A: PMS Distributors | 90 days |
| Mutual fund sales / distribution | Series V-A: Mutual Fund Distributors | 90 days |
| Dealer | Series VIII: Equity Derivatives, and Series V-A | 90 days |
| Fund accounting (Operations) | Series V-B: Mutual Fund Foundation | 180 days |
| Compliance officer | Series III-A: Securities Intermediaries Compliance | 180 days |
| PMS portfolio manager | Series XXI-B: Portfolio Managers | Before appointment |

Someone who misses the deadline moves to a role that does not need the certificate until they pass.

## 6. Internships

### 6.1 Summer internship

- **Yes, we offer summer internships.** The programme runs for **8 weeks, from the first Monday of May to the end of June**, at the Bengaluru head office.
- Open to MBA and CFA candidates and to final-year undergraduates in finance, economics, statistics or engineering.
- **12 places** each year: 8 in Research, 2 in Risk, 2 in Operations and Technology.
- Applications open on 1 December and close on **31 January**. Shortlisted candidates do a written stock note and two interviews in February; offers go out by 15 March.
- Each intern has a mentor and delivers a final presentation to the Head of Research or the function head in the last week.
- A paid stipend is provided. Outstation interns get a travel allowance.

### 6.2 Winter research internship

A shorter **6-week** research internship runs from mid-December to the end of January for 4 students, mainly for first-year MBA candidates.

### 6.3 What interns can and cannot do

Interns follow the Code of Conduct and Ethics and the Personal Trading Policy like any employee. Interns have read-only access to general company documents; they do not see client data, Investment Committee papers or unpublished research, and they may not speak to clients or the media.

### 6.4 Pre-placement offers

Interns rated "strong" in their final review are considered for a pre-placement offer as Research Associate or Analyst. In 2025, 5 of 12 summer interns received pre-placement offers.

## 8. Equal opportunity and fraud warning

Kaveri hires on merit and does not discriminate on gender, religion, caste, disability, age or sexual orientation. **We never ask candidates for money** at any stage. If someone asks you to pay a "registration" or "training" fee in Kaveri's name, report it to careers@kaveri.example.
