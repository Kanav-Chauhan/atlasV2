# Suspicious Transaction Reporting SOP

## 1. Objective

1.1 This SOP explains, step by step, how an alert or employee concern becomes (or does not become) a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) filed with the Financial Intelligence Unit – India (FIU-IND). It implements clause 6 of the Anti-Money Laundering Policy (KCA/CMP/POL-012).

1.2 The fixed rule is: **the STR must be filed with FIU-IND within 7 days of suspicion being established** by the Principal Officer. The 7 days run from the date of the Principal Officer's written determination, not from the date of the original alert.

## 2. Sources of Suspicion

- Automated alerts from the transaction monitoring system (rules TM-01 to TM-07).
- Internal referrals from Relationship Managers, branch staff, Operations or the Registrar.
- Sanctions or adverse-media hits on an existing client.
- Requests or notices from law enforcement agencies, SEBI or FIU-IND.
- Attempted transactions that were refused, including offers of cash.

2.1 Attempted transactions must be reported even if the transaction was never completed.

## 3. Process

| Step | Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Raise internal referral on the "Concern Form" in the compliance portal | Any employee | Same working day the concern arises |
| 2 | Log the alert or referral in the AML case register with a case ID (format AML-YYYY-NNNN) | AML Analyst | Within 1 working day |
| 3 | Preliminary review: client profile, KYC, 12-month transaction history, linked folios | AML Analyst | Within 7 working days |
| 4 | Escalate to Deputy Principal Officer with a recommendation (close or investigate) | AML Analyst | Within 10 working days of alert |
| 5 | Detailed investigation, including RM interview and source-of-funds review | Deputy Principal Officer | Within 15 working days of alert |
| 6 | Determination: suspicion established or not established, recorded in writing | Principal Officer (CCO) | On completion of step 5 |
| 7 | Prepare STR in FIU-IND format with grounds of suspicion | AML Analyst | Within 3 days of determination |
| 8 | Review and file STR on the FINnet portal | Principal Officer | Within 7 days of determination |
| 9 | Decide on account action (continue with monitoring, restrict, exit) | Principal Officer with Head of Operations | Within 7 days of filing |
| 10 | Report STR count (no names) to Board | Principal Officer | Next quarterly Board meeting |

## 4. Writing the Grounds of Suspicion

4.1 The grounds of suspicion must be a factual narrative of 300–800 words covering: who the client is; what transactions occurred (dates, amounts, schemes, bank accounts); why they are unusual for this client; what enquiries Kaveri made; and why the explanation, if any, was not accepted.

4.2 Avoid opinion words ("clearly criminal", "fraudster"). State the facts and the red flags.

4.3 Attach: KYC record, transaction statement, bank mandate history and any correspondence.

## 5. Common Red Flags at Kaveri

- Subscription far above declared income (rule TM-01), especially lump sums above ₹1 crore from first-time investors.
- Redemption shortly after subscription with a request to change the bank mandate first.
- Several unrelated investors using the same mobile number, email or address.
- PMS contributions in securities transferred from a demat account not in the client's name.
- Reluctance to provide source-of-funds documents when asked under EDD.
- An AIF investor's offshore structure that cannot be traced to a natural person.

## 6. Confidentiality and Tipping-off

6.1 The existence of a case, the STR and its contents shall be known only to the Principal Officer, the Deputy Principal Officer, the assigned AML Analyst and, where needed, the Designated Director.

6.2 Relationship Managers may be asked factual questions but must not be told that an STR is being considered. Client communications during an investigation must be routine and must not reveal the investigation.

6.3 Case files are stored in the restricted "AML Cases" folder. Access is logged and reviewed monthly by the Principal Officer.

## 7. Account Handling After an STR

7.1 Filing an STR does not by itself require closing the account. Kaveri shall continue normal processing unless (a) a freeze order is received, (b) the client appears on a sanctions list, or (c) the Principal Officer decides to exit the relationship.

7.2 An exit shall be done by refusing fresh subscriptions and allowing redemption only to the registered bank account. The client shall be given the standard notice without reasons that could tip them off.

## 8. Decisions Not to File

8.1 Where the Principal Officer concludes that suspicion is not established, the reason shall be recorded in the case register. A sample of 10% of "not established" decisions is reviewed by Internal Audit every half year.

## 9. Record Retention

9.1 STRs, case files and decisions not to file shall be retained for at least 5 years from the date of the STR or closure of the case, whichever is later.

## 10. Key Metrics (FY2025-26)

## 11. Training

11.1 AML Analysts shall complete an FIU-IND FINnet filing refresher every year. A mock STR exercise is run every October.
