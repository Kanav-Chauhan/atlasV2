# Distributor (MFD) Empanelment Agreement – Standard Terms

These standard terms apply to every mutual fund distributor ("Distributor") empanelled by Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. ("Kaveri") to distribute the regular plans of its mutual-fund-style schemes. They are signed with the empanelment form and may only be varied by a written addendum approved by the Chief Compliance Officer.

## 1. Eligibility and empanelment

1.1 The Distributor must hold, and keep valid throughout the term, an AMFI Registration Number (ARN) and the NISM Series V-A: Mutual Fund Distributors certificate for every person who sells or advises on Kaveri schemes.

1.2 Corporate distributors must also provide incorporation documents, a board resolution, the list of employees with their EUIN (employee unique identification number), and a beneficial ownership declaration.

1.3 Kaveri carries out its own KYC on the Distributor (CKYC lookup and PAN verification of the entity and its principals) and screens them against sanctions lists before empanelment and daily after it.

1.4 The term is **3 years** from the empanelment date, renewable on fresh due diligence.

## 2. Commission

2.1 Commission is paid **only from the regular plan TER** and is **trail-based only**. Kaveri pays **no upfront commission** of any kind, in cash or kind.

2.2 Trail commission is paid monthly, by the 10th of the following month, by bank transfer to the Distributor's registered account, on the average AUM of the Distributor's investors in the month.

| Scheme | Annual trail commission (on regular plan AUM) |
|---|---|
| Kaveri Bluechip Fund | 0.80% |
| Kaveri Flexi Cap Fund | 0.85% |
| Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities | 0.62% |
| Kaveri Short Duration Fund | 0.45% |
| Kaveri Arbitrage Fund | 0.55% |

2.3 Commission is not paid on: direct plan investments; investments made after the Distributor's ARN has lapsed; investments made through a transaction later found to breach KYC rules; or units redeemed within 365 days where an exit load applied (commission paid on those units in the first year is recovered).

2.4 The Distributor may not pass on any part of the commission to investors as a rebate, and may not share it with any person who does not hold a valid ARN.

2.5 Rates may be changed by Kaveri with 30 days' written notice. Commission rates are disclosed in investors' consolidated account statements.

## 3. KYC and AML duties of the Distributor

3.1 The Distributor shall ensure every investor it introduces has completed KYC before the first transaction. Kaveri's KYC Desk performs the CKYC lookup and PAN verification; the Distributor must collect correct documents and must not submit a transaction for an investor whose KYC is incomplete or on hold.

3.2 The Distributor shall support Kaveri's periodic re-KYC cycle: every **2 years** for high-risk investors, every 5 years for medium-risk and every **8 years** for low-risk investors.

3.3 **No cash.** The Distributor shall **never accept cash** from an investor for any Kaveri investment, in any amount, and shall never collect money into its own account. All payments must go directly from the investor's own bank account to the scheme's account. Acceptance of cash is a material breach.

3.4 The Distributor shall tell Kaveri's Principal Officer (the CCO) within **2 working days** of noticing any transaction or behaviour that appears suspicious, so that Kaveri can file a Suspicious Transaction Report with FIU-IND **within 7 days of suspicion being established**. The Distributor shall not tell the investor (no tipping-off).

3.5 Investors who are politically exposed persons (PEPs) must be flagged to Kaveri at the time of the application; Kaveri onboards them only with CCO approval.

## 4. Conduct

4.1 The Distributor shall follow the AMFI Code of Conduct for intermediaries and the conduct standards of Kaveri's Code of Conduct and Ethics that apply to third parties. In particular, the Distributor shall:

- recommend only schemes suitable for the investor's risk profile and horizon;
- never promise or imply guaranteed returns;
- use only marketing material approved by Kaveri Compliance, carrying the line "Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully";
- disclose to the investor all commissions it receives from Kaveri and other fund houses;
- not encourage churning (frequent switching to earn commission).

4.2 Kaveri may give promotional items of nominal value only, up to ₹3,000 per person per year. Cash, vouchers and gift cards are never given.

4.3 Investor complaints received by the Distributor must be forwarded to Kaveri Client Services within **1 working day**.

## 5. Records and audit

5.1 The Distributor shall keep application forms, risk-profile records and communications with investors for at least **8 years**.

5.2 Kaveri, or an auditor it appoints, may inspect the Distributor's records relating to Kaveri investors with 7 days' notice. Distributors with more than ₹50 crore of Kaveri AUM are audited at least once every 2 years.

## 6. Monitoring

| Red flag | Kaveri action |
|---|---|
| More than 3 complaints alleging mis-selling in a year | Compliance review; commission held pending review |
| Switch ratio above 40% of the Distributor's book in a quarter | Written warning and churning review |
| ARN or NISM certificate lapsed | Commission stopped from the lapse date |
| Use of unapproved marketing material | Written warning; second instance is a material breach |

## 7. Termination

7.1 **Either party** may end the empanelment without cause by giving **30 days' written notice**.

7.2 **Kaveri may end the empanelment immediately**, by written notice, on any of these grounds:

1. Acceptance of cash from an investor, or collection of investor money into the Distributor's own account.
2. Mis-selling established by Compliance, or a promise of guaranteed returns.
3. Lapse or cancellation of the ARN, or action by SEBI or AMFI debarring the Distributor.
4. Submission of forged or incomplete KYC documents.
5. Breach of the no-tipping-off or confidentiality obligations.
6. Sharing commission with investors or with persons without an ARN.
7. Insolvency, or a criminal conviction involving fraud or dishonesty.
8. Any other material breach not cured within 15 days of written notice.

7.3 On termination, trail commission stops from the termination date for termination for cause under 7.2. For termination without cause, trail commission continues on existing assets as long as the investor keeps the Distributor as the distributor of record and the ARN remains valid.

7.4 The Distributor's investors are informed of the termination and may continue with another distributor or switch to the direct plan.

## 8. Confidentiality and data protection

8.1 Investor data received by the Distributor may be used only to service the investor and shall be protected in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Any data breach must be reported to Kaveri within 24 hours.

## 9. Governing law

9.1 These terms are governed by the laws of India. Disputes are referred to arbitration seated in Bengaluru, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, before a sole arbitrator.
