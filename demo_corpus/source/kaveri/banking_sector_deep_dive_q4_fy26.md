# Banking Sector Deep Dive – Q4 FY26

**Sector stance: OVERWEIGHT (unchanged).** Q4 FY26 results confirm that the credit cycle is healthy: loan growth held above 12%, asset quality improved again and margins fell less than feared after 75 basis points of repo cuts. Banks remain our largest overweight in the house view.

## 1. Key takeaways

1. **Loan growth steady** – system loan growth was 12.8% year-on-year in March 2026; deposit growth 10.9%. Our coverage banks grew loans by 14.1% on average.
2. **Margins bottoming** – average NIM of coverage fell 11 basis points quarter-on-quarter to 3.42%. We expect NIMs to stabilise in Q2 FY27 as deposit repricing catches up.
3. **Asset quality at decade best** – coverage gross NPA ratio fell to 2.1% (Q4 FY25: 2.6%); net NPA 0.5%.
4. **Credit costs benign** – 0.62% annualised for coverage, below our 0.8% through-cycle assumption.
5. **Unsecured retail stress contained** – slippages in personal loans and credit cards eased for the second straight quarter.

## 4. Margins: how much more pain?

The repo rate fell from 6.25% to 5.50% during FY26. About 60% of coverage loans are linked to external benchmarks and repriced within one quarter, while term deposits reprice over 9–12 months. The resulting squeeze has largely played out:

| Quarter | Coverage NIM | Change (bps) |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 FY26 | 3.71% | — |
| Q2 FY26 | 3.62% | -9 |
| Q3 FY26 | 3.53% | -9 |
| Q4 FY26 | 3.42% | -11 |
| Q1 FY27E | 3.38% | -4 |
| Q2 FY27E | 3.40% | +2 |

We assume no further rate cuts in FY27, consistent with the house view.

Microfinance remains the one pocket of stress; it is less than 2% of coverage loans.

## 6. Capital and liquidity

All coverage banks have CET-1 above 13%. The average liquidity coverage ratio is 124%, comfortably above the regulatory 100%. Kalinga State Bank raised ₹5,000 crore through a QIP in February 2026, lifting CET-1 to 13.8%.

## 8. Top picks

1. **Sahyadri Bank Ltd (BUY, TP ₹2,140)** – best-in-class deposit franchise; CASA ratio 41%; RoA 1.9%.
2. **Kalinga State Bank (BUY, TP ₹880)** – cheapest large bank at 1.1x FY28E P/ABV; RoA has improved from 0.6% to 1.1% in three years.
3. **Tungabhadra Bank Ltd (BUY, TP ₹1,480)** – retail-led growth with improving fee income.

## 9. Risks

- Deposit competition from small savings schemes and mutual funds keeps funding costs high.
- A renewed spike in unsecured retail slippages.
- Any reversal of the RBI's liquidity stance.
- Regulatory changes to expected credit loss (ECL) provisioning, scheduled from FY28, could add 20–40 bps to credit costs in the transition year.

*Rating definitions: BUY – expected return above 15% over 12 months; ADD – 5% to 15%; HOLD – -5% to 5%; REDUCE – below -5%.*
