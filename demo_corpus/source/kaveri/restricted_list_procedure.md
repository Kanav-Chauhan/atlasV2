# Restricted List Procedure

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how Kaveri Capital maintains its **restricted list**, **watch list** and **"do not buy" list**, who can add or remove securities, and what each list means for scheme trading and personal trading. It supports the Personal Trading Policy, the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations and the Investment Process and IC Charter.

## 2. The three lists

| List | Purpose | Visible to | Effect on schemes | Effect on personal trading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restricted list | Stocks where the firm may hold UPSI or has a conflict | All access persons | No trading by any scheme | Complete blackout |
| Watch list | Stocks under research initiation or rating change; confidential | Compliance only | No restriction, but trades monitored | Pre-clearance refused for research staff |
| "Do not buy" list | Stocks exited for governance reasons by IC decision | Investment team, Compliance, Risk | No new purchases in any scheme | No effect (normal pre-clearance) |

## 3. When a stock goes on the restricted list

A security must be added to the restricted list when:

1. an employee receives unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI) about the company, for example through a management meeting, an expert call or a wall-crossing for a block deal or QIP;
2. a Kaveri employee or director joins the board of the company;
3. Kaveri is engaged as an anchor investor in an IPO or QIP of the company, from the date of wall-crossing until public announcement;
4. a scheme is in the process of acquiring 5% or more of the company, triggering SAST disclosure obligations;
5. the CCO decides it is necessary to manage a conflict.

## 4. Adding a stock

| Step | Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee who receives UPSI notifies Compliance by email to compliance@kaveri.example (subject: "RL ADD") | Employee | Immediately; within 1 hour |
| 2 | Compliance assesses and adds the stock to the restricted list in the OMS and pre-clearance system | Compliance | Within 2 hours of notification |
| 3 | OMS hard-blocks orders in the stock for all schemes | Technology (automatic) | Immediate on entry |
| 4 | Notification sent to all access persons (without reason) | Compliance | Same day |
| 5 | Entry logged in the restricted list register with reason, source and expected removal date | Compliance | Same day |

Employees must not discuss the reason for a restriction with anyone outside the need-to-know group.

## 5. Removing a stock

5.1 A stock is removed when the UPSI has become public or is no longer price-sensitive, or the conflict has ended.

5.2 Removal requires the approval of the CCO, recorded in the register.

5.3 A minimum **2 trading days** must elapse after public disclosure of the information before removal, so that the market has absorbed it.

5.4 Compliance reviews every stock on the list at least **monthly**. Stocks on the list for more than **90 days** are reported to the MD & CEO with reasons.

## 6. Watch list

- Research informs Compliance in writing when it begins an initiation or plans a rating change. The stock goes on the watch list until publication plus 5 days.
- Compliance compares scheme and personal trades against the watch list each day.
- Example: Konkan Rail Systems Ltd was placed on the watch list on 13 March 2026, after IC 47 asked Research to initiate coverage, and removed on 31 March 2026, five days after the initiation report was published on 26 March 2026.

## 7. "Do not buy" list

7.1 A stock is added on an IC resolution to exit for governance reasons, normally for **24 months**.

7.2 Existing holdings may be sold down in an orderly manner as directed by the IC; no new purchases are allowed in any scheme or PMS account.

7.3 Removal before the end of the period requires a fresh IC resolution supported by a research note.

7.4 Current entries:

## 8. Personal trading interaction

- **Restricted list:** blackout on any stock on the restricted list. No buying, selling, pledging or gifting by covered persons, regardless of value. This blackout is enforced through the pre-clearance system.
- If an employee already holds a stock when it is added to the list, they must continue to hold it until removal, unless the CCO grants a hardship exception.
- Violations of the restricted list by any employee must be reported to the CCO within 1 working day.

## 9. Controls and monitoring

| Control | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| OMS hard block test (attempt a dummy order) | Monthly | Compliance with Technology |
| Reconciliation of restricted list register vs OMS | Weekly | Compliance |
| Review of trades in watch-list stocks | Daily | Compliance |
| Report to Board Risk Committee: list changes, breaches | Quarterly | CCO |

## 10. Records

The restricted list register, watch list and supporting emails are retained for **8 years**. In FY2025-26, 47 stocks were added to the restricted list (mostly wall-crossings for QIPs and block deals); the average time on the list was 11 trading days; there were no scheme trading breaches.
