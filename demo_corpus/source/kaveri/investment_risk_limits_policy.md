# Investment Risk Limits Policy

## 1. Purpose and Authority

1.1 This Policy sets the investment risk limits within which every scheme, the Portfolio Management Service (PMS) and the AIF Category III fund managed by Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. must operate. It is approved by the Board of Directors on the recommendation of the Board Risk Committee.

1.2 Limits in this Policy are in addition to, and never looser than, the limits in SEBI regulations and each scheme's offer document. Where a regulatory limit is tighter, the regulatory limit applies.

1.3 The Chief Risk Officer (CRO), Varun Pillai, owns this Policy. The Risk team is independent of the investment team and reports to the MD & CEO, with a direct line to the Chair of the Board Risk Committee.

## 2. Monitoring Bands

2.1 Every limit is monitored daily in the risk system on the following traffic-light bands:

| Band | Definition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Below 90% of the limit | None |
| Amber | 90% to 100% of the limit | CRO alerts fund manager and CIO the same day; no further additions to the exposure |
| Red (breach) | Above the limit (or below a floor) | Breach procedure in section 7 |

## 3. Concentration Limits – Equity Schemes

3.1 **Single stock:** no more than **10% of scheme NAV** in the equity of a single company, for every equity and hybrid scheme.

3.2 **Sector:** no more than **25% of scheme NAV** in any single sector (as classified by the AMFI industry classification), except that **Kaveri Bluechip Fund may hold up to 35% in banks**, reflecting the weight of banks in its Nifty 100 TRI benchmark.

3.3 **Group exposure:** no more than 20% of scheme NAV in companies of a single business group.

3.4 **Minimum number of holdings:** Bluechip 35, Flexi Cap 40, Small Cap Opportunities 60.

3.5 **Unlisted and illiquid securities:** unlisted equity not permitted in open-ended schemes.

## 4. Market Risk Limits

4.1 **Value-at-Risk:** the 1-day Value-at-Risk at **95% confidence must not exceed 2.5% of NAV for any equity scheme**. VaR is computed daily using historical simulation over 500 trading days.

4.2 **Tracking error (ex-post, 1-year, annualised):** Bluechip ≤ 6%; Flexi Cap ≤ 8%; Small Cap ≤ 9%.

4.3 **Beta to benchmark:** between 0.80 and 1.15 for Bluechip and Flexi Cap.

## 5. Liquidity Risk Limits

5.1 **Small Cap liquidity floor:** at least **15% of the Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities portfolio must be liquidatable within 5 trading days at 30% participation** of each stock's 3-month average daily traded volume. This is a floor; falling below it is a breach.

5.2 Other equity schemes are monitored against the same metric with an internal early-warning trigger of 40% (Flexi Cap) and 60% (Bluechip). These are triggers for review, not limits.

5.3 Debt and arbitrage schemes must hold at least 10% of NAV in liquid assets (cash, T-bills, G-secs, repo and overnight instruments).

5.4 **Subscription controls:** when the Small Cap liquidity measure is below 17%, the CRO may recommend to the MD & CEO a cap on daily lump-sum subscriptions per PAN.

## 6. Debt and Other Products

| Product | Limit |
|---|---|
| Kaveri Short Duration Fund | Macaulay duration 1 to 3 years; single issuer ≤ 10% of NAV; AA+ and below ≤ 20% of NAV; no paper below AA- |
| Kaveri Arbitrage Fund | Unhedged net equity exposure ≤ 1% of NAV; single stock gross ≤ 10% |
| Kaveri Focused Equity PMS | Single stock ≤ 12% of model portfolio; maximum 25 stocks; cash ≤ 20% |
| Kaveri Long-Short AIF III | Gross exposure ≤ 180% of NAV; net long exposure 0% to 70%; single stock long ≤ 10%; single stock short ≤ 5%; leverage within SEBI's 2x cap |

## 7. Breach Procedure

7.1 **Active breach** (caused by a purchase or sale decision): the fund manager must reverse the breach within 1 working day. The CRO reports it to the CCO and MD & CEO the same day.

7.2 **Passive breach** (caused by market movement, redemptions or corporate actions): the fund manager must restore compliance within **10 working days**, unless the CRO approves a written remediation plan with a longer deadline, not exceeding 90 calendar days.

7.3 **Liquidity floor breach:** because liquidity cannot always be restored quickly without harming investors, the fund manager shall submit a remediation plan to the CRO within 5 working days. The plan must name the stocks to be reduced, the target date, and an interim checkpoint. The plan is approved by the CRO and CIO, noted by the Investment Committee, and reported to the Board Risk Committee.

7.4 All breaches, active or passive, are recorded in the breach register and reported to the Board Risk Committee every quarter.

## 8. Reporting

| Report | Frequency | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| Daily limit monitor | Daily by 10:00 am | Fund managers, CIO, CRO, CCO |
| Risk Dashboard | Quarterly | Board Risk Committee, MD & CEO, CCO |
| Stress Test Results | Quarterly (March, June, September, December) | Board Risk Committee |
| Breach register | Monthly | MD & CEO, CCO |

## 9. Model Governance

9.1 VaR and liquidity models are back-tested quarterly. More than 12 VaR exceptions in 250 trading days requires a model review.

9.2 Market volume data used for liquidity is refreshed every trading day from exchange data.

## 10. Changes in Version 3.0

- Bluechip bank sector limit raised from 30% to 35%.
- Small Cap liquidity floor raised from 12% to 15%, and participation rate lowered from 40% to 30%.
- Passive breach rectification shortened from 30 to 10 working days.

## 11. Review

11.1 The CRO shall review this Policy every year and present changes to the Board Risk Committee. The Board approves all changes to limits.
