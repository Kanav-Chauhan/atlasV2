## 7. Fund manager commentary

The fund returned 17.6% over FY2025-26 against 15.1% for the Nifty 500 TRI, an outperformance of 2.5 percentage points. Stock selection in capital goods and mid-cap financials drove most of the excess return; Trishul Heavy Engineering and Vajra Power Equipment both benefited from record order books in transmission and railway electrification.

During the year we raised mid-cap exposure from 21% to 24.7%, funded by trimming large-cap IT services, where we see slower deal conversion and pricing pressure. In March we began building a position in the railways capex theme across signalling and rolling-stock suppliers; Sethu Signalling Technologies entered the top 10.

Small-cap exposure was held at 12.3%, below our 15% comfort level, given elevated valuations in that segment. We continue to run a moderate cash buffer of 4.6% to take advantage of volatility.
