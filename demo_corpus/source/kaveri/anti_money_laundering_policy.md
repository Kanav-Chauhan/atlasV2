# Anti-Money Laundering Policy

## 1. Policy Statement

1.1 Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. ("Kaveri") will not allow its schemes, its Portfolio Management Service or the Kaveri Long-Short AIF III to be used for money laundering or the financing of terrorism. This Policy, approved by the Board of Directors, sets the framework; detailed procedures are in the KYC and Customer Due Diligence Policy (KCA/CMP/POL-011), the Suspicious Transaction Reporting SOP (KCA/CMP/SOP-013) and the Politically Exposed Persons Procedure (KCA/CMP/SOP-014).

1.2 The Policy is issued under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA), the PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005, the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967, and the SEBI Master Circular on AML/CFT for intermediaries.

## 3. Three Lines of Defence

3.1 **First line:** Relationship Managers, the KYC Desk and Operations collect KYC, apply payment rules and raise alerts.

3.2 **Second line:** Compliance runs transaction monitoring, investigates alerts, decides on STRs and trains staff.

3.3 **Third line:** Internal Audit reviews AML controls every year and reports to the Audit Committee.

## 4. Key Controls

4.1 **No cash.** Kaveri does not accept cash in any form for subscriptions, PMS contributions or AIF capital calls. As a result, Kaveri files a "nil" Cash Transaction Report (CTR) confirmation with FIU-IND each month.

4.2 **Own-account payments only.** Money must come from and go back to a bank account in the client's own name. Redemption proceeds are never paid to a third party.

4.3 **Risk-based approach.** Clients are rated Low, Medium or High risk at onboarding. High-risk clients, including all PEPs, undergo Enhanced Due Diligence and are reviewed every 2 years.

4.4 **Sanctions screening.** All clients, beneficial owners and authorised signatories are screened at onboarding and daily against the UN Security Council lists and orders under Section 51A of the UAPA. A confirmed match leads to an immediate freeze and reporting within 24 hours to the authorities named in the UAPA order.

4.5 **High-risk jurisdictions.** Clients resident in jurisdictions on the FATF "call for action" list shall not be onboarded. Clients from jurisdictions under FATF "increased monitoring" require CCO approval.

## 5. Transaction Monitoring Rules

5.1 The monitoring system generates alerts on the following scenarios. Thresholds are reviewed each year by the Principal Officer.

| Rule | Scenario | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| TM-01 | Large subscription inconsistent with declared income | Subscription above 5x declared annual income |
| TM-02 | Quick in-and-out | Redemption of more than 75% of a subscription within 30 days |
| TM-03 | Multiple folios, same contact details | 3 or more unrelated PANs sharing a mobile or email |
| TM-04 | Frequent bank mandate changes | 2 or more bank mandate changes in 90 days |
| TM-05 | Structuring | 3 or more subscriptions just below ₹50,000 within 7 days |
| TM-06 | Off-market transfer of units to unrelated party | Any instance |
| TM-07 | PMS contribution in securities from third party demat | Any instance |

5.2 Each alert must be closed with a written rationale within 15 working days. Alerts open beyond 30 days are reported to the Designated Director monthly.

## 6. Reporting to FIU-IND

| Report | Trigger | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) | Suspicion established by the Principal Officer | Within 7 days of suspicion being established |
| Cash Transaction Report (CTR) | Cash transactions above ₹10 lakh (none, since cash is not accepted) | Nil confirmation by the 15th of the following month |
| Counterfeit Currency Report | Not applicable (no cash handled) | — |
| Non-Profit Organisation Transaction Report | Receipts above ₹10 lakh from an NPO | By the 15th of the following month |

6.1 **Tipping-off is prohibited.** No employee shall tell a client, distributor or any outsider that an STR has been or may be filed. Breach of this clause is a disciplinary offence and may be a criminal offence.

## 7. Record Keeping

7.1 Transaction records shall be kept for 5 years from the date of the transaction. KYC and identity records shall be kept for 5 years after the relationship ends. Records relating to an STR or an ongoing investigation are kept until the case is closed, even if this exceeds 5 years.

## 8. Training

8.1 Every employee shall complete the AML e-learning module within 30 days of joining and a refresher every year by 31 December. The pass mark is 80%.

8.2 Operations, Client Services and Compliance staff shall in addition attend one classroom session per year run by the Principal Officer. Completion is reported to the Board each quarter; FY2025-26 completion was 98.4% of 310 employees.

## 9. Employee Screening

9.1 New employees are screened against sanctions lists and for adverse media before joining. Employees in Operations and Compliance are re-screened every 2 years.

## 10. Reporting to the Board

10.1 The Principal Officer shall present to the Board each quarter: the number of alerts generated and closed, STRs filed, sanctions matches, training completion and any regulatory observation. In FY2025-26, the system generated 1,284 alerts, 1,271 were closed within timeline, and 6 STRs were filed with FIU-IND.

## 11. Review

11.1 This Policy shall be reviewed at least annually and approved by the Board. Interim changes required by law may be approved by the Designated Director and ratified at the next Board meeting.
