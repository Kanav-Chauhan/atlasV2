# Code of Conduct and Ethics

## 1. Purpose

Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. ("Kaveri Capital" or "the Company") acts as a fiduciary for 41,000 retail investors, 1,150 PMS clients and 85 institutional and family-office mandates. This Code sets the minimum standard of behaviour expected of every person who works for us. It supplements, and does not replace, the SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations and other applicable law.

## 2. Scope

This Code applies to all permanent employees, fixed-term staff, interns, consultants and directors of the Company, across the Bengaluru head office and the Mumbai, New Delhi, Chennai and Pune branches. "Access persons" are employees who have access to non-public information about portfolio holdings or trades; all investment, research, dealing, risk, operations and compliance staff are access persons by default.

## 3. Core principles

1. **Fiduciary duty** – place the interests of clients ahead of the Company's and your own.
2. **Integrity** – be honest in every representation to clients, colleagues and regulators.
3. **Fair treatment** – allocate trades fairly across schemes and clients; never favour one client over another.
4. **Confidentiality** – protect client data and portfolio information.
5. **Compliance** – follow the letter and the spirit of the law and of Company policies.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

4.1 Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) in writing within **5 working days** of becoming aware of it.

4.2 Outside directorships, partnerships or paid advisory roles require prior written approval from the CCO and the MD & CEO.

4.3 Employees must not invest in, or have a financial interest in, a vendor, broker or counterparty that they deal with on behalf of the Company without prior approval.

4.4 Relatives employed at brokers, issuers or distributors must be declared at joining and updated annually.

## 5. Gifts and hospitality

| Item | Limit | Approval / record |
|---|---|---|
| Gifts received (per giver, per year) | Up to ₹5,000 | Record in gift register within 3 working days |
| Gifts received above ₹5,000 | Not permitted | Return or hand over to Compliance |
| Hospitality received (meal, event) | Up to ₹10,000 per occasion | Pre-approval from line manager; record in register |
| Gifts to distributors or clients | Up to ₹3,000 per person per year | Must be branded / nominal; Compliance log |
| Cash or cash equivalents (vouchers, gift cards) | Never permitted | — |

Gifts from brokers during the period in which broker empanelment or broker-vote ratings are being finalised (the last 15 days of each quarter) are not permitted at any value.

## 6. Confidential and price-sensitive information

6.1 Portfolio holdings, trade orders, research ratings before publication and IC discussions are confidential.

6.2 Unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI) about a listed company must not be traded on or communicated except on a need-to-know basis. Any employee who receives UPSI must inform Compliance immediately so that the stock can be added to the restricted list.

6.3 Front-running (dealing ahead of a scheme or client order) is gross misconduct and will result in termination and reporting to SEBI.

## 7. Personal trading

All personal trading is governed by the **Personal Trading Policy** (KCA/CMP/POL-022). In summary: pre-clearance is required for every trade in listed equities and derivatives, the minimum holding period is 30 days, no dealing is permitted in stocks on the restricted list, and violations are reported to the CCO within 1 working day.

## 8. Communication with media and on social media

- Only the MD & CEO, the CIO (Equity), the Head of Fixed Income and the Head of Research may speak to the media about markets or the Company, and only after Compliance has been informed.
- Employees must not recommend specific securities on social media, messaging groups or podcasts.
- Fund performance may be quoted externally only in the SEBI-prescribed format, with the statutory market-risk disclaimer.

## 9. Anti-bribery and anti-corruption

No employee may offer, promise, give or accept any bribe, kickback or facilitation payment. Payments to government officials of any kind, including "speed money", are prohibited. Breaches will be dealt with under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 and may be reported to law enforcement.

## 10. Fair dealing and trade allocation

- Orders for multiple schemes in the same security are aggregated and allocated pro rata before execution.
- Any deviation from pro-rata allocation must be documented by the dealer and approved by the CIO the same day.
- Inter-scheme transfers are permitted only at prevailing market price and only with the approval of the CIO and the CRO.

## 11. Respectful workplace

The Company has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination or bullying. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee constituted under the POSH Act, 2013. The Internal Committee is chaired by Deepa Nambiar, Head of HR, and includes an external member.

## 12. Whistle-blowing

12.1 Concerns may be raised in good faith with the CCO, or anonymously through the ethics hotline (ethics@kaveri.example), which is monitored by the Chair of the Audit Committee.

12.2 No employee will face retaliation for a good-faith report. Retaliation is itself a breach of this Code.

12.3 Every report is acknowledged within **7 days** and investigated within **45 days**.

## 13. Annual certification

Every employee must certify compliance with this Code:

| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| At joining | Before system access is granted |
| Annual certification | By 30 April each year |
| After a material revision of the Code | Within 30 days of circulation |

## 14. Breaches and consequences

| Severity | Examples | Typical action |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Late gift register entry; late annual certification | Written caution |
| Moderate | Trading without pre-clearance; undisclosed conflict | Warning, disgorgement of profit, bonus impact |
| Serious | Front-running, misuse of UPSI, bribery, data leak | Termination, regulatory reporting |

Profits made in breach of this Code are disgorged to the Kaveri Capital CSR fund.

## 15. Ownership and review

This Code is owned by the Chief Compliance Officer and approved by the Board of Directors. It is reviewed annually, or earlier if regulations change.
