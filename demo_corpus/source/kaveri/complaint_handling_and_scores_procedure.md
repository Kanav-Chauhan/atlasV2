# Complaint Handling and SCORES Procedure

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This procedure sets out how Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. receives, records, resolves and reports investor complaints, including those lodged on SEBI's complaints redress system (SCORES) and escalated to the online dispute resolution (ODR) mechanism.

1.2 Our standard: every complaint acknowledged within **1 working day** and resolved within **21 calendar days** of receipt, which is also the SEBI timeline for SCORES complaints.

## 2. What is a Complaint

2.1 A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about a Kaveri product or service where the investor expects a response, for example: delayed redemption, wrong NAV applied, non-receipt of statement, mis-selling, fee dispute, KYC rejection, or staff behaviour.

2.2 A query ("What is my folio balance?") is not a complaint, unless the investor says it is or the same query is repeated after the service timeline is missed.

## 4. Process

| Step | Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Log in the complaints system; assign ID (CMPL-YYYY-NNNNN) | Client Services | On receipt |
| 2 | Acknowledge to investor with ID and expected resolution date | Client Services | Within 1 working day |
| 3 | Categorise (service, transaction, mis-selling, fee, KYC, other) and assign to resolver team | Client Services Team Lead | Within 1 working day |
| 4 | Investigate and propose resolution | Resolver team (Operations, PMS desk, Sales) | Within 10 calendar days |
| 5 | Approve resolution; compensation above ₹25,000 needs CCO approval | Grievance Officer / CCO | Within 14 calendar days |
| 6 | Communicate final response to investor with escalation options | Client Services | Within 21 calendar days |
| 7 | Close complaint; record root cause | Grievance Officer | On closure |

4.1 **Mis-selling complaints** are always investigated by Compliance, not by the Sales team.

4.2 Where an investor's loss was caused by Kaveri's error (for example, a wrong NAV applied after a cut-off error), Kaveri shall compensate the investor for the difference, with interest at 15% per annum for any delay in redemption payout beyond the SEBI timeline.

## 5. SCORES Complaints

5.1 The Compliance Grievance Officer checks SCORES daily at 10:00 am.

5.2 The Action Taken Report (ATR) must be uploaded on SCORES within **21 calendar days** of receipt. If the investor is not satisfied, they may request a review, which Kaveri must answer within 10 calendar days.

5.3 A SCORES complaint is never closed without the ATR being uploaded and the investor's documents attached.

## 6. Escalation for the Investor

6.1 Every final response shall tell the investor of the following escalation path:

1. Grievance Redressal Officer, Kaveri Capital Advisors (if not already involved);
2. SEBI SCORES;
3. Online Dispute Resolution through the SMART ODR portal for conciliation and arbitration.

## 7. Internal Escalation

| Condition | Escalate to | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Complaint open for more than 14 days | Grievance Officer | Day 15 |
| Complaint open for more than 21 days | Chief Compliance Officer | Day 22 |
| Any complaint naming a director or the CEO | Chief Compliance Officer | Same day |
| More than 5 complaints on the same issue in a week | Head of Operations and CCO | Within 1 working day |

## 8. Root-Cause Analysis

8.1 The Grievance Officer prepares a monthly root-cause report. Any root cause appearing in more than 10 complaints in a quarter requires a corrective action plan with an owner and date.
