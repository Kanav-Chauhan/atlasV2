# Expense Ratio and TER Computation Note

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This note explains how Finance computes, accrues and checks the Total Expense Ratio (TER) of each Kaveri mutual-fund-style scheme, and how the management fee portion becomes Kaveri's revenue. It is used by the Fund Accounting team (Operations) and by Finance for the monthly revenue close.

## 2. What TER Includes

2.1 TER is the annual percentage of a scheme's daily net assets charged to the scheme for all recurring expenses. It includes:

- investment management and advisory fee (Kaveri's revenue);
- GST on the management fee (18%);
- registrar and transfer agent fees;
- custody and fund accounting fees;
- audit, trustee-type oversight and legal fees;
- investor education and awareness charge (2 basis points, mandatory);
- marketing and selling expenses;
- distributor commission (regular plans only).

2.2 Brokerage and transaction costs are **not** part of TER; they are added to the cost of investments, within the regulatory cap of 0.12% of trade value for cash-market trades.

## 3. Regulatory Maximum TER

3.1 The maximum permissible TER falls as scheme assets grow. The equity slabs below apply to equity-oriented schemes (including Kaveri Arbitrage Fund); debt schemes use slabs 0.25 percentage points lower.

| Daily net assets (equity) | Maximum TER on that slab |
|---|---|
| First ₹500 crore | 2.25% |
| Next ₹250 crore | 2.00% |
| Next ₹1,250 crore | 1.75% |
| Next ₹3,000 crore | 1.60% |
| Next ₹5,000 crore | 1.50% |

3.2 GST on the management fee and certain expenses specifically permitted by SEBI are charged over and above the slab limits. Fund Accounting computes the permissible maximum for every scheme daily, on that day's net assets, and raises an alert if the headroom between the regular-plan TER and the maximum falls below 5 basis points.

## 5. Daily Accrual

5.1 TER is accrued every calendar day (including holidays) in the NAV:

**Daily accrual = Net assets × TER ÷ 365** (366 in a leap year)

5.2 Example – Bluechip, direct-plan equivalent on full AUM of ₹6,420 crore:

| Item | Calculation | Daily amount |
|---|---|---|
| Total TER accrual | 6,420 cr × 0.82% ÷ 365 | ₹14.42 lakh |
| Of which management fee (Kaveri revenue) | 6,420 cr × 0.55% ÷ 365 | ₹9.67 lakh |

5.3 For holidays, accrual for the holiday is included in the next business day's NAV.

5.4 If expenses actually incurred in a month are lower than accrued, the excess accrual is reversed by the end of that month; expenses above the TER cap are borne by Kaveri, not the scheme, and are shown in the P&L as "scheme expenses borne".

## 6. Monthly Revenue Close

| Step | Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fund Accounting extracts daily management fee accruals for all schemes and plans | Operations – Fund Accounting | Working day 2 |
| 2 | Reconcile accrued fee to amounts actually transferred from scheme bank accounts | Finance – Revenue team | Working day 3 |
| 3 | Check every scheme's TER against the maximum and against the TER published on the website | Finance | Working day 3 |
| 4 | Book revenue and GST liability | Finance | Working day 4 |
| 5 | CFO review of revenue variance vs budget (flag above ±3%) | Gaurav Agarwal | Working day 5 |

## 7. Changes to TER

7.1 Any change in TER (up or down) must be approved by the CFO and the CIO, published on the website at least 3 working days before it takes effect, and communicated to investors by email for increases.

7.2 Finance keeps a TER change register with date, scheme, plan, old TER, new TER, approver and reason.

## 8. Controls and Exceptions

8.1 Fund Accounting reconciles the expense accrual daily. A difference above ₹50,000 between system and manual check must be explained to the Head of Operations the same day.

8.2 Any TER above the regulatory maximum, even for a day, is reported to the Chief Compliance Officer within 1 working day and the excess is refunded to the scheme by Kaveri.

8.3 FY2025-26: no instance of TER exceeding the regulatory maximum. Expenses borne by Kaveri above TER caps were ₹1.9 crore (included in "distribution, marketing and scheme expenses borne").
