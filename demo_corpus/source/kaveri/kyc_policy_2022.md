# KYC Policy 2022

> **Superseded.** This Policy was withdrawn on 01 April 2026 and replaced by the KYC and Customer Due Diligence Policy (version 4.0). It is retained for reference and for audit of accounts opened between April 2022 and March 2026 only. Do not apply it to new or existing clients.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This Policy describes the Know Your Customer (KYC) process followed by Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. for investors in its schemes and Portfolio Management Service, in line with the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 and the applicable SEBI circulars as in force in 2022.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Policy applies to all investors onboarded at the Bengaluru head office and the Mumbai, New Delhi and Chennai branches. (The Pune branch was opened after this Policy was issued.)

## 3. Identification Requirements

3.1 Every investor shall provide a copy of PAN and one proof of address. PAN shall be verified against the Income Tax Department database.

3.2 The KYC Desk shall check whether a KYC Registration Agency (KRA) record exists. A search of the Central KYC Records Registry (CKYC) was **optional** under this Policy and was performed only where the KRA record was incomplete.

3.3 In-Person Verification (IPV) shall be done by a Kaveri employee or an empanelled distributor. Video IPV was permitted from 01 October 2022 under Amendment 1.

3.4 For non-individual investors, beneficial owners holding more than 25% in a company or more than 15% in a partnership shall be identified.

## 4. Risk Categorisation

4.1 Investors shall be classified as Low, Medium or High risk at onboarding.

| Category | Examples | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Resident individuals, investment below ₹5 lakh | KYC officer |
| Medium | HUFs, NRIs, PMS clients | KYC supervisor |
| High | PEPs, trusts, clients from high-risk jurisdictions | Head of Operations and CCO |

4.2 PEP accounts required the joint approval of the Head of Operations and the CCO.

## 5. Periodic Updation

5.1 Investor KYC shall be updated on the following cycle:

| Risk category | Re-KYC frequency |
|---|---|
| High | Every 3 years |
| Medium | Every 6 years |
| Low | Every 10 years |

5.2 Reminders shall be sent 30 days before the due date. Accounts not updated within 180 days after the due date shall be blocked for fresh purchases.

## 6. Mode of Payment

6.1 Cash shall not be accepted. Payments shall be received by cheque, NEFT/RTGS, UPI or net banking from the investor's own bank account.

## 7. Suspicious Transactions

7.1 Any suspicious transaction shall be reported by the KYC Desk to the Principal Officer, who shall file a Suspicious Transaction Report with FIU-IND within 7 days of arriving at a conclusion that the transaction is suspicious.

## 8. Record Retention

8.1 KYC records shall be retained for 5 years from the date of cessation of the relationship.

## 9. Reasons for Supersession (added April 2026)

9.1 The Compliance Committee, at its meeting of 18 February 2026, recommended replacing this Policy because:

- the re-KYC cycle of 3 / 6 / 10 years was longer than the cycle now adopted (2 / 5 / 8 years) following the 2025 internal audit observation on stale high-risk KYC;
- CKYC lookup is now mandatory at onboarding rather than optional;
- the beneficial ownership threshold for companies was lowered from 25% to 10%;
- PEP approval was moved to the CCO alone, to give a single accountable approver; and
- the 180-day grace period after the re-KYC due date was cut to 90 days.

9.2 Accounts whose next re-KYC date was computed under this Policy shall be re-dated under the new cycle by 30 September 2026.
