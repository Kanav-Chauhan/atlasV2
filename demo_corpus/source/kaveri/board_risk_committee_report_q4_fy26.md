## 2. Matters for the Board's Attention

### 2.1 Small Cap liquidity breach

- Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities' 5-day liquidity measure was **11.8%** at 31 March 2026, below the **15% floor** in the Investment Risk Limits Policy. This is the first breach of the floor since it was raised from 12% to 15% in October 2025.
- Cause: a 34% fall in small-cap traded volumes in February–March 2026, inflows of ₹212 crore in the quarter, and six low-volume positions forming 13.3% of NAV.
- Remediation: trim the six illiquid names (including a full exit from Vindhyagiri Cementworks) to about 6.0% of NAV by **30 June 2026**; interim checkpoint 13.5% by 15 May 2026; the existing cap on lump-sum subscriptions of ₹2 lakh per PAN per day stays in force.
- The BRC questioned the fund manager on the investment case for the six names. The BRC accepted the plan but asked that the CRO report to the Chair by email within 1 working day if the 15 May checkpoint is missed.

### 2.2 Stress test outcome

- Under a 25% redemption run over 10 trading days, Small Cap would face a funding shortfall of ₹30.0 crore. The BRC endorsed the CFO's proposal to arrange a committed borrowing line of ₹150 crore across schemes by 31 July 2026.
- Under a 20% equity market fall, annualised fee income would fall by about ₹38 crore. The BRC asked for a budget downside case for its July 2026 meeting.
