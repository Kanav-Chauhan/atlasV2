# Treasury and Investment of Own Funds Policy

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This Policy governs how Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. invests its own (proprietary) funds, as distinct from client and scheme money, which is never mixed with the Company's funds. It is approved by the Board and administered by the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

1.2 Objectives, in order of priority: (a) **safety** of capital; (b) **liquidity** to meet operating needs, regulatory capital and seed commitments; (c) a reasonable **return**; and (d) **no conflict** with clients.

## 2. Size of Own Funds

2.1 As of 31 March 2026, own funds available for investment were ₹286.3 crore: investments in own schemes ₹96.4 crore, other investments (G-secs, T-bills, bank deposits) ₹158.3 crore, and cash and bank balances ₹31.6 crore.

## 3. Liquidity Buffer

3.1 The Company shall at all times hold an **operating liquidity buffer equal to at least 6 months of operating expenses** in cash, bank deposits maturing within 3 months, T-bills, or overnight/liquid instruments. For FY2026-27 this is about **₹75 crore** (6 months of the budgeted ₹148.8 crore operating expenses).

3.2 If the buffer falls below this level, the CFO shall inform the MD & CEO within 1 working day and restore it within 30 days.

## 4. Permitted Investments and Limits

| Instrument | Limit (% of investible own funds) | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Government securities and T-bills | No limit | Maturity up to 5 years |
| Bank fixed deposits | Up to 40% in total; max 15% with any one bank | Only scheduled commercial banks rated AA+ or above |
| Kaveri Short Duration Fund, Kaveri Arbitrage Fund | Up to 40% in total | Units of own debt/arbitrage schemes |
| Kaveri equity schemes, PMS and AIF | Seed and mandatory skin-in-the-game only | See section 5 |
| Highly rated corporate bonds (AAA) | Up to 15% | Max 5% per issuer; maturity up to 3 years |

4.1 **Prohibited:** direct investment in listed or unlisted equity shares; derivatives other than for hedging foreign-currency exposure; units of other asset managers' equity schemes; loans to employees beyond the HR staff loan scheme; any investment in a company held by a Kaveri scheme.

4.2 Investments in Kaveri's own schemes are made only through the direct plan, and are subject to the same cut-off times and exit loads as any other investor.

## 5. Seed and Skin-in-the-Game Investments

5.1 Kaveri shall invest in its own schemes the minimum amounts required by SEBI for each scheme, based on the scheme's risk level. These investments are held for the life of the scheme and may not be redeemed while the requirement applies.

5.2 **Seed capital for new products** requires Board approval. The Board on 25 April 2026 approved seed capital of **₹25 crore** for the proposed GIFT City feeder fund, to be invested at launch (target January 2027) and held for at least 3 years.

5.3 Current seed and mandatory investments:

| Product | Amount (₹ crore, 31 March 2026) |
|---|---|
| Mutual-fund-style schemes (mandatory, all five schemes) | 52.6 |
| Kaveri Long-Short AIF III (sponsor commitment) | 26.3 |
| Kaveri Short Duration Fund (surplus deployment) | 17.5 |
| **Total investments in own schemes** | **96.4** |

5.4 The AIF sponsor commitment is maintained at not less than 5% of the AIF corpus or ₹10 crore, whichever is lower, as required for Category III AIFs; Kaveri has chosen to hold 7.5% of the ₹350 crore corpus.

## 6. Approval Authority

| Transaction size (per transaction) | Approval |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹25 crore within permitted limits | CFO |
| Above ₹25 crore up to ₹75 crore | CFO and MD & CEO jointly |
| Above ₹75 crore, or any new instrument type | Board (or Board Risk Committee by delegation) |
| Any seed investment in a new product | Board |

6.1 Every transaction requires two authorised signatories on the bank instruction. Treasury dealing is done by Finance, never by fund managers or scheme dealers, to avoid conflicts.

## 7. Conflict of Interest Rules

7.1 Own-fund investments in Kaveri schemes shall not be redeemed during the 10 trading days before an expected large client redemption that the CFO knows of, or when a scheme is in a liquidity breach. For example, no own-fund redemption from Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities is permitted until the current liquidity breach is closed.

7.2 Kaveri shall not use own funds to support a scheme's NAV or to absorb losses that belong to unit-holders.

## 8. Monitoring and Reporting

| Report | Frequency | To |
|---|---|---|
| Treasury position and limit compliance | Monthly | MD & CEO |
| Treasury report with returns and buffer | Quarterly | Board |
| Counterparty bank rating review | Half-yearly | CFO |

8.1 Treasury return in FY2025-26: 7.1% on average investible funds (other income ₹6.9 crore).

## 9. Breaches

9.1 Any breach of a limit in this Policy shall be reported by the CFO to the MD & CEO within 1 working day and corrected within 15 working days, and reported to the Board at its next meeting.

## 10. Review

10.1 This Policy is reviewed annually by the CFO and approved by the Board. Version 2.1 adds the GIFT City seed approval and the liquidity-breach redemption restriction in clause 7.1.
