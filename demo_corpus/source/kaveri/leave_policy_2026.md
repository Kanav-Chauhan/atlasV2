# Leave Policy 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the leave entitlements of employees of Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. for calendar year 2026, and how leave is applied for, approved and recorded. Leave is part of looking after yourself; the firm expects employees to use their entitlement.

## 2. Scope and leave year

2.1 This policy applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees across all offices. Interns are covered by their internship letter.

2.2 The leave year is the **calendar year (1 January to 31 December)**. This differs from the financial year (April–March) used for budgets and performance.

## 3. Leave entitlements

| Leave type | Days per calendar year | Credit | Carry forward | Encashment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Privilege leave (PL) | 20 | 5 days per quarter, in advance | Up to 30 days accumulated | On exit, up to 30 days at basic salary |
| Casual leave (CL) | 8 | Full credit on 1 January | No | No |
| Sick leave (SL) | 10 | Full credit on 1 January | Up to 30 days accumulated | No |
| Optional holidays | 2 | From the published list | No | No |

Each employee therefore receives **20 days of privilege leave, 8 days of casual leave and 10 days of sick leave** per calendar year. Employees joining mid-year receive a pro-rata credit, rounded to the nearest half day.

## 4. Privilege leave

4.1 PL is intended for planned vacations and must be applied for at least **10 working days in advance** for periods of 3 days or more.

4.2 PL may be taken in half-day units.

4.3 Dealers and fund managers must also take **mandatory block leave** of 10 consecutive working days every year under the Mandatory Block Leave Policy (KCA/HR/POL-002). PL is used for the block leave.

4.4 PL above the 30-day accumulation cap lapses on 31 December each year.

## 5. Casual leave

5.1 CL is for short, unplanned personal needs. A maximum of **3 consecutive days** of CL may be taken at one time.

5.2 CL cannot be combined with PL in the same continuous absence.

5.3 Unused CL lapses on 31 December.

## 6. Sick leave

6.1 SL may be taken for the employee's own illness or to care for an ill immediate family member.

6.2 A medical certificate is required for SL of more than **2 consecutive days**.

6.3 Where SL is exhausted, PL may be used; beyond that, leave without pay applies, subject to HR approval.

## 7. Other leave

| Leave type | Entitlement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks paid | As per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961; 12 weeks for a third child |
| Adoption leave | 12 weeks paid | For adoption of a child below 3 months |
| Paternity leave | 10 working days | Within 3 months of birth or adoption |
| Bereavement leave | 5 working days | Death of an immediate family member |
| Marriage leave | 5 working days | Once during employment |
| Examination leave | Up to 5 days per year | For NISM / CFA / CA exams under the Learning and Certification Policy |
| Leave without pay | Up to 60 days | Approval of function head and Head of HR |

## 8. Public holidays 2026

The firm observes **12 fixed holidays** in 2026, aligned to the NSE trading-holiday calendar where possible, plus 2 optional holidays. The location-wise list is published on the HR portal in December. Operations and dealing staff required to work on an exchange working day that is an office holiday receive a compensatory off within 30 days.

## 9. Applying for leave

9.1 Leave is applied for in the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager.

9.2 Managers must act on leave requests within **2 working days**; unactioned requests are escalated to the function head.

9.3 Leave taken without approval is treated as unauthorised absence. Unauthorised absence of more than **3 consecutive working days** may lead to disciplinary action.

## 10. Leave during key periods

The following periods are "restricted leave windows" for the named teams, and PL in these windows needs function-head approval:

| Team | Restricted window |
|---|---|
| Operations (NAV and fund accounting) | Last 3 and first 3 working days of each month |
| Finance | 1–20 April and 1–15 October (results and audit) |
| Compliance | 1–15 April (annual SEBI filings) |
| Research | 10 days either side of the quarterly house-view publication |

## 11. Leave balance and records

Leave balances are visible in the HR portal. HR sends a reminder in **October** to employees with more than 15 days of unused PL. Disputes about leave balances must be raised with HR within 30 days of the balance being shown.

## 12. Exit

On resignation, PL balance up to 30 days is encashed at basic salary. Leave may not be used to shorten the notice period without the function head's approval.
