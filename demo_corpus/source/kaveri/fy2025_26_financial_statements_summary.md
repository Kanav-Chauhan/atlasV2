# FY2025-26 Financial Statements Summary

Summary of the standalone audited financial statements of Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. for the year ended 31 March 2026, prepared under Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS). The statements were audited by Raghavan & Mehta LLP, Chartered Accountants, who issued an **unmodified (clean) opinion**, and were adopted by the Board on 25 April 2026. All amounts in ₹ crore unless stated.

## 1. Highlights

| Metric | FY2024-25 | FY2025-26 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total revenue | 181.0 | 212.0 | +17.1% |
| Profit after tax (PAT) | 47.7 | 58.0 | +21.6% |
| Cost-to-income ratio | 62.2% | 61.0% | −1.2 pts |
| Closing AUM | 15,920 | 18,650 | +17.1% |
| Employees (31 March) | 284 | 310 | +26 |
| Earnings per share (₹) | 11.93 | 14.50 | +21.6% |
| Return on average equity | 19.6% | 20.3% | +0.7 pts |

1.1 Revenue grew on higher average AUM, strong net inflows into Flexi Cap and Small Cap, and PMS and AIF performance fees. Cost-to-income improved to 61%.

## 6. Auditor's Report

6.1 Raghavan & Mehta LLP issued an unmodified opinion dated 25 April 2026.
