# Sales Sheet – Kaveri Focused Equity PMS (for Relationship Managers)

**Internal use only. Do not send this sheet to clients.** Use the approved investor presentation and the Disclosure Document with clients. Data as on 31 March 2026.

## 1. The strategy in one line

A concentrated portfolio of 20–25 high-quality Indian businesses, managed by our CIO, bought at sensible prices and held for the long term. "Patient capital, clear thinking."

## 4. Fee options you can offer

| Option | Management fee | Performance fee | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Option A – Fixed** | 2.0% p.a. | None | Clients who want predictable costs |
| **Option B – Hybrid** | 1.0% p.a. | 20% of returns above a 10% p.a. hurdle, with high-water mark | Clients who prefer to pay more only when returns are strong |

- These are the **only two fee options**. RMs may not offer discounts, rebates or a custom fee. Any non-standard fee needs CFO and MD & CEO approval and is normally offered only to institutional mandates.
- The client may switch option once a year, on the account anniversary.
- Other charges: custody, fund accounting, demat and audit capped at 0.25% p.a.; brokerage at actuals; no onboarding fee. GST extra.
- Exit load: 3% in year 1, 2% in year 2, 1% in year 3, nil after 3 years (from the date of each contribution).

## 5. Talking points

1. **CIO-managed.** The PMS is run by our CIO, with a 10-year track record and support from 34 analysts covering 142 stocks.
2. **Own demat account.** Clients see every stock they own; nothing is pooled.
3. **Concentrated but disciplined.** Single stock capped at 10% at cost; sector at 25% (banks up to 30%); at least 60% large cap.
4. **Quality filters.** Return on capital employed above 15% through a cycle, clean governance, entry only when expected 5-year IRR exceeds 15%.
5. **Fee choice.** Fixed or performance-linked, the client decides.
6. **Lower drawdown in the last 3 years.** Maximum drawdown of -14.2% against -15.8% for the benchmark.

## 6. Who it suits, and who it does not

| Suits | Does not suit |
|---|---|
| Investors with ₹50 lakh or more and a horizon of at least 5 years | Anyone who may need the money within 3 years (exit load and volatility) |
| Investors comfortable with a concentrated equity portfolio | Investors with a "conservative" risk profile |
| HNIs and family offices wanting direct ownership of stocks | Investors who want daily liquidity at NAV (suggest our mutual-fund-style schemes) |

## 7. What an RM must never do or promise

- **Never promise or imply guaranteed returns**, a minimum return, or that the strategy will beat the benchmark. Say "there is no assurance that the objectives will be achieved".
- Never project the past 5-year return into the future ("you will double your money in 4 years").
- Never offer a fee discount, cashback, gift or rebate to win a client. Gifts above ₹3,000 per person per year are not allowed under the Code of Conduct.
- Never accept cash, and never accept a cheque from someone other than the client.
- Never share current holdings beyond the published top 5, or any Investment Committee or research view that is not public.
- Never sign up a client whose risk profile does not match the product.
- Never send this sheet, WhatsApp screenshots of performance, or your own charts to a client; use only Compliance-approved material.

Breaches are treated as mis-selling. Mis-selling complaints are investigated by Compliance, not by Sales.

## 8. Handling common objections

| Client says | Suggested answer |
|---|---|
| "2% is expensive." | Offer Option B: 1.0% fixed and a performance fee only above a 10% hurdle and high-water mark. |
| "Why not a mutual fund?" | A mutual fund is cheaper and more liquid; the PMS offers concentration, direct ownership and a CIO-run portfolio. Both are valid choices. |
| "The small cap fund underperformed last year." | That is a different product; the PMS holds at most 15% small caps. Show the PMS numbers only. |
| "Can I start with ₹30 lakh?" | No. SEBI sets the PMS minimum at ₹50 lakh. Suggest the Flexi Cap Fund instead. |
