# Compliance Monitoring Calendar FY27

Working calendar for the Compliance team for 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2027. Each line has an owner and a hard due date. Items move to "Done" in the compliance tracker only when evidence is uploaded.

## 1. How to use this calendar

- **Owner codes:** CCO = Shalini Kapoor; AML = AML Analyst; GRO = Grievance Redressal Officer; CMP = Compliance Manager; OPS = Head of Operations (Mohit Arora); RSK = CRO office (Varun Pillai); FIN = CFO office (Gaurav Agarwal).
- If a due date falls on a holiday, the item is due on the **previous** working day.
- Anything overdue by more than 3 working days goes on the CCO's weekly exception list.

## 2. Recurring – every working day

- Check SCORES for new complaints at 10:00 am (GRO).
- Review overnight sanctions and PEP screening hits (AML).
- Review pre-clearance requests for personal trades and update the restricted list (CMP).
