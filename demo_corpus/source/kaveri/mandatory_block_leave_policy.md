# Mandatory Block Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

Mandatory block leave is a **fraud-control measure**. When a person who controls trades is away from the desk for a continuous period, any concealed positions, unauthorised trades, mis-booked orders or improper broker arrangements are more likely to surface, because someone else must run their book. The policy is also designed to support well-being in roles with high market-hours pressure.

## 2. Who must take block leave

2.1 Block leave is mandatory for:

- all **fund managers**, including co-fund managers and the CIO (Equity) and Head of Fixed Income in their capacity as fund managers;
- all **dealers** on the equity and fixed-income dealing desks in Mumbai;
- the PMS portfolio managers and the AIF portfolio manager.

2.2 As at 1 January 2026, 19 employees are covered: 9 fund managers / portfolio managers and 10 dealers.

2.3 The CRO may designate additional roles (for example, fund accountants with booking rights) by written notice to HR.

## 3. The requirement

3.1 Each covered employee must take **10 consecutive working days** of leave in every calendar year.

3.2 Weekends and public holidays falling within the block do not count toward the 10 working days, but do not break continuity.

3.3 The block is debited from the employee's privilege leave (20 days per year under the Leave Policy 2026). If PL is insufficient, HR will grant special leave for the shortfall.

3.4 The block may not be split. A block interrupted by a recall (see section 7) must be retaken in full.

## 4. Restrictions during block leave

During block leave the employee:

1. must not log in to the order management system (OMS), dealing terminals or Bloomberg-style market terminals;
2. must not place, amend or instruct any order, including by phone or message to a colleague;
3. must not access the Company email or Teams, except to respond to an explicit request from the CRO or CCO;
4. must hand over all physical tokens (hardware keys for the OMS) to the alternate.

IT disables OMS and dealing-line access for the full period on the start date and restores it on the return date. Access logs are reviewed by Risk after the block ends.

## 5. Alternates and handover

| Role on leave | Alternate | Approver of plan |
|---|---|---|
| Fund manager – Bluechip / Focused Equity PMS | Co-fund manager or Divya Menon | CIO (Equity) |
| Fund manager – Flexi Cap | Ashok Venkataraman | CIO (Equity) |
| Fund manager – Small Cap / AIF III | Designated co-fund manager | CIO (Equity) |
| Fund manager – Short Duration / Arbitrage | Senior fixed-income analyst | MD & CEO |
| CIO (Equity) as fund manager | Head of Research as acting chair of IC; Divya Menon for trades | MD & CEO |
| Dealer | Another dealer on the same desk | Head of Dealing |

A written handover listing all open orders, pending corporate actions, unsettled trades and broker commitments must be given to the alternate at least **1 working day** before the block starts.

## 6. Scheduling

6.1 Covered employees submit their proposed block dates by **31 January** each year.

6.2 HR and the CIO's office publish the annual block-leave calendar by **15 February**, ensuring that no two fund managers of the same scheme and no more than **2 dealers** from the same desk are on block leave at the same time.

6.3 Block leave should not be scheduled in the last 5 working days of a quarter or around a scheme's NFO.

6.4 Blocks must be completed by **30 November**. HR sends reminders on 1 August and 1 October to anyone yet to complete their block.

## 7. Recall during block leave

7.1 An employee may be recalled only in a genuine emergency, with the written approval of the MD & CEO and the CRO.

7.2 A recall is logged and reported to the Board Risk Committee. The employee must retake a full 10-working-day block within 90 days.

## 8. Review after the block

During and after each block, the Risk team performs:

- a reconciliation of the employee's book against custodian records;
- a review of broker confirmations received during the absence;
- a check of exceptions, cancelled or amended trades for the preceding 3 months.

Findings are reported to the CRO within 10 working days of the employee's return.

## 9. Non-compliance

| Situation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Block not completed by 31 December | Reported to Board Risk Committee; access to OMS suspended until block is taken |
| Login to OMS during block | Treated as a breach of the Code of Conduct and Ethics; investigation by Risk |
| Handover not provided | Block start deferred; reminder recorded in performance file |

Completion of block leave is a gating condition for the annual bonus of covered employees under the Performance and Bonus Framework.

## 10. Exemptions

Exemptions are granted only by the MD & CEO on the recommendation of the CRO, for a single year, and only where an alternative fraud control is put in place (for example, a full independent review of the employee's book). In calendar year 2025, no exemptions were granted and all 18 covered employees completed their block.
