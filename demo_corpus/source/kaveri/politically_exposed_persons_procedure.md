# Politically Exposed Persons Procedure

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This procedure sets out how Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. identifies, approves and monitors Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs), their family members and close associates. It supports clause 5.2 of the KYC and Customer Due Diligence Policy (KCA/CMP/POL-011).

1.2 **Every PEP relationship requires the prior written approval of the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO).** No account, folio, PMS agreement or AIF commitment may be activated for a PEP before that approval is recorded.

## 2. Who is a PEP

2.1 For Kaveri's purposes a PEP is an individual who is, or has been in the last 5 years, entrusted with a prominent public function, in India or abroad, including:

- Heads of State or Government, Union and State Ministers, Members of Parliament and of State Legislatures;
- senior officials of the Union or State Governments at the level of Joint Secretary or above;
- judges of the Supreme Court and High Courts;
- senior military officers of the rank of Major General or equivalent and above;
- chairpersons, managing directors and whole-time directors of public sector undertakings and public sector banks;
- office-bearers of national and state political parties.

2.2 The definition extends to:

| Category | Who is included |
|---|---|
| Family members | Spouse, parents, children and their spouses, siblings |
| Close associates | Business partners, persons with joint ownership of a legal entity with the PEP, and persons known to act on the PEP's behalf |
| PEP-controlled entities | Companies, trusts and family offices in which a PEP is a beneficial owner |

2.3 A person who left public office more than 5 years ago may be de-classified by the CCO after a documented review, unless adverse information exists.

## 3. Identification

3.1 Every client is asked in the onboarding form: "Are you, or is any family member or close associate, a Politically Exposed Person?"

3.2 Independently of the declaration, the KYC Desk screens every applicant and beneficial owner against the commercial PEP database at onboarding, and the full client base is re-screened every night.

3.3 A potential match is reviewed by the KYC Desk supervisor within 2 working days and either discounted (with reasons) or confirmed as a PEP.

## 4. Approval Process

| Step | Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collect EDD pack: source-of-wealth statement, source-of-funds evidence (last 3 years' ITR or audited accounts), adverse-media report | Relationship Manager | Before submission |
| 2 | Review EDD pack and prepare PEP Approval Note with risk rating | Compliance – AML Analyst | Within 3 working days |
| 3 | Approve or decline | Chief Compliance Officer | Within 5 working days of receiving the Note |
| 4 | Inform MD & CEO of approved PEP relationships with investment above ₹5 crore | CCO | Within 2 working days of approval |
| 5 | Tag account as "PEP – High Risk" in the registrar and PMS systems | KYC Desk | Same day as approval |

4.1 The CCO's approval cannot be delegated. If the CCO is on leave, the PEP application waits; it is not passed to another approver.

4.2 Where an existing client becomes a PEP (e.g. is elected or appointed), the relationship may continue only if the CCO approves its continuation within 30 days of Kaveri becoming aware. Until then, fresh subscriptions are blocked; redemptions are allowed.

## 5. Ongoing Monitoring

5.1 PEP clients are permanently in the High-risk category and therefore subject to re-KYC **every 2 years**.

5.2 In addition, the AML Analyst performs an annual PEP review covering: transactions in the year against the stated source of funds; fresh adverse media; and whether the PEP still holds office. The review is signed off by the CCO.

5.3 Any single transaction by a PEP above ₹1 crore generates a mandatory alert, which must be reviewed within 5 working days.

## 6. Declined Applications

6.1 The CCO shall decline a PEP application where the source of wealth cannot be reasonably explained, where there is credible adverse media relating to corruption, or where the PEP is from a jurisdiction on the FATF "call for action" list.

6.2 Reasons for decline are recorded internally. The applicant is told only that Kaveri is unable to open the account.

## 7. PEP Register

7.1 Compliance maintains a PEP Register with: client name, folio/PMS code, PEP category, date of CCO approval, investment value, next re-KYC date, and date of last annual review.

7.2 As of 31 March 2026, the PEP Register contains 37 active relationships: 21 in mutual-fund schemes, 14 PMS clients and 2 AIF investors. Total investment from PEP clients was ₹186 crore.

## 8. Reporting

8.1 The CCO reports the number of PEP relationships, new approvals and declines to the Board every quarter. In FY2025-26, the CCO approved 9 new PEP relationships and declined 3.
