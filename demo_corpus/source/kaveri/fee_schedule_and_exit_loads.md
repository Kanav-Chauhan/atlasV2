# Fee Schedule and Exit Loads

This Schedule applies to all Kaveri Capital Advisors products from 1 April 2026 and forms part of the PMS Client Agreement – Standard Terms (KCA/PRD/AGR-001) and the AIF contribution agreement. All fees are exclusive of GST.

## 1. Mutual-Fund-Style Schemes

1.1 Total Expense Ratio (TER) of the direct plan as of 1 April 2026. Regular-plan TER includes distributor commission and is higher.

| Scheme | TER – Direct | TER – Regular | Exit load |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kaveri Bluechip Fund | 0.82% | 1.74% | 1% if redeemed within 365 days |
| Kaveri Flexi Cap Fund | 0.91% | 1.86% | 1% if redeemed within 365 days |
| Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities | 1.12% | 1.84% | 1% if redeemed within 365 days |
| Kaveri Short Duration Fund | 0.38% | 0.92% | Nil |
| Kaveri Arbitrage Fund | 0.35% | 1.02% | 0.25% if redeemed within 15 days |

1.2 Exit load on equity schemes is calculated on a first-in, first-out basis, unit by unit. Switches between Kaveri schemes are treated as redemptions for exit load purposes. Units allotted on dividend reinvestment carry no exit load.

1.3 No entry load is charged on any scheme.

## 2. Kaveri Focused Equity PMS

2.1 **Minimum investment: ₹50 lakh.** The Client chooses one fee option at onboarding.

| Fee option | Management fee | Performance fee | Hurdle | High-water mark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Option A – Fixed | 2.0% p.a. | None | — | — |
| Option B – Hybrid | 1.0% p.a. | 20% of returns above hurdle | 10% p.a. | Yes |

### 2.2 How the performance fee works (Option B)

- The hurdle is 10% per year on the capital, pro-rated for part-years and adjusted for additions and withdrawals.
- Performance fee is charged only on returns above **both** the hurdle and the high-water mark.
- It is computed and charged once a year on the anniversary date.

### 2.3 Worked example (Option B)

| Item | Amount (₹) |
|---|---|
| Opening Portfolio value (also High-Water Mark) | 1,00,00,000 |
| Closing value before fees after 1 year | 1,18,00,000 |
| Less management fee at 1.0% (approx.) | 1,09,000 |
| Value after management fee | 1,16,91,000 |
| Hurdle value (10% on ₹1 crore) | 1,10,00,000 |
| Excess above hurdle | 6,91,000 |
| Performance fee at 20% | 1,38,200 |
| Closing value after all fees | 1,15,52,800 |
| New High-Water Mark | 1,15,52,800 |

Under Option A the same Portfolio would pay about ₹2,18,000 in fixed fees.

## 3. Kaveri Long-Short AIF III

3.1 **Minimum commitment: ₹1 crore** (₹25 lakh for eligible employees and directors, as permitted by SEBI).

| Fee | Rate |
|---|---|
| Management fee | 1.5% p.a. of NAV, charged quarterly |
| Performance fee | 15% of returns above the hurdle |
| Hurdle | 8% p.a., with high-water mark |
| Lock-in | 12 months from each contribution |
| Exit after lock-in | 1% if redeemed in months 13–24; nil after |
| Redemption frequency | Monthly, with 30 days' notice |

## 4. Institutional and Family-Office Mandates

4.1 Fees for institutional mandates are negotiated per mandate within the grid in the Institutional Mandate Term Sheet Template (KCA/PRD/AGR-003). Any fee below the grid floor requires CFO and MD & CEO approval.

## 5. Changes to Fees

5.1 For PMS and AIF clients, any increase in fees requires 30 days' written notice; the client may exit without exit load during the notice period.

5.2 For mutual-fund-style schemes, TER changes are notified on the Kaveri website at least 3 working days in advance, as required by SEBI.

## 6. Distributor Commission (for information)

6.1 Distributor commission is paid only from the regular plan TER and is fully trail-based; no upfront commission is paid. Commission rates are disclosed in the investor's account statement.
