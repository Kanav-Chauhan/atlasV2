# GIFT City Feeder Fund Proposal

Board paper for the meeting of 25 April 2026. The Board approved this proposal (Resolution 58.3).

## 1. Recommendation

1.1 Kaveri should set up a fund management entity (FME) in GIFT City International Financial Services Centre (IFSC), Gandhinagar, and launch a US-dollar feeder fund, the **Kaveri India Flexi Cap Feeder Fund (IFSC)**, investing substantially all its assets in the Kaveri Flexi Cap Fund. Target launch: **January 2027**.

1.2 The Board is asked to approve: (a) set-up costs of ₹6.5 crore in FY2026-27; (b) seed capital of ₹25 crore from own funds; and (c) authority for the MD & CEO to make filings with the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA).

## 2. Why Now

- **Demand.** Kaveri receives about 40 enquiries a month from NRIs in the Gulf, Singapore, the UK and the US who want Indian equity exposure but face KYC, FATCA and repatriation friction with domestic schemes.
- **Track record.** The Flexi Cap Fund returned 17.6% in the year to 31 March 2026 against 15.1% for the Nifty 500 TRI, and has outperformed over 3 and 5 years.
- **Tax and currency.** An IFSC fund lets investors subscribe and redeem in USD, with IFSC tax treatment, and avoids rupee repatriation steps for the investor.
- **Peers.** Several mid-sized Indian managers have launched IFSC feeders since 2024; Kaveri risks losing NRI clients to them.

4.2 Revenue includes the incremental management fee earned in the Flexi Cap Fund on feeder money and a feeder-level fee. Fee levels will be finalised with the offer document and are not part of this approval.

## 5. Team

5.1 Five new roles (included in the FY27 headcount of 338): Principal Officer – IFSC; compliance officer – IFSC; fund operations (2); investor relations (1). Divya Menon remains fund manager of the underlying fund; no separate investment team is needed in year 1.

## 6. Seed Capital

6.1 ₹25 crore (about USD 3 million) will be invested at launch and held for at least 3 years, under the Treasury and Investment of Own Funds Policy. This seed provides a starting AUM and meets IFSCA's manager contribution requirement.

## 7. Risks and Mitigants

| Risk | Mitigant |
|---|---|
| IFSCA approval delay | File FME application by 30 June 2026; legal counsel engaged; launch has 2 months' slack |
| Weak demand | Pre-marketing to 300 existing NRI clients; soft commitments target USD 15 million before launch |
| Currency volatility | Disclosed to investors; no hedging at fund level in year 1 |
| Flexi Cap capacity | Feeder money capped at 10% of Flexi Cap AUM; CIO reviews quarterly |
| Compliance burden (FATCA/CRS, foreign KYC) | IFSC compliance officer hire; outsourced KYC review for non-Indian investors |
| Reputational risk if underperforming | Same fund manager and process as domestic fund; no separate strategy |
