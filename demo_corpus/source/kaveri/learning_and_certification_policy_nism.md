# Learning and Certification Policy – NISM

## 1. Purpose

SEBI requires that certain categories of employees of portfolio managers, mutual funds, investment advisers and research analysts hold valid certifications from the National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM). This policy sets out which certifications are mandatory at Kaveri Capital, the deadlines to obtain and renew them, and the support the firm provides for professional learning more broadly.

## 2. Scope

All employees. Mandatory certifications depend on role; voluntary programmes are open to all permanent employees with at least 6 months of service.

## 3. Mandatory certifications by role

| Role | Mandatory NISM certification | Deadline for new joiners |
|---|---|---|
| PMS portfolio managers and principal officer | NISM Series XXI-B: Portfolio Managers | Before appointment |
| PMS relationship managers and distribution staff | NISM Series XXI-A: PMS Distributors | Within 90 days of joining |
| Research analysts | NISM Series XV: Research Analyst | Within 90 days of joining |
| Mutual fund distribution / sales staff | NISM Series V-A: Mutual Fund Distributors | Within 90 days of joining |
| Compliance officers | NISM Series III-A: Securities Intermediaries Compliance | Within 180 days of joining |
| Investment advisory staff (wealth) | NISM Series X-A and X-B: Investment Adviser | Within 180 days of joining |
| Operations (fund accounting) | NISM Series V-B: Mutual Fund Foundation | Within 180 days of joining |
| AIF team | NISM Series XIX-C: Alternative Investment Fund Managers | Within 90 days of joining |

An employee who does not pass the required certification by the deadline is moved to a non-client-facing role (or a role that does not require the certificate) until it is obtained. They may not sign research reports, advise clients or manage portfolios.

## 4. Renewal

4.1 NISM certificates are valid for **3 years**. Employees must renew through the Continuing Professional Education (CPE) programme or by re-taking the examination.

4.2 HR tracks expiry dates and sends reminders **120 days** and **60 days** before expiry.

4.3 Renewal must be completed at least **30 days before expiry**. A lapsed certificate is treated the same as a missed initial deadline.

4.4 As at 31 March 2026, 214 employees held at least one NISM certificate and 3 certificates were due for renewal in Q1 FY27.

## 5. Firm support for NISM

| Item | Support |
|---|---|
| Examination fee | Reimbursed in full for the first two attempts |
| CPE programme fee | Reimbursed in full |
| Study material | Provided by the Learning team |
| Study leave | Up to 5 days of examination leave per year (see Leave Policy 2026) |
| Third and later attempts | Fee borne by the employee |

## 6. Voluntary professional qualifications

The firm sponsors selected professional qualifications that build investment capability.

| Qualification | Eligible | Sponsorship | Service bond |
|---|---|---|---|
| CFA (Levels I–III) | Investment, research, risk staff | 100% of exam and registration fees per level passed; 50% if failed | 12 months after last reimbursement |
| FRM (Parts I–II) | Risk and fixed-income staff | 100% per part passed | 12 months |
| CAIA | AIF and product staff | 75% per level passed | 12 months |
| Executive programmes (IIM / ISB) | G2 and above | Up to ₹4 lakh per programme | 24 months |

Employees who leave before the end of the service bond repay the sponsored amount pro rata.

## 7. Annual learning budget

7.1 Each employee has an annual learning budget of **₹40,000** (G3 and G4) or **₹75,000** (G1 and G2) for courses, conferences and books, in addition to NISM and qualification sponsorship.

7.2 The firm-wide learning budget for FY2026-27 is ₹1.6 crore.

7.3 Every employee must complete at least **24 hours** of learning in the financial year. Mandatory compliance modules (Code of Conduct, insider trading, AML/KYC, information security, POSH) count toward this.

## 8. Mandatory internal modules

| Module | Frequency | Pass mark |
|---|---|---|
| Code of Conduct and Ethics | Annual | 80% |
| Insider trading prevention (SEBI PIT) | Annual | 80% |
| AML / KYC awareness | Annual | 80% |
| Information security | Annual | 80% |
| POSH awareness | Annual | Completion |

## 9. Process to claim reimbursement

1. Obtain manager approval in the HR portal before registering.
2. Register and pay; keep receipts.
3. Upload the result or completion certificate within **30 days**.
4. HR verifies and Finance pays with the next payroll.

## 10. Link to performance

Failure to hold a mandatory certification by the deadline is a gating condition under the Performance and Bonus Framework: no bonus is paid for that year.

## 11. Ownership

Owner: Head of HR. The Chief Compliance Officer confirms the list of mandatory certifications each April against SEBI circulars.
