## 7. Fund manager commentary

Indian large caps delivered positive returns in FY2025-26 despite a volatile third quarter, supported by steady domestic flows and an improving credit cycle. The fund outperformed the Nifty 100 TRI by 1.3 percentage points over the year (14.2% versus 12.9%).

The main contributors were our overweight in private and PSU banks, where loan growth of around 13% and stable asset quality supported earnings, and our position in capital goods, which benefited from a strong order pipeline in power transmission and railways. The underweight in FMCG also helped as volume growth stayed muted. Detractors were our holding in Sindhu Petro, which lagged on weaker refining margins, and our underweight in IT services during a short rally in January.

We continue to prefer domestic cyclicals – banks, capital goods and select automobiles – over export-oriented sectors. The portfolio remains diversified across 48 stocks, with cash at 3.8%.
