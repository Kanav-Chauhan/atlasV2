# Institutional Mandate Term Sheet Template

## 1. How to use this template

1.1 This template is used for institutional and family-office mandates, which are managed by Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. under its PMS registration (INP000006120) or as investment advisory. Kaveri manages 85 institutional and family-office mandates as of 31 March 2026.

1.2 Text in **[square brackets]** is completed per client. Terms marked **(Standard)** may not be changed without Compliance approval. Commercial terms outside the grid in section 4 need CFO and MD & CEO approval.

1.3 The term sheet is non-binding except for clauses 10 (Confidentiality) and 11 (Exclusivity). The binding agreement is the PMS Client Agreement – Standard Terms (KCA/PRD/AGR-001) with an Institutional Annexure.

## 4. Fee Grid

4.1 Management fee on average daily assets, charged quarterly in arrears:

| Mandate size | Equity – discretionary | Fixed income – discretionary | Advisory (any asset class) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₹100 crore to ₹250 crore | 0.60% p.a. | 0.25% p.a. | 0.30% p.a. |
| ₹250 crore to ₹500 crore | 0.50% p.a. | 0.20% p.a. | 0.25% p.a. |
| Above ₹500 crore | 0.40% p.a. | 0.15% p.a. | 0.20% p.a. |
| **Floor (cannot go lower without CFO and MD & CEO approval)** | **0.35% p.a.** | **0.12% p.a.** | **0.15% p.a.** |

4.2 Optional performance fee for equity mandates: up to 10% of outperformance over the benchmark, measured over rolling 3 years, with high-water mark. If a performance fee is agreed, the management fee is reduced by 0.10% p.a.

4.3 Fees are tiered on the whole mandate (not slab-wise) and are exclusive of GST.

## 6. Guideline Breaches (Standard)

6.1 Active breaches of client guidelines are corrected within 1 working day at Kaveri's cost; any resulting loss is compensated by Kaveri.

6.2 Passive breaches are notified to the client within 2 working days and corrected within 10 working days unless the client agrees otherwise in writing.

## 7. Voting (Standard)

7.1 Kaveri votes proxies in line with its Stewardship Code and voting policy, unless the client reserves voting rights in Annexure A. Voting records are reported quarterly.
