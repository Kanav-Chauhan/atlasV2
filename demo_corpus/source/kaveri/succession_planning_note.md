# Succession Planning Note

Prepared for the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC) and the Board, as committed at the Board meeting of 25 April 2026. Strictly confidential: circulation limited to directors and the MD & CEO. Individuals named as successors have **not** been told of their status unless stated.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 To make sure that the loss of any key leader, planned or sudden, does not disrupt the management of ₹18,650 crore of client assets, regulatory standing, or client confidence.

1.2 The plan covers 11 critical roles: the MD & CEO, the investment leaders, and the heads of control and support functions.

## 2. Readiness Scale

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| R0 | Ready now |
| R1 | Ready in 1–2 years |
| R2 | Ready in 3–5 years |
| EXT | No internal candidate; external search needed |

3.1 **Emergency succession** means the interim successor steps in immediately (within 1 working day) for up to 6 months while the Board decides on a permanent appointment.

## 4. MD & CEO Succession

4.1 Mr. Iyengar's current term as Managing Director ends on **31 March 2028**. He has indicated willingness to serve one further term but has asked the Board to have a ready successor by then.

4.2 If Mr. Iyengar is unavailable suddenly, **Ashok Venkataraman** becomes interim MD & CEO. During that period, **Divya Menon** acts as interim CIO (Equity), and the Flexi Cap Fund is co-managed by Arjun Mehra.

4.3 Development plan for Mr. Venkataraman: lead the GIFT City launch steering group; attend all BRC and Audit Committee meetings from July 2026; executive leadership programme in FY27; mentoring by the Chairperson.

4.4 The NRC will run an external benchmarking exercise in FY28 so that the Board can compare internal and external candidates before 30 September 2027.

## 5. Key-Person Risk – Kaveri Long-Short AIF III

5.1 Rahul Saxena is the named **key person** in the AIF's private placement memorandum. If he ceases to manage the fund, a key-person event occurs: new investments are suspended, and investors holding at least 50% of commitments may vote to wind up the fund or approve a replacement within 90 days.

5.2 Because the successor for Mr. Saxena is rated R2, this is the most serious gap in the plan. Actions:

- appoint Sneha Kulkarni as co-fund manager of the AIF by 31 December 2026, subject to NISM certification, so that a second key person can be named in the next PPM amendment;
- extend Mr. Saxena's deferred long-term incentive vesting to 4 years.

## 6. Control Function Gaps

6.1 The CCO and Head of HR roles have no internal successor (EXT). For the CCO role, the NRC recommends hiring a Deputy CCO in FY27 within the 3 risk and compliance roles approved in the budget.
