# Board Meeting Minutes – 25 April 2026

Minutes of the 58th meeting of the Board of Directors of Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. held on Saturday, 25 April 2026 at 11:00 am at the registered office, 12th Floor, Prestige Meridian II, MG Road, Bengaluru 560001, with video-conference facility.

## 1. Attendance

**Directors present**

- Ms. Nandini Krishnan – Independent Director and Chairperson
- Mr. Ramesh Iyengar – Managing Director & CEO
- Ms. Leela Fernandes – Independent Director; Chair, Audit Committee
- Mr. Prakash Hegde – Independent Director; Chair, Board Risk Committee (by video-conference from Mumbai)
- Mr. Anil Mathur – Non-Executive Director

**In attendance**

- Mr. Gaurav Agarwal – Chief Financial Officer
- Ms. Shalini Kapoor – Chief Compliance Officer
- Mr. Varun Pillai – Chief Risk Officer
- Mr. Ashok Venkataraman – Chief Investment Officer (Equity) (items 5 and 6)
- Ms. Kavya Rao – Company Secretary
- Partner, Raghavan & Mehta LLP, statutory auditors (item 3 only)

1.1 The Chairperson confirmed that the quorum was present throughout. All directors confirmed there was no conflict of interest on any agenda item.

## 2. Minutes of the Previous Meeting

2.1 The minutes of the 57th meeting held on 23 January 2026 were confirmed and signed.

## 3. Audited Financial Statements FY2025-26

3.1 The CFO presented the audited financial statements: total revenue ₹212 crore (FY25: ₹181.0 crore), profit after tax ₹58 crore (FY25: ₹47.7 crore), cost-to-income ratio 61%. AUM at 31 March 2026 was ₹18,650 crore.

3.2 The audit partner confirmed that Raghavan & Mehta LLP would issue an unmodified (clean) opinion. A management letter with five observations (one rated High, on PMS performance fee computation) will follow and be tracked by the Audit Committee.

**Resolution 58.1:** "RESOLVED THAT the audited standalone financial statements of the Company for the year ended 31 March 2026, together with the Auditors' Report, be and are hereby approved and adopted, and that the MD & CEO and CFO be authorised to sign them."

## 4. FY2026-27 Budget

4.1 The CFO presented the FY27 budget: revenue ₹248 crore, employee cost ₹96 crore, technology spend ₹14 crore, total operating expenses ₹148.8 crore, PAT ₹69.7 crore, and 28 net new roles taking headcount to 338.

4.2 Ms. Fernandes asked that the March draft's technology line of ₹13.2 crore be increased to cover the straight-through processing project flagged by audit. The CFO agreed to raise it to ₹14 crore within the same PAT target. Mr. Hegde asked for a downside case reflecting the March 2026 stress test, to be presented to the Board Risk Committee in July 2026.

**Resolution 58.2:** "RESOLVED THAT the budget for FY2026-27, with total revenue of ₹248 crore, employee cost of ₹96 crore and technology spend of ₹14 crore, be and is hereby approved."

## 5. GIFT City Feeder Fund

5.1 The CIO and CFO presented the proposal for a USD-denominated feeder fund at GIFT City (IFSC), investing in the Kaveri Flexi Cap Fund, aimed at NRI and foreign investors. Target launch **January 2027**. Set-up cost ₹6.5 crore over FY27; seed capital ₹25 crore from own funds.

5.2 The Board discussed regulatory approval timelines with IFSCA, currency risk, and whether demand estimates were conservative. Mr. Mathur asked that the business case be reviewed after the first 12 months.

**Resolution 58.3:** "RESOLVED THAT the Company be and is hereby authorised to establish a fund management entity in GIFT City IFSC and launch a feeder fund investing in Kaveri Flexi Cap Fund, with a target launch in January 2027, and to invest seed capital of up to ₹25 crore; and that the MD & CEO be authorised to make all filings with IFSCA and other authorities."

## 6. Board Risk Committee Report Q4 FY26

6.1 Mr. Hegde presented the Board Risk Committee report. The Board noted the breach of the Small Cap liquidity floor (11.8% against the 15% minimum) and the approved remediation plan to trim six illiquid holdings by 30 June 2026, with an interim checkpoint of 13.5% by 15 May 2026, while the existing cap on lump-sum subscriptions of ₹2 lakh per PAN per day stays in force.

6.2 Kaveri Bluechip Fund VaR was 1.9% against the 2.5% limit. The Board directed the CRO to report to the Chairperson by email if the 15 May checkpoint is missed.

6.3 The Board noted the report. No resolution required.

## 7. Interim Dividend

7.1 The CFO confirmed that, after the dividend, the liquidity buffer under the Treasury Policy (6 months of operating expenses, about ₹75 crore) and all regulatory net worth requirements would be met with large headroom.

**Resolution 58.4:** "RESOLVED THAT an interim dividend of ₹3.00 per equity share of ₹10 each, aggregating ₹12 crore, be and is hereby declared for FY2026-27 out of accumulated profits, to shareholders on the register as on 8 May 2026, payable on or before 25 May 2026."

## 8. Appointment of Independent Director

8.1 On the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board considered the appointment of **Justice (retd.) S. Venugopal**, former judge of a High Court, as an Independent Director. The NRC confirmed that he meets the independence criteria and has no relationship with the Company or its promoters.

**Resolution 58.5:** "RESOLVED THAT Justice (retd.) S. Venugopal be and is hereby appointed as an Additional Director in the category of Independent Director with effect from 1 May 2026, for a term of five years, subject to approval of the shareholders at the next general meeting."

8.2 Justice (retd.) Venugopal will join the Audit Committee and the Board Risk Committee.

## 9. Other Items

9.1 **Treasury Policy v2.1:** approved, adding GIFT City seed approval and a bar on own-fund redemptions from a scheme in liquidity breach (Resolution 58.6).

9.2 **Compliance report:** the CCO reported 6 STRs filed in FY26, 37 PEP relationships, and 216 complaints received with 2 pending at year-end. Noted.

9.3 **Succession planning:** the Chairperson informed the Board that the NRC would circulate a confidential succession planning note before the next meeting.

## 10. Next Meeting

10.1 The next Board meeting will be held on **24 July 2026** in Mumbai (BKC).

The meeting ended at 2:10 pm with a vote of thanks to the Chair.
