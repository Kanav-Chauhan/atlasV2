# FMCG Sector Note

**Sector stance: UNDERWEIGHT (reiterated).** Volume growth is stuck in low single digits, the rural recovery is patchy and valuations remain among the highest in the market. We see better risk-reward in discretionary consumption and would use any defensive rally in staples to reduce exposure further.

## 2. Demand: still waiting for the rural turn

- **Rural volume growth** was 4.8% in Q4 FY26, ahead of urban at 2.6% for the fourth straight quarter, but from a low base. Rural wage growth is about 6% nominal, barely 2% real.
- **Urban demand** is soft at the mass end. Premium products continue to grow at 10–12%, mass products at 1–2%.
- **Quick commerce** now accounts for about 9% of urban FMCG sales in the top 8 cities, adding channel-margin pressure and reducing the advantage of large distribution networks.
- **Monsoon** – a normal monsoon is assumed. A deficit above 10% would cut our FY27 volume estimate by 1–1.5 percentage points.

## 5. Company views

### 5.1 Nirmalaya Consumer Products – HOLD, TP ₹2,560
Market leader in soaps and hair oils with 46% rural sales. Strong balance sheet and dividend payout of 85%, but volume growth has averaged only 3% over eight quarters. We would turn positive only on evidence of sustained rural volume growth above 6%. Bluechip holds 3.1% of NAV against a benchmark weight of 3.9%.

### 5.2 Anandam Foods – REDUCE, TP ₹5,300
Packaged foods and biscuits. Wheat and cocoa inflation will compress gross margin by 120–150 bps in H1 FY27. At 52x FY28E earnings, valuations do not reflect slowing growth.

### 5.3 Shubhra Home Care – ADD, TP ₹1,260
Home and personal care challenger with a strong position in quick commerce (17% of sales). Fastest revenue growth in coverage and improving margins. Our preferred pick in the sector.

## 8. Upgrade triggers

We would move to neutral if two of the following occur:

- rural volume growth above 6% for two consecutive quarters;
- sector P/E falling below 40x one-year forward;
- a cut in GST rates on mass personal-care products;
- a monsoon at or above 105% of the long-period average.

*Rating definitions: BUY – expected return above 15% over 12 months; ADD – 5% to 15%; HOLD – -5% to 5%; REDUCE – below -5%.*
