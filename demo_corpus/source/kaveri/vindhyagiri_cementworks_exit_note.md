# Vindhyagiri Cementworks Exit Note

*Paper for IC Meeting 48 (9 April 2026). Scheme: Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities. Vindhyagiri Cementworks Ltd is a fictional company.*

## 1. Recommendation

Exit the entire holding in **Vindhyagiri Cementworks Ltd** from Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities due to governance concerns, in an orderly manner by **15 May 2026**, and place the stock on the "do not buy" list for all schemes for at least 24 months.

*Outcome: approved at IC 48 on 9 April 2026 as Resolution 48/3 (5 for, 0 against; the fund manager did not vote).*

## 3. Original thesis (August 2023)

Vindhyagiri Cementworks is a 7.5 million tonne per annum regional cement producer with plants in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. The thesis was:

1. consolidation in South Indian cement would lift utilisation from 62% to above 75%;
2. a new 2 MTPA grinding unit near Hyderabad would lower freight costs by ₹180 per tonne;
3. the stock traded at an EV/tonne of USD 62, a 40% discount to peers.

Utilisation did improve to 71% and the grinding unit was commissioned in FY25, so the operating thesis was largely on track.

## 4. Governance concerns

| # | Concern | Date | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Statutory auditor resignation | 18 February 2026 | Auditor resigned mid-term, citing "inadequate information and explanations" on related-party transactions |
| 2 | Related-party advances | Q3 FY26 disclosures | ₹186 crore of advances to a promoter-group real-estate entity, not approved by shareholders; equal to 14% of net worth |
| 3 | Promoter pledge | June 2025 – March 2026 | Pledged shares rose from 18% to 46% of promoter holding in nine months |
| 4 | Delayed results | Q3 FY26 | Results filed 21 days after the regulatory deadline; stock exchange penalty imposed |
| 5 | Board changes | January 2026 | Two independent directors resigned within one month; one cited "differences on governance matters" |

Research downgraded Vindhyagiri Cementworks to REDUCE on 20 February 2026 and flagged the stock to the IC at IC 47.

## 5. Management engagement

The fund manager and the cement analyst met the managing director and CFO on 26 February 2026 and wrote to the board on 3 March 2026 asking for:

- a forensic review of the related-party advances by an independent firm;
- a timeline for the release of pledged shares;
- the appointment of a new auditor of national standing.

The company's reply of 24 March 2026 did not commit to a forensic review and gave no timeline on pledges. We consider the response unsatisfactory.

## 6. Governance checklist result

| Checklist item | Status |

Under the Investment Process and IC Charter, a failed governance checklist requires IC review. Four of five items have failed.

## 8. Alternatives considered

| Option | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Hold and engage further | Rejected: management unresponsive; risk of further value-destructive transfers |
| Partial exit (50%) | Rejected: governance risk is binary; partial holding leaves exposure |
| Vote against at AGM | Will be done if still holding, but insufficient alone |
| Full orderly exit | **Recommended** |

## 9. Lessons for the process

1. Promoter pledge should be monitored monthly, not quarterly, for small-cap holdings; an increase of more than 10 percentage points in a quarter should trigger an automatic review.
2. Auditor resignation should trigger a special IC review within 10 working days.
3. The governance checklist will be re-run for all 74 Small Cap holdings by 31 May 2026.

## 10. Conflict declaration

The fund manager has declared that a relative was employed by Vindhyagiri Cementworks until 2023. Neither the fund manager nor any IC member holds shares in the company.
