# Small Cap Liquidity Breach Remediation Plan

## 1. Breach Details

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Scheme | Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities |
| Scheme AUM (31 March 2026) | ₹2,310 crore |
| Limit | At least 15% of portfolio liquidatable in 5 trading days at 30% participation |
| Actual on 31 March 2026 | 11.8% |
| Breach type | Passive (market volumes and inflows) |
| Breach register ID | BR-FY26-08 |
| Plan submitted | 3 April 2026 |
| Plan approved | 6 April 2026 by CRO and CIO |
| Noted by | Investment Committee 48, 9 April 2026 |
| Final deadline | 30 June 2026 |

## 2. Root Cause

2.1 Traded volumes in the small-cap segment fell by about 34% in February and March 2026. Because our liquidity measure uses the 3-month average daily volume, the fall reduced the liquidity score of almost every holding.

2.2 Net inflows of ₹212 crore in Q4 FY26 were largely added to existing positions, including several low-volume names, rather than to more liquid holdings.

2.3 Six holdings together account for 13.3% of NAV but need between 20 and 41 trading days each to exit at 30% participation. These six names are the main drag on the liquidity score.

All names below are portfolio companies held by the scheme. Current weight as of 31 March 2026.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Vindhya Castings Ltd | 2.6% | 33 | 1.3% | 30.0 |
| 3 | Shivalik Textiles Ltd | 2.4% | 29 | 1.2% | 27.7 |
| 4 | Tapti Specialty Chemicals Ltd | 2.2% | 24 | 1.1% | 25.4 |
| 5 | Nilgiri Agro Foods Ltd | 2.1% | 22 | 1.0% | 25.4 |

3.1 The first five positions will be roughly halved. Vindhyagiri Cementworks will be sold in full by 15 May 2026, as decided by Investment Committee 48 for governance reasons (see the Vindhyagiri Cementworks Exit Note); its sale is part of this plan.

3.2 Proceeds will be redeployed into existing holdings that can be exited within 5 days, chiefly in the capital goods and financials buckets, subject to the 25% sector limit.

## 4. Projected Liquidity

| Checkpoint | Date | Target liquidity (5 days, 30%) | Expected cumulative trimming |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start | 31 March 2026 | 11.8% (actual) | 0% |
| Interim | 15 May 2026 | 13.5% | 50% of planned sales |
| Final | 30 June 2026 | 15.0% minimum (projected 16.1%) | 100% of planned sales |

4.1 The projection assumes market volumes stay at March 2026 levels. If volumes recover, the target will be met earlier.

4.2 Because the Vindhyagiri Cementworks exit is due by 15 May 2026, most of the improvement to the interim checkpoint is expected to come from that sale.

## 5. Execution Rules

5.1 Dealers will not exceed 25% of a stock's daily traded volume on any day, to avoid moving prices against unit-holders. The only exception is Vindhyagiri Cementworks, where Resolution 48/3 permits selling up to 30% of daily traded volume.

5.2 No block sale below a 3% discount to the previous close without CIO approval.

5.3 Trades will be spread across the quarter; no more than 40% of the planned sale in any name in a single week.

## 6. Interim Investor-Protection Measures

6.1 The existing cap on lump-sum subscriptions into Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities of **₹2 lakh per PAN per day** stays in force for the duration of this plan. SIPs and STPs are not affected. The cap will not be relaxed until liquidity has stayed above 17% for 10 consecutive trading days.

6.2 No new positions will be opened in stocks requiring more than 10 days to exit at 30% participation until the breach is closed.

## 7. Monitoring and Escalation

| What | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| Daily liquidity reading | Risk Analytics to Rahul Saxena, CIO, CRO | Every trading day by 10:00 am |
| Weekly progress note (sales done vs plan) | Rahul Saxena to CRO | Every Friday |
| Status to Chief Compliance Officer | CRO | Fortnightly |
| Escalation if interim checkpoint missed | CRO to MD & CEO | Within 1 working day of 15 May 2026 |
| Report to Board Risk Committee | CRO | July 2026 meeting |

7.1 If the 15 May 2026 checkpoint is missed, the CIO may direct a faster sale schedule, and the MD & CEO decides whether to suspend lump-sum subscriptions altogether until the breach is closed.
