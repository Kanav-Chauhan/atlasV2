# PMS Client Agreement – Standard Terms

These Standard Terms form part of every Portfolio Management Services Agreement between Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. (SEBI PMS Registration No. INP000006120), having its registered office at 12th Floor, Prestige Meridian II, MG Road, Bengaluru 560001 ("the Portfolio Manager"), and the client named in the Client Schedule ("the Client"). They are entered into under the SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2020.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Portfolio** means the funds and securities placed by the Client with the Portfolio Manager, together with all accretions.

1.2 **Strategy** means the investment approach chosen by the Client in the Client Schedule. The principal discretionary strategy is the Kaveri Focused Equity PMS, benchmarked to the Nifty 500 TRI.

1.3 **High-Water Mark** means the highest value of the Portfolio, net of fees and adjusted for capital inflows and withdrawals, on which a performance fee has previously been charged.

## 2. Appointment and Services

2.1 The Client appoints the Portfolio Manager to manage the Portfolio on a **discretionary** basis (unless the Client Schedule specifies non-discretionary or advisory service), within the Strategy and the investment restrictions in clause 4.

2.2 The Portfolio Manager shall act in a fiduciary capacity and shall keep the Client's funds and securities segregated in a separate bank account and a separate demat account held in the Client's name, with the custodian appointed under clause 9.

## 3. Capital Contribution

3.1 **Minimum investment: ₹50 lakh**, as prescribed by SEBI. The contribution may be in cash (through banking channels, never physical cash) or in listed securities, valued at the closing price on the day before transfer.

3.2 Additional contributions may be made in multiples of ₹5 lakh, with a minimum of ₹10 lakh.

3.3 Partial withdrawals are permitted provided the Portfolio value after withdrawal does not fall below ₹50 lakh. If it would, the Client must withdraw in full.

## 4. Investment Restrictions

4.1 The Portfolio Manager shall not:

- invest more than 12% of the Portfolio in a single stock or hold more than 25 stocks in the Focused Equity strategy;
- borrow on behalf of the Client, or lend the Client's securities, except as permitted by SEBI;
- invest in unlisted securities beyond 25% of the Portfolio (and in practice the Focused Equity strategy holds none);
- invest in securities of its associates or related parties beyond SEBI limits;
- invest in derivatives except for hedging.

## 5. Fees and Charges

5.1 The Client shall choose one of the following fee structures in the Client Schedule. The fees are detailed in the Fee Schedule and Exit Loads (KCA/PRD/AGR-002).

| Option | Management fee | Performance fee |
|---|---|---|
| A – Fixed | **2.0% p.a.** of average daily Portfolio value | None |
| B – Hybrid | **1.0% p.a.** of average daily Portfolio value | **20% of returns above a 10% p.a. hurdle**, subject to High-Water Mark |

5.2 Management fees are charged quarterly in arrears. Performance fees are charged **only once a year**, on the anniversary of the Portfolio's activation, or on full withdrawal.

5.3 Goods and Services Tax at the prevailing rate is charged in addition to all fees.

5.4 Operating expenses (custody, fund accounting, demat and audit charges) are capped at **0.25% p.a.** of average Portfolio value. Brokerage is charged at actuals and disclosed in the quarterly report.

5.5 The Client may switch between Option A and Option B once a year, effective from the next anniversary date.

## 6. Exit Load

6.1 On full or partial withdrawal, the following exit load applies on the amount withdrawn, measured from the date of each contribution:

| Withdrawal within | Exit load |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | 3% |
| Year 2 | 2% |
| Year 3 | 1% |
| After 3 years | Nil |

## 7. Reporting to the Client

7.1 The Portfolio Manager shall provide:

- a **quarterly report** within 30 days of quarter-end, showing holdings, transactions, fees, expenses and performance against the benchmark;
- an **annual audited statement** of the Portfolio, certified by an independent chartered accountant;
- online access to daily Portfolio value through the Kaveri client portal.

## 8. Client Obligations

8.1 The Client shall keep KYC current, inform Kaveri of any change in address, bank account, tax residence or PEP status within 15 days, and pay fees when due.

8.2 The Client confirms that the funds invested are from legitimate sources and that the Client is not acting for any undisclosed third party.

## 9. Custody

9.1 The Client's securities shall be held with an independent SEBI-registered custodian appointed by the Portfolio Manager. The custodian is named in the Client Schedule.

## 10. Risk Disclosure

10.1 The Client acknowledges that investments in securities are subject to market risk; that past performance does not guarantee future returns; that a concentrated portfolio of up to 25 stocks may be more volatile than the benchmark; and that the Portfolio Manager does not guarantee any return or protect capital.

## 11. Termination

11.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving 30 days' written notice, subject to the exit load in clause 6.

11.2 The Portfolio Manager may terminate by giving **60 days'** written notice, or immediately if the Client breaches KYC or anti-money laundering obligations, or on SEBI direction.

11.3 On termination, the Portfolio Manager shall, at the Client's option, transfer the securities to the Client's own demat account or sell them and pay the proceeds, within **30 days** of the termination date, after deducting fees, exit load and expenses due.

## 12. Grievances and Dispute Resolution

12.1 Complaints may be raised with the Grievance Redressal Officer at service@kaveri.example. If not resolved, the Client may approach SEBI through SCORES and then the online dispute resolution (SMART ODR) mechanism.

12.2 Subject to the above, disputes shall be referred to arbitration seated in Bengaluru under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, and the courts at Bengaluru shall have jurisdiction.

## 13. Governing Law

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
