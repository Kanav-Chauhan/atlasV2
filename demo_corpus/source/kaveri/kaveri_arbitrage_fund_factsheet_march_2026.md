## 2. Investment objective

To generate income by taking advantage of the price difference between the cash and derivatives segments of the equity market, with the balance invested in debt and money-market instruments. There is no assurance that the investment objective of the scheme will be achieved.

## 3. How the fund works

The fund buys a stock in the cash market and simultaneously sells the same quantity in the futures market at a higher price. At expiry the two positions converge and the fund earns the spread, regardless of whether the stock price rises or falls. Spreads are typically higher in periods of strong market activity and near month-end.

## 9. Fund manager commentary

The fund returned 7.3% over FY2025-26, marginally behind the Nifty 50 Arbitrage index at 7.5%, mainly because of higher cash holdings in May–June 2025 when spreads were thin. Arbitrage spreads averaged about 7.2% annualised over the year and widened to 7.6% in March 2026 on heavy futures activity around quarter-end.

With the RBI repo rate at 5.50%, arbitrage funds continue to offer a competitive alternative to liquid and ultra-short funds for investors with a horizon of 3 months or more, with the added benefit of equity taxation. We expect spreads to remain between 6.5% and 7.5% in FY2026-27.
