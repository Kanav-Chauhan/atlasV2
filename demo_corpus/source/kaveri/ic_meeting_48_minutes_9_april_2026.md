## 4. Adoption of the April 2026 house view

The Head of Research presented the *India Strategy Outlook FY27*. Main points: Nifty 50 year-end target of 26,800 (18.5x September 2027E EPS of ₹1,450); overweight banks and capital goods; underweight IT services and FMCG; preference for large and mid caps over small caps.

The CIO asked whether the target was too conservative given falling bond yields. The Head of Research said the target assumes no re-rating and that the bull case of 28,900 captures a re-rating scenario.

**Resolution 48/1:** RESOLVED that the Committee adopts the April 2026 house view, including the Nifty 50 year-end target of 26,800 and the sector stances presented, as the reference for portfolio positioning until the July 2026 review. Carried unanimously (6–0).

## 6. Exit from Vindhyagiri Cementworks Ltd (Small Cap)

Rahul Saxena presented the *Vindhyagiri Cementworks Exit Note*. The fund held 1.1% of NAV (about ₹25 crore) in Vindhyagiri Cementworks Ltd at 31 March 2026. The governance concerns are:

1. resignation of the statutory auditor in February 2026, citing inadequate information on related-party transactions;
2. ₹186 crore of advances to a promoter-group entity, not approved by shareholders;
3. promoter pledge rising from 18% to 46% of their holding in nine months;
4. a 21-day delay in filing Q3 FY26 results.

Members agreed that the investment thesis (regional cement consolidation) no longer outweighed the governance risk. The CCO confirmed that no member of the Committee held Vindhyagiri Cementworks shares personally.

**Resolution 48/3:** RESOLVED that the Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities fund **exit its entire position in Vindhyagiri Cementworks Ltd due to governance concerns**, in an orderly manner by 15 May 2026, selling no more than 30% of the stock's daily traded volume, and that Vindhyagiri Cementworks be placed on the internal "do not buy" list for all schemes for a minimum of 24 months.

Voting: 5 for, 0 against; Rahul Saxena did not vote. **Carried.**
