# Q4 FY26 Risk Dashboard

Quarter ended 31 March 2026. All figures as of 31 March 2026 unless stated. Limits as per the Investment Risk Limits Policy (KCA/RSK/POL-001, v3.0).

## 1. Summary

- **One limit in breach:** Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities 5-day liquidity at **11.8%** against the 15% floor (RED). Remediation plan approved: trim 6 illiquid names by **30 June 2026**.
- **VaR within limits** for all equity schemes. Kaveri Bluechip Fund VaR (95%, 1-day) at **1.9%** against the 2.5% limit. Small Cap VaR at 2.4% is AMBER.
- **Flexi Cap banks exposure** at 23.9% against the 25% sector limit (AMBER).
- **One passive breach in the quarter**, closed: Flexi Cap single-stock weight rose to 10.4% on 18 February 2026 after a sharp rally; restored to 9.6% on 20 February 2026 (2 working days).
- Total AUM ₹18,650 crore (Q3: ₹17,980 crore).

## 3. Liquidity

| Scheme | % liquidatable in 5 days at 30% participation | Floor / trigger | Q3 FY26 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities | 11.8% | 15% floor | 14.1% | RED – BREACH |

3.1 The Small Cap measure fell from 14.1% at 31 December 2025 to 11.8% at 31 March 2026. Causes: (a) a 34% fall in traded volumes across the Nifty Smallcap 250 in February and March 2026; (b) net inflows of ₹212 crore in the quarter, deployed largely into existing positions; (c) six positions in lower-volume names, including Vindhyagiri Cementworks, that together form 13.3% of NAV.

3.2 Per section 7.3 of the Policy, the fund manager (Rahul Saxena) submitted the remediation plan on 3 April 2026; it was approved by the CRO and CIO on 6 April 2026 and noted by Investment Committee 48 on 9 April 2026. Details are in the Small Cap Liquidity Breach Remediation Plan (KCA/RSK/NOTE-015). Interim checkpoint: 13.5% by 15 May 2026.

3.3 Cash and equivalents in Small Cap: 7.1% of NAV. Lump-sum subscriptions remain capped at ₹2 lakh per PAN per day.
