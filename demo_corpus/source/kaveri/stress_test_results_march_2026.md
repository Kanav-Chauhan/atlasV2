# Stress Test Results March 2026

Quarterly stress test as of 31 March 2026 portfolios, run under section 8 of the Investment Risk Limits Policy (KCA/RSK/POL-001). Results are presented to the Board Risk Committee.

## 1. Key Findings

1. Under the severe equity scenario (Nifty 50 −20% instantaneous), Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities would lose an estimated **27.9%** of NAV, the largest loss of any product.
2. Under the redemption stress (25% of Small Cap AUM redeemed in 10 trading days), the scheme would face a **funding shortfall of ₹30.0 crore**, which would have to be met by borrowing. This is consistent with the open liquidity breach (11.8% vs 15% floor).
3. Kaveri Short Duration Fund would lose **3.9%** under a +150 bp parallel rate shock; no redemption shortfall arises.
4. The AIF long-short book loses least in equity scenarios because of its 38% net long exposure.
5. No scheme would breach the 2.5% VaR limit in the base case; under the stressed-volatility scenario, Bluechip VaR would rise from 1.9% to 3.1%.

| ID | Scenario | Type | Key shocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Equity crash | Hypothetical | Nifty 50 −20%; Nifty Smallcap 250 −28%; instantaneous |

| S3 | Covid replay | Historical | Market moves of 20 Feb – 23 Mar 2020 applied to current holdings |
| S4 | Rate shock | Hypothetical | +150 bp parallel shift in G-sec and corporate bond curve; credit spreads +75 bp |
| S5 | Redemption run – Small Cap | Liquidity | 25% of AUM redeemed over 10 trading days |
| S6 | Credit event | Hypothetical | Largest AA-rated issuer in Short Duration downgraded to BB; 25% haircut |
| S7 | Stressed volatility | Hypothetical | Volatility doubled; correlations raised to 0.9 |

| Kaveri Short Duration Fund | −0.1% | −1.2% | −0.9% | −3.9% |

3.1 In rupee terms, S1 would reduce total AUM by about ₹3,165 crore, from ₹18,650 crore to about ₹15,485 crore, before any redemptions.

3.2 The Short Duration loss under S4 reflects its Macaulay duration of 2.6 years. A rate fall of the same size would add about 3.7%.

## 4. Redemption Stress – Small Cap (S5)

| Item | ₹ crore | % of AUM |
|---|---|---|
| Scheme AUM | 2,310.0 | 100.0% |
| Assumed redemptions over 10 trading days | 577.5 | 25.0% |
| Cash and equivalents available | 164.0 | 7.1% |
| Equity liquidatable in 10 days at 30% participation | 383.5 | 16.6% |
| Total available | 547.5 | 23.7% |
| **Shortfall** | **30.0** | **1.3%** |

4.1 The shortfall could legally be met by temporary borrowing (SEBI permits borrowing for redemptions up to 20% of net assets, for up to 6 months), but borrowing would increase cost to remaining unit-holders.

4.2 Selling faster than 30% participation would cover the shortfall but is estimated to cost 2.5% to 4.0% in market impact on the six illiquid names.

4.3 Once the remediation plan is complete (target liquidity of 16.1% at 5 days), S5 shows a surplus of about ₹41 crore instead of a shortfall.

4.4 The same test on Flexi Cap and Bluechip shows no shortfall; both could meet 25% redemptions within 3 trading days.

## 5. Credit Event (S6)

5.1 The largest AA-rated issuer in Kaveri Short Duration Fund is 3.2% of NAV. A downgrade to BB with a 25% valuation haircut would reduce NAV by about **0.80%**. Under the offer document, the scheme could segregate the portfolio (side-pocket) on a downgrade below investment grade; this would protect remaining investors from forced selling.

## 6. Stressed Volatility (S7) – VaR

| Scheme | VaR 95% 1-day (base) | VaR 95% 1-day (stressed) | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kaveri Bluechip Fund | 1.9% | 3.1% | 2.5% |
| Kaveri Flexi Cap Fund | 2.1% | 3.5% | 2.5% |
| Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities | 2.4% | 4.2% | 2.5% |

6.1 VaR limits apply to actual daily VaR, not stressed VaR. The stressed figures show that in a volatility shock all three equity schemes would move into breach together, and limit relief would need Board Risk Committee approval.

## 7. Firm-Level Impact

7.1 Under S1, Kaveri's management fee income would fall by about ₹38 crore on an annualised basis, assuming no recovery. FY2026-27 budgeted revenue (₹248 crore) assumes no such fall. The CFO has been asked to model this downside for the July Board Risk Committee.

## 9. Methodology Notes

- Historical scenarios apply each stock's actual return over the period; for stocks listed after the period, the sector median return is used.
- Liquidity uses 3-month average daily traded volume on NSE and BSE combined.
- Results exclude second-round effects (redemptions triggered by losses) except in S5.
