## 5. Theme proposal: Indian Railways capex

Tanvi Joshi presented the theme paper *Capital Goods and Railways Capex Theme*. Key points:

- Railway capex budget estimate of ₹2.65 lakh crore for FY27, with the mix moving towards signalling, automatic train protection and rolling stock.
- Six theme companies with a combined order book of ₹38,600 crore (3.9x trailing revenue).
- Valuation is the main risk; the sector trades at 34x one-year forward earnings against a 10-year average of 26x.

Discussion: The MD & CEO asked how the theme interacts with the Small Cap liquidity rule. The CRO confirmed that any small-cap theme position must keep the fund within its liquidity floor (at least 15% of the portfolio liquidatable in 5 days at 30% participation) and that new positions would be assessed for liquidity before purchase. Divya Menon proposed that Flexi Cap take the lead. The CCO reminded members that theme stocks under research initiation would be added to the research watch list.

**Resolution 47/1:** RESOLVED that the Committee approves the adoption of **Indian Railways capex** as an investment theme across Kaveri equity portfolios, with the following allocation ranges:

| Portfolio | Allocation range | Single-stock cap within theme |
|---|---|---|
| Kaveri Flexi Cap Fund | 4%–6% of NAV | 3.0% |
| Kaveri Bluechip Fund | 2%–4% of NAV (large caps only) | Per scheme limit |
| Kaveri Small Cap Opportunities | Up to 5% of NAV | 1.5% |
| Kaveri Focused Equity PMS | Up to 8% of model portfolio | 5% |

Voting: 6 for, 0 against. **Carried unanimously.**

**Resolution 47/2:** RESOLVED that Research initiate coverage on Konkan Rail Systems Ltd within 15 days, and that no scheme buy the stock until the initiation report is published.

Voting: 6 for, 0 against.

## 6. House view preview

The Head of Research previewed the April house view, to be adopted at IC 48: a constructive stance on Indian equities, continued overweight in banks, an upgrade of capital goods to overweight, and continued underweights in IT services and FMCG. The year-end Nifty target would be finalised after the RBI policy in early April.

## 7. Small Cap liquidity review

Rahul Saxena tabled the list of 9 Small Cap holdings with average daily traded value below ₹6 crore, together accounting for 15.8% of NAV. The CRO reported that the fund's 5-day liquidity measure stood at 13.9% on 10 March, below the 15% floor. The Committee asked the fund manager and CRO to present a remediation plan at IC 48. The Committee noted that Vindhyagiri Cementworks Ltd was on the list and that Research had flagged governance concerns about it in February; Rahul Saxena will bring a separate note.

## 8. Fixed income

Farida Sheikh reported that the Short Duration Fund's Macaulay duration was 2.47 years and YTM 6.98%. The Committee approved keeping duration in the 2.2–2.6 year range for Q1 FY27.

**Resolution 47/3:** RESOLVED that the Short Duration Fund maintain Macaulay duration between 2.2 and 2.6 years until reviewed. Carried unanimously.
