# Personal Trading Policy

## 1. Purpose

Kaveri Capital manages ₹18,650 crore on behalf of clients. Employees routinely see portfolio orders, research ratings and Investment Committee decisions before they are public. This policy makes sure that personal investing by employees never conflicts with client interests and never uses confidential or price-sensitive information.

## 2. Who is covered

2.1 All employees, directors, interns and consultants of Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd.

2.2 **Immediate relatives** whose investment decisions the employee influences: spouse, dependent children, dependent parents and any person sharing the same household.

2.3 All demat and trading accounts in which a covered person has a beneficial interest, including HUF accounts.

## 3. Covered and exempt securities

| Category | Covered? | Pre-clearance? |
|---|---|---|
| Listed equity shares, IPOs, OFS | Yes | Yes |
| Equity and stock derivatives (F&O) | Yes | Yes (and see 6.4) |
| Corporate bonds, NCDs, REITs, InvITs | Yes | Yes |
| Units of Kaveri Capital schemes | Yes | No, but 30-day holding applies |
| Third-party mutual fund units | No | No |
| Government securities, T-bills, SGBs | No | No |
| Fixed deposits, PPF, NPS | No | No |

## 4. Pre-clearance

4.1 **Pre-clearance is required for every personal trade in a covered security**, before the order is placed.

4.2 Requests are submitted on the Compliance pre-clearance form in the employee portal, stating security, buy or sell, quantity and approximate value.

4.3 Compliance will respond by **2:00 pm** for requests received before 12:00 noon on a trading day.

4.4 An approval is valid for the **same trading day and the next trading day** only. Unexecuted approvals lapse and a fresh request must be made.

4.5 Compliance will refuse pre-clearance if:
- the stock is on the restricted list;
- any Kaveri scheme or PMS account has an open or pending order in the stock;
- a Kaveri scheme has traded the stock in the previous **7 calendar days** (fund managers and dealers) or **3 calendar days** (other access persons);
- the research team has a rating change on the stock under preparation.

## 5. Minimum holding period

5.1 Securities bought must be held for a **30-day minimum holding** period, counted from the trade date.

5.2 The CCO may grant a hardship exception (for example, medical emergency, home purchase) in writing. Exceptions are logged and reported to the Board Risk Committee quarterly.

5.3 Any profit from a sale within 30 days without an exception is disgorged to the Company's CSR fund.

## 6. Blackout and prohibited activity

6.1 There is a **blackout on any stock on the restricted list**. No covered person may buy, sell, pledge or gift such a stock while it is on the list, regardless of value.

6.2 The restricted list is maintained under the Restricted List Procedure (KCA/INV/SOP-001) and is visible to all access persons in the employee portal.

6.3 Research analysts may not trade any stock in their coverage universe, and may not trade any stock in the 30 days before or 5 days after publishing a report on it.

6.4 Intraday trading and short-selling in personal accounts are prohibited. F&O positions are permitted only for hedging an existing holding.

6.5 Fund managers may not hold any stock in a personal account that is held by a scheme they manage, except holdings declared at joining.

## 7. Disclosures

| Disclosure | Who | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Initial holdings statement | All covered persons | Within 10 days of joining |
| Quarterly transaction report | All covered persons | Within 15 days of quarter end |
| Annual holdings statement | All covered persons | By 30 April each year |
| New demat or trading account | All covered persons | Within 7 days of opening |
| Contract notes for pre-cleared trades | All covered persons | Within 2 trading days of trade |

Brokers may be asked to send duplicate contract notes directly to Compliance.

## 8. Violations

8.1 Any violation, whether self-identified or identified by Compliance monitoring, must be **reported to the CCO within 1 working day** of being discovered.

8.2 Line managers who become aware of a violation by a team member must report it within the same 1-working-day timeline.

8.3 Consequences:

| Violation | First instance | Repeat within 24 months |
|---|---|---|
| Trade without pre-clearance (stock not restricted) | Written warning; disgorgement | Bonus reduction of up to 25%; trading suspension for 6 months |
| Sale within 30-day holding period | Disgorgement of profit | Trading suspension for 6 months |
| Trade in a restricted-list stock | Final warning; disgorgement; Board Risk Committee informed | Termination |
| Late disclosure | Written caution | Written warning |
| Front-running or misuse of UPSI | Termination and reporting to SEBI | — |

8.4 Compliance reports a summary of violations to the Board Risk Committee every quarter. In FY2025-26 there were 9 violations, all minor (7 late disclosures, 2 trades without pre-clearance).

## 9. Monitoring

Compliance reconciles quarterly transaction reports against pre-clearance approvals and against scheme trade blotters. A random sample of 10% of access persons is selected each quarter for detailed review of demat statements.

## 10. Questions

Contact Compliance at compliance@kaveri.example or the CCO, Shalini Kapoor. When in doubt, do not trade until you have written confirmation.
