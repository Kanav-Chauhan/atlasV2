# Investment Process and IC Charter

## 1. Purpose

This charter sets out the constitution, authority and procedures of the Investment Committee (IC) of Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd., and describes the investment process that the IC oversees for all mutual-fund-style schemes, the Kaveri Focused Equity PMS and the Kaveri Long-Short AIF III. It was approved by the Board of Directors and takes effect from 1 April 2026.

## 2. Role of the IC

The IC is responsible for:

1. adopting the quarterly house view and sector stances;
2. approving investment themes and their allocation ranges;
3. approving new stocks above the thresholds in section 6;
4. approving exits on governance grounds;
5. reviewing scheme performance, attribution and risk every month;
6. approving deviations from internal (non-regulatory) investment limits;
7. oversight of the research coverage universe.

Fund managers retain day-to-day discretion within the limits set by the scheme documents, the Investment Risk Limits Policy and IC resolutions.

## 4. Meetings

4.1 The IC **meets monthly**, normally on the second Thursday of each month at 4:00 pm.

4.2 Special meetings may be called by the Chair or the MD & CEO at 24 hours' notice, for example for a governance event in a portfolio company.

4.3 **Quorum:** four voting members, which must include either the Chair or the MD & CEO.

4.4 Agenda and papers are circulated at least **3 working days** before the meeting. Theme proposals and exit notes must be circulated at least 5 working days before.

4.5 Draft minutes are circulated within **5 working days** and confirmed at the next meeting. Minutes are retained for 8 years.

## 5. Decision-making

- Decisions are taken by simple majority of voting members present. The Chair has a casting vote.
- A member with a conflict of interest must declare it at the start of the meeting and must not vote on that item.
- Urgent decisions between meetings may be taken by circular resolution signed by at least four voting members, including the Chair, and are ratified at the next meeting.

## 6. Approval thresholds

| Action | Approval needed |
|---|---|
| Buying a stock within research coverage, up to 2% of scheme NAV | Fund manager |
| Buying a stock within coverage, above 2% of NAV | IC |
| Buying any stock outside research coverage | IC (and Research must initiate within 60 days) |
| Adopting an investment theme | IC |
| Exit for governance reasons | IC (or circular resolution if urgent) |
| Single stock above 8% of NAV (limit 10%) | CIO and CRO jointly |
| Any breach of an internal limit | IC ratification; CRO reports to Board Risk Committee |
| Unrated or below-AA debt instrument | Not permitted in Short Duration Fund |

## 7. Investment process

### 7.1 Idea generation
Research covers 142 stocks. Each covered stock has a published rating (BUY, ADD, HOLD or REDUCE), a 12-month target price and a model. Ideas may also come from fund managers, subject to research initiation.

### 7.2 Due diligence
Before a new position above 1% of NAV, the following must be on file: research report or initiation, management meeting notes, governance checklist (auditor, related-party transactions, pledges, board independence), and liquidity assessment.

### 7.3 Portfolio construction
Fund managers build portfolios within the following key limits (detailed in the Investment Risk Limits Policy):

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Single stock | Maximum 10% of scheme NAV |
| Sector | Maximum 25% (banks up to 35% for Bluechip) |
| Small Cap liquidity | At least 15% of portfolio liquidatable in 5 days at 30% participation |
| VaR (95%, 1-day), equity schemes | Maximum 2.5% |

### 7.4 Execution
All orders go through the order management system to the Mumbai dealing desk. Orders for multiple schemes are aggregated and allocated pro rata. Best-execution reports are reviewed monthly by the CRO.

### 7.5 Monitoring and review
Risk monitors limits daily. Monthly IC review covers performance, attribution, risk, liquidity and compliance exceptions. Research reviews every holding at least once a quarter.

### 7.6 Sell discipline
A holding must be reviewed by the IC if: the stock falls more than 25% below cost relative to benchmark; the research rating moves to REDUCE; the governance checklist fails; or the original thesis is invalidated.

## 8. Governance exits

When a governance concern is identified (auditor resignation, unexplained related-party transactions, sharp rise in promoter pledge, delayed results, regulatory action), the fund manager prepares an exit note within 10 working days. If the IC approves an exit, the stock is placed on the internal "do not buy" list for at least 24 months.

## 9. Interaction with Compliance

- The CCO maintains the restricted list and the "do not buy" list (see Restricted List Procedure).
- No scheme may trade a stock on the restricted list.
- The IC may not approve any trade that breaches a SEBI regulation or scheme document.
