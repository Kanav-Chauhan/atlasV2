# Project Plan – GIFT City Feeder Fund Launch

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This plan turns the Board's approval of 25 April 2026 (Resolution 58.3) into a delivery plan. The Board approved setting up a fund management entity (FME) in GIFT City IFSC, Gandhinagar, and launching the **Kaveri India Flexi Cap Feeder Fund (IFSC)**, a US-dollar fund investing substantially all its assets in the Kaveri Flexi Cap Fund. **Target launch: January 2027.**

1.2 The business case, budget and structure are in the GIFT City Feeder Fund Proposal (KCA/LDR/NOTE-059). This plan covers who does what, by when, and what could go wrong.

## 6. Key design decisions already taken

| Decision | Detail |
|---|---|
| Currency | US dollar; no currency hedging at fund level in year 1 |
| Minimum investment | USD 150,000 |
| Dealing | Daily subscriptions, weekly redemptions |
| Eligible investors | NRIs, foreign individuals, family offices and institutions; Indian residents are not offered the fund |
| Capacity | Feeder money capped at 10% of Flexi Cap Fund AUM; CIO reviews quarterly |
| Fund manager | Divya Menon (same as the underlying fund); no separate investment team in year 1 |

## 7. Dependencies

- WS2 (custody) cannot finish until WS1 has filed with IFSCA, because the custodian needs the FME registration number to open accounts.
- WS3 user acceptance testing needs the administrator from WS2 selected by 31 August 2026.
- WS4 pre-marketing may only start once WS1 confirms the offer document has been filed.
- WS6 hires must include the IFSC compliance officer before the fund filing in November 2026.

## 8. Risk register

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | IFSCA approval delayed beyond November 2026 | Medium | High | Early filing by 30 June; pre-filing meeting with IFSCA; 2 months' slack | CCO |
| R2 | Custodian onboarding takes longer than 8 weeks | Medium | High | Shortlist of 3 custodians already present in GIFT City | Head of Operations |
| R3 | Weak NRI demand at launch | Medium | Medium | Soft-commitment target of USD 15 million before launch from 300 existing NRI clients | Fund Manager, Flexi Cap |
| R4 | Principal Officer not hired in time | Low | High | Interim Principal Officer from Compliance team approved by IFSCA if needed | Head of HR |
| R5 | Office fit-out delayed | Low | Medium | Serviced office space in GIFT City booked as fallback for 6 months | Head of HR |
| R6 | Flexi Cap capacity pressure | Low | Medium | 10% cap on feeder money; quarterly CIO review | CRO |
| R7 | FATCA/CRS reporting errors | Medium | Medium | Outsourced KYC review for non-Indian investors; IFSC compliance officer | CCO |

## 9. Launch readiness checklist (go/no-go on 15 December 2026)

1. IFSCA registration of the FME and fund filing cleared.
2. Custodian, administrator and USD bank accounts live.
3. NAV process tested for 10 consecutive days in parallel run.
4. Investor portal live with foreign KYC flow.
5. At least USD 10 million of soft commitments received.
6. Seed capital transfer approved by the CFO.

The steering committee makes the go/no-go decision. If any of items 1–4 is not met, launch moves to February 2027 and the Board is informed.
