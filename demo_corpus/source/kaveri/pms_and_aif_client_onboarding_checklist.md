# PMS and AIF Client Onboarding Checklist

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This checklist is used by Relationship Managers (RMs) and the Client Services team to onboard a new client into the **Kaveri Focused Equity PMS** or the **Kaveri Long-Short AIF III**. It follows the KYC and Customer Due Diligence Policy (KCA/CMP/POL-011), the PMS Client Agreement – Standard Terms (KCA/PRD/AGR-001) and the Fee Schedule and Exit Loads (KCA/PRD/AGR-002).

1.2 No account may be activated, and no money invested, until every mandatory item below is ticked and the Client Services checker has signed the file. RMs collect documents but never approve their own clients.

## 2. Minimums at a glance

| Product | Minimum investment | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kaveri Focused Equity PMS | **₹50 lakh** | SEBI minimum; cash through banking channels or eligible listed securities |
| Kaveri Long-Short AIF III | **₹1 crore** | ₹25 lakh for eligible employees and directors |
| Top-up (PMS) | ₹5 lakh | Any time after the account is active |

2.1 Securities brought in by a PMS client are valued at the closing price on the day before transfer. If that value is below ₹50 lakh, the shortfall must be topped up in money before activation.

## 3. Step-by-step checklist

### Step 1 – First meeting and suitability (RM)

| # | Item | Mandatory |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Give the client the Disclosure Document (PMS) or Private Placement Memorandum (AIF) and record the date | Yes |
| 1.2 | Complete the risk-profiling questionnaire; the product must match the client's risk profile | Yes |
| 1.3 | For the AIF, confirm the client understands the 12-month lock-in and monthly redemptions with 30 days' notice | Yes (AIF) |
| 1.4 | Record the client's source-of-funds declaration | Yes |

### Step 2 – KYC (KYC Desk, Operations)

| # | Item | Mandatory |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **CKYC lookup** using the client's PAN; download the record if it exists | Yes |
| 2.2 | **PAN verification** online for the client, every joint holder and every authorised signatory; status must be "Valid" | Yes |
| 2.3 | Proof of address (one Officially Valid Document) if not in CKYC | Yes |
| 2.4 | In-person or video verification (recorded; client located in India for video IPV) | Yes |
| 2.5 | Non-individuals: incorporation documents, board resolution, authorised signatory list and beneficial ownership declaration | Yes (non-individual) |
| 2.6 | Sanctions screening of client and beneficial owners | Yes |
| 2.7 | Risk category assigned: Low, Medium or High (PMS clients are at least Medium) | Yes |

### Step 3 – Approvals for higher-risk clients

| Client type | Approval needed before activation |
|---|---|
| Medium risk (default for PMS) | KYC Desk supervisor |
| High risk (trusts, complex ownership, high-risk jurisdictions, offshore AIF structures) | Chief Compliance Officer, after enhanced due diligence |
| **Politically exposed person (PEP)** or close relative / associate of a PEP | **Prior written approval of the Chief Compliance Officer, Shalini Kapoor**; cannot be delegated |

3.1 For a PEP, the RM must also obtain a source-of-wealth statement and the client is reviewed every 2 years under the high-risk re-KYC cycle.

### Step 4 – Agreement and fee choice (RM and Client Services)

| # | Item | Mandatory |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | PMS Client Agreement – Standard Terms signed by all holders (or AIF contribution agreement) | Yes |
| 4.2 | Fee option chosen and initialled on the fee annexure | Yes (PMS) |
| 4.3 | Nomination form (or opt-out declaration) | Yes |
| 4.4 | Power of attorney for the PMS demat account (operational only; no power to withdraw money to third parties) | Yes (PMS) |

4.5 PMS fee options the client may choose:

| Option | Management fee | Performance fee |
|---|---|---|
| Option A – Fixed | 2.0% p.a. | None |
| Option B – Hybrid | 1.0% p.a. | 20% of returns above a 10% p.a. hurdle, with high-water mark |

The client may switch option once a year on the account anniversary. PMS exit load is 3% in year 1, 2% in year 2, 1% in year 3, nil after 3 years. AIF fees are 1.5% p.a. plus 15% over an 8% hurdle.

### Step 5 – Account opening (Operations)

| # | Item | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Open custody account and a dedicated demat account in the client's name | Operations | 3 working days after KYC approval |
| 5.2 | Open client bank account mapping for PMS | Operations | Same time |
| 5.3 | AIF: allot client ID in the AIF register | Operations | 2 working days |

### Step 6 – First funding

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 6.1 | **Cash is never accepted**, in any amount. Funds must come by cheque, NEFT or RTGS from a bank account in the client's own name (first holder) |
| 6.2 | Third-party payments are rejected and returned to source |
| 6.3 | Securities are transferred from the client's own demat account only |
| 6.4 | Funding must reach the account within 30 days of agreement signing, or the file is re-verified |

6.5 PMS money is deployed into the portfolio typically within **15 working days** of funds being received.

### Step 7 – Portal access and welcome (Client Services)

| # | Item | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | Client portal login sent to registered email and mobile | Within 1 working day of first funding |
| 7.2 | Welcome call by RM, confirming fee option and first review date | Within 5 working days |
| 7.3 | First monthly statement | Following month |
| 7.4 | First quarterly review with RM | End of the first full quarter |

## 4. Turnaround targets

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Complete documents to KYC approval (Low/Medium risk) | 3 working days |
| Complete documents to KYC approval (High risk / PEP) | 7 working days |
| KYC approval to account opening | 3 working days |
| Overall: documents received to account active | 10 working days |

## 5. Common reasons files are rejected

1. PAN inoperative or not linked to Aadhaar.
2. Payment from a joint account where the client is not the first holder.
3. Beneficial ownership declaration missing for a family-office company.
4. Video KYC done while the client was outside India.
5. Fee annexure not initialled.

## 6. Records

The complete onboarding file is scanned into the client record system and kept for at least 5 years after the relationship ends. Internal Audit tests 5% of new accounts every quarter.
