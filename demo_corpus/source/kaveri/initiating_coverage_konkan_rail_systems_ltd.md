# Initiating Coverage – Konkan Rail Systems Ltd

**Rating: BUY | Target price: ₹820 | CMP: ₹612 | Upside: 34% | Sector: Capital goods (railways)**

*Konkan Rail Systems Ltd is a fictional company used for illustration.*

## 1. Investment summary

Konkan Rail Systems Ltd (KRSL) is a Pune-headquartered supplier of railway signalling, automatic train protection (ATP) equipment and coach electrical systems. It is the purest listed play on the shift in railway spending from civil works to safety and technology, which is the core of the railways capex theme approved by the Investment Committee at IC 47 on 12 March 2026.

We initiate with a **BUY rating and a 12-month target price of ₹820**, based on 26x FY28E EPS of ₹31.5. We expect revenue to grow at a 24% CAGR and EPS at a 27% CAGR over FY26–28E, driven by an order book of ₹9,850 crore (4.0x FY26E revenue).

KRSL has three plants (Pune, Nashik and Hosur) and an R&D centre in Bengaluru with 420 engineers. Indian Railways and its zonal units account for 81% of revenue; metro rail corporations 14%; exports (East Africa, Bangladesh) 5%.

## 4. Investment thesis

1. **ATP rollout is a decade-long opportunity.** Coverage is targeted to rise from about 6,500 route-km today to 34,000 route-km by FY30. KRSL is one of three approved vendors and has won 31% of ATP tenders by value since FY24.
2. **Order book gives visibility.** ₹9,850 crore of orders at end-December 2025, of which 58% is signalling and ATP; execution of 30–35% a year implies revenue visibility to FY29.
3. **Margin expansion.** The rising share of ATP and AMC (both above 22% EBITDA margin) should lift group EBITDA margin from 17.6% in FY26E to 19.2% in FY28E.
4. **Healthier working capital.** Receivable days fell from 142 in FY23 to 96 in FY26E, as railway payment cycles have improved.
5. **Clean governance.** Board with five of nine independent directors; no related-party transactions above 1% of revenue; Big-4-affiliated auditor rotated in FY25 as scheduled.

## 6. Valuation

We value KRSL at **26x FY28E EPS of ₹31.5**, giving a target price of ₹820 (rounded). This is a 10% discount to Sethu Signalling Technologies (29x), reflecting KRSL's lower-margin coach electricals business, and a premium to the broader capital goods mid-cap peer average of 22x, reflecting its higher growth and net-cash balance sheet.

## 7. Key risks

- **Tender concentration** – 81% of revenue from Indian Railways; any slowdown in ATP tendering hurts growth.
- **Competition** – a fourth ATP vendor approval could pressure bid margins.
- **Execution** – field installation depends on track-access blocks granted by zonal railways.
- **Technology** – migration to newer signalling standards could require R&D investment above the current 4.5% of revenue.
- **Liquidity** – average daily traded value of ₹38 crore limits position sizes for larger schemes.
