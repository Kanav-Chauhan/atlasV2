# Earnings Season Review Q4 FY26

## 1. Summary

The Q4 FY26 (January–March 2026) results season was better than feared. Of the 142 companies under Kaveri research coverage, 139 had reported by 31 May 2026. Aggregate profit after tax for coverage grew **12.4% year-on-year**, ahead of our pre-season estimate of 10.1%. Financials and capital goods drove the beat; IT services and FMCG disappointed, validating the April house view.

## 3. Highlights by sector

### 3.1 Banks – strong
Loan growth of 14% for coverage and credit costs of 0.62% (annualised) drove a 17.2% rise in PAT. Sahyadri Bank (+18.6%) and Kalinga State Bank (+24.3%) were the standouts. NIMs fell 11 basis points quarter-on-quarter, in line with expectations. See the Banking Sector Deep Dive – Q4 FY26 for detail.

### 3.2 Capital goods – best sector
Order inflows for the 16 coverage companies rose 31% year-on-year to ₹68,400 crore. Railway and transmission orders accounted for 44% of inflows. Konkan Rail Systems reported revenue growth of 29% and EBITDA margin of 18.1%, ahead of our 17.6% estimate, and won a ₹1,240 crore ATP order in March. Trishul Heavy Engineering and Vajra Power Equipment both beat on execution.

### 3.3 IT services – weak
Constant-currency revenue fell 0.3% quarter-on-quarter on average. Codestream Systems guided FY27 revenue growth of 2–4%, the lowest in a decade. Infoweave Technologies guided 4–6%, at the low end of our expectations. Book-to-bill dipped to 1.15x.

### 3.4 FMCG – weak
Volume growth averaged 3.1%. Anandam Foods' gross margin fell 140 basis points on wheat and cocoa inflation. Shubhra Home Care was the only beat among large FMCG names, helped by quick-commerce growth.

## 7. Implications for portfolios

1. Overweight banks and capital goods continues to be rewarded; no change.
2. IT services underweight confirmed; consider further trimming Codestream where held.
3. FMCG underweight confirmed; prefer Shubhra Home Care within the sector.
4. Small caps: margin disappointments in building materials argue for continued selectivity.

## 8. Coverage changes

Coverage remains at 142 stocks. Three companies were yet to report as of 31 May 2026 and are excluded from the counts above. Research plans to initiate on two further railway-capex names in Q2 FY27, keeping total coverage within the team capacity of about 4–5 companies per analyst.
