# Discretionary vs Non-Discretionary PMS Explainer

## 1. Why this note exists

1.1 Relationship Managers often get asked: "What is the difference between discretionary and non-discretionary PMS, and which one should I choose?" This note gives a standard answer so that every client hears the same thing, and so that the choice is properly recorded in the Client Schedule of the PMS Client Agreement (KCA/PRD/AGR-001).

1.2 It is an internal explainer for Relationship Managers, Compliance and Finance staff. It is not marketing material and must not be sent to clients as-is.

## 2. The three PMS service types

| Feature | Discretionary | Non-discretionary | Advisory |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who decides what to buy/sell | Kaveri's portfolio manager | The client, on Kaveri's recommendation | The client |
| Who places the trades | Kaveri | Kaveri, only after client approval of each trade | The client, through their own broker |
| Client approval per trade | Not required | Required (email, portal or recorded call) | Not applicable |
| Where securities are held | Client's demat with Kaveri's custodian | Client's demat with Kaveri's custodian | Client's own demat |
| Minimum investment | ₹50 lakh | ₹50 lakh | ₹50 lakh |
| Kaveri offering | Kaveri Focused Equity PMS | Kaveri Custom Equity (non-discretionary) | Kaveri Equity Advisory |
| Clients at 31 March 2026 | 1,046 | 81 | 23 |

2.1 In total Kaveri had 1,150 PMS clients at 31 March 2026. The Kaveri Focused Equity PMS (discretionary) accounts for ₹1,350 crore of PMS assets.

## 3. Discretionary PMS in practice

3.1 The client signs once and Kaveri manages the portfolio within the Focused Equity strategy: up to 25 stocks, no single stock above 12% of the portfolio, benchmark Nifty 500 TRI. The fund manager is Ashok Venkataraman.

3.2 All discretionary portfolios follow the model portfolio. Differences from the model arise only from timing of contributions, client-specific restrictions (for example, "no tobacco or alcohol stocks"), or securities brought in at onboarding that are being sold down.

3.3 Rebalancing is done centrally on the same day for all portfolios, so no client gets a better or worse price because of when their trade was placed (trade allocation follows the order allocation policy).

## 4. Non-discretionary PMS in practice

4.1 Kaveri recommends each trade; the client must approve before execution. Approvals received after **2:30 pm** are executed the next trading day.

4.2 If the client does not respond within **2 trading days**, the recommendation lapses. Kaveri will not chase or execute on assumed consent.

4.3 Because of approval delays, non-discretionary portfolios often diverge from the model. Performance is reported against the benchmark, not against the model.

4.4 SEBI requires that non-discretionary and advisory performance is reported separately from discretionary performance. Do not quote Focused Equity PMS returns to a non-discretionary prospect.

## 5. Fees

5.1 The fee options (2.0% fixed, or 1.0% plus 20% above a 10% hurdle with high-water mark) and the 3% / 2% / 1% exit load apply equally to discretionary and non-discretionary clients.

5.2 The advisory service charges a flat **0.75% p.a.** of assets under advice, billed quarterly, with no exit load, because Kaveri does not hold or trade the assets.

## 6. Which one suits whom

| Client profile | Usually suits |
|---|---|
| Busy professional, wants Kaveri's full strategy | Discretionary |
| Experienced investor who wants a final say on every trade | Non-discretionary |
| Family office with its own dealing desk and custodian | Advisory |
| Client with large legacy holdings they won't sell | Non-discretionary or advisory |

## 7. What Relationship Managers must and must not do

- **Must** record the chosen service type in the Client Schedule and in the CRM before activation.
- **Must** explain that non-discretionary does not mean lower risk; the client still bears full market risk.
- **Must not** promise that discretionary clients will get the same returns as the published model.
- **Must not** take trade instructions from a non-discretionary client over WhatsApp or unrecorded phone; only email, the client portal or the recorded line 1800-425-0112 are valid.

## 8. Switching service type

8.1 A client may switch from non-discretionary to discretionary (or vice versa) once a year by signing a revised Client Schedule. The switch takes effect from the first business day of the following month. No exit load applies to a switch, as the assets remain with Kaveri.

## 9. Frequently asked questions

**Is the custodian the same?** Yes, for discretionary and non-discretionary.

**Can a non-discretionary client sell a stock on their own?** No. All trades in the PMS account go through Kaveri. The client can, however, instruct Kaveri to sell, and Kaveri will execute within 1 trading day.

**Can a discretionary client exclude certain stocks?** Yes. Up to 5 named stocks or 2 sectors may be excluded in the Client Schedule.
