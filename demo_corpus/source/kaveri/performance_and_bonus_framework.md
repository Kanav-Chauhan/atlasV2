# Performance and Bonus Framework

## 1. Purpose

This framework explains how performance is assessed at Kaveri Capital and how the annual variable pay (bonus) pool is determined and allocated. It applies from the FY2026-27 performance year (1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027).

## 2. Performance cycle

| Milestone | Timing |
|---|---|
| Goal setting (KRAs agreed in HR portal) | By 30 April |
| Mid-year check-in | October |
| Self-assessment | 1–15 April (following year) |
| Manager assessment and calibration | 16 April – 15 May |
| Ratings and bonus communicated | By 31 May |
| Bonus paid | With June payroll |

## 3. Rating scale

| Rating | Label | Expected distribution (guide) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | Up to 10% |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations | 20–25% |
| 3 | Meets expectations | 50–55% |
| 2 | Partly meets | 10% |
| 1 | Does not meet | Up to 5% |

Distribution is a guide used in calibration, not a forced curve. Calibration panels are chaired by the function head with HR present.

## 4. What is assessed

### 4.1 Investment roles (fund managers)

| Component | Weight | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| Fund performance vs benchmark | 50% | Rolling 3-year excess return (60%) and 1-year excess return (40%), direct plan |
| Risk and process discipline | 20% | Limit breaches, IC documentation, risk-adjusted return |
| Client and distributor engagement | 15% | Reviews, meetings, investor communication |
| Team and culture | 15% | Mentoring, compliance record |

A fund manager whose scheme is in the bottom quartile of its peer group on a 3-year basis cannot be rated above 3, regardless of other components.

### 4.2 Research analysts

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Quality and accuracy of recommendations (12-month hit rate and alpha of ratings) | 40% |
| Fund manager feedback (semi-annual survey) | 25% |
| Coverage maintenance and timeliness | 20% |
| Process, compliance and teamwork | 15% |

### 4.3 Control and support functions

Compliance, Risk, Operations, Finance, HR and Technology are assessed on functional KRAs (60%) and behaviours (40%). **Bonuses for control functions are not linked to fund performance or to revenue**, to protect their independence.

## 5. Bonus pool

5.1 The total bonus pool is approved by the Board's Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC) each May.

5.2 The pool is set at **12–18% of profit before bonus and tax**, depending on the firm scorecard:

| Firm scorecard item | Weight |
|---|---|
| Profit before tax vs budget | 40% |
| AUM growth vs budget | 25% |
| Share of equity AUM outperforming benchmark (3-year) | 20% |
| Regulatory and audit findings | 15% |

For FY2025-26, with PAT of ₹58 crore and a cost-to-income ratio of 61%, the NRC approved a pool at 15% of profit before bonus and tax.

## 6. Individual bonus targets

| Grade | Target bonus (% of annual fixed pay) | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | 60% | 150% |
| G2 | 40% | 100% |
| G3 | 25% | 60% |
| G4 | 15% | 30% |

Payout multiplier by rating: rating 5 – 1.5x; rating 4 – 1.2x; rating 3 – 1.0x; rating 2 – 0.5x; rating 1 – nil.

## 7. Deferral and clawback (investment staff)

7.1 For fund managers and the CIO, in line with SEBI guidance on skin in the game, **20% of variable pay** is paid in units of the scheme(s) they manage and locked in for **3 years**.

7.2 For any employee whose bonus exceeds ₹25 lakh, **40%** of the excess is deferred and paid in three equal instalments over the next three years.

7.3 Unvested deferred bonus and units may be clawed back in case of fraud, gross misconduct, a material limit breach caused by the employee, or restatement of performance.

## 8. Gating conditions

No bonus is paid to an employee who, during the performance year:

- has a serious breach under the Code of Conduct and Ethics or Personal Trading Policy;
- has not completed mandatory block leave (where applicable);
- has not completed mandatory NISM certification by the deadline under the Learning and Certification Policy – NISM;
- has not submitted the annual Code of Conduct certification.

## 9. Eligibility

- Employees must be on the rolls on the payment date to receive a bonus.
- Employees who joined before 1 January are eligible for a pro-rated bonus; those who joined on or after 1 January are not eligible for that year.
- Employees serving notice on the payment date are not eligible.

## 10. Appeals

An employee may appeal a rating within **10 working days** of communication, in writing to the Head of HR. The appeal is reviewed by a panel of the function head and one other G1 leader, and the decision is communicated within 15 working days.

## 11. Ownership

Owner: Head of HR. Annual review by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.
