# Monthly Macro Dashboard April 2026

Data as of 30 April 2026 unless stated. Internal reference for the investment team; refreshed in the first week of every month.

## 1. One-line summary

Growth is steady, inflation is benign, the RBI is on hold at 5.50% and domestic flows are offsetting foreign selling. Nothing in April's data changes the April house view (Nifty 50 year-end target 26,800; overweight banks and capital goods; underweight IT services and FMCG).

## 9. What we are watching

1. **Monsoon** – the IMD's first forecast (April) is 103% of the long-period average. A normal monsoon supports our FY27 GDP forecast of 6.7%.
2. **Crude oil** – every USD 10/bbl rise adds about 0.4% of GDP to the current account deficit.
3. **US tariffs** – any escalation affecting pharmaceuticals or IT services exports.
4. **Q4 earnings** – 61 of 142 coverage companies had reported by 30 April; 44% beat estimates.
