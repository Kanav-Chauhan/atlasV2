# About Kaveri Capital

> *Patient capital, clear thinking.*

This page is the starting point for every new joiner at Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. It explains who we are, what we manage, how the firm is organised and where to find people. It is refreshed every April after the annual results are closed.

## 1. Who we are

Kaveri Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd. is a SEBI-registered asset manager and wealth advisory firm, founded in **2012 in Bengaluru**. We run mutual-fund-style schemes, a portfolio management service (PMS) and an AIF Category III long-short fund. Our investment approach is research-led, benchmark-aware and deliberately unhurried: we would rather own fewer businesses we understand well than chase every quarter.

## 5. How the firm is organised

- **Investments** – equity (CIO Ashok Venkataraman), fixed income (Farida Sheikh), and the Investment Committee (IC), which meets monthly and approves themes, new positions above threshold, and exits.
- **Research** – 34 analysts under Ishita Banerjee, covering 142 stocks across 18 sectors. Research publishes the quarterly house view and sector notes.
- **Risk** – Varun Pillai's team monitors scheme limits (single stock, sector, liquidity, VaR) daily and reports to the Board Risk Committee every quarter.
- **Compliance** – Shalini Kapoor's team owns KYC, AML, personal trading pre-clearance, the restricted list and SEBI reporting.
- **Operations** – Mohit Arora's team runs NAV computation, fund accounting, settlements and investor servicing.
- **Finance, HR, Technology** – support functions based mostly in Bengaluru.

## 7. Our values

1. **Client first** – every decision is tested against "would the client agree if they saw this?"
2. **Intellectual honesty** – write down the thesis, the risks and what would make us wrong.
3. **Patience** – we measure ourselves over three to five years, not three months.
4. **Discipline** – limits, pre-clearance and documentation are not optional.
5. **Respect** – for colleagues, clients and the regulator.
