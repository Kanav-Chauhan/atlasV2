# Compensation Bands and Bonus Pool FY2026-27

## 1. Purpose and confidentiality

1.1 This document sets the fixed-pay bands for every grade and role family at Kaveri Capital for FY2026-27, the size of the FY2026-27 bonus pool, and the deferral rules for fund managers and other high earners. It is the only document in the firm that states pay ranges.

1.2 It is shared with the Head of HR, the HR business partners, the MD & CEO and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC). Function heads may be told the band for a specific role when making an offer or a promotion case, but may not circulate this document.

1.3 The rules for ratings, bonus targets and gating conditions are in the Performance and Bonus Framework (KCA/HR/POL-003). This document only adds the numbers.

## 3. Fixed-pay bands (annual, ₹ lakh)

3.1 Bands are annual fixed pay (basic + allowances + employer PF), excluding variable pay. New offers should normally be made between the band minimum and the midpoint; an offer above the midpoint needs the Head of HR's approval, and an offer above the band maximum needs the MD & CEO's approval.

| G4 | Client Services Executive | 5 | 6.5 | 8 |

3.2 G1 pay is set individually by the NRC and is not banded.

## 4. Annual increment matrix (April 2026 cycle)

4.1 Increments are based on the FY2025-26 rating and on where the employee's pay sits in the band (compa-ratio = pay ÷ band midpoint).

| Rating | Compa-ratio below 0.90 | 0.90–1.10 | Above 1.10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 – Exceptional | 18% | 15% | 11% |
| 4 – Exceeds | 14% | 11% | 8% |
| 3 – Meets | 10% | 8% | 5% |
| 2 – Partly meets | 4% | 2% | 0% |
| 1 – Does not meet | 0% | 0% | 0% |

4.2 Promotion increments are a further 10% to 15% on top of the matrix, subject to the employee reaching at least the new band minimum.

## 5. FY2026-27 bonus pool

5.1 Under the Performance and Bonus Framework the pool is set at 12–18% of profit before bonus and tax. For FY2026-27 the budget provides a **variable pay pool of ₹22.0 crore** (FY2025-26: ₹19.8 crore). The NRC confirms the final pool in May 2027 on actual results, and it may be lower or higher than the budget provision.

5.2 The pool is split across role families as follows (indicative, before calibration):

| Role family | Share of pool | ₹ crore |
|---|---|---|
| Fund managers and CIO | 24% | 5.28 |
| Research | 22% | 4.84 |
| Sales, distribution and PMS relationship managers | 20% | 4.40 |
| Dealing | 6% | 1.32 |
| Control functions (Compliance, Risk, Internal Audit) | 10% | 2.20 |
| Operations, Client Services, Finance, HR, Technology | 18% | 3.96 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **22.00** |

5.3 The control-function share is fixed before fund results are known, so that Compliance and Risk bonuses are not linked to fund performance or revenue.

## 6. Deferral rules

### 6.1 Fund managers and the CIO

- **20% of variable pay** is paid in units of the scheme(s) the person manages and is locked in for **3 years**.
- The remaining 80% is paid in cash with the June payroll, subject to clause 6.2.

### 6.2 All employees with a bonus above ₹25 lakh

- **40% of the amount above ₹25 lakh** is deferred and paid in three equal instalments in June of each of the next three years.
- Example: a bonus of ₹55 lakh has ₹30 lakh above the threshold; ₹12 lakh is deferred (₹4 lakh a year for three years) and ₹43 lakh is paid now.

### 6.3 Forfeiture and clawback

| Event | Unvested deferred cash | Locked-in scheme units |
|---|---|---|
| Resignation | Forfeited | Released at end of lock-in, not forfeited (regulatory units) |
| Termination for cause | Forfeited | Clawed back to the extent permitted by SEBI |
| Fraud, gross misconduct, or a material limit breach caused by the employee | Clawed back | Clawed back |
| Retirement or death | Paid on schedule | Released on schedule |
