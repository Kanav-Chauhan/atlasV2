# Master Services Agreement – Harbor Logistics Inc.

This is Legal's summary of the signed Master Services Agreement (MSA) between Nimbus Infotech Pvt. Ltd. and Harbor Logistics Inc. It records the key commercial and legal terms for the delivery, sales and finance teams. The signed agreement in the contract repository (reference **CTR-2023-0417**) prevails over this summary in case of any difference.

## 2. Term and renewal

- **Initial term:** three years, from 1 October 2023 to **30 September 2026**.
- **No automatic renewal.** The MSA ends on 30 September 2026 unless the parties sign a renewal or extension.
- **Notice of non-renewal:** a party that does not intend to renew must give the other party written notice at least **90 days before the end of the term**, that is, by 2 July 2026. Neither party has given such notice.
- **Holdover:** if no renewal is signed by the end of the term, services continue month to month for up to **90 days** at the then-current rates, while the parties negotiate.
- **Annual escalation:** rates increase by **3%** on each 1 October anniversary.

| Term | Detail |

## 4. Service levels and service credits

Service levels apply to the managed cloud operations under SOW-HL-002. Service credits are calculated on the **monthly managed cloud operations fee**.

| Service level | Target | Service credit if missed |
|---|---|---|
| Availability of managed production workloads | 99.9% per month | Below 99.9%: 3%; below 99.5%: 6% |
| P1 response | 15 minutes | 2% if fewer than 95% of P1s met in the month |
| P1 resolution | 4 hours | 2% if fewer than 90% of P1s met in the month |
| P1 outage of a business-critical application longer than 2 hours | – | 5% per incident |
| Critical patch compliance (within 14 days) | 95% | 1% |

- **Service credit cap:** total service credits in any month are capped at **10% of the monthly managed cloud operations fee**.
- Service credits are Harbor's sole financial remedy for service level failures, except where a failure also gives a right to terminate under Section 8.
- Credits are applied to the next invoice; Harbor must claim them within 60 days of the month end.
- Outages caused by Harbor, its other suppliers or AWS region-wide events are excluded.

## 5. Limitation of liability

- **Liability cap:** each party's total liability under the MSA in any contract year is limited to the **fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the event** giving rise to the claim.
- **Super-cap:** for breaches of confidentiality or data protection obligations, the cap is **two times (2x)** the 12-month fees.
- **Uncapped:** fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, and each party's indemnity for third-party intellectual property claims.
- Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses, including loss of profit, except under the uncapped items.

These caps are within the Nimbus standard position; any increase at renewal needs CFO and Legal approval, as set out in the Contract Review and Signing Authority Guideline and the Discount Approval Policy.

## 6. Data protection and security

- Harbor data is hosted only in Harbor's own AWS accounts in **us-east-1** (primary) and **us-west-2** (DR). Nimbus staff in India access it remotely through Harbor's privileged access gateway; no Harbor production data is copied to Nimbus systems.
- Nimbus must notify Harbor of any security incident affecting Harbor data within **48 hours** of becoming aware of it.
- Nimbus maintains ISO 27001:2022 certification and provides its certificate and a summary of its annual penetration test on request.
- Harbor may audit Nimbus's compliance once per contract year, with 30 days' notice.
- Nimbus must keep cyber liability insurance of at least **USD 10 million** and professional indemnity insurance of at least USD 5 million.

## 7. Personnel

- Nimbus names key personnel for the engagement lead and the migration lead. Key personnel cannot be replaced without **30 days' notice** and Harbor's consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld.
- Neither party may solicit the other's staff working on the engagement during the term and for 12 months afterwards.

## 8. Termination

| Ground | Notice | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Harbor for convenience | **120 days'** written notice | Harbor pays for work done plus a termination fee equal to 2 months of the managed cloud operations fee |
| Either party for material breach | 30 days to cure after written notice | Termination if not cured |
| Either party for insolvency | Immediate | Termination |
| Harbor for repeated service failure | Service credits at the 10% cap in 3 consecutive months | Harbor may terminate SOW-HL-002 without the termination fee |

On termination or expiry, Nimbus provides **exit assistance for up to 6 months** at rate card rates, including knowledge transfer and handover of runbooks and documentation.

## 9. Law and disputes

- Governing law: the laws of the State of New York.
- Disputes go first to the steering committee, then to the two companies' CEOs for 30 days, and then to arbitration seated in New York under a single arbitrator.
