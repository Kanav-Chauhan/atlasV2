# Contract Review and Signing Authority Guideline

## 1. Purpose

This guideline sets out which contracts the Legal team must review, how non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) are handled, who may sign contracts on behalf of Nimbus Infotech Pvt. Ltd. at each value, and how long Legal takes to review each type of contract. A contract signed by someone without authority under this guideline may not bind Nimbus and is a disciplinary matter under the Code of Conduct.

## 2. Scope

Applies to every agreement that commits Nimbus to pay, deliver, license or keep information confidential: client contracts (MSAs, SOWs, change requests, NimbusOps subscription orders), supplier and vendor contracts, NDAs, leases, partnership agreements and employment-related agreements other than standard offer letters. It applies in all locations: Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Dubai.

## 3. Contracts Legal must review

| Contract type | Legal review required? |
|---|---|
| Master Services Agreement (client or supplier) | **Always** |
| Statement of work on the client's paper or with non-standard terms | Always |
| SOW on the Nimbus template with no changes, value up to ₹1 crore | No, if the account team uses the latest template |
| SOW value above ₹1 crore | Always |
| NimbusOps order form on the standard terms | No |
| NimbusOps order with changes to the subscription terms or SLA | Always |
| Data processing agreement or any personal data clause | Always (Legal and the CISO's team) |
| NDA on the Nimbus template, no changes | No |
| NDA on third-party paper or with changes | Always |
| Lease, real estate, partnership, joint venture | Always |
| Any contract with uncapped liability, liability above 12 months' fees, or unlimited indemnities | Always, plus CFO approval |

## 4. Non-disclosure agreements

- Use the Nimbus mutual NDA template **NIM-NDA-v4** wherever possible. It can be signed by the requesting VP or department head without Legal review if no clause is changed.
- Standard NDA terms: mutual, **3-year** confidentiality period from the date of disclosure, governed by Indian law with courts in Pune.
- One-way NDAs where only Nimbus receives information are acceptable; one-way NDAs where only Nimbus discloses need Legal review.
- NDAs must not contain non-solicitation, exclusivity or non-compete clauses. If the other party insists, Legal must review.
- Signed NDAs are uploaded to the contract repository within 2 business days.

## 5. Signing authority matrix

Value means the total contract value (TCV) over the full term, excluding GST. Foreign currency contracts are converted at the Finance planning rate (₹84.50 per USD for FY27).

| Total contract value | Who may sign (client contracts) | Who may sign (supplier contracts) |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹50 lakh | VP of the region or function (e.g. Arjun Nair, VP Sales North America, or Kavya Reddy, VP Delivery) | Department head with budget |
| Above ₹50 lakh and up to ₹5 crore | Chief Financial Officer (Rohan Bhatia) | Chief Financial Officer |
| **Above ₹5 crore** | **Founder & CEO (Vikram Kulkarni)** | Founder & CEO, with CFO recommendation |
| Any MSA, regardless of value | Founder & CEO or CFO | Founder & CEO or CFO |
| Leases and real estate | Founder & CEO, after Board approval if above ₹10 crore | – |

Rules that apply to every signature:

1. Signing authority is **not** pricing approval. Any discount, free resource or non-standard payment term must already be approved under the **Discount Approval Policy** (NIM/SAL/POL-001) and recorded in the CRM before the contract is signed. Finance checks the signed price against the approved price.
2. Contracts are signed only through the company e-signature tool. Wet-ink signatures are allowed only when the counterparty requires them, with a scanned copy uploaded the same day.
3. Contracts may not be split into smaller contracts to stay under a signing limit.
4. When the authorised signatory is away, the next level up signs; authority is never delegated downward.

## 6. Review turnaround times

Times are in business days from the day Legal receives a complete request (contract draft, deal summary, approved pricing and the counterparty's contact).

| Request | Turnaround |
|---|---|
| NDA on third-party paper | **2 business days** |
| NDA on Nimbus template with changes | 1 business day |
| SOW or change request | 3 business days |
| NimbusOps order with non-standard terms | 3 business days |
| Supplier contract | 5 business days |
| New MSA (client or supplier) | **10 business days** for the first mark-up |
| Data processing agreement | 5 business days |

Urgent requests can be raised with a business reason; Legal aims to respond within 1 business day but urgent handling is limited to 3 requests per week per business unit.

## 7. Standard positions

Legal negotiates against these standard positions. Moving away from them needs the approval shown.

| Clause | Standard position | Deviation approved by |
|---|---|---|
| Liability cap | Fees paid or payable in the previous 12 months | CFO and Head of Legal |
| Data breach super-cap | Up to 2x the annual fees | CFO and Head of Legal |
| Service credit cap | 10% of the monthly fee | CFO (valued as a discount under the Discount Approval Policy) |
| Payment terms | Net 45 days | Per Discount Approval Policy |
| Termination for convenience by client | 90 days' notice minimum | VP of the region |
| Governing law | India (Indian clients), New York (US clients), DIFC (UAE clients) | Head of Legal |
| Annual escalation | India 4%, North America 3%, Middle East/Europe 3% | Per Discount Approval Policy |

## 8. Contract repository

Every signed contract, amendment and NDA is stored in the contract repository with a reference number (format **CTR-YYYY-NNNN**), the expiry date and the renewal notice date. The repository sends reminders to the account owner **120 days** and 60 days before each renewal notice date.
