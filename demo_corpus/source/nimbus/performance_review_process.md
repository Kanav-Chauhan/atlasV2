# Performance Review Process

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how Nimbus sets goals, reviews performance and decides ratings each year. Ratings feed into merit increases, variable pay, promotions and development plans, so the process must be consistent and fair across teams and cities.

## 2. Scope

All permanent employees in grades L1 to L7 who have completed at least **3 months** of service by 31 March. Employees who joined after 31 December are reviewed in the following cycle; their goals are still set within 30 days of joining.

## 3. Review cycle and calendar

The performance year is the financial year (April to March).

| Phase | Window | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Goal setting | 1 April – 30 April | Employee drafts, manager approves |
| Mid-year check-in | 1 October – 31 October | Manager |
| Self-assessment | 1 March – 15 March | Employee |
| Manager assessment and proposed rating | 16 March – 31 March | Manager |
| Calibration meetings | 1 April – 20 April | Business unit head, HR Business Partner |
| Ratings final and communicated | By 30 April | Manager |
| Merit increase and variable pay effective | 1 July (paid in July payroll, arrears from 1 April for merit) | HR Operations, Payroll |

## 4. Goal setting

1. Each employee has **4 to 6 goals**, recorded in the HRMS Performance module.
2. At least one goal must relate to learning or capability, and for L5 and above, at least one to people leadership.
3. Goals carry weights that add up to 100%; no single goal may exceed 40%.
4. Goals are SMART and linked to the team's objectives for the year.
5. Goals may be revised at the mid-year check-in, or within 30 days of a change of role or project.

## 5. Mid-year check-in

The manager and employee hold a documented conversation covering progress against goals, feedback received, and any support needed. No rating is given at mid-year. Employees at risk of a rating of 2 or below must be told explicitly at mid-year, with examples, and may be placed on a performance improvement plan as described in the Manager Playbooks.

## 6. Rating scale

| Rating | Label | Meaning | Calibration guide (share of employees) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | Consistently far exceeded all goals; role model | Up to 10% |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations | Exceeded most goals | About 25% |
| 3 | Meets expectations | Achieved goals; solid, reliable contributor | About 55% |
| 2 | Partially meets | Missed some important goals | About 8% |
| 1 | Does not meet | Missed most goals; serious concerns | About 2% |

The calibration guide is a reference, not a forced distribution. Deviations of more than 5 percentage points in any band for a business unit must be justified to the Chief People Officer.

## 7. Inputs to the assessment

- Goal achievement (70% of the assessment).
- Behaviours against Nimbus values: clarity, ownership, client trust, respect (30%).
- 360-degree feedback from at least 3 peers or stakeholders, collected in the HRMS during the self-assessment window. For client-facing roles, client feedback captured by the delivery manager is included.

## 8. Calibration

1. Calibration meetings are held per business unit and grade band (L1–L3, L4–L5, L6–L7).
2. Each meeting is chaired by the business unit head and facilitated by the HR Business Partner.
3. Managers present proposed ratings with evidence; ratings may move up or down.
4. Ratings of 5 and 1 require written justification recorded in the HRMS.
5. Calibration outcomes are confidential and may not be shared with employees except as their own final rating.

## 9. Communicating results

Managers hold a 1:1 review conversation with each employee by 30 April, covering the final rating, the main reasons, strengths and development areas. Merit increase and variable pay letters are released separately in June.

## 10. Promotions

- Promotions are decided once a year as part of this cycle, effective 1 July.
- Eligibility: rating of 4 or 5 in the current cycle, and at least 18 months in the current grade (12 months for L1 to L2).
- Promotion cases are nominated by managers in the calibration meeting and approved by the business unit head; promotions to L6 and L7 also need approval by the CEO.
- L1 Trainees who complete their 6-month training programme with a rating of 3 or above are moved to L2 at the end of training, outside the annual cycle.

## 11. Appeals

An employee who disagrees with their final rating may appeal in writing to the HR Business Partner within **10 working days** of the review conversation. An appeal panel (skip-level manager and a business unit head from another unit) reviews the case and decides within 15 working days. The panel's decision is final.

## 12. Links to other processes

| Process | Link |
|---|---|
| Merit increase | Rating determines the merit range (Compensation guidelines, HR only) |
| Variable pay | Rating sets the individual performance factor |
| PIP | Rating 1, or rating 2 for a second consecutive year, triggers a PIP |
| Promotion | See section 10 |

## 13. Records

Performance records are retained in the HRMS for the duration of employment plus 7 years.
