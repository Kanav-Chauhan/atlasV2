# Variable Pay Plan

## 1. Purpose

The Variable Pay Plan (VPP) links part of each employee's pay to company results and individual performance. This document sets out the plan rules for FY2026-27. Sales roles are covered by a separate Sales Incentive Plan and are excluded.

## 2. Eligibility

- Permanent employees in grades L2 to L7 who are not on a sales incentive plan.
- Employees must be on the payroll on 31 March 2027 and on the payout date, except as stated in section 8.
- Joiners during the year participate pro rata from the first day of the month after joining.
- L1 Trainees are not eligible.

## 3. Target variable pay

Target variable pay is a percentage of CTC, between **10% and 20%** depending on grade:

| Grade | Target variable pay (% of CTC) |
|---|---|
| L2 Software Engineer | 10% |
| L3 Senior Software Engineer | 10% |
| L4 Tech Lead | 12% |
| L5 Engineering Manager / Architect | 15% |
| L6 Senior Manager / Principal | 18% |
| L7 Director | 20% |

## 4. Payout formula

**Payout = Target variable pay × Company factor × Individual factor**

The payout for any employee is capped at **150%** of target.

### 4.1 Company factor

The company factor is set by the CFO after the audited FY2026-27 results, based on revenue and EBITDA against the Board-approved budget (revenue target ₹1,340 crore).

| Measure | Weight | Threshold (factor 50%) | Target (factor 100%) | Stretch (factor 130%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 50% | ₹1,240 crore | ₹1,340 crore | ₹1,420 crore |
| EBITDA margin | 50% | 17.5% | 19.5% | 21.0% |

Results between points are interpolated in a straight line. If either measure is below threshold, that measure contributes 0%. If both are below threshold, no variable pay is paid unless the Board decides otherwise.

### 4.2 Individual factor

| Performance rating | Individual factor |
|---|---|
| 5 – Outstanding | 130% |
| 4 – Exceeds expectations | 115% |
| 3 – Meets expectations | 100% |
| 2 – Partially meets | 50% |
| 1 – Does not meet | 0% |

### 4.3 L6 and L7 business unit component

For L6 and L7, 30% of the company factor is replaced by the business unit's contribution margin against budget, using the same threshold, target and stretch structure set out in each business unit's annual plan.

## 5. Worked example

An L4 Tech Lead with CTC of ₹25 lakh has target variable pay of 12% of CTC = ₹3.0 lakh. If the company factor is 105% and the individual rating is 4 (factor 115%):

Payout = ₹3.0 lakh × 1.05 × 1.15 = **₹3.62 lakh** (rounded).

## 6. Payment schedule

| Component | Timing |
|---|---|
| Annual payout for FY2026-27 | July 2027 payroll, after audited results and ratings |
| Advance (optional, L2–L4 only) | 25% of target paid quarterly in July, October and January, adjusted against the annual payout |

If advances paid exceed the final annual payout, the excess is recovered from the July payout and, if needed, from the next two monthly salaries.

## 7. Changes during the year

- **Promotion:** target is prorated by months in each grade.
- **Transfer between business units:** L6–L7 business unit component prorated by months in each unit.
- **Long leave:** maternity leave counts as service. Unpaid leave of more than 30 days reduces the payout pro rata.

## 8. Leavers

| Reason for leaving | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Resignation before 31 March 2027 | No payout; quarterly advances already paid are not recovered |
| Resignation after 31 March 2027 but before payout | Payout made with full and final settlement, at individual factor 100% |
| Termination for misconduct | No payout; advances recovered |
| Death, permanent disability, retirement, redundancy | Pro-rata payout at target, paid with full and final settlement |

## 9. Governance

- The CFO certifies the company factor; the Chief People Officer certifies individual factors after calibration.
- The CEO may reduce the payout pool by up to 20% in exceptional circumstances, with Board notification.
- Total cost at target for FY2026-27 is estimated at ₹78 crore, included in the approved FY2026-27 budget.
- This plan does not create a contractual right to a payout; Nimbus may amend it for future years.

## 10. Questions

Employees receive their individual target in the annual compensation letter. Questions go to the HR Business Partner; plan-design questions go to Total Rewards.
