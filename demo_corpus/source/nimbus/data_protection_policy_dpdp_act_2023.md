# Data Protection Policy – DPDP Act 2023

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Nimbus Infotech Pvt. Ltd. complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the rules made under it when it processes digital personal data. It also covers our obligations where we process personal data on behalf of clients, and our approach to foreign data protection laws (such as the EU GDPR and US state privacy laws) that apply through client contracts.

## 2. Scope and roles

Nimbus processes personal data in two capacities:

| Capacity | Examples | Our role under the DPDP Act |
|---|---|---|
| For our own purposes | Employee and candidate records, payroll, visitor logs, NimbusOps customer account contacts | **Data Fiduciary** |
| On behalf of clients | Client customer data in projects, managed SOC logs for Al Noor Healthcare, data platforms for Crestline Insurance | **Data Processor**, acting under the client's instructions and contract |

## 3. Governance

- **Data Protection Officer (DPO):** The CISO, Sameer Khan, acts as DPO and is the point of contact for data principals and the Data Protection Board of India. Contact: dpo@nimbus.example.
- **Privacy champions:** Each business unit and the NimbusOps team nominates one privacy champion who keeps the unit's records of processing up to date.
- The DPO reports to the Board twice a year on privacy compliance.

## 4. Principles

When Nimbus acts as Data Fiduciary, we:

1. Process personal data only for a lawful purpose, on the basis of consent or a legitimate use permitted by the Act (for example, employment purposes).
2. Give a clear notice describing the data collected, the purpose, and how to exercise rights, in English and, on request, in any language listed in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution.
3. Collect only the data necessary for the stated purpose.
4. Keep data accurate and complete, especially when it is used for decisions about the person.
5. Retain data only as long as needed for the purpose or required by law, then erase it.
6. Protect data with reasonable security safeguards as set out in the *Information Security Policy*.

## 5. Rights of data principals

| Right | How to exercise | Our response time |
|---|---|---|
| Access information about processing | Email dpo@nimbus.example or HRMS privacy request form | 30 days |
| Correction, completion, updating | Same | 30 days |
| Erasure | Same; subject to legal retention requirements | 30 days |
| Grievance redressal | Same; escalation to DPO | 30 days |
| Nominate another person | HRMS (employees) or by written request | 30 days |
| Withdraw consent | Same channel used to give consent | Processing stops within 7 days |

If a data principal is not satisfied, they may approach the Data Protection Board of India after exhausting our grievance process.

## 6. Children's data

Nimbus does not knowingly process personal data of children (under 18) except dependants' data required for employee insurance and benefits, which is collected from the employee with their verifiable consent as parent or guardian, and used only for that purpose. No tracking or targeted advertising is directed at children.

## 7. Processing on behalf of clients

1. We process client personal data only on documented instructions in the contract or data processing agreement (DPA).
2. Sub-processors (including cloud providers) are engaged only with client approval where the contract requires it.
3. We assist clients with data principal requests within 5 working days of the client's request.
4. At contract end, client personal data is returned or deleted within 30 days, as described in the *Data Classification and Handling Policy*.

## 8. Cross-border transfers

Personal data may be transferred outside India except to countries restricted by notification of the Central Government. NimbusOps customer data is hosted in India (AWS ap-south-1 Mumbai, DR in ap-south-2 Hyderabad). Transfers of employee data to the Dubai office are limited to what is needed for local HR administration.

## 9. Personal data breaches

### 9.1 Internal reporting

Any suspected personal data breach must be reported immediately under the *Incident Reporting Procedure*. A breach involving Restricted personal data (health, financial, biometric) is treated as at least SEV2.

### 9.2 Notification

| Party | When | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Data Protection Board of India | Initial intimation without delay; detailed report within 72 hours of becoming aware, as required by the rules | DPO |
| Affected data principals | Without delay, in plain language, with steps they can take | DPO with Corporate Communications |
| Clients (where Nimbus is processor) | Within 24 hours of confirmation, or sooner if the contract requires | Delivery head |
| CERT-In (if a cyber incident) | Within 6 hours of noticing | CISO's office |

## 10. Training

All employees complete the Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023) e-learning within 7 days of joining and every year thereafter. HR, Finance and NimbusOps support staff complete an additional role-based module.

## 11. Records

Each business unit maintains a record of processing activities listing: data categories, purposes, legal basis, retention, systems, sub-processors and transfers. Records are reviewed every 6 months by the privacy champion and the DPO.

## 12. Penalties and enforcement

The DPDP Act provides for penalties of up to ₹250 crore per instance for failure to take reasonable security safeguards. Internally, breaches of this policy are handled under the *Code of Conduct*.
