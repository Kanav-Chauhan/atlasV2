# Rate Card FY2026-27

## 1. Purpose and use

This rate card sets Nimbus Infotech's list prices for professional services, managed services and NimbusOps subscriptions from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. It is for internal use by Sales, Delivery and Finance when preparing proposals. **Never share this document with clients**; clients receive only the rates in their signed contract.

All discounts from these list rates require approval under the Discount Approval Policy (NIM/SAL/POL-001). Existing contracts keep their contracted rates plus the contractual annual escalation.

## 2. Changes from FY2025-26

- Offshore rates increased by an average of **4.5%**; onshore North America by **3.5%**.
- New role: GenAI Engineer (offshore and onshore).
- NimbusOps Enterprise plan price increased from USD 4.75 to **USD 5.00** per host per month.

## 9. Fixed-price and outcome-based deals

- Fixed-price proposals must include a **risk contingency of 10–15%** on effort (15% for new clients or new technology).
- Target deal gross margin: 35% for T&M, 32% for fixed price, 40% for managed services, 70% contribution for NimbusOps.
- Price in the client's contracting currency; Finance sets the USD/INR planning rate at **₹84.50** for FY27 proposals.
