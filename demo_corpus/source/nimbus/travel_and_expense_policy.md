# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Nimbus employees book business travel, what the company pays for, and how to claim reimbursement. The aim is to keep travellers safe and comfortable while spending client and company money responsibly.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees, interns and contractors travelling on Nimbus business, including travel billed to clients. Where a client contract has stricter travel rules, the client's rules apply and the project manager must tell the traveller before booking.

## 3. Approval before travel

1. All travel is requested in the expense tool's *Travel Request* form before booking.
2. Approvals required:

| Trip type | Approver |
|---|---|
| Domestic, billable to client | Project manager |
| Domestic, non-billable | Reporting manager |
| International, any | Reporting manager + business unit head |
| International, cost above ₹5 lakh per trip | Additionally the CFO |

3. Domestic travel should be requested at least 7 days in advance and international travel at least 21 days in advance. Late requests need a reason.

## 4. Air and rail travel

### 4.1 Class of travel

- **Domestic flights:** economy class for all grades.
- **International flights:** economy class when the scheduled flight time is under 8 hours; premium economy when the flight time is above 8 hours.
- **Rail:** AC 2-tier, or AC chair car for day trips under 6 hours.

Flight time means the scheduled duration of the longest single sector, not total journey time including layovers.

### 4.2 Booking

All flights are booked through the travel desk in the expense tool. Self-booked tickets are reimbursed only if they cost no more than the lowest fare the travel desk quoted for the same day. Change and cancellation fees are reimbursed only when the change is due to a business reason.

## 5. Hotels

Book hotels through the travel desk from the approved list. Nightly room-rate caps (excluding taxes):

| City category | Cap per night |
|---|---|
| Metro (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune) | ₹8,000 |
| Non-metro | ₹5,000 |
| International (USA, UK, UAE) | USD 220 |
| International (other) | USD 180 |

Stays longer than 14 nights in the same city should use a company-leased serviced apartment where available.

## 6. Daily allowance

Instead of claiming meals and incidentals against bills, travellers receive a flat daily allowance (DA):

| Location | Daily allowance |
|---|---|
| Metro city in India | ₹3,500 per day |
| Non-metro city in India | ₹2,500 per day |
| International | USD 75 per day |

- DA is paid for each calendar day away from the base location. Travel days count as full days if departure is before 2:00 pm, otherwise half days.
- When a client or Nimbus provides meals (for example, at a conference), DA is reduced by 50% for that day.
- DA is not paid for travel within the base city.

## 7. Local transport

- Airport and local taxis (app-based cabs) are reimbursed at actuals.
- Self-driven own car: ₹12 per km; own two-wheeler: ₹5 per km, with the route logged in the claim.
- Car rentals abroad need prior manager approval and must include full insurance.

## 8. Other reimbursable expenses

| Expense | Rule |
|---|---|
| Visa and passport fees for business travel | Actuals |
| Travel insurance (international) | Arranged by the travel desk; no personal policy claims |
| Laundry on trips over 5 days | Up to ₹1,000 per week (India), USD 30 per week (international) |
| Client entertainment | Pre-approval by business unit head; attendee names on the claim |
| Mobile roaming | International roaming pack chosen by the travel desk |

Non-reimbursable: alcohol (except approved client entertainment), minibar, personal entertainment, fines and penalties, spouse or family travel, loyalty-programme upgrades.

## 9. Claiming expenses

1. Submit claims in the expense tool within **30 days** of returning. Claims submitted after 60 days are rejected unless the CFO approves.
2. Attach itemised receipts for every expense above ₹500. DA does not need receipts.
3. The reporting manager approves within 5 working days; Finance audits and pays with the next weekly payout run (every Thursday).
4. Foreign-currency expenses are converted at the rate on the card statement, or the RBI reference rate on the day of the expense for cash.

## 10. Corporate cards and advances

- Employees who travel more than 4 times a year may apply for a Nimbus corporate card with a monthly limit of ₹2 lakh.
- Travel advances are available for international trips, up to 80% of the expected DA. Unused advances must be settled within 15 days of return.

## 11. Non-compliance

Claims that breach this policy are returned for correction. Deliberate false claims are gross misconduct under the *Code of Conduct*.
