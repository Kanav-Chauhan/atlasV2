# POSH Policy – Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## 1. Policy statement

Nimbus Infotech Pvt. Ltd. is committed to a workplace free from sexual harassment. This policy is framed under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (the "POSH Act") and its Rules. While the Act protects women, Nimbus extends the complaint mechanism in this policy to employees of all genders.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to all employees, trainees, interns, contractors, consultants and visitors, and covers:

- All Nimbus offices (Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Dubai) and client sites.
- Business travel, offsites, client events, company transport and accommodation.
- Online and remote interactions: Slack, email, video calls and social media where the conduct is linked to work.

## 3. What is sexual harassment

Sexual harassment includes any one or more of the following unwelcome acts or behaviour, whether directly or by implication:

1. Physical contact and advances.
2. A demand or request for sexual favours.
3. Making sexually coloured remarks, jokes or messages.
4. Showing pornography or sexually explicit material.
5. Any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.

It also includes implied or explicit promises of preferential treatment, threats of detrimental treatment, interference with work, or creating an intimidating or hostile work environment, when linked to such conduct.

## 4. Internal Committee (IC)

Nimbus has constituted an Internal Committee at each Indian office as required by the POSH Act.

| Office | Presiding Officer | Members |
|---|---|---|
| Pune | Anjali Deshpande, Chief People Officer | 2 employee members, 1 external member from an NGO |
| Bengaluru | Senior woman leader, Bengaluru delivery | 2 employee members, 1 external member |
| Hyderabad | Kavya Reddy, VP Delivery | 2 employee members, 1 external member |

- At least half of each IC's members are women.
- IC members serve a term of 3 years.
- Current names and contact details are displayed at each office and on the intranet POSH page. The Dubai office routes complaints to the Pune IC.

## 5. How to make a complaint

1. Write to the IC at **posh@nimbus.example**, or hand a written complaint to any IC member.
2. The complaint should be made within **3 months** of the incident (or of the last incident in a series). The IC may extend this by a further 3 months if it is satisfied that circumstances prevented a timely complaint.
3. If the complainant cannot write the complaint, any IC member will help them put it in writing.
4. Complaints can also be made on behalf of the aggrieved person by a relative, friend or co-worker with their consent, as allowed by the Rules.

## 6. Process and timelines

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| IC acknowledges complaint | Within 2 working days |
| Copy of complaint sent to respondent | Within 7 days of receipt |
| Respondent submits reply | Within 10 days of receiving the copy |
| Conciliation (only if complainant requests it; no monetary settlement) | Before inquiry starts |
| Inquiry completed | Within 90 days of the complaint |
| IC report to management | Within 10 days of completing inquiry |
| Management acts on recommendations | Within 60 days of the report |

Either party may appeal the outcome to the appropriate authority within 90 days of the recommendations, as provided in the Act.

## 7. Interim relief

During the inquiry, on the written request of the complainant, the IC may recommend:

- Transfer of the complainant or respondent to another team or location.
- Up to 3 months of paid leave for the complainant, in addition to normal leave.
- Restraining the respondent from reporting on the complainant's work.

## 8. Outcomes

If the allegation is proven, the IC may recommend actions including a written apology, warning, withholding of promotion or increment, counselling, community service, deduction from salary to pay compensation to the complainant, or termination of employment.

## 9. False or malicious complaints

A complaint that cannot be proven is **not** a false complaint. Action may be recommended only where the IC finds that a complaint was made knowing it to be false, or with forged or misleading documents.

## 10. Confidentiality and non-retaliation

The identity of the complainant, respondent and witnesses, and the details of the inquiry, must not be published or disclosed. Any retaliation against a complainant or witness is misconduct under the *Code of Conduct*.

## 11. Awareness and training

- All new joiners complete the POSH Awareness e-learning within 7 days of joining.
- All employees repeat the module every year by 31 December.
- IC members attend a full-day orientation each year.
- People managers (L5 and above) attend an additional 90-minute workshop on responding to disclosures.

## 12. Annual report

Each IC submits an annual report to Nimbus and to the District Officer by **31 January** each year, covering complaints received, disposed of and pending. A summary (numbers only) is included in Nimbus's annual report to the Board.
