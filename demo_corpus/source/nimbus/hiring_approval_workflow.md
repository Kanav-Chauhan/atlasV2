# Hiring Approval Workflow

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how a manager gets approval to open a position, how candidates move through selection, and who approves the offer. It ensures hiring stays within the FY2026-27 headcount plan approved by the Board and that every hire has a clear business case.

## 2. Scope

All permanent and fixed-term hiring in India, at all grades L1 to L7. Contractor engagements follow the Vendor Onboarding procedure. Campus (L1) hiring is run centrally by Talent Acquisition and only steps 5 onwards apply.

## 3. Types of requisition

| Type | Definition | Budget check |
|---|---|---|
| Replacement | Backfill for a leaver or internal mover in the same grade and cost centre | Within existing budget |
| Planned growth | New position in the approved headcount plan for the business unit | Within approved plan |
| Unplanned growth | New position not in the plan (e.g. new client win) | Needs Finance sign-off |
| Billable client position | Position funded by a signed SOW or change request | Linked SOW number required |

## 4. Requisition approval matrix

| Requisition | L1–L3 | L4–L5 | L6 | L7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Replacement | Manager's manager | Business unit head | BU head + CPO | CEO |
| Planned growth | Business unit head | Business unit head | BU head + CPO | CEO |
| Unplanned growth | BU head + Finance business partner | BU head + CFO | CFO + CPO | CEO |
| Billable (signed SOW) | Delivery manager | VP Delivery | VP Delivery + CPO | CEO |

Approvals are recorded in the HRMS *Requisition* workflow. Approvers have **3 working days** to act; after that the request is escalated automatically to the next level.

## 5. Step-by-step workflow

1. **Raise requisition (Manager).** In HRMS > Requisition, enter grade, location (Pune, Bengaluru or Hyderabad), cost centre, type, skills and a justification. Attach the SOW number for billable positions.
2. **Approve (as per matrix).** HRMS routes automatically.
3. **Internal posting (Talent Acquisition).** All approved roles at L3 and above are posted on the internal job board for **7 days** before external sourcing. Internal candidates who meet the criteria must be interviewed.
4. **External sourcing (Talent Acquisition).** Employee referrals, job boards, and empanelled agencies. Agencies may only be used for L4 and above or hot-skill roles, with a fee capped at 8.33% of first-year fixed pay.
5. **Interview loop (Manager and panel).**

| Grade | Rounds |
|---|---|
| L2 – L3 | Online coding test, 2 technical rounds, manager round |
| L4 – L5 | 2 technical/design rounds, manager round, HR round |
| L6 – L7 | Architecture or leadership round, business unit head, CPO or CTO round, HR round |

Feedback must be entered in the HRMS within 24 hours of each interview.

6. **Selection decision (Hiring manager).** Requires at least 2 "hire" recommendations and no "strong no-hire".
7. **Offer approval.** Talent Acquisition prepares the offer within the salary band guidance provided by Total Rewards. Offer approvals:

| Offer | Approver |
|---|---|
| Within guidance | Talent Acquisition lead |
| Above guidance, within band | Business unit head |
| Above 110% of band midpoint | Chief People Officer |
| Joining bonus up to ₹2 lakh | Business unit head |
| Joining bonus above ₹2 lakh | Chief People Officer + CFO |

8. **Background verification.** Started on offer acceptance by the empanelled BGV vendor: education, last two employers, address, criminal record database check. For client-facing roles, any extra checks the client requires.
9. **Pre-joining.** Manager completes the *New Joiner Onboarding Checklist* tasks before day 1.

## 6. Service levels

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Requisition approval | 3 working days |
| First shortlist to manager | 7 working days after approval |
| Offer released after final interview | 3 working days |
| Overall time to offer | 30 days (L2–L4), 45 days (L5+) |

## 7. Headcount control

- Finance tracks approved requisitions against the FY2026-27 headcount plan each month.
- Requisitions open for more than 90 days without an offer are closed automatically, unless re-approved.
- During a hiring freeze declared by the CEO, only billable positions with signed SOWs and critical replacements approved by the CFO can be opened.

## 8. Fairness and compliance

- Job descriptions must use gender-neutral language and list only necessary requirements.
- Interviewers complete the *Structured Interviewing* course before joining a panel.
- Candidate personal data is handled under the *Data Protection Policy – DPDP Act 2023*; records of unsuccessful candidates are deleted after 1 year.
- Relatives of the hiring manager may not be hired into the manager's reporting chain.
