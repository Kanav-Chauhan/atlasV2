# Handling Performance Issues – PIP Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide helps managers address performance problems early and fairly, and, where informal support is not enough, run a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). A PIP is a structured, time-bound plan to help an employee return to the expected standard. It is not a formality before exit: in FY2025-26, 58% of employees on a PIP completed it successfully.

## 2. When to use this guide

| Signal | Recommended step |
|---|---|
| A single missed deliverable or behaviour issue | Feedback in the next 1:1 |
| The same issue raised in 3 or more 1:1s over 6 weeks | Informal improvement conversation (section 4) |
| Informal plan not met after 4–6 weeks | Formal PIP (section 5) |
| Annual rating of 1, or rating of 2 for a second consecutive year | Formal PIP is mandatory |
| Misconduct (dishonesty, harassment, policy breach) | Not a PIP; contact your HR Business Partner for the disciplinary process |

## 3. Before you start

1. Rule out causes outside performance: health issues, caregiving, harassment, unclear role, wrong project fit. Offer the Employee Assistance Programme where relevant.
2. Check the employee knew the expectation. If goals were never written down, write them down first and allow a fair period.
3. Gather evidence: specific examples with dates, impact, and earlier feedback given.
4. Talk to your HR Business Partner before any formal step.

## 4. Informal improvement conversation

- Hold it in a private 1:1, not by message.
- Describe the gap using the SBI model (Situation, Behaviour, Impact).
- Agree 2–3 specific improvements and a check-in date within 4 to 6 weeks.
- Send a short summary email afterwards. This is not a formal warning and is not placed on the HR file.

## 5. Formal PIP

### 5.1 Duration and structure

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Duration | **60 days** (may be extended once by up to 30 days) |
| Objectives | 3 to 5 measurable objectives, each with a clear success measure |
| Check-ins | Documented review every **2 weeks** (at least 4 reviews) |
| Participants | Manager and employee; HR Business Partner attends the first and final meetings |
| Template | HRMS > Performance > PIP |
| Approval to start | Skip-level manager and HR Business Partner |

### 5.2 Writing good PIP objectives

| Weak objective | Strong objective |
|---|---|
| "Improve code quality." | "At least 80% of your pull requests are approved without a 'changes requested' review in weeks 3–8; zero SEV2+ incidents traced to your changes." |
| "Communicate better with the client." | "Send the Harbor Logistics daily status by 7:00 pm IST on every working day; no client escalations about missed updates." |
| "Be more proactive." | "Raise blockers in stand-up within 1 working day; deliver the 3 agreed tickets per sprint." |

### 5.3 Support the manager must provide

- Named mentor or senior engineer for pairing, at least 2 hours a week.
- Relevant training on the Learning Portal.
- Clear priorities; no new major responsibilities during the PIP.

### 5.4 The PIP meeting

1. Explain the gap, the evidence and why a formal plan is needed.
2. Walk through each objective and how it will be measured.
3. Explain possible outcomes (section 6) honestly.
4. Give the employee 2 working days to add comments before both sign the plan in the HRMS.

## 6. Outcomes

| Outcome | What happens |
|---|---|
| Successfully completed | Employee returns to normal performance management. Merit increase withheld under the merit guidelines is reviewed at this point. |
| Partially met | PIP extended once, by up to 30 days, with revised objectives |
| Not met | Manager and HRBP recommend separation; approved by the business unit head. Notice is given as per the *Notice Period and Exit Process*. |

An employee may not be placed on a second formal PIP within 12 months of successfully completing one; a recurrence in that period goes straight to the business unit head for a decision.

## 7. Effects during a PIP

- The employee is not eligible for promotion or internal transfer.
- Variable pay follows the final annual rating, not the PIP itself.
- Leave may be taken normally; the PIP end date moves by the number of leave days above 5.

## 8. Do's and don'ts

**Do:** document every check-in on the same day; stay factual; recognise progress; keep the PIP confidential between the employee, you, your manager and HR.

**Don't:** start a PIP within 30 days of an employee returning from maternity or medical leave without HR approval; discuss the PIP with the team; change objectives mid-plan without a documented review.

## 9. Templates and support

The PIP template, sample objectives library and check-in form are in the HRMS. For complex cases (for example, a client asking for an employee's removal), contact the HR Business Partner and the delivery manager together.
