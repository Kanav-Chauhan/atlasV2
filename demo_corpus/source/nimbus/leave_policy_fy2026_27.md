# Leave Policy FY2026-27

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the leave entitlements of Nimbus employees in India for the financial year 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027, how leave accrues, how to apply, and how unused leave is treated. It supersedes the *Leave Policy FY2024-25* (NIM/HR/POL-003), which remained in force for FY2025-26.

## 2. Scope

All permanent employees in grades L1 (Trainee) to L7 (Director) based in Pune, Bengaluru and Hyderabad. Fixed-term contract staff receive leave pro rata to their contract. The Dubai office follows UAE labour law and a local addendum.

## 4. Earned leave

### 4.1 Accrual

Employees receive **18 days of earned leave** per financial year, accrued at 1.5 days at the end of each completed month of service. Employees who join after the 15th of a month start accruing from the next month.

### 4.2 Applying

- Apply in the HRMS at least **7 days in advance** for 3 or more consecutive days, and at least 2 working days in advance for shorter leave.
- Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days needs approval from the manager's manager as well.
- Managers respond within 2 working days; requests not acted on are escalated automatically to the skip-level manager.

### 4.3 Carry forward and lapse

At the end of the financial year, up to **30 days** of unused earned leave are carried forward into the next year. **EL beyond 30 days lapses on 31 March.** There is no encashment of earned leave while in service. On exit, accumulated EL up to 30 days is encashed on basic pay as part of full and final settlement.

### 4.4 Sandwich rule

Weekends and holidays falling between two days of earned leave are not counted as leave.

## 5. Casual/sick leave

1. Employees receive **10 days of casual/sick leave** per financial year, credited on 1 April. Joiners receive a pro-rata credit for the remaining months, rounded up to the nearest half day.
2. Casual/sick leave may be taken in half-day units and does not need advance approval for illness; inform your manager before 10:00 am on the day.
3. A medical certificate is required for sick absence of more than 3 consecutive days.
4. Casual/sick leave cannot be combined with earned leave to extend a vacation beyond 15 consecutive working days without skip-level approval.
5. Unused casual/sick leave lapses on 31 March and is not encashed.

## 6. Parental leave

### 6.1 Maternity leave

Women employees receive **26 weeks** of paid maternity leave under the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, for the first two children, of which up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected date of delivery. For the third child onwards, 12 weeks apply. Adopting mothers of a child below 3 months and commissioning mothers receive 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over. Eligibility requires at least 80 days of work in the 12 months before the expected delivery date.

### 6.2 Paternity leave

Employees receive **2 weeks** (10 working days) of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption, in one or two blocks.

### 6.3 Return to work

Employees returning from maternity leave may use the reduced office-attendance arrangement in the *Hybrid Work Policy*.

## 7. Other leave

| Leave | Rule |
|---|---|
| Bereavement | 5 working days on the death of spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law |
| Marriage | 5 working days, once during employment, within 1 month of the wedding |
| Leave without pay (LWP) | Up to 30 days per year, with CPO approval, only after EL is exhausted |
| Sabbatical | Up to 6 months unpaid for employees with 5+ years of service, approved by CPO |
| Voting | Paid time off as required by law on polling day in the employee's constituency |

## 8. Public holidays

Each employee receives **12 public holidays** per financial year. The list varies by city and is published in the *Holiday Calendar FY2026-27* in the Company Handbook.

## 9. Leave during notice period

Earned leave may be taken during the notice period only with the manager's approval and does not shorten the notice period. See the *Notice Period and Exit Process*.

## 10. Unauthorised absence

Absence without approval for 3 or more consecutive working days is treated as unauthorised. HR will attempt contact by phone and email; absence of 10 or more consecutive working days without contact may be treated as abandonment of employment after a written notice.

## 11. Key changes from FY2024-25

| Item | FY2024-25 | FY2026-27 |
|---|---|---|
| Earned leave | 15 days | 18 days |
| Casual/sick leave | 12 days | 10 days |
| EL carry-forward cap | 24 days | 30 days |
| Paternity leave | 5 working days | 2 weeks |

## 12. Administration

HR Operations manages leave records in the HRMS. Balance discrepancies must be raised within 30 days of the month in question with hr-help@nimbus.example.
