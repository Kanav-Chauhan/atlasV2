# Q1 FY2026-27 Revenue and Collections Report

For the quarter 1 April to 30 June 2026. Figures are unaudited management accounts, standalone, in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise. USD revenue is converted at the actual monthly average rate (Q1 average ₹84.20 per USD).

## 1. Summary

- Q1 revenue was **₹318.6 crore**, up 13.1% on Q1 FY2025-26 (₹281.7 crore) and 23.8% of the FY27 budget of ₹1,340 crore.
- North America remained the largest region at **₹197.4 crore (62%)** of revenue.
- Collections were ₹301.2 crore, a collection efficiency of 94.5% against invoices due in the quarter.
- Days sales outstanding (**debtor days**) improved to **58 days** at 30 June 2026, from 61 days at 31 March 2026.
- Full-year revenue forecast: **₹1,326 crore**, which is ₹14 crore (1.0%) below the FY27 budget.

## 5. Forecast against FY27 target

| ₹ crore | FY27 budget | Q1 actual | Q2–Q4 forecast | Full-year forecast | Gap to budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 1,340.0 | 318.6 | 1,007.4 | **1,326.0** | (14.0) |
| EBITDA | 261.3 | 59.9 | 196.3 | 256.2 | (5.1) |
| EBITDA margin | 19.5% | 18.8% | 19.5% | 19.3% | (0.2) pts |

Main reasons for the forecast gap:

1. **Crestline Insurance Phase 2** delayed: signature now expected in September 2026 rather than July, moving about ₹9 crore of revenue out of FY27.
2. **Harbor Logistics renewal** (term ends 30 September 2026) forecast at the target year-1 value in the renewal plan; any further rate reduction would reduce FY27 revenue by up to ₹4 crore.
3. Upside not yet in the forecast: the freight visibility data platform pilot at Harbor (about ₹5.4 crore if signed by January 2027).
