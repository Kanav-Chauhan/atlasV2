# Leave Policy FY2024-25

**Status note:** This version is superseded by the Leave Policy FY2026-27 with effect from 01 April 2026. It is retained for reference only. It applied to FY2024-25 and, by extension approved by the Chief People Officer, to FY2025-26.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out leave entitlements for Nimbus employees in India for the financial year 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025, how to apply for leave, and how unused leave is treated.

## 2. Scope

All permanent employees in grades L1 to L7 based in Pune, Bengaluru and Hyderabad.

## 3. Summary of entitlements

| Leave type | Entitlement per financial year | Accrual | Carry forward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earned leave (EL) | 15 days | 1.25 days per completed month | Up to 24 days |
| Casual/sick leave (CL/SL) | 12 days | Credited on 1 April | Not carried forward |
| Public holidays | 12 days | As per city holiday calendar | — |
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks | — | — |
| Paternity leave | 5 working days | — | — |
| Bereavement leave | 3 working days per event | — | — |
| Compensatory off | 1 day per holiday worked | — | Within 30 days |

## 4. Earned leave

### 4.1 Accrual

Employees receive **15 days of earned leave** per financial year, accrued at 1.25 days per completed month.

### 4.2 Applying

Apply in the HRMS at least 7 days in advance for 3 or more consecutive days. Leave longer than 10 working days requires skip-level approval.

### 4.3 Carry forward

Up to **24 days** of unused EL may be carried forward. EL beyond 24 days lapses on 31 March. On exit, accumulated EL is encashed on basic pay.

## 5. Casual/sick leave

1. **12 days of casual/sick leave** per financial year, credited on 1 April, pro rata for joiners.
2. A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive days of sick leave.
3. Unused CL/SL lapses on 31 March.

## 6. Parental leave

- **Maternity:** 26 weeks for the first two children under the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961; 12 weeks from the third child.
- **Paternity:** 5 working days, within 1 month of the birth.

## 7. Other leave

| Leave | Rule |
|---|---|
| Bereavement | 3 working days for immediate family |
| Leave without pay | Up to 15 days per year, with HR approval |

## 8. Public holidays

12 public holidays per financial year as per the city holiday calendar published by People Operations.

## 9. Approval responsibilities

| Request | Approver | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| EL or CL/SL up to 10 working days | Reporting manager | 3 working days |
| EL above 10 working days | Reporting manager + skip-level manager | 5 working days |
| Leave without pay | Reporting manager + HR Business Partner | 5 working days |
| Maternity or paternity leave | HR Operations (on submission of documents) | 3 working days |

Requests not acted on within the response time are escalated automatically to the skip-level manager. Employees on client deployments must also inform the client project manager of planned leave at least 2 weeks in advance.

## 10. Unauthorised absence

Absence without approval for 3 or more consecutive working days is treated as unauthorised and may lead to disciplinary action.

## 11. Administration

HR Operations maintains leave records in the HRMS. Queries: hr-help@nimbus.example.
