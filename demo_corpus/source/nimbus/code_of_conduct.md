# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

This Code sets out the standards of behaviour Nimbus Infotech Pvt. Ltd. ("Nimbus") expects from everyone who works for or with it. It applies to all employees, trainees, interns, contractors and consultants in every Nimbus location: Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Dubai, and at client sites.

The Code does not replace detailed policies. Where a specific policy exists (for example the *POSH Policy*, the *Information Security Policy* or the *Travel and Expense Policy*), that policy applies in addition to this Code.

## 2. Responsibilities

- **Every employee** must read this Code, complete the Code of Conduct e-learning within 7 days of joining and re-certify every year by 30 June.
- **Managers** must model the Code, make sure their teams complete certification, and escalate any concern raised with them within 2 working days.
- **The Ethics Committee** (Chief People Officer as chair, CFO, CISO and one independent senior leader nominated by the CEO) oversees investigations and reports quarterly to the Board.

## 3. Respect in the workplace

1. We treat colleagues, clients and vendors with dignity regardless of gender, religion, caste, region, language, age, disability, sexual orientation or marital status.
2. Harassment of any kind, including sexual harassment, bullying and intimidation, is prohibited. Sexual harassment complaints are handled by the Internal Committee under the *POSH Policy*.
3. Hiring, promotion and pay decisions are based on merit and documented criteria only.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest exists when a personal interest could influence, or appear to influence, your work decisions.

### 4.1 Situations you must declare

- A financial interest above ₹5 lakh, or any directorship, in a client, vendor or competitor.
- A close relative working at a client or vendor you deal with, or reporting into your chain at Nimbus.
- Any outside employment or paid freelance work. Unpaid open-source work does not need declaration unless it competes with NimbusOps.

### 4.2 How to declare

Submit the Conflict of Interest form in the HRMS within 15 days of the situation arising. Your manager and the Chief People Officer review it and record a decision (no action, mitigate, or exit the situation) within 30 days.

## 5. Gifts, hospitality and anti-bribery

Nimbus has zero tolerance for bribery, consistent with the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 and the anti-bribery laws of the countries where our clients operate (including the US FCPA and UK Bribery Act).

| Item | Limit | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Gifts received from a client or vendor | Up to ₹5,000 per gift, ₹15,000 per year per giver | None; record in the gift register |
| Gifts above these limits | Not to be accepted | Return politely or hand to Admin |
| Hospitality (meals, events) received | Up to ₹10,000 per occasion | Manager approval above this |
| Gifts given to clients | Up to ₹5,000 per person | VP-level approval above this |
| Anything to a government official | Not permitted | — |

Cash, gift cards and vouchers may never be accepted, at any value.

## 6. Confidential information and client data

1. Treat all client information as at least **Confidential** under the *Data Classification and Handling Policy*.
2. Never use client data, code or credentials outside the project for which it was provided.
3. Do not paste client code or data into public generative-AI tools. Use only the AI tools listed as approved in the *Acceptable Use Policy*.
4. Confidentiality obligations continue after you leave Nimbus.

## 7. Use of company assets

Laptops, accounts, software licences and office facilities are provided for business use. Limited personal use is allowed as described in the *Acceptable Use Policy*. Asset loss must be reported to the IT ServiceDesk within 24 hours.

## 8. Insider information and social media

- Do not trade in securities of a listed client or partner while in possession of unpublished price-sensitive information about it.
- Do not post about clients, deals or unreleased NimbusOps features on social media. Only Corporate Communications speaks for Nimbus publicly.

## 9. Raising concerns (whistle-blowing)

You may raise a concern in good faith through any of these channels:

| Channel | Details |
|---|---|
| Your manager or HR Business Partner | In person or by email |
| Ethics hotline (anonymous) | ethics@nimbus.example or the web form on the intranet |
| Ethics Committee chair | Anjali Deshpande, Chief People Officer |
| Audit Committee chair (for concerns about senior leadership) | Via the Company Secretary |

The Ethics Committee acknowledges every report within 3 working days and aims to close investigations within 45 days. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a serious breach of this Code.

## 10. Breaches and consequences

Breaches are dealt with under the disciplinary procedure. Depending on severity, outcomes range from a written warning to termination and, where the law requires, reporting to authorities. Examples of breaches treated as gross misconduct include bribery, theft, falsifying timesheets or expense claims, and wilful disclosure of client data.

## 11. Annual certification

Each year between 1 June and 30 June, all employees certify in the HRMS that they have read the Code and declared any conflicts. Employees who do not certify by 30 June have their access to the HRMS self-service restricted until they do, and this is noted in their annual review.
