# Accounts Payable and Vendor Payment Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Nimbus Infotech onboards vendors, approves and checks invoices, deducts taxes and pays vendors and employees on time. It applies from 1 April 2026 for the financial year April 2026 to March 2027 and covers all Indian entities and the Dubai sales office.

## 2. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Requester / cost centre owner | Raises the purchase order (PO), confirms receipt of goods or services |
| Procurement | Vendor selection, onboarding, PO issue |
| Accounts Payable (AP) team | Invoice checks, tax deductions, payment runs |
| Financial Controller (Deepa Krishnan) | Owns this policy, approves exceptions up to ₹10 lakh |
| Chief Financial Officer (Rohan Bhatia) | Approves exceptions above ₹10 lakh and all advance payments |

## 3. Vendor onboarding

No payment is made to a vendor that is not onboarded in the ERP vendor master. To onboard a vendor, Procurement collects:

1. PAN and GST registration certificate (GSTIN validated on the GST portal).
2. Cancelled cheque or bank letter; bank details are verified by a **₹1 test credit** before the first payment.
3. MSME (Udyam) registration certificate, if the vendor is a micro or small enterprise.
4. Signed vendor code of conduct and, for vendors that will access Nimbus systems or personal data, a completed security assessment approved by the CISO's team.
5. Lower TDS deduction certificate, if the vendor holds one.

Onboarding is completed within **5 business days** of receiving all documents. Changes to vendor bank details need a request on the vendor's letterhead plus a call-back to a known contact, recorded by AP; this prevents payment fraud.

## 4. Purchase orders and invoice approval

All purchases above ₹25,000 need a PO raised **before** the order is placed ("no PO, no pay"). Exceptions: utilities, statutory payments, rent under signed leases and employee expense claims.

Invoices are approved in the ERP workflow at these levels (invoice value excluding GST):

| Invoice value | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹1 lakh | Cost centre owner |
| Above ₹1 lakh and up to ₹10 lakh | Department head |
| Above ₹10 lakh and up to ₹50 lakh | Chief Financial Officer |
| Above ₹50 lakh | Chief Financial Officer and Founder & CEO |

### 4.1 Three-way match

AP matches every PO-based invoice against the PO and the goods receipt note (GRN) or service confirmation. Differences within **2% or ₹5,000, whichever is lower**, are accepted automatically. Larger differences are sent back to the requester and the payment clock does not start until the invoice is corrected.

## 5. Tax checks

- **GST:** the invoice must show the vendor's GSTIN, Nimbus's GSTIN for the correct state, HSN/SAC code, place of supply and tax breakup. Input tax credit is claimed only when the invoice appears in GSTR-2B; if it does not appear within 60 days, AP holds the GST portion of the next payment.
- **TDS:** AP deducts TDS under the Income-tax Act at the applicable rate (for example, contractors under section 194C, professional and technical services under section 194J, rent under section 194-I) and issues TDS certificates every quarter.
- **E-invoicing:** invoices from vendors above the e-invoicing threshold must carry a valid IRN and QR code; invoices without them are rejected.
- **Imports of services** (for example, US cloud or software subscriptions) are checked for reverse-charge GST and equalisation levy where applicable.

## 6. Payment terms and payment runs

| Vendor type | Standard payment terms |
|---|---|
| Standard vendors | **45 days** from receipt of a valid invoice |
| Micro and small enterprises (MSME registered) | Within 45 days, as required by the MSMED Act; never later |
| Utilities, statutory dues, rent | By the due date |
| Subcontractors on client projects | 45 days, or back-to-back with client payment only if agreed in writing at onboarding |
| Overseas vendors | 45 days, paid in the invoice currency via the bank's foreign remittance desk |

- Vendor payment runs happen **every Tuesday and Friday**. An invoice is paid in the last run on or before its due date.
- Early payment is allowed only for a discount of at least 1.5% and with Financial Controller approval.
- **Advance payments** need CFO approval and are limited to **20% of the PO value**, except for software subscriptions and event bookings, which may be paid fully in advance.

## 7. Employee expense claims

Employee expense claims follow the Travel and Expense Policy; this section only covers the finance processing side.

- Claims must be submitted in the expense tool within **30 days** of the trip or expense. Claims submitted after **60 days** are rejected unless the CFO approves.
- The reporting manager approves within 5 working days; AP audits a sample of claims and every claim above ₹50,000.
- Approved claims are paid in the **weekly payout run every Thursday**.
- Daily allowances are paid at the rates in the Travel and Expense Policy (for example, ₹3,500 per day for metro cities, ₹2,500 for non-metro cities and USD 75 for international travel); AP does not change these rates.
- Corporate card statements must be reconciled in the expense tool within 10 days of the statement date.

## 8. Month-end and year-end

- AP cut-off is the **3rd business day** of the following month; invoices for services received but not invoiced by then are accrued by the cost centre owner.
- At year-end (31 March), all open POs older than 180 days are closed unless the owner confirms they are still needed.
- AP ageing is reviewed every month by the Financial Controller; any MSME invoice older than 40 days is escalated the same day.

## 9. Controls and audit

- Segregation of duties: the person who creates a vendor cannot approve invoices for that vendor or release payments.
- Payment files are released by two authorised signatories in the bank portal (dual control).
- Duplicate invoice checks run on every invoice (same vendor, invoice number and amount).
- Breaches of this policy, such as splitting POs to avoid an approval level, are reported to the CFO and handled under the Code of Conduct.
