# Employee Benefits Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide summarises the benefits Nimbus provides to permanent employees in India for FY2026-27 (1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027). It is a summary; the insurance policy documents and the relevant HR policies govern in case of difference. Benefits for the Dubai office are described in a separate local addendum.

## 2. Eligibility

All permanent employees in grades L1 to L7 are eligible from their date of joining. Interns and contractors are not covered unless their contract states otherwise. Insurance cover for newly added dependants starts from the date they are added in the HRMS, provided they are added within 30 days of the life event (marriage, birth or adoption).

## 3. Insurance

### 3.1 Group medical insurance

| Feature | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Sum insured | ₹5 lakh per family per year (floater) |
| Who is covered | Employee, spouse or partner, up to 2 children |
| Parents or parents-in-law | Optional top-up of ₹3 lakh, premium paid by employee through payroll |
| Maternity | ₹75,000 for normal delivery, ₹1 lakh for caesarean, within the sum insured |
| Room rent | Up to 2% of sum insured per day for a standard room |
| Pre-existing diseases | Covered from day 1 |
| Cashless hospitals | Network of the insurer's TPA; list in the benefits portal |

Employees in grade L6 and above receive a sum insured of ₹10 lakh per family.

### 3.2 Group term life insurance

Cover equal to **3 times annual fixed pay**, with a minimum of ₹20 lakh, paid by Nimbus. Nominations are updated in the HRMS.

### 3.3 Group personal accident insurance

Cover equal to **5 times annual fixed pay** for accidental death or permanent disability, 24x7 worldwide.

## 4. Retirement benefits

- **Provident Fund (PF):** Nimbus contributes 12% of basic pay, matching the employee contribution, under the EPF Act.
- **Gratuity:** Paid under the Payment of Gratuity Act on completing 5 years of continuous service.
- **National Pension System (NPS):** Optional; employees may ask for up to 10% of basic pay to be routed to NPS from their CTC.

## 5. Allowances and reimbursements

| Benefit | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Internet reimbursement | Up to ₹1,000 per month | Bill upload in the expense tool, quarterly |
| Mobile reimbursement | Up to ₹750 per month | L4 and above, or on-call roster members |
| Night-shift allowance | ₹400 per night | Rostered shifts covering 10:00 pm–6:00 am |
| On-call allowance | ₹2,000 per week on primary on-call | Engineering and Managed Services rosters |
| Meal card | ₹2,200 per month | Optional, part of CTC |
| Fuel and vehicle maintenance | As per CTC structure | L5 and above |

## 6. Leave-related benefits

Leave entitlements are set out in the *Leave Policy FY2026-27* (HR Policies). In summary, each financial year employees receive 18 days of earned leave, 10 days of casual/sick leave and 12 public holidays. Maternity leave is 26 weeks under the Maternity Benefit Act and paternity leave is 2 weeks.

## 7. Wellbeing

1. **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP):** Free, confidential counselling for employees and immediate family, 6 sessions per person per year, 24x7 helpline.
2. **Annual health check-up:** Free for employees aged 30 and above, and for all L5 and above, at partner diagnostic centres.
3. **Fitness:** ₹6,000 per year towards gym, yoga or sports memberships, claimed through the expense tool.
4. **Crèche support:** Up to ₹5,000 per month per child under 6 years, for employees using a crèche near the Pune, Bengaluru or Hyderabad office.

## 8. Learning and development

- **Certification sponsorship:** Nimbus reimburses exam fees for approved cloud, data, SAP, Salesforce and security certifications on passing, up to ₹50,000 per financial year per employee.
- **Learning Portal:** Unlimited access to the internal Learning Portal and licensed online course libraries.
- **Higher education:** Up to ₹2 lakh per year for approved part-time degrees, with a 12-month service commitment after completion.

## 9. Other benefits

- **Employee Stock Options (ESOP):** Granted to selected employees at L5 and above under the Nimbus ESOP Plan; grants are communicated individually.
- **Referral bonus:** See the *Referral Bonus Policy* (HR Policies).
- **Relocation:** Employees moving between Nimbus cities at company request may be eligible for relocation assistance; HR explains the details when the move is agreed.
- **Transport:** Shuttle buses on all working days from major residential areas to the Pune and Hyderabad offices.

## 10. Claims and help

| Need | Contact |

Reimbursement claims for a financial year must be submitted by **15 March** of that year; unclaimed amounts lapse and are not carried forward.
