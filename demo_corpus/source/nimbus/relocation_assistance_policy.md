# Relocation Assistance Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the financial support Nimbus provides when an employee moves city at the company's request, or when a new hire relocates to join. It aims to make the move simple and predictable while keeping costs consistent across grades.

## 2. Scope and eligibility

| Situation | Eligible? |
|---|---|
| New hire relocating more than 150 km to join Pune, Bengaluru or Hyderabad | Yes, if stated in the offer letter |
| Existing employee transferred at company request (e.g. to staff the Hyderabad campus) | Yes |
| Employee-requested transfer for personal reasons | No relocation lump sum; one-way travel only |
| International assignment (e.g. Dubai, client site in the US) | No; covered by the Global Mobility Policy |
| L1 campus trainees | Travel to training location and 15 days' company accommodation only |

## 3. Relocation package by grade

| Grade | Lump-sum relocation allowance | Temporary accommodation | Household goods move |
|---|---|---|---|
| L2 – L3 | ₹50,000 | Up to 15 nights | Up to ₹40,000 |
| L4 | ₹75,000 | Up to 21 nights | Up to ₹60,000 |
| L5 | ₹1,00,000 | Up to 30 nights | Up to ₹90,000 |
| L6 | ₹1,50,000 | Up to 30 nights | Up to ₹1,25,000 |
| L7 | ₹2,00,000 | Up to 45 nights | Actuals, with CPO approval |

### 3.1 Lump-sum allowance

The lump sum covers miscellaneous costs: brokerage, deposits, school admission fees, vehicle transport and setting up a new home. It is paid in the first payroll after joining or transfer and is taxable as salary.

### 3.2 Temporary accommodation

Company-booked serviced apartment or hotel near the office, at the metro hotel cap in the *Travel and Expense Policy*. Daily allowance is **not** paid during temporary accommodation in the new base city.

### 3.3 Household goods

Packing, moving and insurance by one of the two empanelled movers, billed directly to Nimbus up to the grade limit. Any excess is deducted from salary with the employee's consent.

### 3.4 Travel to the new location

One-way economy air or AC 2-tier rail for the employee, spouse and up to 2 children. One house-hunting trip of up to 3 days for the employee and spouse is covered for L4 and above.

## 4. Approval

1. For new hires, the relocation package is included in the offer letter, approved by the Talent Acquisition lead.
2. For transfers, the transfer request in the HRMS includes the relocation package, approved by both the current and receiving business unit heads.
3. Packages above the grade table need Chief People Officer approval.

## 5. Claims and timelines

- Relocation expenses other than the lump sum must be claimed within **60 days** of the move, with receipts, in the expense tool.
- The relocation benefit must be used within 6 months of the joining or transfer date.

## 6. Clawback

If the employee resigns or is terminated for cause within **12 months** of the relocation date, the relocation costs are recovered:

| Months of service after relocation | Recovery |
|---|---|
| 0 – 6 months | 100% of relocation costs |
| 7 – 12 months | 50% of relocation costs |
| After 12 months | No recovery |

Recovery is made from the full and final settlement. Employees sign a relocation agreement before any payment.

## 7. Hyderabad campus expansion transfers

For FY2026-27, employees who volunteer to transfer from Pune or Bengaluru to Hyderabad to staff the new campus receive:

- 1.5 times the lump-sum allowance in section 3.
- An extra 15 nights of temporary accommodation.
- A reduced clawback period of 6 months instead of 12.

This programme is open to transfers approved between 1 June 2026 and 31 March 2027, within the transfer quota set by the VP Delivery (Hyderabad).

## 8. Tax treatment

The lump-sum allowance is taxable. Actual-cost reimbursements of moving household goods and travel on transfer are treated in line with the Income-tax Act; Payroll confirms the treatment in the employee's Form 16.

## 9. Contacts

Relocation coordinator: People Operations, relocation@nimbus.example. Movers' contact details are shared after approval.
