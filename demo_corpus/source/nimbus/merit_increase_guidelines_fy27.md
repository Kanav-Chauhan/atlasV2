# Merit Increase Guidelines FY27

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines explain how merit increases for the FY2026-27 cycle (FY27) are budgeted, calculated and approved. They are for HR Business Partners and the Total Rewards team; managers receive their team-level worksheets but not this full document.

## 2. Budget

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Average merit budget | **9.5%** of eligible fixed payroll |
| Promotion budget (separate) | 1.5% of eligible fixed payroll |
| Market correction pool | 0.5% of eligible fixed payroll, held centrally by the CPO |
| Effective date | 1 April 2026 (paid in July 2026 payroll with arrears) |
| Approved by | CEO and CFO, February 2026 |

The 9.5% average budget is allocated to each business unit in proportion to its eligible fixed payroll. Business unit heads may not exceed their allocation; unspent budget returns to the central pool.

## 3. Eligibility

1. Employees who joined on or before 31 December 2025 and have a final rating for FY2025-26.
2. Employees on a performance improvement plan on 1 April 2026 receive no merit increase until the plan is completed successfully; a successful outcome triggers a review at that time.
3. Employees serving notice are not eligible.
4. Employees promoted in this cycle receive the promotion increase (12% to 18% under the *Salary Bands FY2026-27*) instead of, not in addition to, a merit increase.

## 4. Merit matrix

The merit increase depends on the performance rating and the employee's position in the salary band (compa-ratio). Managers recommend within the range; the guideline figure is the default.

| Rating | Compa-ratio below 0.90 | 0.90 – 1.10 | Above 1.10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 – Outstanding | 16% – 20% (guide 18%) | 13% – 16% (guide 14%) | 10% – 12% (guide 11%) |
| 4 – Exceeds | 12% – 15% (guide 13%) | 10% – 12% (guide 11%) | 7% – 9% (guide 8%) |
| 3 – Meets | 8% – 10% (guide 9%) | 7% – 9% (guide 8%) | 4% – 6% (guide 5%) |
| 2 – Partially meets | 0% – 4% (guide 3%) | 0% – 3% (guide 2%) | 0% |
| 1 – Does not meet | 0% | 0% | 0% |

When applied to the expected rating distribution and current compa-ratios, the guide figures produce a weighted average of about 9.4%, leaving a small buffer within the 9.5% budget.

## 5. Above-maximum employees

If the increase would take an employee's fixed pay above the band maximum, the portion above the maximum is paid as a **one-time lump sum** in the July payroll and is not added to fixed pay.

## 6. Hot-skill adjustments

Employees in roles on the hot-skills list (SAP S/4HANA finance, Kafka/streaming, Kubernetes platform engineering, security operations) with a rating of 3 or above may receive an extra 2% to 4% from the market correction pool, approved by the Chief People Officer.

## 7. Process and timeline

| Step | Date | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Budget allocation sent to business unit heads | 5 May 2026 | Total Rewards |
| Manager worksheets released in HRMS | 8 May 2026 | HR Operations |
| Manager recommendations due | 22 May 2026 | Managers |
| Business unit review and balancing | 23 May – 3 June 2026 | BU heads, HRBPs |
| CPO and CFO sign-off | 10 June 2026 | Anjali Deshpande, Rohan Bhatia |
| Letters released to employees | 20 June 2026 | HR Operations |
| Paid with arrears | July 2026 payroll | Payroll |

## 8. Approval thresholds

| Recommended increase | Approver |
|---|---|
| Within matrix range | Manager + business unit head |
| Above matrix range, up to +3 percentage points | HR Business Partner + business unit head |
| More than +3 percentage points above range, or any increase above 20% | Chief People Officer |
| Any increase for L7 | CEO |

## 9. Communication guidance for managers

1. Discuss increases only after letters are released on 20 June 2026.
2. Explain the increase in terms of rating and market position, not comparison with colleagues.
3. Do not quote the overall budget percentage or other employees' increases.
4. Direct questions you cannot answer to your HR Business Partner.

## 10. Cost summary (FY27)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Eligible fixed payroll (India) | ₹612 crore |
| Merit cost at 9.5% | ₹58.1 crore |
| Promotion cost at 1.5% | ₹9.2 crore |
| Market correction pool at 0.5% | ₹3.1 crore |
| **Total FY27 increase cost (annualised)** | **₹70.4 crore** |

The FY27 increase cost is included in the FY2026-27 budget approved by the Board.

## 11. Audit

Total Rewards runs a pay equity check after recommendations close, comparing increases by gender, city and grade. Unexplained gaps above 2 percentage points are reviewed with the business unit head before sign-off.
