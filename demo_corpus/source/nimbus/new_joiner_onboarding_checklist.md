# New Joiner Onboarding Checklist

A checklist for **hiring managers**. Good onboarding is the single biggest lever on early attrition: in FY2025-26, joiners whose managers completed this checklist were half as likely to leave in their first 6 months. Tick items in the HRMS *Onboarding* task list so People Operations can see progress.

## 1. Before day 1 (from offer acceptance)

| # | Task | When | Done by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Confirm joining date and base office with Talent Acquisition | On offer acceptance | Manager |
| 1.2 | Raise the laptop request in ServiceDesk (standard or developer model) | At least 10 working days before joining | Manager |
| 1.3 | Raise access requests for team tools: Jira, Confluence, GitHub, project Slack channels | 5 working days before | Manager |
| 1.4 | Nominate an onboarding buddy (same team, L3 or above, at least 12 months at Nimbus) | 5 working days before | Manager |
| 1.5 | Book a desk for day 1 and the first anchor days in the Desk Booking app | 3 working days before | Buddy |
| 1.6 | Send a welcome email with first-day time (9:30 am), location and buddy's name | 3 working days before | Manager |
| 1.7 | Prepare the 30-60-90 day plan draft | Before day 1 | Manager |
| 1.8 | For client-facing roles: check client background-verification and NDA requirements with the delivery manager | 10 working days before | Manager |

## 2. Day 1

- [ ] Meet the joiner after the 11:00 am People Operations induction (it ends around 1:00 pm).
- [ ] Lunch with the team (team lunch on day 1 is reimbursed up to ₹750 per person, max 8 people).
- [ ] Confirm laptop works, MFA is registered and the joiner can log in to email and Slack.
- [ ] Introduce the buddy and walk through the team's working agreement (anchor days, stand-up time, on-call expectations).
- [ ] Share the *Welcome to Nimbus* page and the Company Handbook collection in Atlas.

## 3. Week 1

- [ ] First 1:1 (45 minutes): role expectations, 30-60-90 plan, how you like to communicate. See *Running Effective 1:1s*.
- [ ] Confirm mandatory e-learning is completed **within 7 calendar days**: Information Security Awareness, Code of Conduct, POSH Awareness, Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023).
- [ ] Give a small, real first task that can be finished within the week (a bug fix, a doc improvement, a test).
- [ ] Introduce to 3–5 key stakeholders outside the team.
- [ ] Engineers: walk through the team's runbooks and architecture docs; add to the on-call shadow rota (no primary on-call in the first 8 weeks).

## 4. First 30 days

- [ ] Weekly 1:1s.
- [ ] Goals for the current performance year entered in the HRMS within 30 days of joining (4 to 6 goals, weights adding to 100%).
- [ ] Buddy check-ins twice a week for the first 2 weeks, then weekly.
- [ ] 30-day check-in form completed by manager in the HRMS.
- [ ] Joiner completes the 30-day pulse survey (sent automatically by People Operations).

## 5. Days 31–90

- [ ] Move to the team's standard 1:1 cadence (weekly or fortnightly).
- [ ] 60-day check-in: progress against plan; any early concerns raised explicitly, with examples.
- [ ] First code review led, first customer call or first design discussion, as relevant to the role.
- [ ] 90-day review completed in the HRMS. This is also the point after which any referral bonus for the hire is paid.

## 6. Probation (6 months)

- [ ] Month 5: decide whether to confirm, extend (once, by up to 3 months) or not confirm.
- [ ] Inform HR Operations by the 10th of the month in which probation ends.
- [ ] Confirmation letter is issued by HR within 5 working days of the manager's recommendation.

Remember: during probation the notice period on either side is 15 days; after confirmation it becomes the normal notice period for the grade.

## 7. Red flags to escalate to your HR Business Partner

- Laptop or access not ready by day 3.
- Joiner has not had a 1:1 in the first week.
- Joiner says the role is different from what was described at interview.
- Joiner is assigned to a project with no clear tasks for 2 or more weeks ("on bench") in the first 90 days.
