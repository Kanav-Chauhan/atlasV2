# Welcome to Nimbus

Welcome aboard. This page is the starting point for every new Nimbus employee. It explains who we are, how we are organised, where things live and what your first weeks will look like. Bookmark it; most other handbook pages link back here.

## 1. Who we are

Nimbus Infotech Pvt. Ltd. is an IT services and product engineering company founded in Pune in 2009 by Vikram Kulkarni, who is still our Founder & CEO. Our tagline is **"Engineering clarity."** and it describes how we want to work: clear designs, clear communication, clear ownership.

## 2. What we do

We are organised into four service lines and one product team:

1. **Cloud & Platform Engineering** – cloud migrations, Kubernetes platforms, landing zones and DevOps.
2. **Data & AI** – data platforms, analytics engineering, ML and generative-AI solutions.
3. **Enterprise Apps** – SAP (including S/4HANA programmes) and Salesforce implementation and support.
4. **Managed Services** – our 24x7 NOC and SOC, run from Pune and Hyderabad.
5. **NimbusOps** – our in-house IT operations monitoring SaaS, used by about 140 customers. The NimbusOps team is led on the engineering side by Neha Gupta.

## 5. How we work

- **Working week:** Monday to Friday, 9.5 hours a day including breaks.
- **Hybrid:** 3 office days a week. See the *Hybrid Work Policy* for anchor days and exceptions.
- **Leave:** 18 days of earned leave, 10 days of casual/sick leave and 12 public holidays per financial year. See the *Leave Policy* (HR) and the *Holiday Calendar FY2026-27* for your city.
- **Notice period:** 60 days, or 90 days for Senior Manager (L6) and above.

## 6. Your first two weeks

### 6.1 Day 1

1. Report to the reception of your base office at 9:30 am with your original ID and education documents.
2. Collect your laptop from the IT desk. It arrives encrypted (BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on macOS) and enrolled in device management.
3. Set your password (minimum 14 characters) and register MFA on the Nimbus Authenticator app. You cannot access email, Slack or the HRMS without MFA.
4. Attend the 2-hour induction session run by People Operations at 11:00 am.

### 6.2 Week 1

- Complete the mandatory e-learning modules on the Learning Portal within 7 calendar days: Information Security Awareness, Code of Conduct, POSH Awareness and Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023).
- Meet your manager for your first 1:1 and agree 30-60-90 day goals.
- Meet your onboarding buddy, who is assigned before you join.

### 6.3 Week 2

- Get access to project tools (Jira, Confluence, GitHub) through the ServiceDesk portal; access is granted only on your manager's approval.
- Submit bank and PF/UAN details in the HRMS by the 20th of the month to be paid in that month's payroll.

## 8. Our values

- **Clarity over cleverness.** Write things down. Prefer the simple design.
- **Own the outcome.** If you see a problem, you own it until someone else explicitly does.
- **Earn client trust daily.** Our clients run warehouses, hospitals and stores on what we build.
- **Respect, always.** We have zero tolerance for harassment and discrimination; see the *Code of Conduct* and the *POSH Policy*.
