# Board Meeting Minutes – 14 May 2026

Minutes of the 68th meeting of the Board of Directors of Nimbus Infotech Pvt. Ltd., held on Thursday 14 May 2026 at 10:30 IST at the registered office, 7th Floor, Tower B, Panchshil Tech Park, Hinjewadi Phase 2, Pune 411057, with the option to attend by video conference.

| Name | Designation | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Vikram Kulkarni | Founder, CEO and Managing Director (Chairman) | In person |
| Revathi Subramaniam | Independent Director; Chair, Audit Committee | In person |
| Dr. Sunita Bhide | Independent Director; Chair, Nomination and Remuneration Committee | Video conference |
| Gautam Sethi | Nominee Director (Tidewater Growth Partners) | In person |
| Meera Iyer | Whole-time Director and Chief Technology Officer | In person |

**In attendance (invitees):** Rohan Bhatia (CFO), Anjali Deshpande (Chief People Officer, item 7), Kavya Reddy (VP Delivery, item 6), Sameer Khan (CISO, item 8), and Pooja Shetty (Company Secretary).

Quorum: all five directors present; quorum confirmed. Mr. Vikram Kulkarni chaired the meeting.

## 2. Leave of absence and declarations

No leave of absence was requested. Directors confirmed there was no change in their disclosures of interest. Mr. Gautam Sethi noted his interest as nominee of Tidewater Growth Partners in item 7 (ESOP) and did not vote on it.

## 3. Confirmation of previous minutes

The minutes of the meeting held on 12 February 2026 were confirmed and signed.

## 4. FY2025-26 audited financial results

The CFO presented the audited financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026. Key figures:

| Measure | FY2025-26 | FY2024-25 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue from operations | ₹1,180.0 crore | ₹1,035.1 crore | +14.0% |
| EBITDA | ₹226.6 crore | ₹190.5 crore | +19.0% |
| EBITDA margin | 19.2% | 18.4% | +0.8 pts |
| Profit after tax | ₹147.3 crore | ₹124.8 crore | +18.0% |
| Headcount at year end | 4,200 | 3,860 | +340 |

The Audit Committee chair reported that the committee had reviewed the statements with the statutory auditors on 11 May 2026 and recommended adoption; the auditors had issued an unmodified opinion.

**Resolution 68.1:** RESOLVED THAT the audited standalone financial statements for FY2025-26 be and are hereby approved, and that the Managing Director and CFO be authorised to sign them.

## 5. FY2026-27 budget

The CEO and CFO presented the FY2026-27 budget and headcount plan. Highlights: revenue target of **₹1,340 crore** (+13.6%), EBITDA margin target 19.5%, closing headcount 4,780, and capital expenditure of ₹78 crore including the Hyderabad campus expansion.

Discussion:

- Mr. Qureshi asked about North America concentration (62% of FY26 revenue). The CEO noted the Middle East pipeline and the Data & AI practice as diversification levers; the Board asked for a quarterly concentration report.
- Ms. Narayanan asked about the Harbor Logistics renewal due 30 September 2026, as Harbor is a top-5 account. The CFO confirmed that the budget assumes renewal at no less than USD 3.9 million annual value and that a downside case without renewal reduces revenue by about ₹33 crore.
- Dr. Bhide asked that attrition targets be included in the quarterly HR dashboard.

**Resolution 68.2:** RESOLVED THAT the FY2026-27 annual budget with a revenue target of ₹1,340 crore, as presented, be and is hereby approved.

## 6. Hyderabad campus expansion

Ms. Kavya Reddy presented the proposal to expand the Hyderabad (HITEC City) delivery centre by leasing an additional 1.6 lakh sq ft, adding approximately 2,000 seats, with capital expenditure of ₹45 crore for fit-out, IT infrastructure and a new SOC floor. The Board noted that Hyderabad seat occupancy had reached 94% on peak days.

The Board asked management to negotiate a lock-in period of no more than 5 years and to phase the fit-out so that phase 2 spending can be deferred if FY27 hiring is below plan.

**Resolution 68.3:** RESOLVED THAT capital expenditure of up to **₹45 crore** for the Hyderabad campus expansion be and is hereby approved, and that the CEO and CFO be jointly authorised to execute the lease and related contracts, subject to a lock-in period not exceeding 5 years.

## 7. ESOP pool top-up

Ms. Anjali Deshpande presented a proposal to top up the Nimbus Employee Stock Option Plan 2019 pool by **1.5%** of the fully diluted share capital, to support retention of L5 and above and key hires for the Data & AI practice. The current pool has 0.4% unallocated. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee had recommended the proposal on 7 May 2026.

**Resolution 68.4:** RESOLVED THAT, subject to approval of the shareholders by special resolution, the ESOP pool be increased by 1.5% of the fully diluted share capital, and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee be authorised to grant options from the enlarged pool. (Mr. Qureshi abstained.)

The Board approved convening the Annual General Meeting on 24 July 2026, including the special resolution for the ESOP top-up.

## 8. Information security and risk update

Mr. Sameer Khan reported:

- ISO 27001:2022 surveillance audit completed in April 2026 with zero major and two minor non-conformities, both closed.
- The NimbusOps SOC 2 Type II report for the period ending 31 March 2026 was issued with no exceptions.
- Phishing simulation click rate fell to 3.1% from 5.4% a year earlier.
- DPDP Act 2023 readiness programme 80% complete; target completion 30 September 2026.

The Board noted the update.

## 9. Any other business

- The CEO informed the Board that the full regional disaster recovery drill for NimbusOps on 18 April 2026 had passed (RTO 47 minutes against a 1-hour target).
- No dividend was recommended for FY2025-26 in view of the campus capital expenditure.

## 10. Next meeting

The next Board meeting is scheduled for 12 August 2026 in Hyderabad, combined with a site visit to the HITEC City delivery centre.

The meeting concluded at 13:40 IST with a vote of thanks to the Chair.
