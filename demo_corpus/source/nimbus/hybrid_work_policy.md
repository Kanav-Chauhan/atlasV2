# Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

Nimbus works in a hybrid model: people spend part of the week in a Nimbus office and part working remotely. This policy sets the minimum office attendance, the working hours, and the rules for working from home, so that teams get the benefits of both.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees in grades L1 to L7 based in Pune, Bengaluru and Hyderabad. It does not apply to:

- Employees deployed full-time at a client site, who follow the client's attendance rules.
- 24x7 NOC and SOC shift staff in Managed Services, who follow the shift roster.
- The Dubai sales office, which follows a separate local arrangement.

## 3. Working hours

1. The standard working week is **Monday to Friday, 9.5 hours a day including breaks**.
2. Core collaboration hours are **11:00 am to 4:00 pm IST**. Meetings with internal colleagues should be scheduled inside these hours.
3. Teams supporting North American clients may agree a shifted day (for example 1:00 pm to 10:30 pm IST). Shifted hours must be agreed with the manager in writing and recorded in the HRMS.
4. A night-shift allowance applies only to rostered shifts covering 10:00 pm to 6:00 am, as described in the *Employee Benefits Guide*.

## 4. Office attendance

### 4.1 Minimum attendance

Every employee in scope works from the office **3 days a week**. Attendance is measured by badge swipe and averaged over each calendar month, so a week with 2 office days can be balanced by a week with 4.

### 4.2 Anchor days

Each business unit sets two anchor days on which the whole team is expected in the office. The default anchor days are **Tuesday and Wednesday**. A unit head may choose different anchor days by informing People Operations at least 30 days in advance.

### 4.3 Monthly compliance

| Monthly average office days | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 3.0 or more per week | Compliant | None |
| 2.5 to 2.9 | Below expectation | Manager conversation |
| Below 2.5 for 2 consecutive months | Non-compliant | Written note from HR Business Partner; considered in annual review |

Approved leave days, public holidays and business travel days are excluded when calculating the average.

## 5. Working from home

### 5.1 Expectations

- Be reachable on Slack and phone during core hours.
- Work from a quiet place with a stable connection of at least 50 Mbps. Nimbus reimburses up to ₹1,000 per month towards internet costs (see the *Employee Benefits Guide*).
- Connect to internal systems only through the company VPN as described in the *Remote Access and VPN Guideline*.
- Keep your screen out of view of family members or visitors when handling Confidential or Restricted data.

### 5.2 Working from another city

Employees may work remotely from a city other than their base location for up to **20 working days per financial year**, with manager approval in the HRMS at least 5 working days in advance. Working from outside India is not permitted without prior approval from the CISO and the Chief People Officer, because of client contractual and data-residency obligations.

## 6. Exceptions

| Situation | Arrangement | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Pregnancy (from second trimester) | Full remote working until maternity leave starts | Manager + HR Business Partner |
| Return from maternity leave | Up to 2 office days per week for the first 6 months | Manager |
| Medical condition (with doctor's certificate) | Reduced office days for up to 3 months, renewable | HR Business Partner |
| Caregiving for a seriously ill family member | Full remote for up to 1 month | Manager + HR Business Partner |
| Persons with disability | Agreed individually | Chief People Officer |

Requests are raised through the HRMS under *Hybrid Exceptions* and decided within 5 working days.

## 7. Office facilities

- Desks in Pune, Bengaluru and Hyderabad are booked through the Desk Booking app, up to 14 days ahead.
- Unused bookings are released at 11:00 am if you have not swiped in.
- Cafeteria meals are subsidised on office days; the shuttle service runs on all working days in Pune and Hyderabad.

## 8. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Employee | Meet attendance, keep calendar and HRMS status accurate |
| Manager | Agree team rhythms, approve exceptions, discuss non-compliance |
| People Operations | Publish monthly attendance reports by the 5th of each month |
| Admin | Seat capacity, desk booking, transport |
