# Hyderabad Campus Expansion Proposal

Submitted to the Board of Directors for the meeting of 14 May 2026.

## 1. Recommendation

Approve capital expenditure of **₹45 crore** to lease and fit out an additional **1.6 lakh sq ft** next to our existing Hyderabad delivery centre in HITEC City, adding approximately **2,000 seats** in two phases, including a new Security Operations Centre (SOC) floor.

## 2. Why Hyderabad, why now

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Current Hyderabad headcount (31 March 2026) | 1,050 |
| Current seats | 1,150 (1.1 lakh sq ft) |
| Peak-day seat occupancy (Tue–Thu) | 94% |
| Planned headcount, 31 March 2027 | 1,360 (+310) |
| Planned headcount, 31 March 2029 | ~2,600 |

1. **Capacity.** With the hybrid policy of 3 office days per week we plan at 0.8 seats per employee. At 1,360 people we need about 1,090 seats on an average day but over 1,280 on peak days, above current capacity.
2. **Talent.** Hyderabad gives us the strongest hiring pipeline for SAP S/4HANA, Data & AI and SOC analysts. Offer acceptance rates there were 71% in FY26 versus 63% in Bengaluru.
3. **Cost.** Fully loaded seat cost in Hyderabad is about 18% lower than in Bengaluru.
4. **Clients.** Zenith Retail (SAP), Al Noor Healthcare (SOC) and the Crestline data team are already delivered substantially from Hyderabad.

## 3. Options considered

| Option | Description | 5-year cost (₹ crore) | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Expand Pune instead | 132 | Pune campus has 600 spare seats only from FY28; weaker SAP and SOC talent pool |
| B | **Lease adjacent building in HITEC City (recommended)** | 118 | Same campus, shared amenities, phased fit-out |
| C | Managed co-working seats in Hyderabad | 154 | Fast but 30% costlier per seat and unsuitable for the SOC |
| D | Do nothing; increase seat sharing to 0.6 | — | Breaks hybrid policy commitments; limits hiring |

## 4. Proposal detail

### 4.1 Premises

- Building: Tower 3, Skyview Corporate Park, HITEC City, Hyderabad, connected to our current building by a covered walkway.
- Area: 1.6 lakh sq ft across 4 floors.
- Rent: ₹78 per sq ft per month (warm shell), 4.5% annual escalation; maintenance ₹14 per sq ft per month.
- Lease: 9 years with a **lock-in of 5 years**; 3 months' rent-free fit-out period.
- Security deposit: 6 months' rent (₹7.5 crore, refundable; not part of capex).

### 4.2 Phasing

| Phase | Floors | Seats | Ready by | Capex (₹ crore) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | 2 floors + SOC floor section | 1,050 | 1 December 2026 | 27.0 |
| Phase 2 | 2 floors | 950 | 1 July 2027 | 18.0 |
| **Total** | **4 floors** | **2,000** | | **45.0** |

Phase 2 spending can be deferred by up to 9 months if FY27 hiring is below plan.

### 4.3 Capital expenditure breakdown

| Item | ₹ crore |
|---|---|
| Interior fit-out, furniture, HVAC, electrical | 31.2 |
| IT and network (cabling, Wi-Fi, meeting room AV, redundant ISP links) | 8.6 |
| SOC floor (video wall, secure access zone, separate power and UPS) | 3.4 |
| Contingency (4%) | 1.8 |
| **Total** | **45.0** |

### 4.4 The SOC floor

The current Hyderabad SOC has 48 analyst positions and serves Al Noor Healthcare and 9 other managed SOC clients. The new floor provides **120 analyst positions**, a client briefing room and an isolated incident war room, allowing us to bid for regulated BFSI SOC clients that require a dedicated, access-controlled facility. The SOC floor will be designed to ISO 27001:2022 physical security controls and reviewed by the CISO, Sameer Khan.

## 5. Financials

| ₹ crore | FY27 | FY28 | FY29 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capex | 27.0 | 18.0 | — |
| Additional rent and maintenance (opex) | 5.9 | 17.4 | 18.2 |
| Depreciation (fit-out over 9 years) | 1.2 | 4.4 | 5.0 |
| Seats enabled (cumulative) | 1,050 | 2,000 | 2,000 |
| Incremental revenue supported | 48 | 210 | 330 |

- Cost per seat (capex): ₹2.25 lakh.
- Payback on capex: about **3.2 years**, assuming 75% of new seats are used by billable staff at the planned utilisation of 79%.
- Funding: internal accruals. Cash and investments were ₹212 crore at 31 March 2026; no borrowing required.

## 6. Sustainability and people

- Building is IGBC Gold rated; we will target 30% of power from renewable sources through the landlord's open-access agreement.
- Facilities include a crèche for 40 children, a 220-seat cafeteria, a gym and dedicated wellness rooms.
- Shuttle services from Miyapur, Kukatpally and Gachibowli metro stations, plus women's late-shift transport as per our safety policy.

## 7. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Hiring below plan leaves seats empty | Phase 2 deferral clause; sublet option for up to 1 floor |
| Fit-out delays | Fixed-price design-and-build contract with liquidated damages of 0.5% per week |
| Lease lock-in risk | Lock-in capped at 5 years, as required by the Board |
| Concentration of delivery in one city | Business continuity plan with Pune and Bengaluru as alternates; SOC failover to Pune |

## 8. Governance

- Steering group: Kavya Reddy (sponsor), Rohan Bhatia (CFO), Head of Administration, Sameer Khan (CISO), Pradeep Joshi (IT infrastructure).
- Monthly progress report to the CEO; quarterly to the Board.
- Contracts above ₹5 crore require joint CEO and CFO signature.

## 9. Board decision

This proposal was approved by the Board on 14 May 2026 (Resolution 68.3) with a capex limit of ₹45 crore and the condition that the lease lock-in does not exceed 5 years.
