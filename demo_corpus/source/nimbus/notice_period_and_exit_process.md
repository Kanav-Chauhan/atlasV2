# Notice Period and Exit Process

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the notice periods that apply when an employee resigns or is separated, the conditions for early release or notice buy-out, and the steps of the exit process through to full and final settlement.

## 2. Scope

All permanent employees in India in grades L1 to L7. Probationers are covered in section 4. The Dubai office follows UAE labour law.

## 3. Notice periods

| Grade | Notice period (employee resignation) | Notice period (company-initiated, other than for misconduct) |
|---|---|---|
| L1 Trainee to L5 Engineering Manager / Architect | 60 days | 60 days or pay in lieu |
| L6 Senior Manager / Principal and L7 Director | 90 days | 90 days or pay in lieu |

The notice period is counted in calendar days from the day after the resignation is submitted in the HRMS.

## 4. Probation

New joiners are on probation for 6 months. During probation the notice period on either side is **15 days**. Probation may be extended once by up to 3 months, with written reasons.

## 5. Resignation

1. Submit the resignation in the HRMS (*Separation > Resign*). Emails alone are not accepted as a resignation.
2. The manager must hold a conversation with the employee within **3 working days**, and record whether a retention discussion took place.
3. The manager and HR Business Partner agree the last working day (LWD) within 7 working days.
4. A resignation may be withdrawn within 15 days of submission with manager and HR Business Partner approval.

## 6. Early release and notice buy-out

| Option | Condition | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Early release without recovery | Knowledge transfer complete and project not affected | Manager + business unit head |
| Notice buy-out by employee | Shortfall days recovered at gross monthly fixed pay ÷ 30 per day | Manager + HR Business Partner |
| Adjusting earned leave against notice | Not permitted | — |
| Extension of LWD by mutual agreement | Up to 30 days beyond notice | Manager + HR Business Partner |

Leave may be taken during notice only with manager approval and does not shorten the notice period.

## 7. Knowledge transfer

The manager prepares a knowledge transfer (KT) plan within 5 working days of resignation. The plan lists:

- Systems, repositories and client contacts to hand over.
- The named successor or backup for each item.
- KT sessions with dates; recordings stored in the project space.

For client-facing roles, the delivery manager informs the client of the transition at least 30 days before the LWD where the contract requires it.

## 8. Exit checklist

| Step | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| KT sign-off | Manager | 3 working days before LWD |
| Return laptop, security keys, ID card, access card | Employee to IT/Admin | On or before LWD |
| Return corporate card; settle travel advances | Employee to Finance | On or before LWD |
| Revoke client access and remove client VPN profiles | Delivery manager | On LWD |
| Disable SSO and email account | IT | LWD, 6:00 pm |
| Exit interview | HR Business Partner | In the last week |
| Library, assets and dues clearance | Admin, Finance | Within 5 working days after LWD |

Email for leavers is not forwarded. An out-of-office reply naming the new contact runs for 30 days, after which the mailbox is archived.

## 9. Exit interview

Every leaver is offered an exit interview, either with the HR Business Partner or through an anonymous online survey. Themes are reported to the leadership team every quarter and used in retention planning.

## 10. Full and final settlement

1. Full and final (F&F) settlement is paid within **45 days** of the LWD, after clearance is complete.
2. F&F includes salary up to LWD, encashment of earned leave (up to 30 days, on basic pay), gratuity if 5 years of service are complete, reimbursements due, and any pro-rata variable pay that the Variable Pay Plan allows; less notice shortfall recovery, unrecovered advances and dues.
3. The relieving letter and experience letter are issued within 7 days of F&F, by email from hr-help@nimbus.example.
4. PF transfer or withdrawal is initiated by the employee through the EPFO portal; HR approves within 7 working days.

## 11. Company-initiated separation

- **Performance:** only after a documented performance improvement plan has been completed and not met.
- **Misconduct:** after a disciplinary inquiry; no notice or pay in lieu is due for proven gross misconduct.
- **Redundancy:** handled under applicable law with at least the notice period above, plus severance as approved by the Chief People Officer.

## 12. Garden leave

For leavers joining a competitor or with access to Restricted information (for example, compensation or board papers), the company may place the employee on garden leave for all or part of the notice period, with full pay and no system access.

## 13. Rehire

Former employees who left in good standing may be rehired. Prior service counts for gratuity only if the rehire is within 12 months.
