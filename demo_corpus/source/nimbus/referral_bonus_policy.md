# Referral Bonus Policy

## 1. Purpose

Our employees know great engineers. This policy rewards employees who refer candidates who are hired and stay with Nimbus. Referred hires join faster, cost less to recruit and stay longer than other hires: in FY2025-26, 31% of lateral hires came through referrals.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Who can refer

All permanent employees in grades L1 to L5 are eligible for a referral bonus.

### 2.2 Who is not eligible

- Employees in grades L6 and L7.
- Talent Acquisition team members and HR Business Partners.
- The hiring manager for the role, and anyone in the hiring manager's interview panel for that role.
- Employees who are serving notice at the time the referred candidate joins.

### 2.3 Which candidates qualify

- Lateral hires into permanent roles at L2 and above.
- Not eligible: campus hires (L1), interns, contractors, rehires of former employees who left less than 12 months ago, and candidates already in Nimbus's applicant database in the last 6 months.

## 3. Bonus amounts

| Grade of the referred hire | Referral bonus |
|---|---|
| L2 Software Engineer | ₹40,000 |
| L3 Senior Software Engineer | ₹60,000 |
| L4 Tech Lead | ₹80,000 |
| L5 Engineering Manager / Architect | ₹1,00,000 |
| L6 Senior Manager / Principal and above | ₹1,50,000 |

### 3.1 Hot-skill premium

Talent Acquisition publishes a *hot-skills list* each quarter (for FY2026-27 Q1: SAP S/4HANA finance, Kafka/streaming, Kubernetes platform engineering, and security operations). Referrals hired into a hot-skill role earn an additional **₹25,000**.

### 3.2 Diversity hiring

Referrals of women candidates into L4 and above technical roles earn an additional ₹20,000, to support our goal of 30% women in technical leadership by FY2028.

## 4. Payment

1. The bonus is paid after the referred employee completes **90 days** of continuous service, in the next monthly payroll.
2. If the referrer leaves Nimbus before the 90-day mark, no bonus is paid.
3. The bonus is taxable as part of salary; TDS is deducted at source.
4. If two employees refer the same candidate, the first referral recorded in the referral portal with a CV attached counts.

## 5. How to refer

1. Log in to the referral portal (HRMS > *Refer a Friend*).
2. Choose the open requisition, or the general pool if no role fits.
3. Upload the candidate's CV and confirm you have the candidate's consent to share their details, as required by the *Data Protection Policy – DPDP Act 2023*.
4. Track status in the portal. Talent Acquisition contacts the candidate within 5 working days.

Referrals sent by email to recruiters are not tracked and are not eligible.

## 6. Service levels

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Referral acknowledged | 2 working days |
| First screening | 5 working days |
| Feedback to referrer on outcome | 10 working days after final interview |

## 7. Referral campaigns

Talent Acquisition may run time-bound campaigns with higher bonuses for specific roles (for example, the Hyderabad campus expansion). Campaign terms are published on the intranet and override this table for the roles and dates stated.

## 8. Integrity rules

- Referring a candidate in exchange for payment or any benefit from the candidate is gross misconduct.
- Referrers must declare any family relationship with the candidate under the *Code of Conduct*; relatives may be referred but cannot report into the referrer's chain.
- Talent Acquisition may withhold a bonus if the referral process was not followed.

## 9. Disputes

Disputes about referral credit are raised with the Talent Acquisition lead within 30 days of the candidate's joining date. The Manager – HR Operations decides, and the decision is final.

## 10. Budget and reporting

The FY2026-27 referral bonus budget is ₹2.4 crore. HR Operations reports referral hires, bonuses paid and 1-year retention of referred hires to the leadership team every quarter.
