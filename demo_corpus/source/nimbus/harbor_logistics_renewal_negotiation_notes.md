# Harbor Logistics Renewal Negotiation Notes

Working notes for the renewal of the Harbor Logistics MSA and SOW-HL-002. **Internal only – do not forward.** Draft v0.4, updated after the call with Harbor procurement on 19 August 2026.

## 1. Situation

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Current annual contract value | USD 4.2 million |
| Current term ends | 30 September 2026 |
| Auto-renewal | None; services continue month-to-month for up to 90 days at current rates if not renewed |
| Account gross margin FY26 | 34.1% |
| Relationship | Sponsor (Margaret Ellison, CIO) supportive; procurement (Linda Park) pushing hard on price; Tom Reyes (VP Infrastructure) still raising INC-2291 |
| Competition | Harbor has requested pricing from two other offshore providers for managed operations only |

The migration factory (USD 1.65 million of current ACV) winds down after the Memphis exit (target 31 December 2026). Without new scope, run-rate revenue would drop to about USD 2.55 million from FY28. The renewal must therefore expand scope, not just extend term.

## 2. Harbor's position (from 19 August call)

1. Opening ask: **12% reduction** on all rates for the renewal term.
2. Wants service credit cap raised from 10% to 20% of the monthly fee, citing INC-2291.
3. Prefers a 2-year term; open to 3 years "if the price is right".
4. Wants named key personnel for the NOC lead and the engagement lead (Daniel Brooks).
5. Interested in the freight visibility data platform but has no budget approved before January 2027.

## 3. Our proposal (v0.4)

| Component | Year 1 (Oct 2026 – Sep 2027) USD | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Migration completion (waves 10–14) | 620,000 | Fixed price, ends Dec 2026 |
| Managed cloud operations (expanded for Memphis workloads) | 1,620,000 | USD 135,000/month |
| Application support L2 (37 → 52 applications) | 1,080,000 | T&M capped |
| FinOps and landing zone | 340,000 | Fixed fee |
| Freight visibility data platform – discovery and pilot | 640,000 | Starts Jan 2027, subject to Harbor budget |
| **Year 1 total** | **4,300,000** | |

- Term: **3 years**, total contract value approx. **USD 12.4 million** with 3% annual escalation in years 2 and 3.
- Rate reduction offered: **4%** on managed operations and application support rates versus current contract, in exchange for the 3-year term. Within Regional Sales Director authority, but logged with VP Sales.
- NimbusOps edge monitoring for 41 distribution centres offered at Enterprise plan with the 10% volume tier.

## 4. Negotiation limits (authorised)

| Lever | Opening | Target | Walk-away / limit | Approver at limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rate reduction | 4% | 6% | **8%** maximum | VP Sales (Arjun Nair), per 5–10% band |

| Term | 3 years | 3 years | 2 years only with no rate reduction | VP Sales |

| Payment terms | Net 45 | Net 45 | Net 60 (valued as 1% discount) | VP Sales |
| Deal gross margin | 33% | 31% | **30%** floor | Below 30% → CFO; below 28% → CEO |

Rohan Bhatia (CFO) has confirmed by CRM note on 20 August 2026 that he will **not** approve a combined discount above 10% for this renewal. Anything beyond that would need Vikram Kulkarni, and Vikram has said he will not consider it unless Harbor commits to the data platform.

## 5. Give-gets

| If Harbor asks for | We ask for |
|---|---|
| More rate reduction (above 6%) | 3-year term plus signed data platform pilot SOW |
| Higher service credit cap | Exclusion of client-caused and third-party outages; 2-person review commitment from Harbor CAB |
| Named key personnel | 30-day replacement window and Harbor agreement to retention-bonus cost sharing (USD 45,000) |
| Net 60 payment | Annual prepayment for NimbusOps licences |

## 6. Handling INC-2291

- Acknowledge openly; present the 7 corrective actions (5 closed by 15 July, remaining 2 closed by 31 July 2026).
- Point to Q1 FY27 metrics: rollback rate down to 3.4%, average cutover downtime 2.6 hours.
- Do not offer a further goodwill credit beyond the USD 17,500 already applied.
