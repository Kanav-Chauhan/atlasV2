# Discount Approval Policy

## 1. Purpose

Discounts win deals but erode margin permanently, because a discounted rate becomes the client's baseline at renewal. This policy defines who may approve price reductions on services and NimbusOps subscriptions, the margin floors that apply, and the documentation required, so that every discount is deliberate and traceable.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all new contracts, renewals, extensions, change requests and credit notes for all geographies (North America, India, Middle East and Europe), and to all Nimbus service lines and NimbusOps. It applies to anyone who prepares or negotiates commercial proposals.

## 3. Definitions

- **List price:** the rates in the current Rate Card FY2026-27 (NIM/SAL/GDL-001) or the NimbusOps price list.
- **Discount:** the percentage reduction of the total contract value (TCV) against list price, including free resources, free months, rate reductions, waived fees and non-standard payment terms valued by Finance.
- **Deal gross margin:** (revenue − direct delivery cost) ÷ revenue, calculated by Finance using the pricing model template `PRC-v7`.
- **Cumulative discount:** all discounts on the same client in the same financial year are added together for approval purposes.

## 4. Approval matrix

| Discount on TCV | Approver | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 5% | Regional Sales Director | Deal gross margin ≥ 32% |
| Above 5% and up to 10% | VP Sales of the region (e.g. Arjun Nair for North America) | Deal gross margin ≥ 30% |
| Above 10% and up to 15% | Chief Financial Officer (Rohan Bhatia) | Deal gross margin ≥ 28% |
| Above 15% | Founder & CEO (Vikram Kulkarni) | Any margin; CFO recommendation required |

**Margin overrides the discount band.** Regardless of the discount percentage:

| Deal gross margin | Minimum approver |
|---|---|
| 28% – 30% | CFO |
| 25% – 28% | CEO |
| Below 25% | Not permitted, except strategic entry deals approved by the CEO and reported to the Board |

Approval requests for NimbusOps subscriptions follow the same matrix, but the margin test uses NimbusOps contribution margin with a floor of 60%.

## 5. Non-price concessions

These concessions are valued by Finance as discounts and need approval at the resulting level:

| Concession | Valuation rule |
|---|---|
| Free resources or "shadow" FTE | Full rate card value |
| Payment terms longer than net 45 days | 1% of TCV for every additional 15 days |
| Service credit cap above 10% of monthly fee | Treated as a 2% discount |
| Uncapped liability or liability above 12 months' fees | Requires CFO and legal approval regardless of price |
| Rate lock beyond 12 months (no annual escalation) | Treated as a 3% discount per year locked |

## 6. Standard annual escalation

All multi-year contracts must include an annual rate escalation: **minimum 4% for India**, **3% for North America**, and **3% for Middle East/Europe**. Removing or reducing the escalation requires approval as per Section 5.

## 7. Process

1. The account executive builds the price in `PRC-v7` and submits it in the CRM (opportunity stage "Proposal") with the discount reason.
2. Finance validates margin within **2 business days**.
3. The approver approves or rejects in the CRM. Email approvals are not valid unless recorded in the CRM within 1 business day.
4. Approvals are valid for **60 days** or until the proposal expires, whichever is earlier.
5. Signed contracts are checked by Finance against the approved price before countersignature; any variance requires re-approval.

## 8. Acceptable reasons for discount

- Multi-year commitment (3 years or more).
- Volume above 50 FTE or USD 3 million / ₹25 crore annual value.
- Strategic logo entry in a target industry (insurance, healthcare, retail in FY27).
- Competitive displacement with documented competitor pricing.
- Bundling of NimbusOps with services.

"The client asked for it" is not an acceptable reason on its own.

## 9. Credit notes and service credits

Contractual service credits (e.g. for missed SLAs) are not discounts and follow the contract, but any goodwill credit beyond the contract is a discount under this policy. Credit notes above USD 10,000 or ₹8 lakh need CFO approval.

## 10. Reporting

Finance reports monthly to the leadership team: total discount given by region, average discount by approver level, deals below 30% margin, and approvals granted after the proposal was sent (a policy breach). The FY26 average discount was 6.8% of TCV; the FY27 target is **no more than 6%**.

## 11. Breaches

Proposals sent to clients without required approval are a breach of this policy. The first breach results in a written warning; repeated breaches affect variable pay and may lead to disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct.
