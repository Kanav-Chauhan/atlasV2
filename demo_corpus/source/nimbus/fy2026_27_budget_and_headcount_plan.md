# FY2026-27 Budget and Headcount Plan

| Measure | FY2026-27 budget | FY2025-26 actual | Change |

| Revenue | 1,340.0 | 1,180.0 | +13.6% |
| EBITDA | 261.3 | 226.6 | +15.3% |
| EBITDA margin | 19.5% | 19.2% | +0.3 pts |
| Profit after tax | 166.0 | 147.3 | +12.7% |

### 4.3 Hiring assumptions

- Planned voluntary attrition: **16%** (FY26: 17.8%), i.e. about 720 exits.
- Gross hiring required: about **1,300**, of which 450 campus trainees (L1) joining July–September 2026.
- Lateral hiring priority skills: Data & AI (ML engineering, GenAI), SAP S/4HANA, cloud security, SOC analysts.
- Target billable utilisation: 79% (excluding trainees).
- Hiring is released quarterly against booked revenue; the CFO may freeze up to 30% of open positions if bookings fall more than 10% below plan in any quarter.

## 9. Governance

Performance against budget is reviewed monthly by the leadership team and quarterly by the Board. Reforecasts are issued at the end of Q2 and Q3. Any single unbudgeted expenditure above ₹2 crore requires CFO and CEO approval; above ₹10 crore requires Board approval.
