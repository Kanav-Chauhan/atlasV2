# FY2025-26 Annual Financial Summary

For the financial year 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. Figures are audited, standalone, in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise. Presented to the Board on 14 May 2026.

## 1. Highlights

- Revenue grew **14% to ₹1,180 crore** (FY2024-25: ₹1,035.1 crore).
- EBITDA margin improved to **19.2%** from 18.4%, helped by higher utilisation and NimbusOps growth.
- Profit after tax rose 18% to ₹147.3 crore.
- Headcount reached **4,200** on 31 March 2026, up from 3,860.
- Cash and investments of ₹212 crore at year end, with no long-term borrowings.

## 8. Commentary

**Growth.** North America grew 12.2%, led by cloud migration and data engagements. India was the fastest-growing region (19.2%) on the back of the Zenith Retail SAP programme and two new BFSI clients. Middle East and Europe grew 14.0%, mainly managed SOC services from the Dubai sales office.

**Margins.** The 0.8 percentage point EBITDA improvement came from utilisation (+2.3 pts) and a richer NimbusOps mix, partly offset by wage increases and a 23.9% increase in cloud and licence costs as NimbusOps scaled.

**NimbusOps.** Revenue of ₹70.8 crore (+24%), with ~140 customers and net revenue retention of 116%. NimbusOps cloud cost was 24% of its revenue; the FY27 target is 21% or lower.

**Working capital.** DSO improved by 5 days to 61 days after collections process changes in North America.

## 9. Risks noted for FY2026-27

1. Client concentration in North America (62% of revenue) and renewal of a top-5 account (Harbor Logistics) due 30 September 2026.
2. Rupee movement: every ₹1 change in USD/INR affects annual revenue by about ₹8.7 crore.
3. Wage inflation for Data & AI skills.
4. Hyderabad capacity constraints (seat occupancy above 90% on peak days).
