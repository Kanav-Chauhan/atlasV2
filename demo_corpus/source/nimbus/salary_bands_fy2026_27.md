# Salary Bands FY2026-27

## 1. Purpose

This document publishes the fixed-pay salary bands for each Nimbus grade for FY2026-27 and explains how they are used in offers, promotions and merit increases. Salary bands are Restricted information: they may be shared only with HR, Talent Acquisition leads and the leadership team, and must not be forwarded to employees or candidates.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all permanent employees in India in grades L2 to L7. L1 Trainees are paid a fixed campus package (section 5). The Dubai office uses AED bands set separately by the CFO and Chief People Officer.

## 3. Definitions

- **Fixed pay:** annual fixed cash compensation, including basic pay, HRA, special allowance, employer PF contribution and flexible benefits. It excludes variable pay, ESOPs, gratuity, insurance premiums and one-time bonuses.
- **CTC:** fixed pay plus target variable pay.
- **Band midpoint:** the market reference point for a fully proficient employee in the grade.
- **Compa-ratio:** an employee's fixed pay divided by the band midpoint.

| Grade | Title | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L2 | Software Engineer | 6.0 | 8.0 | 10.0 |

| L4 | Tech Lead | 18.0 | 23.0 | 28.0 |
| L5 | Engineering Manager / Architect | 28.0 | 35.0 | 42.0 |
| L6 | Senior Manager / Principal | 42.0 | 51.0 | 60.0 |
| L7 | Director | 60.0 | 75.0 | 90.0 |

In words: L2 is ₹6–10 lakh, L3 ₹10–18 lakh, L4 ₹18–28 lakh, L5 ₹28–42 lakh, L6 ₹42–60 lakh and L7 ₹60–90 lakh per annum fixed.

## 5. L1 Trainees

Campus trainees (L1) receive a fixed package of ₹4.8 lakh per annum during the 6-month training programme. On moving to L2 they are placed at ₹6.0 lakh, the L2 band minimum, unless the Talent Acquisition lead approved a higher entry point for a specialised campus programme.

## 6. Variable pay by grade

Variable pay is described in full in the *Variable Pay Plan*. Target variable pay as a percentage of CTC:

| Grade | Target variable pay (% of CTC) |
|---|---|
| L2 – L3 | 10% |
| L4 | 12% |
| L5 | 15% |
| L6 | 18% |
| L7 | 20% |

## 7. Using the bands

### 7.1 Offers to new hires

| Candidate profile | Offer position |
|---|---|
| Meets role requirements | Minimum to midpoint |
| Exceeds requirements, scarce skill (hot-skills list) | Up to 110% of midpoint |
| Above 110% of midpoint | Needs Chief People Officer approval |
| Above band maximum | Not permitted; consider the next grade |

### 7.2 Promotions

A promotion increase is **12% to 18%** of fixed pay, and must bring the employee to at least the minimum of the new band. If 18% is not enough to reach the new minimum, the increase may go up to the new minimum with business unit head approval.

### 7.3 Employees above band maximum

Employees whose fixed pay is above the band maximum receive merit increases only as a one-time lump sum, not added to fixed pay, as described in the *Merit Increase Guidelines FY27*.

### 7.4 Employees below band minimum

Employees below the minimum of their band (usually after a promotion or a market correction) are brought to the minimum within 12 months, in at most two steps.

## 8. Market benchmarking

Bands are set using two Indian IT services salary surveys (fictional survey providers retained by Total Rewards) with the reference market set at the **60th percentile** for L2–L5 and the **50th percentile** for L6–L7. FY2026-27 bands moved up by an average of 6% compared with FY2025-26:

| Grade | FY2025-26 band | FY2026-27 band | Change at midpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| L2 | 5.5 – 9.5 | 6 – 10 | +5.9% |
| L3 | 9.5 – 17 | 10 – 18 | +5.7% |
| L4 | 17 – 26 | 18 – 28 | +7.0% |
| L5 | 26 – 40 | 28 – 42 | +6.1% |
| L6 | 40 – 57 | 42 – 60 | +5.2% |
| L7 | 57 – 85 | 60 – 90 | +5.6% |
