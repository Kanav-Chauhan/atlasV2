# Holiday Calendar FY2026-27

## 1. Purpose

This calendar lists the public holidays Nimbus observes in each Indian office for the financial year 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. Every employee receives **12 public holidays** in the financial year. The list differs by city because state holidays differ; employees follow the calendar of their base location as recorded in the HRMS.

## 2. General rules

1. Public holidays are in addition to earned leave and casual/sick leave under the *Leave Policy FY2026-27*.
2. Holidays falling on a Saturday or Sunday are not substituted with another day. For FY2026-27, Independence Day (Saturday, 15 August 2026) and Kannada Rajyotsava (Sunday, 1 November 2026) fall on weekends; they are listed in section 6 for information but are not counted in the 12.
3. Employees deployed at a client site follow the client's holiday calendar for that deployment, with the same total of 12 days. The project manager publishes the client calendar to the team before 15 April.
4. Employees working on a public holiday at business request (for example during a go-live or on the NOC/SOC roster) receive a compensatory off, to be taken within 60 days.
5. Employees travelling to another Nimbus city follow their **base** calendar, not the host city's.

## 7. Dubai sales office

The Dubai office follows the UAE public holiday calendar announced by the UAE government, adjusted for moon sightings, plus Diwali (9 November 2026) as a company holiday. The total remains 12 days. The office manager circulates confirmed dates by email at least 7 days before each holiday.

## 8. Moon-sighting changes

Dates of Bakri Eid and Muharram depend on the sighting of the moon. If the state government notifies a different date, People Operations will move the holiday and email all employees in the affected city at least 2 working days before the original date. The HRMS calendar is updated at the same time.

## 9. Managed Services shift staff

NOC and SOC staff are rostered across holidays. Each shift employee still receives 12 holidays in the year: holidays worked are credited as compensatory offs in the HRMS by the shift manager within 3 working days of the holiday.
