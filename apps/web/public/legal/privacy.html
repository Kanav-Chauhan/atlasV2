Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Atlas (p1.kanav.space) answers questions from a company's own documents. It is built and run by Kanav Chauhan in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Atlas handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions: kanavchauhan08@gmail.com.

1. Who this applies to

  • Account users: people who sign in to Atlas for their company (admins, managers, members, guests).
  • Demo visitors: people who try the demo with the made-up companies. The demo needs no account.
  • Website visitors: people who use an Atlas chat bubble on a company's website.

When a company uses Atlas for its staff, that company decides which documents and people are added. For that content the company is responsible for its own data, and we process it on the company's behalf.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile name and picture. Google shares only your name, email and profile picture with us; we never see your Google password.
  • Company content: documents your company uploads, the departments and people it sets up, and who may see which documents.
  • Questions and answers: the questions you ask, the answers Atlas gives, the documents it cites, and any thumbs up or down you give.
  • Website chat: questions a visitor types into a company's chat bubble and the answers. We do not ask website visitors for their name or email.
  • Usage and technical data: pages visited and a few product events (for example "question asked"), a random ID stored in your browser, your IP address (used for rate limits and security), and server logs.

We do not use advertising cookies, we do not record your screen, and we do not collect what you type anywhere other than the question box.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you only the documents you are allowed to see.
  • To answer your questions with sources from your company's documents.
  • To keep Atlas secure: stop abuse, enforce rate limits and investigate problems.
  • To improve answer quality and fix errors, using question and answer records and aggregated usage numbers.
  • To show company admins simple insights (for example how many questions went unanswered).
  • To contact you about your account, the waitlist, or important changes.

We do not sell your information and we do not use your company's documents to train AI models.

4. Services that help run Atlas

We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Clerk: sign-in, accounts and the waitlist (name, email, sign-in details).
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • AI model providers (currently NVIDIA, Groq and Cloudflare, reached through our own gateway): your question and the matching document passages are sent to write the answer. Anthropic may be used for some answers in future.
  • LangSmith: records of how each answer was produced (question, passages used, answer), so we can find and fix mistakes.
  • PostHog: anonymous page views and product events, without cookies or session recording.
  • Our server provider: hosts the servers where documents, accounts and chats are stored.
  • PayU: if paid plans are introduced, payment details are handled by PayU; we do not store card numbers.

Some of these services store data outside India (for example in the United States or the European Union). We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and company data: while the account is active. After you or your company closes it, we delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep it longer by law.
  • Questions and answers: until the company deletes them or closes its account.
  • Website chat memory: the conversation context is cleared after 24 hours; question and answer records are kept for the company's insights as above.
  • Demo: everything done in the demo companies is wiped every night.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited time for security, then deleted.

6. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Each person can only search documents their company allows them to see, and this is checked on the server for every question. Access tokens for AI agents are stored only as secure hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected users about a serious breach as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information, or to withdraw consent, by emailing kanavchauhan08@gmail.com. If your account belongs to a company, we may pass the request to that company's admin, who controls its data. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and respond within a reasonable time. You can also remove Atlas's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account settings.

8. Children

Atlas is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for important changes we will tell account users by email or in the app.

10. Contact and grievances
