4. Earned leave

4.1 Entitlement

1Every full-time employee earns 18 working days of earned leave in each leave year, credited at the rate of 1.5 days at the start of each calendar month. Employees on probation earn leave at the same rate but may use it only after confirmation.

4.2 Carry forward

Unused earned leave of up to 30 days may be carried forward to the next leave year. Any balance above 30 days lapses on 31 March.

Earned leave cannot be combined with casual leave in the same spell, except with the written approval of the reporting manager and HR.
