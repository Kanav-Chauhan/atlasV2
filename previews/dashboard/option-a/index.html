4. Earned leave

4.1 Earned leave applies to all permanent employees from their date of joining.

4.2 Employees earn 18 days of earned leave a year, credited at 1.5 days on the first working day of each month.

4.3 Unused earned leave carries forward, up to a maximum balance of 30 days. Any balance above 30 days lapses on 31 March.

4.4 Earned leave cannot be encashed while in service, except as set out in section 9.
