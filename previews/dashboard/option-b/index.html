What is the salary band for an L4 Tech Lead?

You asked at 10:42

The salary band for an L4 Tech Lead is ₹18 to 28 lakh per annum 1. Where an offer falls inside the band depends on the appraisal rating and the market review done each April 2.

Offers above the top of the band need approval from the Head of HR and the CFO 1.
